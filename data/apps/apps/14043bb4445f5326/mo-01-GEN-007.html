7
1 Wẽnnaam yeela a Nowe yaa: «Kẽ koglgã pʋgẽ, foo ne f pagã la f zakã rãmb fãa, tɩ bõe, mam yãame tɩ fo yaa nin-tɩrg mam taoor zamaan kãngã pʋgẽ. 2 Yãgs rũm-sõma wã buud fãa raad a yopoepoe la yãans a yopoepoe, la rũms nins sẽn ka sõma wã raoog ne yãanga, raoog ne yãanga, 3 la bõn-yɩgdsã me raad a yopoepoe la yãans a yopoepoe, tɩ bãmb buud kel n zĩnd tẽngã zugu. 4 Rasm a yopoe sãn looge, mam na n kɩtame tɩ saag ni tẽng zug rasm pis-naase, wĩntoog ne yʋngo. La mam na n menesa bõn-vɩɩs nins fãa mam sẽn da maanã tɩ b yi tẽngã zugu.» 5 La a Nowe maana bũmb fãa wa Zu-soabã sẽn yeel yẽ wã. 6 Sãoong ko-kãsengã sẽn wa n na n wa tẽng zugã, rẽ t'a Nowe tara yʋʋm kobs-yoobe. 7 A Nowe talla a kom-dibli wã ne a pagã la a kambã pagbã n wa kẽ koom-koglgã pʋgĩi n põs sãoong ko-kãsengã koom. 8 Rũm-sõma wã ne rũms nins fãa sẽn ka sõma wã, la liuuli wã ne bũmb nins fãa sẽn kẽnd tẽngã zugã 9 waa a Nowe nengẽ yiib yiibu n kẽ koglgã pʋgẽ: yɩɩ raoog ne yãang wa Wẽnnaam sẽn da yeel a Nowe wã. 10 Rasm a yopoe sẽn pidi, sãoong ko-kãsengã koom waa tẽng zugu. 11 A Nowe yʋʋm kobs-yoobã wakate, kiuug a yiib soabã rasm piig la a yopoe raare, bog-zulums ko-nin pusame, la saas kʋɩlems pagsame. 12 La saagã nii tẽngã zug rasm pis-naase, wĩntoog ne yʋngo. 13 Baraar bala, a Nowe ne a kom-dibli a Sɛm ne a Kam la a Zafɛt la a pagã la a kambã pagb a tãabã tũu bãmb n kẽ koglgã pʋgẽ. 14 Yɩɩ bãmb ne we-rũms buud fãa la yir dũms buud fãa la bõn-kẽnds buud fãa sẽn kẽnd tẽngã zugu, la liuuli buud fãala bũmb buud fãa sẽn tar pɩgsa. 15 Bõn-vɩɩs buud fãa sẽn tar vɩɩm vʋʋsmã waa a Nowe nengẽ yiib yiibu, n kẽ koglgã pʋgẽ. 16 La bõn-vɩɩs nins fãa sẽn kẽ wã yɩɩ raoog ne yãanga, raoog ne yãang wa Wẽnnaam sẽn yeel a Nowe wã. La Zu-soabã paga a Nowe koglgã pʋgẽ. 17 La sãoong ko-kãsengã nii tẽngã zug rasm pis-naase. La koomã lebga wʋsgo, n dɩk koglgã. La koglgã zẽkame. 18 La koom paasame n zẽk wʋsg tẽngã zugu. La koglgã ra rugda koomã zugu. 19 Koomã zẽka wʋsg tẽngã zugu, hal n lim tãens nins fãa sẽn ya-b lug-lug n yɩɩd fãa saagã tẽngrã. 20 Koomã lima tãensã tɩ b be koomã tẽngr hal sẽn tat kãntɩɩg piig la a nu. 21 La bõn-vɩɩsã fãa sẽn da kẽnd tẽngã zugã kiime: Bõn-yɩgdesã, yir dũmsã, we-rũmsã, bõn-vɩɩs nins sẽn tũud taab wʋsg n saagd tẽngã zugã la ninsaalbã fãa 22 Bõn-vɩɩs nins fãa sẽn da vʋʋsd ne yõor n be tẽngã zugã kiime. 23 La bõn-vɩɩs nins fãa sẽn da be tẽngã zugã menemame, sẽn sɩng ne ninsaalb tɩ ta rũms la bõn-saads ne bõn-yɩgdsã fãa menema tẽng zugu. Yaa a Nowe ne neb nins sẽn da be ne yẽ koglgã pʋgẽ wã bal n kelle. 24 Ko-kãsengã zĩnda tẽngã zug rasm koabg la pis-nu.
