14
1 Zu-soabã goma ne a Moyiiz n yeele 2 «Yeel Israyɛll nebã tɩ b leb poorẽ n sig Pi-Hahirot taoore, Midgall ne mogrã sʋka, zĩig sẽn teesd Baall-Tesefon. Sig-y mogrã noor n tees zĩ-kãngã. 3 Farawõ na n yeelame: Israyɛll nebã saadame n gilimd weoogã pʋgẽ. 4 Mam na n kenga Farawõ sũur t'a babs bãmba. La mam na n paama waoogre Farawõ ne a tãb-biisã fãa yĩnga, la Ezipt rãmbã na bãng tɩ mam yaa Zu-soabã.» La Israyɛll nebã maana wa bãmb sẽn yeelã. 5 La b sẽn togs Ezipt rĩma tɩ nebã zoeeme, yẽ ne a poorẽ dãmbã yam teka nebã yell poorẽ, la b yeelame: «Yaa bõe la tõnd sẽn maan woto n bas Israyɛll nebã tɩ b loog n bas tõnd tʋʋmã?» 6 Farawõ kõo noor tɩ b segl yẽ mobill weef sẽn takdã, la yẽ peega a nebã tɩ b tũ yẽ. 7 Yẽ rɩka mobil-dãmb wiid sẽn takda kobs-yoobe sẽn ya sõma wʋsg n paas Ezipt soolem mobil-dãmb fãa wiid sẽn takda. La zabr tãnsob-n-dãmb bee bãmb fãa pʋsẽ. 8 La Zu-soabã kenga Farawõ sẽn ya Ezipt dẽmã sũuri, la yẽ babsa Israyɛll nebã. La Israyɛll nebã ra yii ne pãng nug sẽn zẽke. 9 La Ezipt rãmba babsa bãmba, yaa Farawõ wiidã ne a mobil-dãmb wiid sẽn takda la a wed-rʋʋdba ne a tãb-bi-kʋʋngã. La Bãmb paama Israyɛll nebã b sikã zĩigẽ koomã noor sẽn be Pi-Hahirot n teesd Baall-Tesefon. 10 Farawõ sẽn kolge, Israyɛll nebã nakame n yã Ezipt rãmbã sẽn pʋged bãmb n watẽ. La rabeem wʋsg yõka bãmba. La Israyɛll nebã zẽka b koees n bool Zu-soabã. 11 La bãmb yeela a Moyiiz yaa: «Yaa yaad zĩig n ka be Ezipt tẽng tɩ yãmb tall tõnd n wa tɩ d ki weoogẽ ka bii? Yaa bõe tɩ yãmb maan tõnd woto n yiis tõnd Ezipt soolmã pʋgẽ? 12 Tõnd da ka reng n gom ne yãmb Ezipt soolmã pʋgẽ n yeel yãmb tɩ y bas tõnd tɩ d tʋm Ezipt nebã tʋʋm bii? Tõnd sãn tʋm Ezipt rãmba tʋʋmã, yõwã n são tõnd sẽn na n ki weoogẽ wã.» 13 La a Moyiiz yeela nebã yaa: «Da zoe-y rabeem ye. Yaas-y n ges-y Zu-soabã sẽn na n fãag yãmb dũnna. Tɩ bõe, Ezipt rãmba yãmb sẽn ne rũnna wã, yãmb ka na n le yã bãmb yɛs abada ye. 14 Zu-soabã na n zab n kõ yãmba. La bɩ yãmb sẽnd sɩɩ.» 15 La Zu-soabã soka a Moyiiz yaa: «Yaa bõe tɩ fo zẽkd f koɛɛg n boond maam? Yeel Israyɛll nebã tɩ b yik n dɩk sore. 16 La bɩ fo zẽk f rasaarã n tẽeg f nugã mogrã zug tɩ Israyɛll nebã tũ tẽn-kʋɛɛng mogrã pʋgẽ n looge. 17 La mam na n kenga Ezipt rãmba sũyã tɩ bãmb pʋgl Israyɛll nebã poore. La mam na n paama waoogre Farawõ ne a tãb-bi-kʋʋngã fãa yĩnga, la yẽ mobil-dãmb wiid sẽn takda ne a wed-rʋʋdba yĩnga. 18 La mam sãn paam waoogre Farawõ ne a mobil-dãmb wiid sẽn takda la a wed-rʋʋdba yĩnga. Ezipt rãmba na bãng tɩ mam yaa Zu-soabã.» 19 Dẽ, Wẽnnaam malɛkã sẽn da be Israyɛll nebã taoorã lebgame n leb bãmb poorẽ. La sawaod-taokã yii bãmb taoorã n tɩ yals bãmb poorẽ, 20 la a yalsa Ezipt nebã ne Israyɛll nebã sʋka. Sawaodgã lebga libsg yʋngã tɛka. La yʋngã tɛka, Ezipt rãmba ne Israyɛll dãmba ka tõe n kolg taab ye. 21 A Moyiiz tẽega a nug mogrã zugu, la yʋngã tɛka, Zu-soabã kɩtame tɩ sob-kãseng yi yaang n pãrg koomã, la mogrã pʋgẽ lebga kʋɛɛnga. 22 La Israyɛll nebã kẽna tẽn-kʋɛɛng zug mogrã pʋga. La koomã lebga wa lalg bãmb dɩtg la b goabga. 23 La Ezipt rãmba pʋgla bãmb n kẽ mogrã pʋgẽ, yaa Farawõ wiidã ne a mobil-dãmb wiid sẽn takda la a wed-rʋʋdbã fãa. 24 Beoog vẽegr wakate, Zu-soabã sẽn be sawaod-taokã pʋgẽ wã sulgame n ges Ezipt rãmba. La bãmb kɩtame tɩ rãmbg zĩnd Ezipt rãmbã sʋka. 25 Bãmb kɩtame tɩ Ezipt rãmba mobil-dãmb wiid sẽn takda nao sud hal tɩ b kẽnd lebg toogo. La Ezipt rãmba yeelame: «Wa-y tɩ d zoe n bas Israyɛll nebã, tɩ bõe, Zu-soabã zabda ne tõnd n sõngd bãmba.» 26 Zu-soabã yeela a Moyiiz yaa: «Bɩ f tẽeg f nug mogrã zug tɩ koomã leb n lim Ezipt rãmbã ne b mobil-dãmb wiid sẽn takda la b wed-rʋʋdbã.» 27 La a Moyiiz tẽega a nug mogrã zugu, la koomã leb n leba a zĩigẽ zĩ-yẽegr wakate. La koomã sẽn segd Ezipt rãmbã, bãmb lebgame n na n zoe, la Zu-soabã luba bãmb mogrã pʋgẽ. 28 Koomã leb n waame n lim Farawõ tãb-bi-kʋʋngã mobil-dãmb wiid sẽn takda ne b wed-rʋʋdbã fãa sẽn pʋgl Israyɛll nebã poor n kẽ mogrã pʋgẽ wã. Bãmb ned baa a yembr ka põs ye. 29 La Israyɛll nebã kẽna tẽn-kʋɛɛngã zug mogrã pʋga. La koomã yɩɩ wa lalg bãmb goabg la b rɩtgo. 30 Baraare, Zu-soabã fãaga Israyɛll dãmbã Ezipt rãmbã nusẽ. La Israyɛll nebã yãa Ezipt rãmba kũum mogrã noore. 31 La Israyɛll nebã yãa pãn-kãseng ning Zu-soabã sẽn maan Ezipt rãmbã. La nebã zoee Zu-soabã, la bãmb tẽe Zu-soabã ne b tʋmtʋmd a Moyiizi.
