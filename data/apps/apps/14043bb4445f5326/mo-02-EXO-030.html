Wusd yõogr tẽng-kugri
= Yik 37.25-28 =
30
1 «Bɩ f maan tẽng-kugri sẽn na n yõog wusd a zugu. 2 Bɩ f maan-a ne akasiya raado. A woglemã na n yɩɩ kãntɩɩg a ye, a yalmã me kãntɩɩg a ye, a zãntelmã na n yɩɩ kãntɩɩg a yiibu. Maan yɩɩl n ning-a. 3 Lud a zugã ne a kɩrems a yiibã fãa, la a yɩɩlã ne sãnem halaale. La maan sãnem kogr n gilg-a. 4 La maan sãnem tuiids a yiib a kɩrems a yiibã fãa yĩnga, n ning bãmb a kogrã tẽngre a kɩremsã gõdgr zĩisẽ. Bãmb yaa tuiids sẽn na n tui dã-bɩlem n zã tẽng-kugrã. 5 La f maan dã-bɩlem ne akasiya raado, n lud bãmb ne sãnem. 6 Bɩ y rɩgl tẽng-kugrã fu-lillidg ning sẽn lill kaset koglgã taoore, la sẽn be bas-taal zĩigã sẽn be kaset koglgã zugã taoorã. Yaa zĩ-kãng la mam na n seg foom. 7 Bɩ a Aarõ yõog wusd sẽn ya noog a zugu. Yẽ na n yõog-a-la yibeoog fãa yẽ sẽn manegd fɩtl-dãmbã wakate. 8 La a Aarõ sãn na n tugl fɩtl-dãmbã zaabre, bɩ a yõog-a. Yaa woto la yãmb na n yõog wusd wakat fãa Zu-soabã taoor yãmb nebã zamaan tɛka. 9 Bɩ y ra yõog wusd zaalg a zug ye. La bɩ y ra yõog maoong bɩ zom kũunã zug ye, la y ra pʋdg bõn-yũudl kũun a zug me ye. 10 Yʋʋmd fãa pʋgẽ, bɩ a Aarõ yãk yel-wẽnã maoong zɩɩm vugr n ning yẽ yɩɩlã zut n maan paoosg yẽ yĩnga. Yaa vugr yʋʋmd pʋgẽ la yẽ na n maan paoosg a zugu yãmb nebã zamaan-dãmbã fãa. Tẽng-kug-kãng na n yɩɩ bũmb sẽn welg toor Zu-soabã yĩnga.»
Yõor yaoodo
11 Zu-soabã goma ne a Moyiiz n yeel-a yaa: 12 Fo sãn sõd Israyɛll nebã n bãng b sõore, bɩ f kɩt tɩ ned Kam fãa yao Zu-soabã a yõor yaoodo, tɩ bã-loangdg da wa yõk bãmb b sõdbã yĩng ye 13 Ad yaood ned kam fãa b sẽn sõd sẽn tog n yao. Yaa wanzuri ligd zɩslem sẽn zems garm a yopoe la bɛka, sẽn zems sɛk-roogã sikɛl pʋɩ-sʋka. Sikɛl yaa gerã pissi. Yaa yaood bãmb sẽn na n yao Zu-soabã. 14 Neb nins b sẽn na n sõdã fãa, sẽn sɩng ne neb nins sẽn tar yʋʋm pissi la sẽn yɩɩd woto wã na n yaoo Zu-soabã b yõor yaoodo. 15 La wakat ning yãmb sẽn na n yao Zu-soabã yãmb yõor yaoodã, arzɛk rãmbã ka na n yao sẽn yɩɩd ye, la naong rãmbã me ka na n yao sẽn ka ta sikɛl pʋɩ-sʋk ye. 16 Bɩ y reeg wanzuri Israyɛll nebã nengẽ tɩ yɩ b yõor yaood sɛk-roogã tʋʋmã yĩnga, tɩ yɩ tẽegr Israyɛll nebã yĩng Zu-soabã taoore, n maan paoosg yãmb yĩnga.»
Waoosg laaga
17 Zu-soabã goma ne a Moyiiz n yeele: 18 «Maan-y pekr laag ne a naoã ne zũud n dɩgl sɛk-roogã ne tẽng-kugrã sʋka, n ning koom a pʋgẽ. 19 A Aarõ ne a kambã na n yãka koomã be n nudg b nusi la b waas b naoã. 20 Bãmb sãn na n kẽ sɛk-roogẽ wã, bɩ b nudg b nusi la b waas b naoã n da wa ki ye. La bãmb sãn na n kolg tẽng-kugrã n maan b tʋʋmde, n yõog maoong bugum Zu-soabã waoogr yĩnga, 21 bɩ b nudg b nusi la b waas b naoã n da wa ki ye. A na n yɩɩ tõog sẽn ka sɛta, a Aarõ ne a yagensã zamaan fãa yĩnga.»
Kaam sẽn pʋdgd neb zutu
22 Zu-soabã goma ne a Moyiiz n yeele: 23 «Bɩ f rɩk tɩdar buud toor-toore sẽn ya sõma wʋsgo, rɩk miir halaal kilo a nii la garm koabg la pis-nii la a nu, la sinamom sẽn tar yũ-noog sẽn zems miirã zɩslem pʋɩ-sʋka, sẽn dat n togs tɩ kilo a naase la garm pis-wɛ la a tãabo, la roozo sẽn tar yũ noog sẽn zems kilo a naase la garm pis-wɛ la a tãabo, 24 la kasse sẽn zems kilo a nii la garm koabga la pis-nii la a nu, sẽn zems sɛk-roogã sẽn tar n makd bũmb zɩslem, la oliiv kaam sẽn zems litr a yoob la bɛka. 25 La bɩ f rɩk yõwã n maan kaam sẽn welg toore, sẽn na n pʋdg maan-kʋʋdbã zutu. Kalem bãmb tɩ b yɩ tɩdar ka-sõngo wa tɩdar maand sẽn maandã. A na n yɩɩ kaam sẽn welge, b sẽn tar n pʋdgd maan-kʋʋdbã zutu. 26 Yãk ka-kãng n tik sɛk-roogã ne kaset koglgã, 27 la taablã ne a teedã fãa, la fɩtl doagendgã ne a teedã fãa, la tẽng-kugr ning b sẽn na n yõog wusd a zugã, 28 la tẽng-kugr ning b sẽn na n yõog maand bugum a zugã ne teedã fãa 1a pekr laaga me ne a naoorã 29 Bɩ y welg bãmb tɩ b lebg bũmb sẽn welg wʋsgo La bũmb sãn sɩɩs bãmba, a na n welgame. 30 La bɩ f pʋdg kaam a Aarõ ne a kambã zutu, n welg bãmb tɩ b maan maan-kʋʋr tʋʋmd mam waoogr yĩnga. 31 Bɩ f togs Israyɛll nebã tɩ kaam bu-kãng sẽn pʋdgd maan-kʋʋdbã zutã welga mam yĩnga, yãmb nebã zamaan-dãmbã fãa. 32 Bɩ y ra sõog ninsaal ne ka-kãng ye. La bɩ y ra rɩk bũmb tɩ zems wa yẽ sẽn tarã n maan kaam tɩ zems a sẽn ya bũmb ningã ye. A yaa bũmb sẽn welg toore. La bɩ y ges-a wa bũmb b sẽn welg toore. 33 Ned sãn maan kaam bu-kãngã, wall ned sãn ning-a ned sẽn ka maan-kʋʋd zugu, bɩ b yiis-a a buud sʋka.»
Wusdu
34 Zu-soabã yeela a Moyiiz yaa: «Rɩk tɩdar buud toor-toore sẽn boond tɩ estakte ne ongl la galbanum. Yaa tɩdar bu-kãens la tɩdar zom meng menga. Bãmb fãa zɩslem na n zemsa taaba 35 Kalem bãmb n maan wusd sẽn yõogd bugum wa tɩdar-maand sẽn maandã, la sẽn tar yamsem n ya sõma zãnga, la sẽn welg toore. 36 La f yãk-a n to tɩ yɩ zom-bãanega, n yãk-a n ning kaset koglgã taoor sɛk-roogã pʋgẽ, zĩig ning mam sẽn na n seg foomã. A na n yɩɩ bũmb sẽn welg wʋsg yãmb nifẽ. 37 Bɩ y ra maan wusd sẽn zems yẽ maoonegã yãmb mens yĩng ye. Ges-y rẽ wa bũmb sẽn welg toor Zu-soabã yĩnga. 38 Ned sãn maan tɩdar sẽn ya wa yẽ a yũugã noom yĩnga, bɩ b yiis a soab a buudã sʋka.»
