B gɩdga nebã tɩ b da rɩ zɩɩm ye
17
1 Zu-soabã goma ne a Moyiiz n yeele: 2 «Gom ne a Aarõ ne a kambã la Israyɛll nebã fãa n yeel b yaa: Ad bũmb ning Zu-soabã sẽn yeele. 3 Israyɛll ned sãn kʋ loalga, wall pesgo, wall bʋʋg sikã zĩig pʋgẽ wall a yɩnga, 4 n ka tall-a n wa sɛk-zĩigã noor n wa kõ Zu-soabã bãmb doogã noore, zɩɩm yell na n zĩnda a soab zugu. Yẽ raaga zɩɩm, la b na n yiisa a soab a buud sʋka. 5 Yaa sẽn na yɩlg tɩ Israyɛll nebã tall maand nins bãmb sẽn dat n maan weoogã pʋgẽ wã n wa kɩs maan-kʋʋdã t'a kõ Zu-soabã sɛk-zĩigã noore La bãmb na n kʋʋ bãmb tɩ yɩ lagem-n-taar maand Zu-soabã waoogr yĩnga. 6 La bɩ maan-kʋʋdã raag zɩɩmã Zu-soabã tẽng-kugrã sẽn be sɛk-zĩigã noorã zugu, la a yõog kaamã bugum t'a yũug yɩ noog Zu-soabã taoore. 7 La bɩ nebã ra le maan maand n kõ zẽn-dãmbã bãmb sẽn tũudã n maan wa nin-yoaadbã ye. Yõwã yaa tõog bãmb ne b yagensã yĩng wakat fãa. 8 La bɩ f yeel bãmb yaa: Israyɛll ned bɩ sãan sẽn be bãmb sʋka sãn maan maoong sẽn yõogd bugum bɩ maoong buud a to 9 n ka tall-a n wa sɛk-zĩigã noor n kõ Zu-soabã, bɩ b yiis a soab a buud sʋka. 10 Israyɛll ned bɩ sãan sẽn be yãmb sʋka sãn dɩ zɩɩm, baa a sẽn ya buud ning fãa, mam na n loba m neng kɛɛng ne-a, a sẽn dɩ zɩɩmã yĩnga. La mam na n yiisa yẽ a buud sʋka. 11 Tɩ bõe, yĩng vɩɩm bee zɩɩm pʋgẽ. La mam kõo yãmb zɩ-kãng tẽng-kugrã zug tɩ y maan paoosg y vɩɩmã yĩnga. Tɩ bõe, yaa zɩɩm n maand paasg ned vɩɩm yĩnga. 12 Yaa rẽ n kɩt tɩ mam yeel Israyɛll nebã tɩ bãmb ne sãamb nins fãa sẽn be bãmb sʋka ra rɩ zɩɩm ye. 13 Israyɛll ned bɩ sãan sẽn be yãmb sʋka sãn kẽng tãoosgo n paam dũng bɩ bõn-yɩgdg sẽn wãbda, bɩ a raag a zɩɩm n bus ne tom. 14 Tɩ bõe, yĩng fãa vɩɩm bee zɩɩm pʋgẽ. La mam yeela Israyɛll nebã tɩ b ra rɩ bũmb zɩɩm ye. Tɩ bõe, bũmb fãa vɩɩm be a zɩɩm pʋgẽ. 15 La ned sãn dɩ-a, b na n menesa a soaba. La sãn ya tẽngã ned bɩ sãan n dɩ bũmb sẽn ki a toore, wall rũng a to sẽn kʋ, bɩ a pegs a fut la a so koom. La yẽ na n yɩɩ rẽgd hal tɩ ta zaabr n yaool n yɩ yɩlemde. 16 La yẽ sãn ka pegs a futã la a so koom, yẽ yel-wẽnã na n zĩnda a meng zugu.»
