Zʋʋg-n-basã
9
1 Zu-soabã goma ne a Moyiiz Sinayi weoogã pʋgẽ yʋʋm a yiib yʋʋmdã pipi kiuugã b sẽn yi Eziptã zugẽ n yeel yaa: 2 «Bɩ f kɩt tɩ Israyɛll nebã maan Zʋʋg-n-bas sẽn zems a wakate. 3 Ki kãnga rasm piig la a naasã zaabr sẽn zems a wakate. Maan-y-yã-a wa sẽn zems tõodo la minund nins fãa sẽn tũud ne a yellã. »
4 A Moyiiz yeela Israyɛll nebã tɩ b maan Zʋʋg-n-base. 5 Bãmb maana Zʋʋg-n-basã pipi kiuugã rasm piig la a naas raarã zaabr Sinayi weoogã pʋgẽ. Israyɛll nebã maana bũmb nins fãa Zu-soabã sẽn yeel a Moyiizã.
6 La neb n be be n sɩɩs kũum n lebg rẽgd n ka tõe n maan Zʋʋg-n-basã baraar ye. Bãmb kẽnga a Moyiiz ne a Aarõ nengẽ, 7 n tɩ yeel yaa: «Tõnd sɩɩsa kũum n lebg rẽgdo. Yaa bõe tɩ b gɩdg tõnd tɩ d ra naag Israyɛll nebã n wa ne maoong Zu-soabã yĩng wakat sẽn zemse?»
8 A Moyiiz yeela bãmb tɩ b yaas t'a sok n bãng bũmb ning Zu-soabã sẽn na n yeel bãmb yĩnga.
9 Zu-soabã yeela a Moyiiz 10 t'a yeel Israyɛll nebã tɩ bãmb neda, wall b yagens sãn lebg rẽgd kũum yĩnga, wall a sãn tog sor n tɩ be zĩ-zãrgẽ, 11 bɩ a maan Zʋʋg-n-bas Zu-soabã yĩng kiuug a yiib soabã rasm piig la a naas raarã zaabre. Bɩ b rɩ-a ne bur sẽn ka rã-bɩll la zĩ-toose. 12 Bɩ b ra bas t'a kell tɩ ta beoog ye, la b ra kaoog a kõbr baa a ye me ye. Bɩ b maan bũmb nins fãa tõodã sẽn yet tɩ b maan Zʋʋg-n-bas yĩngã. 13 La ned sẽn ka rẽgd la a ka tog sor sãn zãgs t'a ka na n maan Zʋʋg-n-basã, bɩ b yiis a soab nebã sʋka, tɩ bala, yẽ ka maan maoong n kõ Zu-soabã sẽn zems a wakat ye. Yẽ soab na n deega a yel-wẽnã sɩbgre.
14 Sãan sãn zĩnd yãmb sʋka n na n maan Zʋʋg-n-bas Zu-soabã yĩnga, bɩ a maan-a wa sẽn zems tõodã ne minundã. Yaa tõog a ye sãan ne tẽng-n-ned fãa yĩnga.
Sawaodgã bee sɛk roogã zugẽ
15 Daar ning b sẽn tilg sɛk-zĩigã, sawaodg luda sɛk-zĩigã sẽn ya kaset roogã. La a paa be wa bugum zĩ-sobd hal tɩ beoog vẽege. 16 Daar fãa yɩɩ woto. Beoog sãn vẽege, yaa sawaodgo, la a sãn ta yʋngo, a lebgda bugum. 17 Sawaodgã sãn yik n bas roogã, Israyɛll nebã yikdame. La a sẽn tɩ yaas zĩig ninga, Israyɛll nebã sigda be. 18 Zu-soabã sãn yeel Israyɛll nebã tɩ b yiki, b yikdame, la bãmb sãn yeel tɩ b sigi, b sigdame. Wakat ning fãa sawaodgã sẽn be sɛk-zĩigã zugu, b kel n paa b sikã zĩigẽ. 19 Sawaodgã sãn yaas sɛk-zĩigã zugẽ n kaoos rasm wʋsgo, Israyɛll nebã sakda Zu-soabã noor n pa be. 20 La a sãn pa sɛk-roogã zug n kaoos rasm bilfu, bãmb sakda Zu-soabã noor n yaas b sikã zĩigẽ. La b sãn yeel tɩ b yiki, b sakda bãmb noorã n yiki. 21 Naoor kẽere, sawaodgã yaasda zaabre tɩ beoog vẽege. La a sãn zẽk yibeoogo, nebã yikdame. Sawaodgã sãn zẽk wĩntoog bɩ yʋngo, nebã yikdame. 22 Sawaodgã sãn yaas sɛk-roogã zug n kaoos rasm a yiib bɩ kiuug wall yʋʋmde, Israyɛll nebã kel n zĩndida b sikã zĩigẽ n ka yi ye. La a sãn zẽke, bãmb yikdame. 23 Yaa ne Zu-soabã noor la b sigda, la yaa ne Zu-soabã noor la b yikda. Bãmb saka Zu-soabã n maan wa b sẽn yeel a Moyiizã.
