Neb yɩlgr koom
19
1 Zu-soabã kõo a Moyiiz ne a Aarõ tõog n yeel yaa: 2 «Yeel-y Israyɛll nebã tɩ b tall nag-sad-zẽeg sẽn ka tar zanga, la sẽn nan ka tʋm n wa kõ yãmb 3 tɩ y kɩs maan-kʋʋd a Eleyazaar t'a yiis-a we-kɛɛng n kɩt tɩ b kʋ-a a nifẽ. 4 Bɩ maan-kʋʋd a Eleyazaar tẽk a nug-bil nag-sadgã zɩɩm pʋgẽ n mes sɛk-roogã noor sẽnẽ naoor a yopoe. 5 Bɩ b yõog nag-sadgã ne a nemdã la a gãongã ne a zɩɩmã la a bẽndã yẽ nifẽ. 6 La bɩ maan-kʋʋdã rɩk sɛɛdr daad ne hizop la gẽs-miidu n lob nag-sadgã bugmã pʋgẽ. 7 Dẽ poore, bɩ maan-kʋʋdã pegs a futu, la a so koom n yaool n lebg n leb sikã zĩigẽ. Yẽ na n yɩɩ rẽgd hal tɩ ta zaabre. 8 Bɩ ned ning sẽn na n yõog-a nag-sadgã bugmã pegs a futu la a so koom. La a na n yɩɩ rẽgd hal tɩ ta zaabre.
9 Bɩ ned sẽn ya yɩlemd wʋk nag-sadgã tom-peglemã n tɩ rʋʋg zĩ-sõngã sẽn be sik zĩigã rapoorã. Yaa tom-peglemã la b na n yãkd n ningd koomẽ n yɩlgd Israyɛll nebã la b yiisd b kongrã. 10 Bɩ ned ning sẽn wʋkd-a naafã tom-peglemã pegs a futu la a so koom. Yẽ na n yɩɩ rẽgd hal tɩ ta zaabre. Yaa tõog Israyɛll nebã ne sãamb nins sẽn be bãmb sʋkã yĩng wakat fãa.
11 Ned sãn sɩɩs kũum, baa sẽn ya kũum ning fãa, a na n yɩɩ rẽgd hal tɩ ta rasm a yopoe. 12 Bɩ a yɩlg a meng ne ko-kãngã rasm a tãabã raare, la rasm a yopoe wã raar n yɩ yɩlemde. La a sãn ka yɩlg a meng rasm a tãabã raar ne dasem a yopoe wã daare, a ka na n yɩ yɩlemd ye. 13 Ned sãn sɩɩs kũum, baa a sẽn ya kũum buud ning fãa n ka yɩlg a menga, a rẽgemda Zu-soabã sɛk-zĩigã. Bɩ b yiis a soab Israyɛll nebã sʋka. Tɩ bõe, b ka kɩrs-a rẽgd yiisg koom ye. A yaa rẽgdo, a rẽgdã ket n be a zugu.
14 Ad ned sẽn ki roog pʋgẽ tõogo. Ned sãn ki roog pʋgẽ, bũmb nins fãa sẽn be roogã pʋgẽ wã na n yɩɩ rẽged hal tɩ ta rasm a yopoe. 15 Laag sãn zĩnd doogã pʋgẽ tɩ b ka fõgen-a, a na yɩɩ rẽgdo.
16 Ned sãn kẽng weoogẽ tɩ yã tɩ b kʋ neda, wall ned sẽn ki a toore, wall ninsaal kõbr la a yã-yã, wall yaoogo, yẽ sãn sɩɩs-a, a na n yɩɩ rẽgd hal tɩ ta rasem a yopoe.
17 Bɩ b yãk kongrã maoong ning b sẽn yõog bugmã tom-peglem, n ning laag pʋgẽ n paas ko-zoetem, 18 tɩ ned sẽn ya yɩlemd dɩk hizop vãad n suk koomã pʋgẽ n mes roogã ne a teedã fãa, la neb nins sẽn da be roogã pʋgẽ wã, ne ned ning sẽn sɩɩs kõbrã bɩ kũum ned a to sẽn kʋ wall sẽn ki a toore wall yaoogã. 19 Bɩ ned sẽn ya yɩlemd zãgs sẽn ya-a rẽgdã rasm a tãabã ne dasem a yopoe wã daar t'a yɩ yɩlemde. Bɩ a pegs a fut la a so koom, la zĩig sãn sobge, a na n yɩɩ yɩlemde. 20 Ned sãn ya rẽgd n ka yɩlg a menga, bɩ b yiis a soab Israyɛll nebã sʋka, a sẽn dẽgem Zu-soabã sɛk-zĩigã yĩnga. B ka zãgs a rẽgd yiisg koom ye. A yaa rẽgdo. 21 A yaa tõog bãmb yĩng wakat fãa.
Bɩ ned ning sẽn zãgsed rẽgd yiisg koomã pegs a futu. Ned sãn sɩɩs rẽgd yiisg koom, a na n yɩɩ rẽgd hal tɩ ta zĩ-sobdo. 22 Ned sẽn ya rẽgd sãn sɩɩs bũmbu, bũmbã na n lebga rẽgdo. La ned sãn sɩɩs-a, a soab na n yɩɩ rẽgd hal tɩ ta zĩ-sobdo.»
