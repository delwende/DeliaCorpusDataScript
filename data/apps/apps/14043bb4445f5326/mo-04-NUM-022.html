A Balak boola a Balaam
22
1 Israyɛll nebã yikame n kẽng Moab weoogã pʋgẽ, Zʋrdẽ kʋɩlgã rʋʋnde, n tees Zeriko.
2 A Tisippoor biig a Balak yãa bũmb nins fãa Israyɛll nebã sẽn maan Amoor-dãmbã. 3 Moab nebã yɛɛsame. Tɩ bõe, Israyɛll nebã yaa wʋsgo la rabeern yõka Moab nebã.
4 Moab nebã yeela Madiyã nebã kãsem-dãmbã tɩ zãma wã na n dɩɩ bũmb nins fãa sẽn be bãmb tẽngã pʋgẽ wa niig sẽn wãbd mood weoog pʋgẽ.
Yaa a Tisippoor biig a Balak n da yaa Moab rĩm wakat kãnga. 5 Yẽ tʋma neb Petoor sẽn pẽ Efart kʋɩlgã, Ammon nebã tẽngã pʋgẽ tɩ b kẽng a Bewoor biig a Balaam nengẽ n bool a t'a wa. Yẽ yeelame yaa: «Gese, neb n yi Ezipt n wa. Bãmb yaa wʋsg n lud tẽng-gãongã zãnga. Bãmb zĩi mam taoor n yegd maam. 6 Wa masã n wa kãab nin-kãensã n kõ maam, tɩ b yɩɩda maam. Daarninga, mam tõee n tõoga bãmb n dig-b tɩ b yi m tẽngã pʋgẽ. Tɩ bõe, mam miime tɩ ned ning yãmb sẽn ningd bark paamda barka, la ned ning yãmb sẽn kãabd paamda toogo.»
7 Maob kãsem-dãmbã ne Madiyã kãsem-dãmbã talla kũun n kẽng n na n tɩ kõ bãngdã. Bãmb kẽnga a Balaam nengẽ n tɩ togs-a a Balak koɛɛgã.
8 A Balaam yeela bãmb tɩ b gãand be baraar yʋng t'a na n wilga bãmb bũmb ning Zu-soabã sẽn na n leok yẽnda. Dẽ, Moab na-biisã paa yẽ yirã. 9 Wẽnnaam waa n goma ne a Balaam n sok-a tɩ neb nins sẽn be yẽ yirã yaa yɛ nebã.
10 A Balaam yeela Wẽnnaam tɩ yaa a Tisippoor biig a Balak sẽn ya Moab naabã n tʋm bãmb yẽ nengẽ tɩ b wa yeel tɩ yaa: 11 «Gese, neb n yi Ezipt n wa ka. La b yaa wʋsg tɩ looge. Wa-y masã n wa kãab bãmb n kõ maam tɩ m paam n zab ne-b n dig-ba.»
12 Wẽnnaam yeela a Balaam t'a ra kẽnge la a ra kãab nebã me ye. Tɩ bala, nebã yaa bark nebã.
13 Beoogã sẽn vẽege, a Balaam yiime n yeel nanams nins a Balak sẽn tʋmã yaa: «Kuil-y tɩ Zu-soabã yeela maam tɩ m da tũ ye.»
14 Moab nanamsã lebgame n tɩ yeel a Balak t'a Balaam yeelame t'a ka na n tũ n wa ye.
15 A Balak le tʋma nanams wʋsg sẽn tar naam n yɩɩd pĩnd-dãmbã. 16 Bãmb kẽnga a Balaam nengẽ n tɩ yeel-a t'a Tisippoor biig a Balak yeelame yaa: «Ra sak tɩ bũmb gɩdg-f tɩ f ka wa mam nengẽ ye. 17 Wa tɩ m na n kõo foom waoogr wʋsgo, la f sẽn dat tɩ m maan bũmb ning fãa, mam na n maaname n kõ-fo. Wa masã n wa kãab nin-kãensã wẽng n kõ maam.»
18 A Balaam leoka a Balak tẽn-tʋmdbã yaa: «Ba a Balak sãn kõ maam doog sẽn pid ne wanzuri la sãnem, mam ka tõe n maan bũmb baa fɩ n kɩɩs Wẽnnaam noor ye. 19 Bɩ y gãand tɩ m le sok Zu-soabã n ges b sẽn na n yeel tɩ m maan bũmb ninga.»
20 Baraar yʋngo, Zu-soabã waa n yeela a Balaam yaa: «Nebã sẽn wa n na n bool foom woto wã, bɩ f yik n tũ-ba, la f maan mam sẽn na n yeel foom tɩ f maan bũmb ningã bala.»
A Balaam bõanga
21 21Beoogã sẽn vẽege, a Balaam yikame n ning a bõang yolgo, n tũ Moab nanamsã. 22 La Zu-soabã sũur puuga ne yẽ a sẽn kẽngã yĩnga, tɩ b kɩt tɩ malɛk yaas sorã zugu n na n zab ne-a. A Balaam zaoo a bõanga t'a tʋmtʋmdb a yiib be ne-a. 23 Bõangã yãame tɩ Zu-soabã malɛkã yas sorã zug n zãad sʋʋga. Bõanga bʋdga pʋʋgã pʋgẽ, la a Balaam pãb-a lame t'a lebg n leb sorã zugu.
24 Dẽ, malɛkã kẽnga taoor n tɩ yaas so-buk zugu pʋt a yiib sẽn tar lals sʋka, tɩ lals a yiibã be sorã kɩrems a yiibã fãa. 25 Bõangã yãa Zu-soabã malɛkã n dɩdg lalga ne a Balaam naoore, t'a Balaam le pãb-a.
26 Zu-soabã malɛkã kẽnga taoor n tɩ yaas zĩig sor sẽn ya sẽk zugu tɩ weer ka be ned sẽn tõe n bʋdg n loog ye. 27 Bõangã yãa Zu-soabã malɛkã n gãand tẽng ne a Balaam. A Balaam sũur yika wʋsg t'a pãb bõangã ne a rasaare. 28 Dẽ, Zu-soabã kɩtame tɩ bõangã gom n yeel a Balaam yaa: «Mam maana yãmb bõe tɩ y pãb maam naoor a tãab woto?»
29 A Balaam leoka bõanga yaa: «Yaa fo sẽn gãand ne maamã yĩnga. M sãn da tarẽ-la sʋʋga, m da na n kodg-f lame.»
30 Bõangã yeela a Balaam yaa: «Mam yaa yãmb bõang tɩ y zombd maam hal daar zãra. Yãmb nan n yãame tɩ mam maan woto baa raar a yembr sɩda?» A Balaam leokame tɩ ayo.
31 Zu-soabã puka a Balaam nin t'a yã bãmb malɛkã sẽn yas sorã zug n zãad sʋʋgã, la yẽ fõgena a neng tẽnga.
32 Zu-soabã malɛka yeelame yaa: «Yaa bõe tɩ fo pãb f bõangã naoor a tãab woto? Gese, yaa maam n yi n dat n zab ne foom, la f ket n modgedame n wat mam nengẽ. 33 Bõangã yãa maam. Yaa naoor a tãab la a bʋdg n bas maam. A sãn da ka bʋdgẽ, mam da na n kʋʋ foom la m da ka na n kʋ bõangã ye.»
34 A Balaam yeela Zu-soabã malɛkã yaa: «Mam kongame, m da ka bãng tɩ yaa yãmb n yas sorã zug n gɩdgd maam ye. La masã, a sãn ka yell ne yãmba, mam na n lebg n kuilame.» 35 Malɛkã yeela a Balaam yaa: «Bɩ f tũ nebã la f togs goam nins mam sẽn kõ foomã bala.» A Balaam tũu nanams nins a Balak sẽn tʋmã.
36 A Balak wʋmame t'a Balaam waame. Yẽ kẽngame n tɩ seg a Balaam Moab Aar sẽn be toadgã zugã Arnon kʋɩlgã noore. 37 A Balak yeela a Balaam yaa: «Yaa bõe tɩ mam bool yãmb tɩ y wa tɩ y ka wa mam nengẽ? Yãmb tagsdame tɩ mam ka tõe n dol yãmb laa?»
38 A Balaam yeela a Balak yaa: «Gese, mam waa yãmb nengẽ. La m ka tõe n gom goam nins m sẽn dat ye. Yaa goam nins Wẽnnaam sẽn na n ning m noorẽ wã bal la m na n gome.»
39 A Balaam tũu a Balak n kẽng Kiryat-Hutsote. 40 A Balak rɩka niig ne piis n maan maoong n tʋm a Balaam ne nanams nins sẽn be ne-a wã. 41 Dẽ, vẽk-n-beoogo, a Balak peega a Balaam n dʋ tãngã n ta Bamot-Baaalle. La be, yẽ tõee n yãa Israyɛll neb nins sẽn zãr wʋsgã.
