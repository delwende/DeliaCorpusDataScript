Nebã sõadg a yiib soaba
26
1 Bã-loangedgã zugẽ, Zu-soabã yeela a Moyiiz ne maan-kʋʋd a Aarõ biig a Eleyazaar 2 tɩ b sõd Israyɛll nebã fãa sẽn tar yʋʋm pissi la sẽn yɩɩd woto wã wa sẽn zems b buud la b roto. Yaa neb nins fãa sẽn tõe n bob lokã. 3 A Moyiiz ne maan-kʋʋd a Eleyazaar goma ne nebã Moab weoogã pʋgẽ, zĩig sẽn pẽ ne Zʋrdẽ kʋɩlgã n teesd Zeriko wã n yeel-b 4 tɩ b sõd neb nins sẽn tar yʋʋm pissi la sẽn yɩɩda, wa Zu-soabã sẽn yeel a Moyiizã.
Ad Israyɛll nebã sẽn yi Eziptã. 5 A Rubɛn sẽn ya a Israyɛll bi-kãsengã yagens la woto: Henok nebã, yẽ biig a Henok yagense, Pallu nebã, yẽ biig a Pallu yagense, 6 Hetsron nebã, yẽ biig a Hetsron yagense, Karmi nebã, yẽ biig a Karmi yagense. 7 A Rubɛn yagensã rot la woto. Bãmb neb nins b sẽn sõdã yaa neb 43.730.
8 A Pallu biig la a Eliyaabe, 9 la a Eliyaab kamb la a Nemuwɛl ne a Datan la a Abiram. Yaa a Datan kãng ne Abirarn kãng n tigim nebã tɩ b zab ne a Moyiiz ne a Aarõ, la b lagem a Koore ne a nebã n zab ne Zu-soabã, 10 tɩ tẽngã yaag a noor n vel bãmb ne a Koore wakat ning nin-kãens sẽn ki wã, la bugum sẽn dɩ neb kobs-yi la pis-nu wã. La bõn-kãng yɩɩ keoogr nebã yĩnga. 11 La a Koore kambã ka ki ye. 12 Ad a Simeyõ yagense, roog roogo: Nemuwɛl roogã yaa a Nemuwɛl yagense, Zamin roogã yaa a Zamin yagense, Zakin roogã yaa a Zakin yagense, 13 Zerak roogã yaa a Zerak yagense, Sayill roogã yaa a Sayill yagense. 14 A Simeyõ yagensã rot la woto. Bãmb yaa neb 22.200.
15 Ad a Gaad yagense, roog roogo: Zefon roogã yaa a Zefon yagense, Hagi roogã yaa a Hagi yagense, Suni roogã yaa a Suni yagense, 16 Ozni roogã yaa a Ozni yagense, Eri roogã yaa a Eri yagense, 17 Arod roogã yaa a Arod yagense, Areli roogã yaa a Areli yagense. 18 A Gaad yagensã rot la woto. Bãmb neb nins b sẽn sõdã yaa 40.500.
19 A Ɩɛr ne a Onan yɩɩ a Zuda kamba, la b kii Kanaã. 20 Ad a Zuda yagense, roog roogo. Sela roogã yaa a Sela yagense, Perɛɛz roogã yaa a Perɛɛz yagense, Zera roogã yaa a Zera yagense. 21 Ad a Perɛɛz yagense: Ɩzron roogã yaa a Ɩzron yagense, Hamul roogã yaa a Hamul yagense. 22 A Zuda yagensã rot la woto. Bãmb neb nins b sẽn sõdã yaa 76.500.
23 Ad a Issakaar yagense, roog roogo: Tola roogã yaa a Tola yagense, Puwa roogã yaa a Puwa yagense, 24 Zasuub roogã yaa a Zasuub yagense, Simron roogã yaa a Simron yagense. 25 A Issakaar yagensã rot la woto. Bãmb neb nins b sẽn sõdã yaa 64.300.
26 Ad a Zabilõ yagense, roog roogo: Serɛd roogã yaa a Serɛd yagense, Elon roogã yaa a Elon yagense, Zahleyɛl roogã yaa a Zahleyɛl yagense. 27 A Zabilõ yagensã rot la woto. Bãmb neb nins b sẽn sõdã yaa 60.500.
28 Ad a Zozɛf yagens a sẽn paam ne a kamb a Manaase ne a Efaryim, roog roogo: 29 A Manaase yagense: Makiir roogã yaa a Makiir yagense, A Makiir n dog a Galaad. Galaad roogã yaa a Galaad yagense. 30 Ad a Galaad yagense: Iyezɛɛr roogã yaa a Iyezɛɛr yagense, Helɛk roogã yaa a Helɛk yagense, 31 Asriyɛl roogã yaa a Asriyɛl yagense, Sekɛm roogã yaa a Sekɛm yagense, 32 Semida roogã yaa a Semida yagense, Hefɛɛr roogã yaa a Hefɛɛr yagense. 33 A Hefɛɛr biig a Zelofehad ka paam bi-ribl ye, la a tara kom-pugli. Bãmb yʋy la a Makla ne a Noa, la a Hogla ne a Milka la a Tirtsa. 34 A Manaase yagensã rot la woto. Bãmb neb nins b sẽn sõdã yaa 52.700.
35 Ad a Efaryim yagense, roog roogo: A Sutela roogã yaa a Sutela yagense, a Bekɛɛr roogã yaa a Bekɛɛr yagense, a Takan roogã yaa a Takan yagense. 36 Ad a Sutela yagense: A Eran roogã yaa a Eran yagense. 37 A Efaryim yagensã rot la woto. Bãmb neb nins b sẽn sõdã yaa 32.500. A Zozɛf yagensã rot la woto.
38 Ad a Benzame yagense, roog roogo: A Bela roogã yaa a Bela yagense, a Asbɛl roogã yaa a Asbɛl yagense, a Ahiram roogã yaa a Ahiram yagense, 39 a Sufam roogã yaa a Sufam yagense, a Hufam roogã yaa a Hufam yagense. 40 Ad a Bela yagens a sẽn paam ne a Ard la a Naamanne: A Ard roogã yaa a Ard yagense, a Naaman roogã yaa a Naaman yagense. 41 A Benzame yagensã rot la woto. Bãmb neb nins b sẽn sõdã yaa 45.600. 42 Ad a Dan yagense, roog roogo: A Suham roogã yaa a Suham yagense. A Dan yagensã roog la woto. 43 B fãa yaa a Suham roogã neba. Bãmb neb nins b sẽn sõdã yaa 64.400.
44 Ad a Azɛɛr yagense, roog roogo: A Imna roogã yaa a Imna yagense, a Isvi roogã yaa a Isvi yagense, a Beriya roogã yaa a Beriya yagense, 45 la a Beriya pendẽ yagensã la woto: A Hebɛɛr roogã yaa a Hebɛɛr yagense, a Malkiyɛl roogã yaa a Malkiyɛl yagense. 46 A Azɛɛr paama bi-pugla tɩ b boond-a t'a Sera. 47 A Azɛɛr yagensã rot la woto. Bãmb neb nins b sẽn sõdã yaa 53.400.
48 Ad a Neftali yagense, roog roogo: A Zahzeyɛl roogã yaa a Zahleyɛl yagense, a Guni roogã yaa a Guni yagense, 49 a Zezɛɛr roogã yaa a Zezɛɛr yagense, a Sillem roogãa yaa a Sillem yagense. 50 A Neftali yagensã rot la woto. Bãmb neb nins b sẽn sõdã yaa 45.400.
51 Israyɛll raop nins b sẽn sõdã yaa neb 601.730.
52 Zu-soabã yeela a Moyiiz yaa: 53 «Pʋɩ tẽngã n kõ bu-bãmba wa sẽn zems b nebã sõor tɩ b sooge. 54 Sã n ya bu-kãsenga, bɩ f kõ-b zĩ-bedre, la buudã sãn ya bilfu, bɩ f kõ b zĩ-bila. Kõ buud fãa zĩis wa sẽn zems b nebã sõore. 55 La Maan-y poeere, tɩ poeerã yãk buud ne b yaab-rãmbã yʋy n kõ-b zĩisi. 56 Yaa poeerã n na n yãk zĩig n kõ buud fãa, wa sẽn zems buud nins neb sẽn ya wʋsg bɩ bilfã.»
57 Ad Levit rãmb nins b sẽn sõdã, roog roogo: A Gerson roogã yaa a Gerson yagense, a Kohat roogã yaa a Kohat yagense, a Merari roogã yaa a Merari yagense.
58 Ad Levit rãmbã rot me: A Libni roogã a Heborõ doogã a Mahli roogã a Musi roogã a Koore roogã. A Kohat yaa a Amram ba. 59 A Amram pagã yʋʋr la a Zokebɛde, n ya a Levi buud b sẽn dog Levit rãmbã sʋk Ezipti. Yẽ roga a Amram kamba a Aarõ ne a Moyiiz la b tão a Miryam. 60 A Aarõ doga a Nadaab ne a Abihu la a Eleyazaar ne a Itamaare. 61 A Nadaab ne a Abihu kii wakat ning b sẽn kolg Zu-soabã ne bug-zẽngã.
62 Bãmb raop nins fãa sẽn ta kiuug la sẽn yɩɩd woto wã b sẽn sõdã yaa 23.000. B ka mag bãmb ne Israyɛll neb a taabã n sõd ye. Tɩ bõe, b ka kõ bãmb zĩig wa b sẽn kõ Israyɛll neb a taabã ye.
63 Israyɛll neb nins a Moyiiz ne a Eleyazaar sẽn sõd Moab tẽn-nabdrã zĩigẽ sẽn pẽ ne Zʋrdẽ kʋllgã n teesd Zeriko wã yaa woto. 64 A Moyiiz ne maan-kʋʋd a Aarõ da ka naag nin-bãmb ned baa a ye n sõd Sinayi weoogã pʋgẽ ye. 65 Tɩ bõe, Zu-soabã da goma neb nins b sẽn sõd Sinayi weoogã pʋgẽ wã yell n yeel tɩ b na n kii weoogã pʋgẽ. Bãmb ned baa a ye ka kell ye, sãn ka a Zefune biig a Kalɛɛb ne a Nun biig a Zozoe bala.
