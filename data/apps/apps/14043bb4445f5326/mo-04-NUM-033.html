Israyɛll nebã kẽn ne b sigb zĩisi
33
1 Ad Israyɛll nebã sik zĩis sẽn zems b buud fãa ne a zĩiga, wakat ning b sẽn yi Ezipt t'a Moyiiz ne a Aarõ yaa b taoor-dãmbã. 2 A Moyiiz gʋlsa bãmb kẽndã fãa yikr zĩis wa Zu-soabã sẽn yeelã. Ad bãmb kẽndã wa sẽn zems b yikr zĩisi:
3 Pipi kiuugã rasm piig la a nu raare, bãmb yika Ramsɛse. Zʋʋg-n-bas kibsã yɩ tɩ zaame, bãmb yii ne pãn-kãseng Ezipt nebã fãa nifẽ. 4 Ezipt nebã ra mumda b kom-dẽemsã fãa Zu-soabã sẽn kʋ wã. La Zu-soabã sãama bãmb wẽnnaam-dãmbã. 5 Israyɛll nebã yika Ramsɛs n tɩ sig Sukkote, 6 la b yika Sukkot n tɩ sig Etam sẽn be weoogã toadgã zugã. 7 B yika Etam n wẽnemd n tees Pi-Hahirot sẽn be Baall-Tesefon yaangã n tɩ sig Migdol taoore. 8 B yika Pi-Hahirot n dʋʋg mogrã n kẽ weoogã pʋgẽ. B kẽna Etam weoogã pʋgẽ dasem a tãab n tɩ sig Mara. 9 B yika Mara n kẽ Elim n tɩ sig be. Ko-bundem zĩis piig la a yiib ne koms pis-yopoe n be Elim. 10 B yika Elim n tɩ sig n pẽneg mog-miuugã. 11 B yika mog-miuugã noor n tɩ sig Sin weoogã. 12 B yika Sin weoogã n tɩ sig Dofka. 13 B yika Dofka n tɩ sig Alusi. 14 B yika Alus n tɩ sig Refidim. La koom ka be be nebã sẽn na n yũ ye. 15 B yika Refidim n tɩ sig Sinayi weoogã. 16 La Sinayi weoogã pʋgẽ, bãmb yikame n kẽng Kibrot-Hattaava. 17 B yika Kibrot-Hattaava n tɩ sig Hatserote. 18 B yika Hatserot n tɩ sig Ritima. 19 B yika Ritima n kẽng Rimmon-Perɛtse. 20 B yika Rimmon-Perɛts n kẽng Libna. 21 B yika Libna n kẽng Rissa. 22 B yika Rissa n kẽng Kehelata. 23 B yika Kehelata n kẽng n kolg Safɛɛr tãngã. 24 La b yika Safɛɛr tãngã sɛɛg n kẽng Harada. 25 B yika Harada n kẽng Makhelote. 26 B yika Makhelot n kẽng Tahate. 27 B yika Tahat n kẽng Tarake. 28 B yika Tarak n kẽng Mitka. 29 B yika Mitka n kẽng Hasmona. 30 B yika Hasmona n kẽng Moserote. 31 B yika Moserot n kẽng Bene-Yaakan. 32 B yika Bene-Yaakan n kẽng Hoor-Gidgaade. 33 B yika Hoor-Gidgaad n kẽng Zotbata. 34 B yika Zotbata n kẽng Abrona 35 B yika Abrona n kẽng Etsiyon-Gebɛɛre. 36 B yika Etsiyon-Gebɛɛr n kẽng Tisin sẽn ya Kadɛse. 37 B yika Kadɛs n kẽng n kolg Hoor tãngã sẽn be Edom toadgã zugã n sig be. 38 Maan-kʋʋd a Aarõ dʋʋ Hoor tãngã wa Zu-soabã sẽn yeelã n ki be Israyɛll nebã sẽn yi Ezipt tɩ yʋʋm pis-naasnẽ. Yaa yʋʋmdã kiuug a nu soabã pipi raare. 39 A Aarõ da tara yʋʋm koabg la pissi la a tãab wakat ning yẽ sẽn ki Hoor tãngã zugã. 40 Araad naab sẽn ya Kanaã ned n be Kanaã Negɛɛvã wʋma Israyɛll nebã waoong kibare. 41 Israyɛll nebã yika zĩig ning sẽn be Hoor tãngã sɛɛgẽ wã n kẽng Tesalmona. 42 Bãmb yika Tesalmona n kẽng Punon. 43 B yika Punon n kẽng Obote. 44 B yika Obot n kẽng Iye Abarim sẽn be Moab toadgã zugã. 45 B yika Iye Abarim n kẽng Dihon Gaade. 46 B yika Dibon Gaad n kẽng Almon Diblatayimi. 47 B yika Almon Diblatayim n kẽng Abarim tãensã sʋk sẽn be Nebo taoorã. 48 B yikame n yi Abarim tãensã n kẽng Moab weoogã sẽn be Zʋrdẽ kʋɩlgã noor n teesd Zeriko wã. 49 Bãmb kolga Zʋrdẽ kʋɩlgã n sig Bet-Zesimot hal tɩ ta Abɛll Sittim, Moab tẽngã pʋgẽ.
50 Zu-soabã goma ne a Moyiiz Moab tẽngã pʋgẽ, Zʋrdẽ kʋɩlgã noore, zĩig ning sẽn teesd Zeriko wã n yeel 51 t'a yeel Israyɛll nebã yaa: «Yãmb sãn dʋʋg Zʋrdẽ kʋɩlgã n kẽ Kanaã tẽngã, 52 bɩ y rig tẽngã nebã fãa n sãam bãmb bõn-naandes sẽn maan ne kuga, la b sẽn kudã, la y sãam bãmb tẽng-kugã fãa. 53 Reeg-y tẽngã n zĩnd a pʋgẽ, tɩ bõe, mam kõo yãmb tɩ y soog-a. 54 Maan-y poeer n pʋɩ tẽngã wa sẽn zems yãmb buudã. Buud sãn tar neb wʋsgo, kõ-y-b zĩ-bedre. La buud sãn tar neb bilfu, kõ-y-b zĩ-bilfu. Poeerã sãn yãk zĩig n kõ neda, yaa yẽ n so. Yãmb na n paama zĩisã wa sẽn zems y yaab-rãmbã roto.
55 La yãmb sãn ka rig tẽngã neb nins sẽn kellã, bãmb na n yɩɩ wa gõos n kõs yãmb nini, la b yɩ wa pĩim n kõs yãmb seese, la b nams yãmb tẽng ning y sẽn na n kẽ n zĩndã pʋgẽ. 56 La mam na n wa maana yãmb wa mam y sẽn da yeel tɩ m na n maana bãmbã.»
