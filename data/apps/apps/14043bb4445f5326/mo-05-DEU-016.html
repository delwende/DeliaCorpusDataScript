Zʋʋg-n-base
= Yik 12.14-20; Maa 23.4-8; Sõd 28.16-25 =
16
1 «Maan-y Zʋʋg-n-basã kibs Abiib kiuugã pʋgẽ Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam yĩnga, tɩ bõe, bãmb yiisa yãmb Ezipt tẽngã pʋgẽ ne yʋngo, Abiib kiuugã pʋgẽ. 2 Bɩ y yãk rũm-bãooneg ne rũm-bɛd n maane. Zʋʋg-n-bas Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam yĩng zĩig ning bãmb sẽn yãk n ning b yʋʋrã. 3 Ra rɩ-y rã-bɩll ne-a ye. Rɩ-y bur sẽn ka rã-bɩll rasm a yopoe. Yaa namsg buri, tɩ bõe, yãmb yii Ezipt ne yãgbo. Yaa sẽn na n tẽegd yãmb daar ning yãmb sẽn yi Eziptã y yõor tɛka. 4 Rasm a yopoe wã pʋgẽ, bɩ y ra bas tɩ rã-bɩll zĩnd y soolmã pʋgẽ ye. La y ra bas maoong ning sẽn kʋʋd pipi raarã zaabrã baa bilf tɩ kel n vẽeg beoog ye.
5 Yãmb ka tõe n kʋ Zʋʋg-n-bas maoong tẽns nins fãa Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam sẽn kõ yãmb tɩ y zĩndã pʋgẽ ye. 6 Yaa zĩig ning Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam sẽn na n yãk n ning b yʋʋrã la yãmb tog n kʋ Zʋʋg-n-bas maoong wẽndgã kõom wakate, sẽn zems wakat ning yãmb sẽn yi Eziptã. 7 Rʋg-y-yã-a n dɩ zĩig ning Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam sẽn na n yãkã. La beoog sãn vẽege, bɩ y lebg n kuili. 8 Rɩ-y bur sẽn ka rã-bɩll rasm a yoobe. La rasm a yopoe wã raar na n yɩɩ tigung raar Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam waoogr yĩnga. Ra tʋm-y baraar ye.
Rasem a yoppoepoe rãmb kibsa
= Maa 23.15-22; Sõd 28.26-31 =
9 Sõd-y rasm a yopoepoe naoor a yopoe, sẽn sɩng ne zĩ-likri. 10 Maan-y dasem a yopoepoe naoor a yopoe wã kibs n kõ yam-leoog kũun wa sẽn zems bũmb ning Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam sẽn kõ yãmbã. 11 Bɩ yãmb ne y kom-dibli wã la y kom-pugli wã ne y yem-daadã, la y yem-pogsã ne a Levi buudã neb nins sẽn be yãmb tẽnsã pʋsẽ wã, la sãambã ne kɩɩbsã la pʋg-kõap nins sẽn be yãmb sʋkã maan sũ-noog Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam taoore, zĩig ning bãmb sẽn na yãk n ning b yʋʋrã. 12 Tẽeg-y tɩ yãmb da yaa yembs Ezipt tẽngã, la y gũus y mens n sak tõ-kãensã fãa.
Zãn kibsa
= Maa 23.33-43; Sõd 29.12-39 =
13 Yãmb sãn pãb y ki n sɛ, la y wẽk y tɩɩsã biis koom n sɛ, bɩ y maan zãn kibs rasm a yopoe. 14 Bɩ yãmb ne y kom-dibli wã, la y kom-pugli wã ne y yem-daadã, la y yem-pogsã ne a Levi buudã nebã, la sãambã ne kɩɩbsã, la pʋg-kõapã sẽn be yãmb tẽnsã pʋsẽ wã maan sũ-noog y kibsã yĩnga. 15 Maan-y kibsã rasm a yopoe Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam waoogr yĩnga, zĩig ning bãmb sẽn yãkã. Tɩ bõe, Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam na n ninga yãmb bark tɩ y paam koodo, la b sõng yãmb ne y tʋʋmã fãa tɩ y paam sũ-noog wʋsgo.
16 Bɩ yãmb daopã fãa wa Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam nengẽ zĩig ning bãmb sẽn na n yãkã naoor a tãab yʋʋmd fãa pʋgã. Yaa bur sẽn ka rã-bɩll kibsã wakate, la rasm a yopoepoe naoor a yopoe wã kibs wakate, la zãnã kibs wakate. Ra kẽng-y be nug-zaal ye. 17 Bɩ ned kam fãa wa ne kũun wa sẽn zems bark ning Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam sẽn kõ yãmbã.
Bʋ-koaadba
18 Yãk-y neb tɩ b yɩ bʋ-kaoodba la kãsem-dãmb tẽns nins Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam sẽn kõ yãmbã pʋsẽ wa sẽn zems y buudã sõore, tɩ b bʋ nebã bʋʋd ne pʋ-peelem. 19 Bɩ y ra bʋ bʋʋdã kɛg ye, la y ra tũus neb ye. Bɩ y ra reeg kũun ye, tɩ bala, kũun leooda yam-dãmb nini, n golemd nin-tɩrs goama. 20 Mao-y n maan-y tɩrg bala, tɩ yãmb tõe n vɩɩmd n soog tẽng ning Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam sẽn kõ yãmbã.
Wẽnnaam-dãmb a taab pʋʋsgo
21 Ra pees-y ra-lugidg n lugl Zu-soabã tẽng-kugr ning yãmb sẽn luglã sɛɛgẽ ye, 22 la y ra pees kugr n lugl ye, tɩ Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam kis-b lame.
