6
1 B rag n paga Zeriko tẽngã kʋɩlemsã tɩ ned ka tõe n yi be wall a kẽ be ye.
2 Zu-soabã yeela a Zozoe yaa: «Gese, mam dɩka Zeriko ne a naabã la a gãndaadã fãa n kõ foom. 3 Bɩ foo ne f tãb-biisã kẽn n gilg tẽngã vugr daar fãa tɩ ta rasem a yoobe. 4 Kɩt tɩ maan-kʋʋdb a yopoe zã bard a yopoe sẽn maan ne pe-raad yɩɩla n lʋɩ kãabg koglgã taoore. La rasem a yopoe raarã, bɩ y gilg tẽngã naoor a yopoe tɩ maan-kʋʋdbã pee bardã. 5 La yãmb sãn wʋm tɩ b peebd bardã, bɩ nebã fãa kaas ne koe-kãsenga, la tẽngã lalgã na n lʋɩɩ tẽnga. Dẽ, bɩ ned kam fãa kel n tũ a taoor n kẽ.»
6 A Nun biig a Zozoe boola maan-kʋʋdbã n yeel-b yaa: «Tʋk-y kãabg koglgã, la bɩ maan-kʋʋdb a yopoe zã bard a yopoe sẽn maan ne pe-raad yɩɩl n lʋɩ Zu-soabã kãabg koglgã taoore.» 7 La yẽ yeela nebã yaa: «Kẽng-y n gilg-y tẽngã. Bɩ tãb-biisã lʋɩ Zu-soabã kãabg koglgã taoore.»
8 La b maana wa a Zozoe sẽn yeelã. Maan-kʋʋdb a yopoe zãa bard a yopoe sẽn maan ne pe-raad yɩɩl lʋɩ Zu-soabã kãabg koglgã taoor n peebdẽ, la Zu-soabã kãabg koglgã pʋgla bãmb poore. 9 Tãb-biis kẽer lʋɩɩ maan-kʋʋdb nins sẽn peebd bardã taoore, tɩ kẽer pʋgl kãabg koglgã poore. Bãmb kẽndame la b peebd bardã. 10 La a Zozoe yeela nebã yaa: «Ra maan-y bʋrg ye, ra sak-y tɩ ned wʋm y koeesã ye, ra sak-y tɩ gomd yi y noyẽ me ye, hal tɩ ta daar ning mam sẽn na n yeel yãmb tɩ y kaasã. Mam sãn yeel yãmb tɩ y kaas bɩ y kaase.» 11 La woto, a Zozoe kɩtame tɩ Zu-soabã kãabg koglgã gilg tẽngã. B gilgda vugri, la b leb b sikã zĩigẽ n tɩ gãande.
12 Vẽk-n-beoogo, a Zozoe yika yibeoog pĩnda, tɩ maan-kʋʋdbã tʋk Zu-soabã kãabg koglgã. 13 La maan-kʋʋdb a yopoe nins sẽn tar bard a yopoe wã lʋɩɩ taoor n peebd bardã, tɩ tãb-biis kẽer be taoor tɩ kẽer pʋgd kãabg koglgã poore. La bardã peebdame tɩ nebã kẽnda. 14 Rasem a yiib raarã, bãmb le gilga tẽngã vugri, n le leb b sikã zĩigẽ. Bãmb maana woto rasem a yoobe.
15 Dasem a yopoe wã daar yibeoogo, bãmb yikame n gilg tẽngã naoor a yopoe. Yaa rasem a yopoe daarã bal la b gilg tẽngã naoor a yopoe. 16 Gilgr a yopoe soabã, maan-kʋʋdbã peeba bardã, la a Zozoe yeela nebã yaa: «Kaas-y tɩ Zu-soabã kõ-y-la tẽngã. 17 Zu-soabã kisga tẽngã ne bũmb nins fãa sẽn be a pʋgẽ wã. Yaa pʋg-yoodr a Rahaab ne neb nins sẽn be yẽ roogẽ wã bal n na n põse, bala yẽ ra solga neb nins tõnd sẽn da tʋmã. 18 Gũus-y ne bũmb nins sẽn kisã n da wa sɩɩs-b n kɩt tɩ Israyɛll nebã lebg bõn-wẽng n paam toog ye. 19 La wanzuri wã ne sãnemã la zũudã ne kutã yaa Zu-soabã rẽnda. B tog n bĩnga yõwã Zu-soabã laog bĩngr zĩigẽ.»
20 Nebã kaasame la maan-kʋʋdbã peeba bardã. Nebã sẽn wʋm bardã peebgã, bãmb fãa kaasa ne koe-kãsenga, la lalgã lʋɩɩ tẽngã, tɩ nebã tab-a n kẽ tẽngã pʋgẽ. Ned kam fãa tũu a sẽn da be zĩig ningã n kẽ. La b tõoga tẽngã. 21 Bãmb sãama bũmb nins fãa sẽn da be tẽngã pʋgẽ wã. Bãmb kʋʋ pagbã ne raopã la kambã ne nin-kẽembã, la niigã ne piisã la bõensã ne zabr sʋʋse.
22 La a Zozoe yeela neb a yiib nins sẽn da kẽng n tɩ ges tẽngã yaa: «Kẽng-y pʋg-yoodrã roogẽ wã n tɩ yiis pagã ne a buudã fãa wa yãmb sẽn da wẽen ne yẽ wã.» 23 Kom-bɩɩs a yiibã sẽn da kẽng n tɩ ges tẽngã kẽngame n tɩ yiis a Rahaab ne a ba wã, la a ma wã, la a kẽem-dãmbã, la a yaopã, la a nebã fãa. Bãmb talla yẽ buudã fãa n tɩ bas Israyɛll dãmba sik-zĩigã poore.
24 Bãmb yõoga tẽngã ne bũmb nins fãa sẽn be a pʋgẽ wã bugum, yaa wanzuri wã ne sãnemã, la zũudã ne kutã bal la b tall n tɩ bĩng Zu-soabã laogã bĩngr zĩigẽ. 25 La a Zozoe fãaga a Rahaab ne a ba wã zak-rãmba la a nebã fãa. La yẽ bee Israyɛll nebã sʋk hal tɩ ta rũnna, yẽ sẽn da solg neb nins a Zozoe sẽn da tʋm tɩ b tɩ gũus Zeriko wã yĩnga.
26 Baraare, a Zozoe kãaba Zeriko n yeel yaa: «Ned sãn wa n na n me tẽng kãngã, bɩ kãab-wẽng zĩnd a soab zug Zu-soabã taoore. Yẽ sãn yẽbg a lasã, a bi-ribl kãsẽngã na n kiime. La yẽ sãn lug a kʋɩlemsã, a bi-yao wã na n kiime.» 27 Zu-soabã bee ne a Zozoe, la a yʋʋrã saaga zĩigã fãa.
