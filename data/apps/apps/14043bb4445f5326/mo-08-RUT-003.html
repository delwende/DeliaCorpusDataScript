A Rut ne a Boaaz bee zɛɛgẽ
3
1 Dẽ, a Rut sɩdã ma a Naomi yeela yẽ yaa: «M biiga, m ka tog n bao vʋʋsg n kõ foom tɩ f paam sũ-noog sɩda? 2 La a Boaaz fo sẽn tũ a soab tʋmtʋmd-pogs n yiis ki wã yaa tõnd sɩdbã buudu. Gese, bãmb na n yelga ki zɛɛgẽ wã yʋngã. 3 Bɩ f so koom n zae kaam la f yeelg f futu n kẽng zɛɛgẽ wã. La f ra sak tɩ b bãng tɩ yaa foom ye, hal tɩ bãmb dɩ la b yũ n sɛ. 4 La wakat ning bãmb sẽn na n gãannã, bɩ f ges zĩig ning bãmb sẽn gãeyã n kẽng n tɩ pilg bãmb nao wã n gãand be. La bãmb na wilg foo bũmb ning fo sẽn tog n maane.»
5 A Rut yeela yẽ yaa: «Yãmb sẽn yeel maam tɩ m maan bũmb ning bala m na n maaname.» 6 Dẽ, yẽ kẽnga zɛɛgẽ wã n tɩ maan wa a sɩdã ma sẽn yeel yẽ t'a maanã.
7 A Boaaz sẽn dɩ la a yũ, a sũur yɩɩ noog t'a kẽng n tɩ gãand ka-dʋrã sɛɛga. La a Rut soosame n tɩ pilg yẽ nao wã n gãand be. 8 La yʋn-sʋka, rao wã yɛɛsame n wẽnemd n yã tɩ pag n gãand n pɩk a nao wã.
9 La yẽ sokame yaa: «Fo yaa ãnnã?»
La a Rut leokame: «Mam yaa yãmb tʋmtʋmd-poak a Ruti. Bɩ y tẽeg y fuug n pil y tʋm-tʋmpoakã, tɩ bõe, yãmb tara noor tɩ yao n deeg maam.»
10 La a Boaaz leokame yaa: «M biiga, Bɩ Zu-soabã ning fo zug barka. Yaoolem bũmb neer ning fo sẽn maanã yaa neere n yɩɩd bũmb nins fo sẽn da maan pĩndã. Tɩ bõe, fo ka bao rasãmb naong rãmbã wall rakã-rãmb sʋk ye. 11 La masã, m biiga, ra maan sik ye. Mam na n maana bũmb nins fo sẽn yeelã fãa n kõ-fo. Tɩ bõe, mam tẽngã nebã fãa miime tɩ fo yaa pʋg-sõngo. 12 Sɩda, mam tara noor tɩ yao n deeg foom, la baoosgo, ned n be n tar noor n yɩɩd maam. 13 Gãand ka yʋngã, la beoogo, yẽ sãn dat n yao n deeg foom, a na yao n deeg-fo. M wẽen ne Zu-soabã sẽn vɩ wã tɩ yẽ sãn ka rat n yao n deeg foom, mam menga na yao n deeg-fo. Bɩ f gãand ka tɩ ta tõd-beoogo.»
14 La yẽ gãanda a Boaaz nao wã zĩigẽ hal tɩ ta tõd-beoogo. La yẽ yika zĩ-yilim-yilim wakat n kuili. La a Boaaz yeelame tɩ b ra sak tɩ ned bãng tɩ pag n wa zɛɛgẽ wã ye.
15 Yẽ yeelame yaa: «Tẽeg f fu-bilã n gãd sõamã.» La a Rut tẽega a fu-bilã. La a Boaaz maka ki wam a yoob n kõ-a. La a Rut lebgame n leb tẽngã pʋgẽ.
16 La yẽ sẽn ta a sɩdã ma nengẽ, a sɩdã ma sokame yaa: «M biiga, maana wãna?»
La yẽ togsa a Naomi bũmb nins fãa a Boaaz sẽn maan n kõ yẽ wã. 17 La yẽ yeelame yaa: «Bãmb kõo maam ki wam a yoob tɩ be ka, la b yeela maam tɩ m da leb n leb m sɩdã ma nengẽ zaalem ye.»
18 Dẽ, a Naomi yeelame yaa: «M biiga, bɩ f zĩnd bãanem hal n wa bãng yel-kãng sẽn na n yɩ bũmb ninga. Bala, yellã sãn ka paam manegr dũnna, rao wã ka na n vʋʋs ye.»
