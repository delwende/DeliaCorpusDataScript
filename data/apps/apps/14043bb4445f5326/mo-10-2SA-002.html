B ninga a Daviid kaam t'a soog Zuda
2
1 Dẽ poore, a Daviid soka Zu-soaba t'a kẽng Zuda tẽng a yembr bii? Zu-soabã yeelame t'a kẽnge. A Daviid soka Zu-soabã t'a kẽng tẽn-bʋgo. La Zu-soabã yeelame t'a kẽng Heborõ. 2 A Daviid kẽnga be ne a pagb a yiibã. Yaa Zizreyɛl biig a Akinoam ne Karmel biig a Abigayill, yẽ ra yaa a Nabal paga. 3 La a Daviid kɩtame tɩ neb nins sẽn da be ne yẽ wã ned kam fãa peeg a zak-rãmb n kẽng n tɩ zĩnd Heborõ tẽnsã pʋse. 4 Zuda nebã kẽnga Heborõ n tɩ sõog a Daviid rẽung kaam n kɩt t'a lebg Zuda rĩma. B wilga a Daviid tɩ yaa Galaad Zabɛs nebã n mum a Sayilli. 5 La a Daviid tʋma ned bãmb nengẽ ta tɩ yeel yaa: «Bɩ Zu-soabã ning yãmb barka y sẽn maan y Zu-soab a Sayil neer n mum-ba yĩnga. 6 La masã, Bɩ Zu-soabã zoe yãmb nimbãanega n maan yãmb neere. La mam me na n maana yãmb neere yãmb sẽn maan bõn-kãngã yĩnga. 7 La masã, bɩ y nang pãnga n yɩ sũ-keelem-dãmba. Tɩ bõe, yãmb Zu-soab a Sayil kiime, Zuda nebã sõoga maam rẽung kaam n kɩt tɩ m lebg b rĩma.»
Zabr a Daviid roogã rãmb ne a Sayil roogã rãmb sʋka
= 1Kib 3.1-4 =
8 A Neer biig a Abneer yaa a Sayil tãb-biisã naaba. yẽ peega a Sayil biig a Is-Bosɛt n kẽng Mahanayim, 9 n tɩ kɩt t'a lebg Galaad ne Asɛɛr la Zizreyɛl ne Efaryim la Benzame la Israyɛll rãmbã fãa rĩma. 10 A Sayil biiga a Is-Bosɛt tara yʋʋm pis-naas wakat ning yẽ sẽn deeg naamã. La a rɩɩ naamã yʋʋm a yiibu. Zuda nebã kẽe a Daviidi. 11 Daviid yɩɩ Zuda nebã rĩm n zĩnd Heborõ yʋʋm a yopoe la kiis a yoobe. 12 A Neer biig a Abneer ne a Sayil biig a Is-Bosɛt tʋmtʋmdbã yii Mahanayim n kẽng Gabawõ. 13 La a Teseruza biig a Zoab ne a Daviid tʋm-tʋmdbã yiime n seg bãmb Gabawõ bãka noore. A Daviid tʋmtʋmdbã bee bãkã kɩreng a yembre, ta Is-Bosɛt tʋmtʋmdbã be kɩreng a to wã. 14 A Abneer yeela a Zoab yaa: «Bɩ kom-bɩɩsã yik n zab ne taaba.» La a Zoab yeelame yaa: «Bɩ b yiki.» 15 La Benzame neb piig la a yiib n yik a Sayil biig a Is-Bosɛt yĩnga, la a Daviid neb piig la a yiib me n yiki, tɩ b sõorã zems taaba. 16 Bãmb nebã ned kam fãa gãda a zabd-n-taag zug n kõs a sɛɛgã ne sʋʋg tɩ b lagem taab n lʋɩ. Zĩ-kãng pẽe ne Gabawõ. B pʋda a yʋʋr tɩ Helkat-Hatsurim. 17 Baraare, zabrã yɩɩ toog wʋsgo, la a Daviid nebã tõoga a Abneer ne Israyɛll rãmbã. 18 A Teseruza kamb a tãab n be be. Yaa a Zoab ne a Abisayi la a Asayɛlle. A Asayɛll naoã faasa wa walg sẽn be weoogẽ naoã. 19 Yẽ riga a Abneer n ka bʋdg baa bilf ye. 20 A Abneer wẽnegame n ges poorẽ n sok yaa: «Ya a Asayɛll bii?» Yẽ leokame tɩ n-ye. 21 Abneer yeelame t'a bʋdg n yõk tãb-biig a ye n yees a zab-teedo. La a Asayɛll ka rat n bas a Abneer ye. 22 A Abneer le yeela a Asayɛll yaa: «Lebg n bas maam. Mam na n kʋʋ foom n dɩk bõe? Mam sãn kʋ foom, m na n le yɩɩ wãn n doog m neng n ges f kẽem a Zoabe?» 23 A Asayɛll zãgsame n ka bas-a ye, la a Abneer tãoo a pʋgã ne kãnde, tɩ kãndã sãag n tɩ yi a poore, ta lʋɩ n ki zĩ-kãng bala. Neb sãn ta zĩig ning a Asayɛll sẽn ki wã, b yaasdame. 24 A Zoab ne a Abisayi riga a Abneere. La wẽndgã sẽn wa n na n kẽ wã, bãmb taa Amma tãngã. A bee Giyak yaanga, Gabawõ weoogã sor zugu. 25 Benzame neb wʋsg tigmame n tũ a Abneer n tɩ yaas tãng zugu. 26 A Abneer zẽka a koɛɛg n yeel a Zoab yaa: «Sʋʋg tara kɛɛb n ka vʋʋsdẽ laa? Yãmb ka bãng ta baoosg na n yɩɩ sũ-toog laa? Rabʋr la y na n yeel y nebã tɩ b bas b saam-biisã rigbu?» 27 A Zoab yeelame yaa: «Mam wẽen ne Wẽnnaam tɩ yãmb sãn da ka gome, da na n taa beoog tɩ nebã yaool n bas b saam-biisã rigbu.» 28 A Zoab peeba baorgo. La a nebã fãa yaasame n ka le rig Israyɛll rãmbã ye. Bãmb basa zabrã. 29 Baraar yʋngã tɛka, a Abneer ne a nebã kẽna Araba tẽngã pʋgẽ. Bãmb dʋʋga Zʋrdẽ kʋɩlgã n tũ Bitron n ta Mahanayimmi. 30 A Zoab basa a Abneer digbu n tigim a nebã fãa n mik ta ãsayɛl ne a neb piig la a wɛ n ka be. 31 La a Daviid tʋmtʋmdbã kʋʋ a Abneer neb kobs-tã la pis-yoobe. Bãmb yaa Benzame buudu. 32 Bãmb talla a Asayɛll kũumã n tɩ mum a ba yaoogẽ Betelhɛmme. A Zoab ne a nebã kẽna baraar yʋnga tɛka. La b taa Heborõ tɩ zĩigã yaool n yẽege.
