3
1 Zabrã kaoosa wʋsg a Sayil buudã ne a Daviid buudã sʋka. A Daviid pãng paasame t'a Sayil buudã pãng booge. 2 Ad a Daviid kom-dibli nins a sẽn dog Heborõ wã: A Amno yaa yẽ bi-kãsenga. A ma la a Akinoam n ya Zizreyɛl biiga. 3 A yiib soabã yaa a Kileyaabe, a ma la a Abigayill n ya Karmel dao a Nabal paga. A tãab soabã yaa a Absalom, a ma la a Maaka n ya Gesuur naab a Talmayi biiga. 4 A naas soabã yaa a Adoniza, a ma la a Haggiti. A nu soabã yaa a Sefatiya, a ma la a Abitalle. 5 A yoob soabã yaa a Zitreyam, a Daviid pag a Egla biiga. Bãmb yaa a Daviid kamb nins a sẽn dog Heborõ wã.
A Abneer kẽnda a Daviid nengẽ
6 6Zabrã sẽn da be a Sayil buudã ne a Daviid buudã sʋkã, a Abneer paama pãng a Sayil buudã sʋka. 7 A Sayil da tara pʋg-bil t'a yʋʋr boondẽ ta Ritespa. yẽ yaa a Azza biiga. La a Is-Bosɛt yeela a Abneer yaa: «Yaa bõe tɩ y peeg m ba wã pag n kẽese?» 8 A Abneer sũur yika wʋsg a Is-Bosbosɛt goamã yĩnga, la yẽ yeelame yaa: «Mam yaa Zuda baag zug laa? Mam maana y ba wã zak-rãmb ne b saam-biisã la b zo-rãmba neer n ka bas t'a Daviid tõog yãmb ye. La baoosgo, fo ninga maam taal pʋg-kãngã poorẽ dũnna. 9 Zu-soabã ra wẽename n yeel a Daviid 10 tɩ bãmb na n deega a Sayil rẽungã n kõ-a t'a soog Israyɛll ne Zuda soolmã fãa, sẽn sɩng ne Dan hal tɩ ta Bɛɛr-Seba. Mam sãn ka kɩt t'a yɩ wa Zu-soabã sẽn wẽen n yeel a Daviidã, bɩ Zu-soabã ra bas m ye.» 11 La a Is-Bosɛt ka leok a Abneer tɩ bũmb ye, tɩ bõe, yẽ zoet-a lame. 12 A Abneer tʋma neb a Daviid nengẽ Heborõ tɩ b tɩ yeel yaa: «Yaa ãe n so tẽngã? Bɩ y wa tɩ d zems taab tɩ m sõng-y tɩ Israyɛll rãmbã kẽ yãmba.» 13 A Daviid yeelame yaa: «Beneere, mam na n maana kaool ne yãmba. La bũmb a ye la y tog n maane. Yãmb sãn ka reng n peeg a Sayil biig a Mikal n kõ maam, yãmb ka na n wa mam nengẽ ye.» 14 A Daviid tʋma ned a Is-Bosɛt nengẽ n yeel yaa: «Bɩ y lebs maam m pag a Mikal tɩ m da wõrsa Filisti nebã yo-gãnd koabg n deeg-a.» 15 A Is-Bosɛt tʋma neb a sɩd a Paltiyɛl sẽn ya a Layis biigã yir tɩ b tɩ reeg-a n wa. 16 A Paltiyɛl tũ-a lame n yãb hal tɩ ta Bakurim. La a Abneer yeela a Paltiyɛl t'a lebg n kuili. La yẽ kuilame. 17 A Abneer yẽsa ne Israyɛll rãmbã kãsem-dãmba n yeel yaa: «Yãmb da ratame t'a Daviid yɩ y rĩma. Masã, bɩ y maan wa y sẽn da ratã. 18 Tɩ bõe, Zu-soabã yeelame tɩ yaa ne bãmb tʋmtʋmd a Daviid la b na n fãag Israyɛll rãmbã Filisti nebã nusẽ la bãmb bɛɛbã fãa nusẽ.» 19 A Abneer goma ne Benzame nebã me. La yẽ kẽnga Heborõ n tɩ togs a Daviid bũmb nins Israyɛll rãmbã ne Benzame nebã sẽn dat n maanã. 20 A Abneer kẽnga a Daviid nengẽ Heborõ, la neb pissi n yãag-a. A Daviid maana kibs a Abneer ne neb nins sẽn tũ yẽ wã yĩnga. 21 Abneer yeela a Daviid yaa: «Mam na n kẽngame n tɩ tigim Israyɛll nebã fãa tɩ b kẽ yãmba. La yãmb na n sooga y sẽn dat b-rãmba.» La a Daviid kõo a Abneer sor t'a lebg n kuili.
A Zoab kʋʋ a Abneere
22 A Daviid tʋmtʋmd a Zoab yii zabrã zĩigẽ n wa ne teed wʋsgo. La a Abneer da ka le be ne a Daviid Heborõ ye. A Daviid da kõo yẽ sor t'a kuili. 23 A Zoab ne tãb-biis nins sẽn be ne yẽ wã sẽn wa, b wilga a Zoab ta Neer biig a Abneer waa n gesa naab a Daviid n lebg n kuili. 24 A Zoab kẽnga naab a Daviid nengẽ n tɩ yeel yaa: «Yaa bõe tɩ yãmb maan woto? Yaa bõe ta Abneer wa yãmb nengẽ tɩ y bas-a ta looge? Yãmb ka mi a Neer biig a Abneer laa? 25 A waame n na n belg yãmb la a bãng yãmb sẽn maand bũmb ninga.» 26 A Zoab yii a Daviid nengẽ n tʋm neb tɩ b babs a Abneer n tɩ paam-a Sira bulgã noor n tall-a n lebg n wa. La a Daviid ka bãng ye. 27 A Abneer sẽn lebg n wa Heborõ tẽngã, a Zoab peeg-a lame n kẽng tẽngã noor wa a rat n goma ne-a. La yẽ kõsa a pʋgã t'a ki n dol a yao a Asayɛll kũumã. 28 A Daviid sẽn wʋm yellã, a yeelame yaa: «Zu-soabã miime tɩ maam ne m rẽungã toor ka be ne a Neer biig a Abneer kũumã ye. 29 Bɩ yellã zĩnd a Zoab ne a buudã zugu, tɩ rʋʋd-sẽneg bɩ wãood zĩnd a buudã sʋka, tɩ ninsaan-pag bɩ ned sẽn pa tãp pʋgẽ wall kom sẽn tar da kong a buudã sʋk ye.» 30 A Zoab ne a yao a Abisayi kʋʋ a Abneer yẽ sẽn da kʋ b yao a Asayɛll zabr zĩigẽ Gabawõ wã yĩnga. 31 Daviid kɩtame t'a Zoab ne a neb nins sẽn be ne yẽ wã pãrs b futã n yeelg bot n lʋɩ kũumã taoor n yãbe. La a Daviid pʋgla kũumã poore. 32 La bãmb muma a Abneer Heborõ. Rĩmã zẽka a koɛɛg n yãb a Abneer yaoogã zĩigẽ, la nebã fãa yãbame. 33 Rĩmã yɩɩla sũ-sãoong yɩɩlle n yeel yaa: A Abneer ka tog n ki wa nin-yoog ye. 34 Abneere, b ka sẽb fo nus ne f nao ye. Fo yaa wa nin-wẽns sẽn kʋ neda.» La nebã fãa le yãba a yĩnga. 35 Nebã kolga a Daviid baraar wĩntoog n yeel a ta rɩ. A Daviid wẽename n yeel yaa: «Mam sãn dɩ rɩɩb tɩ wẽndgã ka kẽ, bɩ Wẽnnaam da bas-m ye.» 36 Nebã fãa bãnga yõwã, la a taa b yam. Tɩ bõe, rĩmã sẽn da maand bũmb ninsã fãa nooma nebã. 37 Baraare, a Daviid nebã ne Israyɛll rãmbã fãa bãngame tɩ ka rĩmã n kɩt tɩ b kʋ a Abneer ye. 38 Rĩmã yeela a tʋmtʋmdbã yaa: «Yãmb ka mi tɩ Israyɛll nin-kãseng n ki rũnna laa? Baa mam sẽn ya rĩmã, mam ka le tar pãng masã ye. 39 Mam ka tõe a Teseruza kambã ye. Bɩ Zu-soabã lebs bãmb wẽng ning b sẽn maanã.» B kʋʋ a Is-Bosɛt
