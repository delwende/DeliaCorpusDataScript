B waa ne kãabg koglgã Zeruzalɛm
= 1Kib. 13:5-14; 1Kib 15.25-16.3 =
6
1 A Daviid tigma Israyɛll nin-kãsemsã fãa. Yaa neb tus pis-tã. 2 A Daviid ne nebã fãa yikame n kẽng Baala-Zuda n na n tɩ rɩk kãabg koglgã n wa. Yaa saasẽ Pãng-soabã yʋʋr la b rɩk n boond kogl-kãnga. Bãmb bee serubẽ-dãmb nins sẽn be koglgã zugã sʋka. 3 Bãmb kẽnga tãngã zug n tɩ yiis Wẽnnaam kãabg koglgã a Abinadaab zakẽ n ning sarɛt paalg zugu. A Abinadaab kamb a Uzza ne a Akzo n tũ sarɛt ning b sẽn sagl koglgã. 4 A Akzo n lʋɩ taoore ta Uzza kẽnd n yegd koglgã. 5 A Daviid ne Israyɛll rãmbã fãa sũy yɩɩ nood tɩ b rɩk ruuds ne koal la sɩlsags ne kɛm la waas n yɩɩl n deeme. 6 Bãmb sẽn ta a Nakon zɛɛgã, niigã maaname tɩ koglgã lenge, la a Uzza tẽega a nug n gãd-a. 7 Zu-soabã sũur yika ne a Uzza tɩ b kɩt t'a ki be bala, yẽ sẽn ka gũusã yĩnga. A Uzza kii kãabg koglgã sɛɛga. 8 Zu-soabã sẽn maan a Uzza bũmb ningã sãama a Daviid sũur wʋsgo. La b pʋda zĩigã yʋʋr tɩ Peretes-Uzza sẽn dat n togs ta Uzza sɩbgr zĩiga. La b ket n boond-a-la woto hal tɩ ta rũnna. Baraare, 9 a Daviid zoee Zu-soabã. yẽ yeelame yaa: «Mam na n maana a wãn n tall Zu-soabã kãabg koglgã m yiri?» 10 Yõwã kɩtame t'a Daviid ka rat n kẽes kãabg koglgã a tẽngã pʋgẽ ye. yẽ kɩtame tɩ b bʋdg n sik-a Gat rao a Obɛɛd-Edom yiri. 11 Zu-soabã kãabg koglgã zĩnda Gat dao a Obɛɛd-Edom zakã pʋgẽ kiis a tãabo. La Zu-soabã ninga a Obɛɛd-Edom ne a yirã rãmb fãa barka. 12 B wilga a Daviid tɩ Zu-soabã ninga a Obɛɛd-Edom yirã rãmb ne a sẽn tar bũmb ninsã fãa bark Wẽnnaam kãabg koglgã yĩnga. La a Daviid tɩ rɩka Wẽnnaam kãabg koglgã a Obɛɛd-Edom zakã pʋgẽ n wa a tẽngã pʋgẽ ne sũ-noogo. 13 Sẽn tʋkb kãabg koglgã sẽn kẽng nao-yakemd a yopoe, yẽ rɩka loalg ne pe-bil a ye n maan maoongo. 14 A Daviid rɩka fuug n sẽb a sɛɛg n sao warb ne a pãngã tɛk Zu-soabã taoore. 15 La a Daviid ne Israyɛll buudã fãa kɩtame tɩ b rɩk Zu-soabã kãabg koglgã n kaasdẽ la b peebd bard n watẽ. 16 Wakat ning Zu-soabã kãabg koglgã sẽn kẽ a Daviid tẽngã pʋgẽ wã, a Daviid pag a Mikal ra bee roog noor n getẽ. yẽ yaa a Sayil biiga. A Mikal yãame tɩ naab a Daviid yɩgd n tiimsdẽ n saood warb Zu-soabã taoore, la yẽ paooga a Daviid a sũur pʋgẽ. 17 Bãmb talla Zu-soabã kãabg koglgã n wa sik a zĩigẽ fu-roog ning a Daviid sẽn da tilg a yĩngã pʋgẽ. La a Daviid maana maand sẽn yõogd bugum ne laafɩ maand Zu-soabã taoore. 18 La a Daviid sẽn maan maand sẽn yõogd bugmã ne laafɩ maandã n sɛ, yẽ ninga nebã bark ne saasẽ Pãng-soabã yʋʋre. 19 La yẽ kõo Israyɛll rãmbã raopã ne pagbã fãa bur a ye-ye ne nemd la sẽbi. La nebã fãa kuilame. 20 La a Daviid kẽngame n na n tɩ ning a yirã rãmb barka. A Sayil biig a Mikal yiime n tɩ seg a Daviid n yeel yaa: «Israyɛll rĩmã tara ziir dũnna. Bãmb yeesa b fut b tʋmtʋmdbã yem-pogs taoor wa nin-yoogo.» 21 Daviid yeela a Mikal yaa: «Yaa Zu-soabã taoor la m maand woto. Bãmb basa fo ba wã ne f buudã fãa n yãk maam n kɩt tɩ m lebg Israyɛll rãmbã naaba. La mam na n maana sũ-noog n waoog Zu-soabã. 22 La mam na n sika m meng n paas tɩ f paoog-ma. La baoosgo, yem-pogs nins fo sẽn gomd b yellã na n waooga maam.» 23 A Sayil biig a Mikal ka tol n paam biig a yõor tek ye.
