A Salomo tʋmtʋmdba, la a tẽn-zãadba
4
1 Woto, Rĩm a Salomo sooga Israyɛll tõrã. 2 Ad a tʋmtʋmdbã taoor dãmba: A Zadok biig a Azariya - maan-kʋʋda, 3 A Sisa kom-dibli a Eliyorɛf ne a Ahiza - sɛb gʋlsedba, A Ahilud biig a Zehosafate - sɛb-bĩngda, 4 A Zehoyada bi-ribl a Benaya - tãb-biisã na-kãsenga, A Zadok ne a Abiyataare - maan-kʋʋdba, 5 A Natã bi-ribl a Azariya - soolem soolem tʋmtʋmdbã taoor soaba, a Natã bi-ribl a Zabudi - maan-kʋʋda la rĩmã meng sagenda, 6 A Abisaare - na-yirã yel-gɛta, A Abda bi-ribl a Adoniram - pãng tʋʋmã yell gɛta. 7 A Salomo dag n tara soolem zãadb piig la a yiib me tɩ b get Israyɛll fãa yelle. Bãmb n get tɩ rĩmã ne na-yirã rãmb paam bũmb nins b sẽn tog n paamã. Bãmb ned kam fãa tog n moenega na-yirã kiuug a yembr yʋʋmd fãa. 8 Ad b yʋya: A Bɛn-Huure - Efaryim tãmsẽ tẽnsẽ wã, 9 A Bɛn-Dekɛɛre - Makaaze, la Saalbim, la Bet Semes la Elon Betanan soolmẽ 10 A Bɛn-Hesɛɛde - Arubbot soolmẽ Soko ne Hefɛɛr vɩʋʋgã fãa dag n ya yẽnda n so, 11 Bɛn-Abinadaabe - Nafot Door soolmẽ a da peega a Salomo bi-pugl a Tafat n maan a paga, 12 A Ahilud biig a Baana - Taanake, la Megido, la Bet San gillã sẽn pẽ Zaretanne, Zezreyɛl tẽngre, sẽn sɩng ne Bet San hal n tãag Abɛll Mehola, Zokmeam rʋʋnde, 13 A Bɛn-Gebɛɛr - Ramot sẽn be Galaad soolmẽ yẽ n da so a Manaase biig a Zayiir zĩisã sẽn be Galaadã, n paas Argob soolmã sẽn be Bazã n tar tẽn-bɛd pis-yoob sẽn gũbg ne lalse la sẽn tar zũud kud-bɩlma kʋɩlemse, 14 A Iddo biig a Ahinadaabe - Mahanayim soolmẽ, 15 A Ahimaaze - Naftali soolmẽ yẽ da peega a Salomo bi-pugl a Basemat n maan a paga, 16 A Husayi biig a Baana - Asɛɛr ne Alot soolmẽ, 17 A Paruwa biig a Zehozafat - Issakaar soolmẽ, 18 A Ela biig a Simeyi - Benzame soolmẽ, 19 A Uri biig a Gebɛɛr - Galaad soolmẽ Amoor-rãmbã rĩm a Sihõ tẽngã ne Bazã rĩm a Oog tẽngã. yẽ a ye bal n dag n zãag soolmã.
A Salomo daar o daar rɩɩbo
20 Zuda ne Israyɛll nebã da waooga wa ko-kãseng noor bĩisri. B dag n dɩtame la b yũuda, la b sũyã da noomame. 21 La a Salomo dag n soo rĩm-dãmbã soolem-dãmba, sẽn sɩng ne mogrã hal n tãag Filisti rãmbã tẽnga, hal n tɩ tãa Ezipt toadgã. Tẽn-kãens fãa dag n wata ne b yãoodo, la b saka a Salomo a yõorã tɛka. 22 Rɩɩb ning sẽn moenegd a Salomo zakã-rãmb da yaa zom-bãaneg konkong kobs-tã la pis-tã, la zom konkong kobs-yoob la pis-yoobe, 23 la yi-kay piiga, la nins sẽn saad n wãbd yãmdã bãmb pissi, la piis ne bʋʋs koabga, la wals ne yãgs la we-bʋʋse, la bõn-pɩgs sẽn tũusi. 24 Bala, yẽ dag n soo soolem-dãmb sẽn be mogrã yaangã fãa, sẽn sɩng ne Tifsa tɩ ta Gaza, la soolmã kɩrems fãa da gãee bãane. 25 A Salomo vɩɩmã tɛka, Zuda ne Israyɛll fãa da gãee bãane, sẽn sɩng ne Dan tɩ ta Berseba, tɩ ned fãa zĩ-a nasaar sib tɩɩg la a kankang tẽngre. 26 A Salomo dag n tara wiid sogds tus a naas saar-rãmbã wiid yĩnga, la wed-moos tus piig la a yiibu. 27 Soolem zãadbã, ned fãa ra wata ne rɩɩb Rĩm a Salomo yĩnga, la neb nins fãa sẽn dɩt naab nugẽ wã yĩnga, ned fãa ne a kiuugu. Bãmb dag n geta rẽ yell sõma tɩ bũmb ka paoogd ye. 28 Bãmb ned fãa da wata ne a orz la yamd pʋɩɩre, zĩig ning sẽn segde, wiid nins sẽn takd saar-dãmbã yĩnga, la wiid a taabã me yĩnga.
A Salomo yam
29 Wẽnnaam kõo a Salomo yam la yãab sẽn ya kãsenga, la wʋm-yaoleng ned sẽn ka tõe n mak n bãng a tɛka, wa bĩisr sẽn be ko-kãseng noorã. 30 A Salomo yamã dag n yɩɩda neb nins fãa sẽn dag n be yaangã yam, la kãseng n yɩɩd Ezipt yamã fãa. 31 yamã dag n yɩɩda neb a taabã, baa ne a Etan sẽn yit Ezra - yam soab n yɩɩd a Maholl kom-dibli a Heman la a Kalkol la a Darda. La a yʋʋrã saaga soolem nins fãa sẽn gũbg yẽ soolmã. 32 A wẽe yel-bũn tus a tãabo, la a yɩɩlã sõor yaa tusr la a nu. 33 A bilga tɩɩs vɩɩm yelle, sẽn sɩng ne Libã sɛɛdr tɩ ta Hizop sẽn yit lals zutã. A goma rũms la liuuli la bõn-vuudsi, la zĩm me yelle. 34 Neb yii soolem-dãmbã fãa n wa n na n kelg a Salomo yam goamã, dũni fãa rĩm-dãmbã sẽn wʋm a yamã kibar n tʋm bãmb tɩ b tɩ kelge.
