16
1 La Zu-soabã koɛɛg waa a Hanani biig a Zehu nengẽ n wilg yell ning sẽn na n paam a Baasa wã: 2 «Mam yigla foom wubsgẽ n kɩt tɩ lebg Israyɛll sẽn ya mam nebã taoor soaba, la fo tũu a Zeroboam sorã, n kɩt tɩ Israyɛll sẽn ya mam nebã maan yel-wẽna, tɩ b yel-wẽnã kɩt tɩ mam sũur yiki. 3 Woto, mam seglame n sɛ n na n sãam a Baasa ne a roogã rãmba, la mam na n maana fo yirã rãmb wa mam sẽn maan a Nebat biig a Zeroboam zakã rãmbã. 4 Baas n na n wãb a Baasa neb nins sẽn na n ki tẽngã pʋgẽ wã, la liuuli sẽn yɩgd yĩngrã na n dɩɩ a neb nins sẽn na n ki-b weoogẽ wã.» 5 Sã n ya ne a Baasa naamã yɛl a taabã, bũmb nins a sẽn maane la a tõogre, b ka gʋls-b tɩ be Israyɛll rĩm-dãmbã kibay sɛbã pʋsẽ sɩda? 6 A Baasa babsa a saam-dãmba, la b mum-a-la Tirtsa. La a bi-ribl a Ela reega yẽ zĩigẽ n lebg rĩma. 7 sẽn paase, Zu-soabã koɛɛg tũniga a Hanani biig a Zehu n gom ne a Baasa la a roogã rãmbã, wẽng ning yẽ sẽn maan Zu-soabã nifẽ wã yĩnga, t'a tʋʋmã a sẽn maanã kɩt tɩ Zu-soabã sũur yiki, la a lebg wa a Zeroboam roogã rãmba - la a sẽn sãam a Zeroboam roogã rãmbã yĩng me.
Israyɛll rĩm a Ela
8 Zuda rĩm a Aza sẽn deeg naam tɩ yʋʋm pissi la yoaablẽ, a Baasa biig a Ela lebga Israyɛll rĩma, n dɩ naam Tirtsa yʋʋm a yiibu. 9 Yẽ tʋmtʋmd a yembre, a Zimri, sẽn so-a yẽ saar-dãmb pʋɩ-sʋkã baoo sor n na n menes-a. A Aza dag n bee Tirtsa wakat kãnga, n yũud rãam rao ning sẽn get-a na-yirã sẽn be Tirtsa wã yellã, a Arza yirã. 10 A Zimri kẽeme n kɛ-a n lubi, n kʋ-a, Zuda rĩm a Aza rẽung yʋʋm pissi la a yopoe wakate. La a reega a zĩig n lebg rĩma. 11 sẽn sɩng naamã rɩɩbo, n zãoond zaoorẽ wã, a kʋʋ a Baasa yirã rãmb fãa. A ka bas nin-daoog baa a yembre, rɛɛmb wall zoa. 12 Woto, a Zimri sãama a Baasa yirã rãmb fãa tɩ yɩ wa gomd ning Zu-soabã sẽn tũnig b no-rɛɛs a Zehu n gome - 13 yel-wẽn nins fãa a Baasa ne a bi-ribl a Ela sẽn maane la b kɩt tɩ Israyɛll rãmbã maanã yĩnga, bala bãmb sẽn tũ wẽn-naands sẽn ka barkã kɩtame tɩ Zu-soabã sẽn ya Israyɛll Wẽnnaam sũur yiki. 14 Sã n ya a Ela naamã yɛl a taabã, la bũmb nins fãa a sẽn maane, b ka gʋls-b tɩ be Israyɛll rĩm-dãmbã kibay sɛbã pʋsẽ sɩda?
Israyɛll rĩm a Zimri
15 Zuda rĩm a Aza sẽn dɩ naam tɩ yʋʋm pissi la yopoɛɛlẽ, a Zimri rɩɩ naam Tirtsa dasem a yopoe. Tãb-biisã dag n sũu tãp Bibbeton sẽn ya Filistẽ rãmbã tẽng sɛɛgẽ. 16 Israyɛll rãmb sẽn dag n be tãb-sikã zĩigẽ wã sẽn wʋm t'a Zimri baoo sor sẽn na tũ n paam n kʋ rĩmã n sɩd tõog n kʋ-a wã, bãmb kõo tãb-biisã naab a Omri naam t'a soog Israyɛll badaar bala, la yɩ tãb-sikã zĩigẽ. 17 Dẽ, a Omri ne Israyɛll rãmbã fãa yii Gebbeton n kẽng n tɩ sik tãp n gũbg Tirtsa. 18 A Zimri sẽn yã tɩ b reega tẽngã, a kẽnga na-yirã gãosgẽ la a ning na-yirã bugum n gũbg a menga. Woto, a kiime, 19 yel-wẽnã a sẽn maane, n maan sẽn ya wẽng Zu-soabã nifẽ la a tũ a Zeroboam soayã, la a maan yel-wẽn nins yẽ sẽn dag n maane la a kɩt tɩ Israyɛll rãmbã maanã yĩnga. 20 Sã n ya ne a Zimri naamã yɛl a taabã, la kɩɩsgã a sẽn lʋɩ a taoorã, b ka gʋls-b tɩ be Israyɛll rĩm-dãmbã kibay sɛbã pʋsẽ sɩda?
Israyɛll rĩm a Omri
21 Wakat kãngã, Israyɛll nebã welga a yiibu. Pʋɩ-sʋkã bee ne a Ginat bi-ribl a Tibni n dat t'a yɩ rĩma, tɩ pʋɩ-sʋk a to wã be ne Omri. 22 La a Omri poorẽ dãmbã wilgame tɩ b pãng yɩɩda neb nins sẽn be a Ginat biig a Tibni poorẽ wã. Woto, a Tibni kiime la a Omri lebga rĩma. 23 Zuda rĩm a Aza rɩ naam tɩ yʋʋm pis-tã la a yembrã, a Omri lebga Israyɛll rĩma, la a rɩɩ naam yʋʋm piig la a yiibu, yʋʋm a yoob Tirtsa. 24 A raa Samari tãngã a Semeer nengẽ wanzuri talã a yiibu, n tilg tẽng be n pʋd a yʋʋr tɩ Samari, n tũnig ne a Semeere, sẽn dag n so-a tãngã yʋʋre. 25 La a Omri maana sẽn ya wẽng Zu-soabã nifẽ, la a maana yel-wẽn n yɩɩd neb nins fãa sẽn deng-b yẽ wã. 26 Yẽ tũu a Nebat biig a Zeroboam soayã fãa la a maan a yel-wẽnã fãa, yel-wẽn nins a sẽn kɩt tɩ Israyɛll rãmbã manã. Ne b wẽn-naandsã sẽn ka barkã, b kɩtame tɩ Zu-soabã sẽn ya Israyɛll Wẽnnaam sũur yiki. 27 Sã n ya ne a Omri naama yɛl a taabã, bũmb nins a sẽn maane la tõogr nins a sẽn tõogã yelle, b ka gʋls-b tɩ be Israyɛll rĩm-dãmbã kibay sɛbã pʋsẽ sɩda? 28 A Omri babsa a saam-dãmba, la b mum-a-la Samari. La a bi-ribl a Akaab n deeg a zĩig n lebg rĩma.
A Akaab lebga Israyɛll rĩma
29 Zuda rĩm a Aza rɩ naam tɩ yʋʋm pis-tã la a niinẽ, a Omri bi-ribl a Akaab lebga rĩm Israyɛlle, la a zĩnda Samari n dɩ Israyɛll naam yʋʋm pissi la a yiibu. 30 A Omri biig a Akaab maana wẽng n yɩɩd neb nins fãa sẽn deng-b yẽnda wã. 31 yẽ ka ges tɩ yẽ sẽn maan yel-wẽn nins a Nebat biig a Zeroboam sẽn maanã yaa yell ye. A pʋs n peega Sidõ rãmbã rĩm a Etbaal bi-pugl a Zezabɛl n maan a paga, n sɩng n pʋʋsd Baa la a waoogd-a. 32 Yẽ mee Baall pʋʋsg roog Samari, n ning tẽng-kugr be Baall yĩnga. 33 A Akaab lugla Asera ra-lugdga la a maan sẽn yɩɩd woto, n maan n yɩɩd neb nins fãa sẽn deng-b yẽnda wã, n kɩt tɩ Zu-soabã sẽn ya Israyɛll Wẽnnaamã sũur yiki. 34 A Akaab wakate, Betɛll rao a Hiyɛl lebsa Zeriko n me. A sẽn na n yẽbg tẽngã yẽbgrã, tũu ne a bi-ribl kãseng a Abiram yõore, la a sẽn na n lugl tẽngã noyã kʋɩlemsã, tũu ne a nugẽ bi-ribl a Segub yõore, la yɩɩ wa gomd ning Zu-soabã sẽn dag n tũnig a Nun biig a Zozoe n yeelã.
