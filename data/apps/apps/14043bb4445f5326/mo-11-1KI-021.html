A Nabot nasaar sãbs pʋʋgo
21
1 Sẽn kaoos bilfu yell n zĩnd nasaar sib pʋʋg poorẽ. Yaa Zezreyɛl rao a Nabot pʋʋgo. Pʋʋgã dag n bee Zezreyɛlle, rĩm a Akaab na-yirã sẽn be Samari wã sɛɛgẽ. 2 A Akaab yeela a Nabot yaa: «Kõ-m f nasaar sib pʋʋgã tɩ m maan m zĩ-maas zĩig t'a pẽe ne m na-yirã. M na n kõo foom nasaar sib pʋʋg a to sẽn ya sõma n yɩɩd a woto n led a zĩiga, la f sãn dat yaood me, m na n yaoo foom sẽn zemse.» 3 La a Nabot leokame n yeel yaa: «Zu-soabã gɩdgame n yeel tɩ mam ka tog n dɩk bũmb ning m yaab-rãmb sẽn bas ne maamã n kõ yãmb ye.» 4 La a Akaab kuila yir ne nen-gãneng la sũ-yikri, Zezreyɛl rao a Nabot sẽn yeel yẽ tɩ «mam ka na n dɩk bũmb ning m yaab-rãmb sẽn bas ne maamã n kõ yãmbã» yĩnga. A tɩ gãanda a gãagã zugu, n gẽ, la a tõdgame t'a ka na n dɩ ye. 5 A pag a Zezabɛl kẽeme n sok-a yaa: «Yaa wãn tɩ y nengã gãneme? Yaa bõe yĩng tɩ y ka rat n dɩ?» 6 A leok-a lame n yeel yaa: Yaa tɩ mam yeela Zezreyɛl dao a Nabot yaa: ‘Koos f nasaar sib pʋʋgã ne maam, wall f sãn data, m na n tek-a-la ne nasaar sib tɩɩs pʋʋg a to. ‘ La a yeelame yaa: ‘Mam ka na n kõ yãmb m nasaar sib pʋʋgã ye. ‘» 7 A pag a Zezabɛl yeelame yaa: «Yaa woto la yãmb a Israyɛll rĩmã maanda? Yik-y n dɩ-y-yã! Yols-y y nengã. Mam na n deega Zezreyɛl rao a Nabot nasaar sib pʋʋgã n kõ yãmba.» 8 Dẽ, a gʋlsa sɛb ne a Akaab yʋʋre, n ning a bãndã b zutu, n tʋm kãsem-dãmbã ne na-buudã sẽn vɩ ne a Nabot tẽn-yendã. 9 Sɛbã pʋse, a gʋlsa woto: Moon-y no-loeer daare, la y zẽnig a Nabot nebã taoore. 10 La y zẽnig neb a yiib sẽn ka mi wẽng n tees-a, n kɩt tɩ b kɩs kaset t'a Nabot tʋʋsa Wẽnnaam ne rĩmã yiib fãa. Dẽ poore, bɩ y tall-a n yi kɛɛng n tɩ lob-a kug t'a ki.» 11 Woto, kãsem-dãmbã ne na-buudã sẽn vɩ tẽng a yembr ne a Nabotã maana wa a Zezabɛl sẽn yeel bãmb sɛbã pʋsẽ tɩ b maanã. 12 B moona no-loeer daare, n zẽnig a Nabot nebã taoore. 13 Dẽ, neb a yiib sẽn ka mi wẽng waa n zĩndame n tees-a, n dɩk yell n dogl a Nabot nebã taoor n yeel yaa: «A Nabot tʋʋsa Wẽnnaam ne rĩmã yiib fãa.» Dẽ, b tall-a lame n yiis tẽngã n tɩ lob-a kug t'a ki. 14 Dẽ poore, b tʋma koɛɛg a Zezabɛl nengẽ n yeel yaa: «D loba a Nabot kug la a kiime.» 15 A Zezabɛl sẽn wʋm tɩ b loba a Nabot kug t'a ki bala, a yeela a Akaab yaa: «Yik-y n soog-y Zezreyɛl dao a Nabot nasaar sib pʋʋgã a sẽn da tõdg t'a kõn koos ne yãmbã. A ka ket n vɩ ye, a kiime.» 16 A Akaab sẽn wʋm t'a Nabot kiime wã, a yikame n sig n tɩ soog a Nabot nasaar sib pʋʋgã. 17 La Zu-soabã gomd waa Tisbe rao a Eli nengẽ n yeel-a yaa: 18 «Yik n tɩ seg Israyɛll rĩm a Akaab sẽn dɩt naam Samari wã. masã wã, a bee a Nabot nasaar sib pʋʋgẽ wã a sẽn kẽng n na n tɩ soogã. 19 Yeel-a yaa: ‘Ad Zu-soabã sẽn yete: Fo ka kʋ ned n deeg a sẽn so sɩda?’ La f yeel-a yaa: ‘Ad Zu-soabã sẽn yete: Zĩig ning baas sẽn leg a Nabot zɩɩmã, zĩ-kãng la baas na n leg fo zɩɩm n-ye, fo zɩɩmã !» 20 A Akaab yeela a Eli yaa: «Woto, m bɛy a Eli, fo baoo maam n yãa!» A leokame yaa: «Mam baoo foom n yãa, bala fo rɩka f mengã n kõ wẽng maaneg Zu-soabã nifẽ. 21 Mam na n kɩtame tɩ sãoong paam foom. Mam na n sãama fo yagensã la m kɩt tɩ raop nins fãa a Akaab kom-dibli wã, hal tɩ ta yaoolem soabã menem Israyɛlle - yembs wall burkẽmba. 22 Mam na n kɩtame tɩ fo yirã lebg wa a Nebat biig a Zeroboam yirã, la a wa a Ahiza biig a Baasa yirã, fo sẽn bao mam sũ-yikri la f kɩt tɩ Israyɛll maan yel-wẽnã yĩnga. ‘ 23 Sẽn paase, ne a Zezabɛl yelle, Zu-soabã yetame tɩ yaa: ‘Baas n na n wãb a Zezabɛl Zezreyɛl lalgã sɛɛga. ‘ 24 Baas n na n wãb a Akaab neb nins sẽn kiid tẽngã pʋgẽ wã, la liuuli sẽn yɩgd yĩngrã na n dɩɩ a neb nins sẽn kiid weoogẽ wã.» 25 Ned ka zĩnd n kõ a meng wẽng maaneg yĩng Zu-soabã nifẽ wa a Akaab ye, la yaa a pag a Zezabɛl n tusd-a. 26 Yẽ maana sẽn ka be neer n yɩɩdã fãa, a sẽn pʋg wẽn-naandsã wa Amoor-rãmbã Zu-soabã sẽn dig n yiis Israyɛll taoorã. 27 A Akaab sẽn wʋm gom kãense, a pãrsa a futu, n yeelg bot fut la a loe noore. A dag n gãee ne bot futã la a kẽnd maaseg maasega. 28 Dẽ, Zu-soabã gomd waa Tisbe dao a Eli nengẽ n yeel yaa: 29 «Fo yãnda a Akaab sẽn sik a meng to-to mam taoorã? A sẽn sik a mengã yĩnga, Mam ka na n tʋm sãoongã a vɩɩmã wakat ye, mam na n tʋma sãoongã a biigã wakate.»
