A Zoaas Manega Wẽnd-doogã
= 2Kib 24.1-14; 24.23-27 =
12
1 A Zoaas lebga rĩm a Zehu reeg naam tɩ yʋʋm yopoɛɛlẽ, la a rɩ naam Zeruzalɛm yʋʋm pis-naase. A ma yʋʋr la a Zibiya, n yit Beer-Seba. 2 A Zoaas maana sẽn ya tɩrg Zu-soabã nifẽ, yʋʋm nins fãa a Zehozada sẽn zãms yẽ wã pʋgẽ. 3 Baoosgo, a ka viig zĩ-zãongelsã ye. Nebã da ket n maanda maand la yõod wusd beene. 4 A Zoaas yeela maan-kʋʋdbã yaa: «Tigs-y ligd ning fãa b sẽn wat-ne Zu-soabã roogẽ n kõt bãmb bãmb yʋʋrã yĩngã - ligdã b sẽn kɩt tɩ yõor fãa yaooda, la ligd neb sẽn wat-ne b sẽn pʋlem yĩnga, la ligd neb sẽn wat n kõt ne yam-leoogo. 5 Bɩ maan-kʋʋd fãa reeg ligd laogã zãadbã ned a yembr nengẽ, n tall n maneg bũmb ning fãa b sẽn yã tɩ sãama wẽnd-doogã pʋgẽ n dat manegrã. 6 La hal tɩ ta a Zoaas naam yʋʋm pissi la a tãabã wakate, maan-kʋʋdbã da nan ka maneg Wẽnd-doogã ye. 7 Dẽ, Rĩm a Zoaas boola man-kʋʋd a Zehozada ne maan-kʋʋdb a taabã n sok-b yaa: «Bõe yĩng tɩ yãmb ka manegd bũmb nins sẽn sãam wẽnd-doogẽ wã? Ned da le yãk ligd paoongã zĩigẽ a meng yĩng ye, rɩk-y-yã-a n maneg Wẽnd-doogã» 8 Maan-kʋʋdbã yeelame tɩ b ka na n leb n dees ligd nebã nengẽ ye, la tɩ bãmb mensã ka na n maneg wẽnd-doogã b toor ye. 9 Maan-kʋʋd a Zehozada rɩka koglg n poog võor a limdẽ wã. A dɩgl-a lame n yegl tẽng-kugrã, tɩ ned sãn kẽed Zu-soabã roogẽ wã, bee a rɩtgo. Maan-kʋʋd ning sẽn gũuda zag-noorã ningda ligd ning fãa b sẽn tar n wat Zu-soabã roogẽ wã koglgã pʋgẽ. 10 Wakat ning fãa b sẽn yã tɩ ligd wʋsg n be koglgã pʋgẽ, rĩmã sɛb-gʋndã ne maan-kʋʋd kãsemã watame n wa sõd ligd ning nebã sẽn tall n wa Zu-soabã roogẽ wã, la b sui yondẽ. 11 B sãn wa ges n bãng a sõorã, b rɩkda ligdã n kõ neb nins b sẽn yãk tɩ b ges wẽnd-doogã tʋʋmdã yellã. B tara ligd-kãng n yaood neb nins sẽn tʋmd Zu-soabã roogã zugã - ra-wãadbã ne met-mitbã, 12 la tãn-metbã ne kug-peesdbã. Bãmb daada ra-bɛda la kug sẽn peese, Zu-soabã roogã manegr yĩnga, la b yaood bũmb nins fãa sẽn kẽed ne wẽnd-doogã manegrã. 13 B ka tar ligd ning b sẽn wat-ne wẽnd-doogẽ wã n maand wanzuri laase, wall sʋʋse, wall la-zulumsi, wall bardo, wall sãnem teoog bɩ wanzuri teoog baa a yembr Zu-soabã roog yĩng ye. 14 Dag n ya tʋmtʋmdbã la b yaooda tɩ b tar n manegd wẽnd-doogã. 15 Bãmb ka sokd neb nins bãmb sẽn kɩt ligdã tɩ b yao tʋmtʋmdbã ligdã sẽn kẽẽ ye, bala b tʋmda ne pʋ-peelem fasɩ. 16 B ka wat ne beegr maoong bɩ yel-wẽn maoong ligd Zu-soabã roogẽ wã ye, yaa maan-kʋʋdbã n so rẽnda. 17 Wakat kãng sẽne, Aram rĩm a Hazayɛl kẽngame n tɩ zab ne Gat n deeg-a. La a yii be n na n wa zab ne Zeruzalɛm. 18 La Zuda rĩm a Zoaas wʋka kaorõngã teed a ba-rãmb - a Zozafat la a Zoram la a Akaziya sẽn yɩ Zuda rĩm-dãmbã - sẽn dag n kõ Wẽnnaamã, n paas kũun nins yẽ mengã sẽn dag n kõ Wẽnnaamã ne sãnem ning b sẽn ya Zu-soabã roogẽ laogã roogẽ wã la sẽn be na-yirã n tʋm tɩ b tɩ kõ Aram rĩm a Hazayɛlle. A sẽn deeg teedã, a lebgame n loog n bas Zeruzalɛm. 19 Sã n ya ne a Zoaas naama yɛl a taabã, la bũmb nins fãa a sẽn maanã, b ka gʋls tɩ be Zuda rĩm-dãmbã kibayã sɛb pʋs sɩda? 20 A tʋmtʋmdbã baoo sor n na n kʋ-a, la b kʋ-a-la Bet Millo, sor ning sẽn dabd Silla wã zugu. 21 tʋmtʋmdb nins sẽn kʋ-b yẽ wã yaa a Simeya biig a Zozabad ne a Someer biig a Zozabade. A kiime tɩ b mum-a n naag a saam-dãmba a Daviid tẽngã pʋgẽ. La a bi-ribl a Amaziya n deeg a zĩig n lebg rĩma.
