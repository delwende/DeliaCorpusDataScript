Zuda rĩm a Azariya
= 2Kib 26.3-4, 21-23 =
15
1 Israyɛll rĩm a Zeroboam naam yʋʋm pissi la yopoe soabã wakate, Zuda rĩm a Amasiya biig a Azariya sɩnga a naam rɩɩbo. 2 A sẽn wa n na n deeg naamã t'a tara yʋʋm piig la a yoobe, la a rɩɩ naamã yʋʋm pis-nu la a yiibu. A ma yʋʋr la a Zekoliya. A ra yita Zeruzalɛm. 3 Yẽ maana sẽn ya tɩrg Zu-soabã nifẽ wa a ba a Amasiya sẽn maanã. 4 Baoosgo, b ka yiis zĩ-zãongelsã ye. Nebã da ket n maand maand be la b yõod wusdu. 5 Zu-soabã kɩtame tɩ wãood yõk rĩmã n tall-a hal t'a tɩ ki, la a vɩɩmda roog toore. Rĩmã biig a Zotam n dag n get na-yirã yelle la a dag n zãad tẽngã nebã. 6 Sã n ya ne a Azariya naamã yɛl a taabã, la bũmb nins fãa a sẽn maane, b ka gʋls tɩ be Zuda rĩm-dãmbã kibay sɛbã pʋs sɩda? 7 A Azariya babsa a saam-dãmba, la b mum-a lame n naag bãmba a Daviid tẽngã pʋgẽ. La yẽ bi-ribl a Zotam deega yẽ zĩigẽ n lebg rĩma.
Israyɛll rĩm a Zakari
8 Zuda rĩm a Azariya naamã yʋʋm pis-tã la a nii wakatã, a Zeroboam biig a Zakari lebga Israyɛll rĩm Samari, la a rɩɩ naamã kiis a yoobe. 9 Yẽ maana sẽn ya wẽng Zu-soabã nifẽ wa a baaba sẽn maanã. yẽ ka wẽnemd n bas a Nebat biig a Zeroboam yel-wẽnã a sẽn kɩt tɩ Israyɛll nebã maanã ye. 10 A Zabɛs biig a Salum baoo sor n na n kʋ a Zakari. A yika zabr ne-a nebã taoor n kʋ-a, la a reeg a zĩig n lebg rĩma. 11 Zakari naamã yɛl a taabã gʋlsa Israyɛll rĩm-dãmbã kibay sɛbã pʋse. 12 Woto, gomd ning Zu-soabã sẽn dag n yeel a Zehu tɩ «Fo yagens na n zĩnda Israyɛll zaoorẽ hal tɩ ta zamaan a naasã» pidsame.
Israyɛll rĩm a Salum
13 A Zabɛs biig a Salum lebga rĩm rẽ tɩ Zuda rĩm a Uziya rɩɩ naam tɩ yʋʋm pis-tã la a wɛɛlẽ, la a rɩɩ naam Samari kiuug a yembre. 14 Dẽ poore, a Gadi biig a Menahɛm yii Tirtsa n wa Samari. yẽ yika zabr ne a Zabɛs biig a Salum Samari pʋgẽ, n kʋ-a la a reeg a zĩig n lebg rĩma. 15 A Salum naamã yɛl a taabã, la yẽ sẽn lʋɩ neb taoor n bao sor n na n kʋ wã gʋlsa Israyɛll rĩm-dãmbã kibay sɛbã pʋse. 16 Wakat kãnga a Menahɛm yii Tirtsa n sɩng zabr ne Tifsa tẽngã, la ne ned fãa sẽn be tẽngã pʋgẽ la a tẽn-kɩremsã, bala b tõdgame tɩ b ka nan pags b zag-noyã ye. A Zaba ne Tifsa, la a weeda tʋter-pʋsã fãa pʋse.
Israyɛll rĩm a Menahɛm
17 Zuda rĩm a Azariya naam yʋʋm pis-tã la a wɛ wã wakate, a Gadi biig a Menahɛm lebga Israyɛll dẽma, la arɩɩ naam Samari yʋʋm piiga. 18 Yẽ maana sẽn ya wẽng Zu-soabã nifẽ. yẽ naamã tɛka, a zɩ n wẽnemd n bas a Nebat biig a Zeroboam yel-wẽnã a sẽn kɩt tɩ Israyɛll rãmbã maanã ye. 19 Dẽ, Assiri rĩmã waa n kẽe n yãmba tẽngã, la a Menahɛm kõo yẽnda wanzuri talã tusri, sẽn na yɩlg la a sõng-a, la t'a na paam pãng n paas n zã a soolmã. 20 A Menahɛm reega ligd kãng fãa Israyɛll rãmbã nengẽ. Arzegsã-rãmb ned fãa da tog n kɩsa wanzuri sekɛl pis-nu tɩ b kõ Assiri rĩmã. Woto, Assiri rĩmã lebg n loogame n ka leb n zĩnd tẽngẽ wã yɛs ye. 21 Sã n ya ne a Menahɛm naamã yɛl a taabã ne bũmb nins fãa a sẽn maanã, b ka gʋls tɩ be Israyɛll rĩm-dãmbã kibay sɛbã pʋs sɩda? 22 A Menahɛm babsa a saam-dãmba, la a bi-ribl a Pekahiya n deeg a zĩig n lebg rĩma.
Israyɛll rĩm a Pekahiya
23 Zuda rĩm a Azariya naam rɩɩb yʋʋm pis-nu wã wakate, a Menahɛm biig a Pakahiya lebga Israyɛll rĩm Samari, la a rɩɩ naam yʋʋm a yiibu. 24 A Pekahiya maana sẽn ya wẽng Zu-soabã nugẽ. yẽ ka lak a meng ne a Nebat biig a Zeroboam yel-wẽnã a sẽn kɩt tɩ Israyɛll nebã maanã ye. 25 Yẽ tãb-biisã naab a yembre, a Peka, a Remaliya biig baoo sor n na n kʋ-a. A peega Galaad neb pis-nu n tɩ kʋ a Pekahiya ne a Argob la a Ariye me, Samari na-yirã gãosgẽ wã. Woto, a Peka kʋʋ a Pekahiya la a reeg a zĩig n lebg rĩma. 26 A Pekahiya naamã yɛl a taabã, la bũmb nins fãa a sẽn maanã gʋlsame tɩ be Israyɛll rĩm-dãmbã kibay sɛbã pʋse.
Israyɛll rĩm a Peka
27 Zuda rĩmã naam yʋʋm pis-nu la a yiib soabã wakate, a Remaliya biig a Peka lebga Israyɛll rĩm Samari, la arɩɩ naamã yʋʋm pissi. 28 Yẽ maana sẽn ya wẽng Zu-soabã nifẽ. yẽ ka lak a meng ne a Nebat biig a Zeroboam yel-wẽnã a sẽn kɩt tɩ Israyɛll maanã ye. 29 Israyɛll rĩm a Peka wakate, Assiri rĩm a Tiglag-Pilezɛɛr waame n deeg Izõ la Abɛll Bet Maaka la Zanoa la Kadɛs ne Hazoore. yẽ reega Galaad la Galile, n paas Neftali soolmã fãa, la a talla nebã n kẽng Assiri. 30 Dẽ, a El biig a Hooze baoo sor n na n kʋ a Remaliya biig a Peka. A yika zabr ne-a, n kʋ-a la a reeg a zĩig n lebg rĩma, a Uziya biig a Zotam naam yʋʋm pissi wakate. 31 Sã n ya ne a Peka naamã yɛl a taabã, la bũmb nins fãa a sẽn maane, b ka gʋls tɩ be Israyɛll rĩm-dãmbã kibay sɛbã pʋs sɩda?
Zuda rĩm a Zotam
= 2Kib 27.1-4, 7-9 =
32 Israyɛll rĩm a Remaliya biig a Peka naam yʋʋm a yiib soabã wakate, Zuda rĩm a Uziya biig a Zotam sɩnga a naam rɩɩbo. 33 A sẽn wa n na n deeg naamã tɩ rẽ t'a tara yʋʋm pissi la a nu, la a rɩɩ naam Zeruzalɛm yʋʋm piig la a yoobe. A ma yʋʋr la a Zeruda, a Zadok bi-pugla. 34 Yẽ maana sẽn ya tɩrg Zu-soabã nifẽ, wa a ba a Uziya sẽn maanã. 35 Baoosgo, b ka yiis zĩ-zãongelsã ye. Nebã ket n maanda maand be la b yõod wusdu. A Zotam lebs n mee Zu-soabã roogã Yĩngr Zag-noorã. 36 Sã n ya ne a Zotam naama yɛl a taabã, la bũmb nins fãa a sẽn maanã, b ka gʋls tɩ be Zuda rĩm-dãmbã kibay sɛbã pʋs sɩda? 37 (Wakat kãnga, Zu-soabã sɩngame n tʋmd Aram rĩm a Rezin ne a Remaliya biig a Peka tɩ b wat n zabd ne Zuda.) 38 A Zotam babsa a saam-dãmba, la b mum-a lame n naag bãmba a Daviid tẽngã pʋgẽ, a baaba tẽngẽ. La a bi-ribl a Akaaz deega yẽ zĩigẽ n lebg rĩma.
