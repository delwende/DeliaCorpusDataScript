A Levi
6
1 A Levi kamb yaa a Gersom ne a Kehat la a Merari. 2 A Kehat kamb yaa a Amram ne a Zitsehaar la a Heborõ ne a Uziyɛlle. 3 A Amram kamb yaa a Aarõ ne a Moyiiz la a Maari. A Aarõ kamb yaa a Nadaab ne a Abihu la a Eleyazaar ne a Itamaare. 4 A Eleyazaar yaa a Fine ba. A Fine yaa a Abisuwa ba. 5 A Abisuwa yaa a Bukki ba. A Bukki yaa a Uzi ba. 6 A Uzi yaa a Zeraka ba. A Zeraka yaa a Merazot ba. 7 A Merazot yaa a Amariya ba. A Amariya yaa a Akitub ba. 8 A Akitub yaa a Tesadok ba. A Tesadok yaa a Akimaats ba. 9 A Akimaats yaa a Azariya ba. A Azariya yaa a Zokanan ba. 10 A Zokanan yaa a Azariya ba. Yaa Azariya kãng n yɩ maan-kʋʋdbã kãsem Wẽnnaam doog ning a Salomo sẽn me Zeruzalɛmmã pʋgẽ. 11 Azariya yaa a Amariya ba. A Amariya yaa a Akitub ba. 12 A Akitub yaa a Tesadok ba. A Tesadok yaa a Salum ba. 13 A Salum yaa a Hilkiza ba. A Hilkiza yaa a Azariya ba. 14 A Azariya yaa a Seraza ba. A Seraza yaa a Zehotsadok ba. 15 Zu-soabã sẽn kɩt t'a Nebukadnetzaar yõg Zuda ne Zeruzalɛm nebã n kẽes yembdã, b maga a Zehotsadok me n yoke. 16 A Levi kamb yaa a Gersom ne a Kehat la a Merari. 17 Ad a Gersom kambã yʋya: A Libni ne a Simeyi. 18 A Kehat kamb yaa a Amram ne a Zitsehaar la a Heborõ ne a Uziyɛlle. 19 A Merari kamb yaa a Makil ne a Musi. Levi-rãmbã yaa woto wa sẽn zems b yaab-rãmbã roto. 20 A Gersom biig yaa a Libni. A Libni biig yaa a Zakate. A Zakat biig la a Zimma. 21 Zimma yaa a Zoake. A Zoak biig la a Iddo. A Iddo biig la a Zerake. A Zerak biig la a Zeyatrayi. 22 A Kehat biig yaa a Amminadaabe. A Amminadaab biig la a Koore. A Koore biig la a Assiri. 23 A Assiir biig la a Ɩlkana. A Ɩlkana biig la a Ebiyazafe. A Ebiyazaf biig la a Assiri. A Assiir biig la a Takate. 24 A Takat biig la a Uriyɛlle. A Uriyɛl biig la a Oziyase. A Oziyas biig la a Sayilli. 25 A Ɩlkana kamb yaa a Amazayi ne a Akimote. 26 A Akimot biig la a Ɩlkana. A Ɩlkana biig la a Tesofayi. A Tesofayi biig la a Nakate. 27 A Nakat biig la a Eliyaabe. A Eliyaab biig la a Zerokam. A Zerokam biig la a Ɩlkana. A Ɩlkana biig la a Samwɛlle. 28 A Samwɛl kamb yaa a bi-kãseng a Zowɛll ne a Abiza sẽn ya a yiib-n-soãbã. 29 A Merari biig la a Makili. A Makili biig la a Libni. A Libni biig la a Simeyi. A Simeyi biig la a Uzza. 30 A Uzza biig la a Simeya, a Simeya biig la a Haggiza, a Haggiza biig la a Asaza.
Wẽnd-doogã yʋʋmba
= Zoz 21.4-39 =
31 Ad neb nins a Daviid sẽn yãk tɩ b soog yɩɩl Zu-soab roog pʋgẽ wakat ning kãabg koglgã sẽn paam zĩig n na vʋʋsã. 32 Bãmb maana b tʋʋmd n yɩɩl sɛk-roogã taoor hal t'a Salomo wa tõog n me Zu-soabã roogã Zeruzalɛm. La bãmb maana b tʋʋmdã wa sẽn zems b rog-n-mikã. 33 Ad neb nins sẽn da yɩɩndã kamb ne b yagens yʋya: A Kehat buudã yaa a Hemanne. yẽ yaa a Zowɛll sẽn ya a Samwɛl biiga. 34 A Samwɛl yaa a Ɩlkana biiga, a Ɩlkana yaa a Zerokam biiga, a Zerokam yaa a Eliyɛl biiga, a Eliyɛl yaa a Toak biiga, 35 a Toak yaa a Tisuf biiga, a Tisuf yaa a Ɩlkana biiga, a Ɩlkana yaa a Makat biiga, a Makat yaa a Amasayi biiga, 36 a Amasayi yaa a Ɩlkana biiga, a Ɩlkana yaa a Zowɛll biiga, a Zowɛll yaa a Azariya biiga, a Azariya yaa a Sofoni biiga, 37 a Sofoni yaa a Takat biiga, a Takat yaa a Assiir biiga, a Assiir yaa a Ebiyazaf biiga, a Ebiyazaf yaa a Koore biiga, 38 a Koore yaa a Zitesehaar biiga, a Zitesehaar yaa Ketat biiga, a Kehat yaa a Levi biiga, a Levi yaa a Israyɛll biiga. 39 A saam-biig a Azaf sẽn yas rɩtgã yaa a Berekiya biiga, a Berekiya yaa a Simeya biiga, 40 a Simeya yaa a Mikayɛl biiga, a Mikayɛl yaa a Baaseza biiga, a Baaseza yaa a Malkiza biiga, 41 Malkiza yaa a Etini biiga, a Etini yaa a Zerak biiga, a Zerak yaa a Adaza biiga, 42 a Adaza yaa a Etan biiga, a Etan yaa a Zimma biiga, a Zimma yaa a Simeyi biiga, 43 a Simeyi yaa a Zakat biiga, a Zakat yaa a Gersom biiga, a Gersom yaa a Levi biiga. 44 Bãmb saam-biig a Etan sẽn ya a Merari yagengã ra yaasda bãmb goabga. yẽ yaa a Kisi biiga, a Kisi yaa a Abdi biiga, a Abdi yaa a Malluk biiga, 45 a Malluk yaa a Hasabiya biiga, a Hasabiya yaa a Amatsiya biiga, a Amastiya yaa a Hilkiza biiga, 46 a Hilkiza yaa a Amtisi biiga, a Amtisi yaa a Bani biiga, a Bani yaa a Semeer biiga, 47 a Semeer yaa a Makili biiga, a Makili yaa a Musi biiga, a Musi yaa a Merari biiga, a Merari yaa a Levi biiga. 48 B yãka bãmb saam-biis nins sẽn ya Levi buudã tɩ b maan Wẽnnaam sɛk-roogã tuum fãa. 49 La yaa a Aarõ ne a kambã n maand maand tẽng-kugr ning b sẽn yõogd maand bugmã zugu, la b yõogd wusd wusd yõogr tẽng-kugrã zugu, la b maand zĩ-sõng n yɩɩdã tʋʋmã fãa, n maand paasg Israyɛll nebã yĩng wa sẽn zems bũmb nins Wẽnnaam tʋmtʋmd a Moyiiz sẽn yeelã. 50 Ad a Aarõ kamb ne a yagense: A Aarõ biig la a Eleyazaare. A Eleyazaar biig la a Fine. A Fine biig la a Abisuwa. 51 Abisuwa biig la a Bukki. A Bukki biig la a Uzi. A Uzi biig la a Zerakza. 52 A Zerakza biig la a Merazote. A Merazot biig la a Amariya. A Amariya biig la a Akitubi. 53 A Akitub biig la a Tesadoke. A Tesadok biig la a Akimatse. 54 Ad tẽns nins b sẽn yãk n kõ bãmb tɩ yɩ b zĩnd zĩisã poeerã yãka zĩ-kãens n kõ a Aarõ yagensã sẽn ya a Kehat buudã, tɩ bala pipi pʋɩɩrã da yaa bãmb n so: 55 B kõo bãmb Zuda Heborõ ne a pʋ-weesã. 56 La b kõo a Zefune biig a Kalɛɛb pʋt ninsi la tẽn-bãooneg nins sẽn gũbg tẽngã. 57 Woto, bãmb kõo a Aarõ yagensã Heborõ sẽn ya tẽng nebã sẽn tõe n tɩ soond a pʋgẽ, n paas Libna, ne Zatiire, ne Estemoa, 58 ne Hiliyɛnne, ne Debiiri, 59 ne Asanne, ne Zuta la Bet-Semes ne b pʋ-weesã. 60 La Benzame buudã tẽns sʋka, b kõo bãmb Gabawõ, ne Geba, ne Alemet la Anatot ne b pʋ-weesã. Tẽns-kãens b sẽn pʋɩ Kohat-rãmbã sõor yaa piig la a tãabo. 61 Poeerã yãka Manaase buudã pʋɩ-sʋkã tẽns piig n kõ a Kehat yagens a taabã sẽn kellã. 62 Poeerã yãka tẽns piig la a tãab Issakaar buudã, ne Asɛɛr buudã, ne Neftali buudã, ne Manaase buudã pʋɩ-sʋk sẽn be Bazã wã sʋk n kõ a Gersom yagensã, roog roogo. 63 Poeerã yãka tẽns piig la a yiib sẽn be Rubɛn buudã, ne Gaad buudã la Zabulon buudã sʋk n kõ a Merari yagensã, roog roogo. 64 Woto, Israyɛll nebã kõo Levi nebã tẽn-bãmbã ne b pʋ-weesã. 65 Tẽns nins b sẽn sõdg poorẽ wã yii Zuda nebã, ne Sɩmeyõ nebã la Benzame nebã tẽnsẽ. 66 Kehat-rãmbã rot kẽer dãmb tẽnsã yɩɩ Efaryim buudã tẽns la b yãgs kõ Levi bãmba. 67 Efaryim soolmã sẽn ya tãensã pʋgẽ, b yãka Sikɛm tẽng nebã sẽn tõe n tɩ kẽ soonde, la Gezɛɛre, 68 la Zokmeanne, la Bet Horon, 69 la Azalon, la Gat-Rimmon ne b pʋ-weesã kõ bãmba. 70 La Israyɛll rãmbã yãka Manaase buudã pʋɩ-sʋkã tẽns sẽn ya Aneer ne Bileam ne b pʋ-weesã n kõ a Kehat yagensã rot sẽn kellã. 71 d tẽns nins b sẽn kõ a Gersom yagensã: Manaase buudã pʋɩ-sʋkã tẽns b sẽn kõ-b yaa Bazã Golan ne Astarot ne b pʋ-weesã. 72 Issakaar buudã tẽns b sẽn kõ-b yaa Kedɛs ne Deborat 73 ne Ramot la Anem ne b pʋ-weesã. 74 Asɛɛr buudã tẽns b sẽn kõ-b yaa Masal ne Abdõ, 75 ne Hukoke, la Rehod ne a pʋ-weesã. 76 Neftali buudã tẽns b sẽn kõ-b yaa Galile Kedɛse, ne Hammon la Kiryatayim ne b pʋ-weesã. 77 Ad tẽns nins b sẽn kõ Meraar-dãmbã Levi neb nins sẽn kellã: Zabulon buudã tẽns b sẽn kõ-b yaa Rimmon la Taboor ne b pʋ-weesã. 78 Rubɛn buudã sẽn be Zʋrdẽ kʋɩlgã rʋʋnde, Zeriko Zʋrdẽ yaangã tẽns b sẽn kõ-b yaa Betsɛɛr sẽn be weoogã pʋgẽ wã, ne Zahtesa, 79 ne Kedemot la Mefaat ne b pʋ-weesã. 80 Gaad buudã tẽns b sẽn kõ-b yaa Galaad Ramote, ne Mahanayim, 81 ne Hesbon la Zaezɛɛr ne b pʋ-weesã.
