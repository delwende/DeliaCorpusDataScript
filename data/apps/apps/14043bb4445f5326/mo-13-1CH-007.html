A Issakaare
7
1 A Issakaar kamba: A Tola, a Puwa, a Zusub la a Simron - b fãa yaa a naase. 2 A Tola yagens kamba: A Uzi, a Refaza, a Zeriyɛlle, a Zakmayi, a Zibsam la a Samwɛlle. Bãmb n ya b yaab-rãmba rot kãsem-dãmba. B sõda a Tola yagensã a Daviid naamã wakat n mik t'a roogã raop sẽn ya tãb-biis yaa bãmb 22.600. 3 A Uzi biiga: A Zizrakza. A Zizrakza kamba: A Mikayɛlle, a Abdiyase, a Zowɛll la a Zisiza. Bãmb b nu wã fãa dag n ya kãsem-dãmba. 4 B sãn ges b roogã nebã, bãmb dag n tara daop 36.000 sẽn tõe n kẽng zabre, bala b dag n tara pagb ne kamb wusgo. 5 La bãmb saam-biisã sẽn ya Issakaar buudã b sẽn sõdg b roogã sõdgr pʋgẽ wã, tãb-biisã yaa neb 87.000.
A Benzame
6 A Benzame kamb a tãabo: A Bela, a Bekɛɛr la a Zediyayɛlle. 7 A Bela kamba: A Etsebõ, a Uzi, a Uziyɛlle, a Zerimot la a Iri. Bãmb yaa b nu, la b yaa buudã kãsem-dãmba. B roogã neb sõdgr pʋgẽ, tãb-biisã sõor yaa bãmb 22.034. 8 A Bekɛɛr kamba: A Zemira, a Zoaase, a Eliyezɛɛre, a Eliyoenayi, a Omri, a Zeremote, a Abiza, a Anatat la a Alamete. Bãmb fãa yaa a Bekiir kamba. 9 Bãmb roogã sõdgr pʋgẽ, b sõda buudã kãsem-dãmba, la tãb-biis 20.200. 10 A Zediyayɛl biiga: A Bilhanne. A Bilhan kamba: A Zesse, a Benzame, a Ehuudi, a Kenaana, a Zetanne, a Tarsis la a Akizakaare. 11 Bãmb fãa yaa a Zediyayɛl kamba, n ya b buudã kãsem-dãmba. B sõda neb nins sẽn tõe n kẽng zabrã n paam neb 17.200. 12 Suppim-dãmbã ne Huppim-dãmbã yaa a Ir yagense, la Husim-dãmbã yaa a Akɛɛr yagense.
A Neftali
13 A Neftali kamba: A Zahtisiyɛlle, a Guni, a Zetesɛɛr la a Salum. Bãmb yaa a Bilha yagense.
A Manaase
14 A Manaase kamb ne a yagense: A Asriyɛl yaa a pag ning sẽn ya Siiri nedã biiga. Pʋg-kãng doga a Makiir sẽn ya a Galaad ba wã. 15 A Makiir peega Huppim-dãmbã ne Suppim-dãmbã kamb n maan a pagba. yẽ tão yʋʋr la a Maaka. A biig a to yaa a Teselofsaade. A Teselofsaad roga kom-pugli bala. 16 A Makiir pag a Maaka roga bi-ribla, n pʋd a yʋʋr t'a Perɛsse. A Perɛs yao la a Serɛse. La a Serɛs kamb yaa a Ulam ne a Rekɛm. 17 A Ulam biiga: A Bedanne. Bãmb yaa a Manaase biig a Makiir biig a Galaad kamba. 18 Yẽ tão Hammelekɛt doga a Isod ne a Abiyezɛɛr la a Makla. 19 A Semida kamb yɩɩ: A Akzanne, a Sikɛm, a Liksi la a Aniyam.
A Efaryim
20 A Efaryim yagense: A Sutelake, a biig a Berɛde, a biig a Takate, a biig a Eleada, 21 biig a Zabaade, a biig a Sutelake. Gat neb sẽn dog be kʋʋ a Ezɛɛr ne a Eleyad wakat ning b sẽn kẽng n na n tɩ kãag bãmb rũmsã. 22 B ba a Efaryim yãb-b-la rasem wʋsgo, la a ba-biisã kẽngame n tɩ bels-a. 23 La a le kẽe ne a pagã, la a rɩka pʋg n dog bi-ribla, t'a pʋd a yʋʋr t'a Beriya, bala, toog paama a buudã. 24 Yẽ bi-pugl la a Seera. Yaa yẽ n sɩng Bet-Horon longã ne a tãngã meebo, n paas Uzzɛn Seera. 25 A biig la a Refake, t'a Refak biig ya a Resɛfe, t'a Resɛf biig ya a Telake, t'a Talak ya a Takanne, t'a Takan biig ya a Laedanne, 26 t'a Laedan biig ya a Ammihudi, t'a Ammihud biig ya a Elisama, 27 t'a Elisama biig yaa a Nunni, t'a Nun biig yaa a Zose. 28 Bãmb soolmã ne b zĩnd-zĩisã naaga Betɛll ne a tẽng-kɩremsã la Naaran yaanga, la yaa Gezɛɛr ne a tẽng-kɩremsã tẽng-kɩremsã taoore, la Sikɛm ne a tẽng-kɩremsã hal tɩ ta Gaza ne tẽng-kɩremsã. 29 A Manaase yagensã sooga Bet-Sean ne a tẽng-kɩremsã, la Taanak ne a tẽng-kɩremsã, la Door ne a tẽng-kɩremsã. A Israyɛll biig a Zozɛf yagensã zĩnda tẽn-bãmba pusẽ.
A Azɛɛre
30 A Azɛɛr kamba: A Zimna, a Zisva, a Zisvi la a Beriya. B tão la a Serake. 31 Beriya kamba: A Hebɛɛr ne a Malkiyɛl sẽn ya a Birzavit ba. 32 A Hebɛɛr yaa a Zaflɛt ne a Someer la a Hotam ne b tão a Suwa ba. 33 A Zaflɛt kamba: A Pasak ne a Bimhall la a Asvate. Bãmb yaa a Zaflɛt kamba. 34 A Someer kamba: A Aki, a Rohega, a Hubba la a Aram. 35 Yẽ yao a Helem kamba: A Tesofake, a Zimna, a Selɛs la a Amalle. 36 A Tesofak kamba: A Suwake, a Harnefɛɛre, a Suwalle, a Beri, a Zimna, 37 a Betsɛɛre, a Hode, a Samma, a Sɩlsa, a Zitram la a Beera. 38 A Zetɛɛr kamba: A Zefune ne a Pispa la a Ara. 39 A Ulla kamba: A Arak ne a Hanniyɛl la a Ritisza. 40 Nin-bãmbã fãa yaa a Azɛɛr yagense - la b yaa b buudã kãsem-dãmba, nin-tũusdsi, gãndaado, la b yaa b nanamsã kãsem-dãmba. Bãmb neb nins sẽn tõe n kẽng zabre, tɩ b yʋyã be b roogã sõdgr pʋgẽ yaa bãmb 26.000.
