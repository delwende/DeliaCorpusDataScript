A Daviid lebgda Israyɛll rĩma
= 2Sam 5.1-3 =
11
1 Israyɛll nebã fãa tigma taab a Divid nengẽ Heborõ n yeel yaa: «Gese, tõnd ne yãmb yaa bũmb a yembre. 2 Wakat ning a Sayil sẽn da yaa rĩma, yaa yãmb n da lʋɩt Israyɛll nebã taoor n kẽnd zabre. La Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam yeela yãmb tɩ ‘y na n yɩɩ bãmb Israyɛll nebã gũud la b naaba.’» 3 Woto, Israyɛll kãsem-dãmbã fãa kẽnga naab a Daviid nengẽ Heborõ. La yẽ maana kaool ne bãmb Zu-soabã taoor be. La b ninga a Daviid rẽung kaam t'a lebg Israyɛll rĩm wa sẽn zems Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam sẽn da yeel a Samwɛllã.
A Daviid zab n deega Zeruzalɛm
= 2 Sam 5.6-10 =
4 A Daviid ne Israyɛll nebã fãa kẽnga Zeruzalɛm b sẽn da boond tɩ Zebuuzã. Zebuuz nebã sẽn da be be wã 5 yeela a Daviid yaa: «Fo ka na n kẽ ka ye.» La a Daviid tõoga Siyõ b sẽn me t'a tar pãngã, tɩ lebg a Daviid tẽnga. 6 A Daviid yeelame tɩ ned ning sẽn na n lʋɩ-a taoor n zab ne Zebuuz nebã na n lebga tãb-biisã naaba. La a Teseruza biig a Zoab n lʋɩ taoore, la a lebga tãb-biisã naaba. 7 A Daviid zĩnda tẽngã pʋgẽ. Woto n kɩt tɩ b boond tẽngã t'a Daviid tẽnga. 8 La yẽ sɩngame n me Millo ne tẽng-kɩremsã n gũbg tengã. La a Zoab manega tẽngã zĩis a taabã. 9 A Daviid pãng paasa wʋsgo, bala Zu-soabã sẽn tõe fãa wã zĩnda ne-a.
A Daviid gãndaado
= 2Sam. 23.8-39 =
10 Ad a Daviid gãndaadã nanamse - bãmb ne Israyɛll nebã fãa sõng-a-la sõma a naamẽ wã, t'a soog soolmã fãa wa Zu-soabã sẽn da kãabã - 11 d a Daviid gãndaadã yʋya: A Hakmon ne a Zasobeam n dag n ya tãn-sob-n-kãsemsã naaba. Yaa yẽ n kʋ neb kobs-tã ne kãnd zabr a ye pʋgẽ wã. 12 Sẽn pʋgd-a yẽ wã yaa Akoh rao a Dodo biig a Eleyazaare. yẽ yaa gãndaad a tãabã ned a ye. 13 Yẽ ra bee ne a Daviid Pas-Dammin, zĩig ning Filisti nebã sẽn da tigim n sũ tãpã. Orz pʋʋgã sẽn be zĩig ningã, tãp-biisã zoee Filisti nebã taoore. 14 La Bãmb yaasa pʋʋgã tẽn-sʋk n zab ne Filisti rãmbã. La Zu-soabã fãaga Israyɛll nebã n kõ-b tõog-kãsenga. 15 Nanams pis-tã wã neb a tãab kẽnga a Daviid nengẽ Adullam tãngã bok pʋgẽ, wakat ning Filisti nebã sẽn be Refayim tãngã long pʋgẽ wã. 16 Wakat kãng t'a Daviid da tɩ soonda tãensã sʋka, la Filisti tãb-biisã ra bee Betelhɛm. 17 Ko-yũud n da tar a Daviid hal t'a yeel yaa: «Ned sãn tõe n tɩ yõng koom bulg ning sẽn be Betelhɛm noorã n wa kõ-m tɩ m yũ!» 18 La gãndaad a taabã modgame n pɩʋʋg Filisti rambã tãb-sikã n tɩ yõng Betelhɛm noor bulgã koom n wa kõ a Daviidi. La a Daviid ka sak n yũ-a ye. yẽ rik-a lame n daag tẽng tɩ yɩ maoong Zu-soabã yĩnga. 19 Yẽ yeelame yaa: «Bɩ Wẽnnaam da sak tɩ m maan woto ye. Tɩ bõe, mam sãn maan woto, na n yɩɩ wa m sẽn yʋʋd nin-kãensã zɩɩm. Bala, bãmb gẽnega b yõey n tɩ yõng koomã n wa.» yẽ ka yũ-a ye. Yaa gãndaad a tãab kãens n maan woto. 20 A Zoab yao a Abisayi n ya gãndaad a tãabã kãsma. Yaa yẽ n kʋ neb kobs-tã ne kãndã, la a yʋʋrã bee neb a tãabã sʋka. 21 Yẽ paama waoogr naoor a yiib n yɩɩd a taabã, baa ne a sẽn dag n ka be bãmb sʋkã. 22 Kabetsɛl rao a Zehozada biig a Benaza yaa gãndaoogo, la a maana tʋʋm-kãsemse. Yaa yẽ n kʋ Moaab gãnda-kãsems a yiibã. La yaa yẽ n kẽ bok pʋgẽ kʋ gɩgemd daar ning sa-pɩlmã sẽn lʋɩ wã. 23 La yẽ kʋʋ Ezipt rao a ye woglem sẽn ta kãntɩɩg a nu. Ezipt rao wã ra zãada kãnd t'a raoogã taglem ta koeer-bõanga. La a Benaza zãa rasaar bal n kẽng n tɩ zab ne-a. yẽ reega Ezipt rao wã kãndã n wẽneg n kʋ-a. 24 A Zehozada biig a Benaza maana bõn-bãmbã n paam yʋʋr gãndaad a tãabã sʋka. 25 Yẽ paama waoogr n yɩɩd neb pis-tã wã, la a ka ta pipi neb a tãabã ye. A Daviid kɩtame t'a lebg a gũudbã naaba. 26 Tãb-biisã gãndaadã yɩɩ: A Zoab yao a Azayɛlle, Betelhɛm dao a Dodo biig a Ɩlkananne, 27 Haroor dao a Sammote, Palon dao a Helɛtse, 28 Tekoa rao a Ikkɛs biig a Ira ne Anatot rao a Abiyezɛɛre, 29 Hus rao a Sibbekayi, Ahoak rao a Ilayi, 30 Netofa roa a Maharayi, Netofa rao a Baana biig a Helede, 31 Benzame Gibeya rao a Ribayi biig a Itayi, Piraton dao a Benaza, 32 Gaas tãngã longẽ rao a Hurayi, Araba rao a Abiyɛlle, 33 Bakarum dao a Azmavɛte, Saalbon rao a Eliyakba, 34 Gizon rao a Bɛn-Hasemme, Haraar rao a Sage biig a Zonatã, 35 Haraar dao a Sakaar biig a Akiyamme, a Uur biig a Elifalle, 36 Makɛɛr dao a Hefɛɛre, Palon dao a Akiza, 37 Karmel dao a Hetsere, a Ezbayi biig a Naarayi, 38 a Natã ba-biig a Zowɛlle, a Hagri biig a Mibkaare, 39 Ammo ned a Teselɛke, Berot rao a Nakrayi sẽn ya a Teseruza biig a Zoab zab-teed zãada, 40 Zetɛɛr roap a Ira ne a Garɛbe, 41 Hɛt ned Uuri, a Aklayi biig a Zabaade, 42 la Rubɛn ned a Siza biig a Adina sẽn ya Rubɛn nebã kãsm a ye, n tar a neb pis-tã, 43 a Maaka biig a Hananne, Mitni rao a Zozafate, 44 Astarot rao a Uziya, Arowɛɛr rao a Hotam kamba a Sama ne a Zehiyɛlle, 45 a Simri biig a Zediyayɛlle, Tiz rao a Zoka sẽn ya a saam-biigã, 46 Makavim dao a Eliyɛlle, a Ɩlnaam kamb a Zeribayi ne a Zosaviya, Moaab ned a Zitma, 47 Mezobayi neb a Eliyɛl ne a Obɛɛd la a Zaasiyɛlle.
