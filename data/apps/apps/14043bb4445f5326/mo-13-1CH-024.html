Maan-kʋʋdbã welgre
24
1 Ad a Aarõ yagensã buud sẽn ya to-to. A Aarõ kamb yaa a Nadaab ne a Abihu la a Eleyazaar ne a Itamaare. 2 A Nadaab ne a Abihu renga b ba wã n ki n ka paam kamb ye. La Eleyazaar ne a Itamaar lebga maan-kʋʋdba. 3 A Eleyazaar yageng a Akimelɛk sõnga a Daviid n pʋɩ a Aarõ yagensã tɩ yɩ sags wʋsgo, n gʋls b yʋy wa sẽn zems tʋʋm nins b sẽn maandã. 4 A Eleyazaar yagensã tara buud kãsem-dãmb wʋsg n yɩɩd a Itamaar yagensã. Woto, bãmb pʋɩɩ a Eleyazaar yagensã sags piig la a yoobe, tɩ kãsem-dãmb piig la a yoob soog-ba. La a Itamaar yagensã yaa sags a nii, tɩ b kãsem-dãmb a nii so-ba. 5 Bãmb kɩtame tɩ poeerã yãk bãmba, tɩ bala, a Eleyazaar ne a Itamaar yagensã fãa tara sɛk-zĩig nanams la Wẽnnaam tʋʋmd nanamse. 6 Gʋlsd a Semaeza sẽn ya Levi ned a Netaneyɛll biig gʋlsa bãmb dẽmã ne nanamsã la maan-kʋʋd a Tesadok ne a Abiyataar biig a Akimelɛke, la maan-kʋʋdbã buudã rot kãsem-dãmba, la Levi nebã rot kãsem-dãmbã taoore. La poeerã yãka a Eleyazaar doog a ye, la a yãka a Itamaar doog a ye me. 7 Yaa a Zehozarib la poeerã yak pipi, yiib soab yaa a Zedaeza, 8 a tãab soab yaa a Harim, a naas soab yaa a Seorim, 9 a nu soab yaa a Malkiza, a yoob soab yaa a Mizamin, 10 a yopoe soab yaa a Hakkotse, a nii soab yaa a Abiza, 11 wɛ soab yaa a Zozoe, piig soab yaa a Sekaniya, 12 piig la a ye soab yaa a Eliyasiibi, piig la a yiib soab yaa a Zakim, 13 piig la a tãab soab yaa a Huppa, piig la a naas soab yaa a Zesebeabe, 14 piig la a nu soab yaa a Bilga, piig la a yoob soab yaa a Immeere, 15 piig la a yopoe soab yaa a Heziiri, piig la a nii soab yaa a Happitsetse, 16 piig la a wɛ soab yaa a Petakza, pissi soab yaa a Ezekiyɛlle, 17 pissi la a yembr soab yaa a Zakinni, pissi la a yiib soab yaa a Gamulli, 18 pissi la a tãab soab yaa a Delaza, pissi la a naas soab yaa a Maaziya.
19 B gʋlsa nin-bãmbã woto tɩ zems bãmb sẽn na n kẽng Zu-soabã roogẽ n tʋm wa sẽn zems tõog ning sẽn sɩng ne b yaab a Aarõ wa Israyɛll Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam sẽn yeelã.
Levit-rãmbã sẽn kellã
20 La ne Levi yagens a taabã, b kãsem-dãmbã la woto: A Amram kambẽ wã yaa a Subayɛlle. A Subayɛl kambẽ wã, yaa a Zekdiya. 21 Rekabiya kãmbẽ wã, yaa bi-kãseng a Zisiza. 22 Zitsehaar buudẽ wã, yaa a Selomote. A Selomot kambẽ wã, yaa a Zakate. 23 A Heborõ kambẽ wã, pipi soab yaa a Zeriza, t'a Amariya ya yiib soaba, t'a Zakaziyɛl ya tãab soaba. 24 A Uziyɛl biig yaa a Mise. A Mise kambẽ wã, yaa a Samiiri. 25 A Mise ba-biig yaa a Zisiza. A Zisiza kambẽ wã, yaa a Zakari. 26 A Merari kamb yaa a Makili ne a Husi. A Zaaziza biig yaa a Beno. 27 A Merari kamba: A Zaaziza kambẽ wã, yaa a Soham ne a Zakkaar la a Ibri. 28 A Makili kambẽ wã, yaa a Eleyazaar sẽn ka paam bi-riblã. 29 A Kis kambẽ wã: A Kis biig a Zerakmeyɛlle. 30 La a Musi kamb a Makili ne a Edɛɛr la a Zerimote. A Levi-rãmbã la woto wa sẽn zems b buudu. 31 Bãmb me maana poeer wa b saam-biisã sẽn ya a Aarõ yagensã sẽn maanã, naab a Daviid ne a Tesadok la a Akimelɛk ne maan-kʋʋdbã buudã kãsem-dãmbã la Levi-rãmbã taoore. B maana kẽem soabã buudã neb wa b sẽn maan yao wã buudã nebã.
