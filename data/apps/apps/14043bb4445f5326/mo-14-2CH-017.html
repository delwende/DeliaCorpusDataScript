Zuda rĩm a Zozafate
17
1 La a biig a Zozafat reega naam. La yẽ modgame n paas a soolmã pãng tɩ Israyɛll da wa tõog-a ye. 2 Yẽ kɩtame tɩ tãb-biis zĩnd Zuda tẽns nins fãa sẽn tar lalsã pʋsẽ. La yẽ kõo tãb-biis nanams noor Zuda tẽngã pʋsẽ la Efaryim tẽns nins a ba a Aza sẽn da reegã pʋsẽ. 3 Zu-soabã zĩnda ne a Zozafat yẽ sẽn maan wa a yaab a Daviid sẽn da sɩng n maanã yĩnga. yẽ ka bao Baall-dãmb ye, 4 yẽ baoo a ba Wẽnnaam la a sak bãmb tõodã. yẽ ka maan wa Israyɛll nebã sẽn da maandã ye. 5 Zu-soabã paasa a Zozafat rẽungã pãnga. La Zuda nebã fãa talla kũun n tɩ kõ-a, la a tara arzegs ne ziir wʋsgo. 6 A sũ-keelem paasa ne Zu-soabã sakre. La a kɩtame tɩ b le yiis kunkub tẽng-kugã ne bõn-naandsã Zuda. 7 Yẽ sẽn deeg naam tɩ yʋʋm tãablẽ, yẽ tʋma a nanams a Bɛn-Hayil ne a Abdiyase, la a Zakari ne a Netaneyɛll la a Mise tɩ b kẽng Zuda tẽnsã pʋsẽ n tɩ zãms nebã. 8 La Levi neb yãaga bãmba, yaa a Semaeza ne a Netaniya, la a Zebadiya ne a Azayɛlle, la a Semiramot ne a Zonatã, la a Adoniza ne a Tobiza, la a Tob-Adoniza ne maan-kʋʋdb a Elisama ne a Zoram. Bãmb zãmsa nebã Zuda. 9 Bãmb tara Zu-soabã tõodã sebr n saagd n kẽngd Zuda tẽnsã fãa n zãmsd nebã. 10 Zu-soabã rabeem yõka tẽns nins fãa sẽn gũbg Zuda wã rĩm-dãmba. La bãmb ka zab ne a Zozafat ye. 11 Filisti neb kẽer talla kũun n tɩ kõ a Zozafate, la b talla wanzuri n tɩ yao b yõor yaoodã. La Araab-rãmb me kõo yẽ pe-raad tus a yopoe la kobs-yopoe 12 A Zozafat lebga kãseng n yɩɩda. yẽ mee tẽns sẽn tar lal-bɛda la laog bĩngr tẽns Zuda. 13 Yẽ ra tara tʋʋm wʋsg Zuda tẽnsã pʋsẽ. La a tara tãb-biis Zeruzalɛm sẽn ya gãndaado. 14 Ad b yʋy sẽn gʋls wa sẽn zems b buudu. Zuda neb tus ka tɛkã nanams yaa a Adna sẽn ya gãndaadã tus kobs-tã naabã, 15 la a yiib soab yaa a Zokanan sẽn ya neb tus kobs-yi la pis-nii naabã, 16 a tãab soab yaa a Zikri biig a Amasiya sẽn maan Zu-soabã tʋʋmd ne yam-leoogã. yẽ yaa gãndaad tus kobs-yi naaba. 17 Benzame neb yaa gãndao-kãseng a Eliyada ne a neb tus kobs-yi sẽn bob logd ne gãndã. 18 A yiib soab yaa a Zozabad ne a neb tus koabg la pis-nii ne b zab-teedã. 19 Yaa nin-bãmbã n maan rĩmã tʋʋmã tɩ b ka gomd neb nins rĩmã sẽn kɩt tɩ b zĩnd tẽns nins sẽn tar lals Zudã fãa pʋgẽ wã ye.
