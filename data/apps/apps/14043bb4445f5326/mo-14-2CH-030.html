A Ezekiyas maana Zʋʋg-n-basã kibsã
30
1 A Ezekiyas tʋma neb tɩ b kẽng Israyɛll ne Zuda tẽnsã fãa pʋsẽ. La yẽ gʋlsa sɛb n yeel Efaryim ne Manaase nebã tɩ b wa Zu-soabã roogã pʋgẽ Zeruzalɛm n wa maan Zʋʋg-n-bas kibs n waoog Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam. 2 Rĩmã ne a nanamsã la Zeruzalɛm nebã fãa yãka yam n dat n maan Zʋʋg-n-bas kibsã kiuug a yiib soabã pʋgẽ. 3 Tɩ bõe, b ka tõee n maan-a a wakatã meng ye. Bala, maan-kʋʋdb nins sẽn maneg b mens n ya yɩlma wã sõor ka sek ye, la nebã nan ka tigim Zeruzalɛm me ye. 4 La bõn-kãng taa rĩmã ne nebã fãa yam. 5 La bãmb yãka yam n kɩt tɩ b moon koɛɛg Israyɛll zĩigã fãa, sẽn sɩng ne Dan hal tɩ ta Beer-Seba, n bool nebã tɩ b wa Zeruzalɛm n wa maan Zʋʋg-n-bas kibs n waoog Israyɛll Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam. Tɩ bõe, neb wʋsg ra ka le maand wa sẽn gʋlsã ye. 6 La b sẽn tʋm b-rãmbã zãa rĩmã ne nanamsã sɛb n kẽng Israyɛll ne Zuda zĩigã fãa. Bãmb saka rĩmã noor n yeel yaa: «Israyɛll neba, bɩ y wa tɩ d le sak a Abraham ne a Izaak la a Israyɛll Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam tɩ b sõng yãmb neb nins Assiri rĩm-dãmbã sẽn ka paam n yõgã. 7 Bɩ y ra yɩ wa y ba-rãmbã ne y saam-biis sẽn maan wẽng n beeg y yaab-rãmbã Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam hal tɩ b bas tɩ b sãam wa yãmb sẽn ne wã ye. 8 Ra keng-y y yũbli wa y yaab-rãmbã sẽn da maanã ye. Bɩ y sak Zu-soabã n kẽng bãmb sɛkã zĩigẽ. Yaa zĩig ning bãmb sẽn welg toor wakat fãa yĩngã. Sak-y Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam tɩ b sũur sẽn yik ne yãmbã sigi. 9 Yãmb sãn lebg n wa sak Zu-soabã, b na n zoee y saam-biisã ne y kamb nins b sẽn da yõgã nimbãaneg n kɩt tɩ b lebg n wa tẽn-kãngã pʋgẽ. Tɩ bõe, Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam yaa nonglem la nimbãan-zoeer soaba. Yãmb sãn lebg n wa bãmb nengẽ, bãmb ka na n wẽneg b neng n bas yãmb ye.»
10 Woto, neb nins a Ezekiyas sẽn tʋmã kẽngame n saag Efaryim ne Manaase tẽnsã pʋse, hal tɩ ta Zabulon. La tẽngã nebã paoog-b lame n yaal-ba. 11 Yaa Efaryim ne Manaase la Zabulon neb bilf bal n sik b mens n kẽng Zeruzalɛm. 12 La Zu-soabã kɩtame tɩ Zuda nebã fãa paam yam a yembr n maan bũmb nins rĩmã ne nanamsã sẽn yeelã n sak Zu-soabã goama. 13 Neb wʋsg tũu taab n kẽng Zeruzalɛm kiuug a yiib soabã n tɩ maan bur sẽn ka rã-bɩll kibsa. Neb nins sẽn tigmã yaa kʋʋn-kãsenga. 14 Bãmb nanga pãng n yiis tẽng-kug nins sẽn be Zeruzalɛm la tẽng-kug nins fãa b sẽn yõogd tɩdar bugm b zutã n lob Sedorõ longã pʋgẽ. 15 La bãmb kʋʋ Zʋʋg-n-bas pe-bi kiuugã rasem piig la a naas raare, tɩ maan-kʋʋdbã ne Levi nebã paam yãnde. La bãmb manega b mens n yɩ yɩlma n tall maand sẽn na n yõog bugum n kẽng Zu-soabã roogã pʋgẽ. 16 La bãmb yaasa b zĩisẽ wa Wẽnnaam dao a Moyiiz tõogã sẽn da yeelã. Levi nebã kɩsa maan-kʋʋdbã maandã zɩɩm tɩ b tɩ kɩrs tẽng-kugrã zugu. 17 Tɩ bõe, neb nins sẽn tigmã, neb wʋsg ka maneg b mens n ya yɩlma ye. Woto, yɩɩ tɩlɛ tɩ Levi nebã kʋ neb nins fãa sẽn ka yilmã Zʋʋg-n-bas pe-bil n kõ-b tɩ b yɩ sõma Zu-soabã yĩnga. 18 Tɩ bõe, neb wʋsg sẽn yi Efaryim ne Manaase la Issakaar ne Zabulon wã ka maneg b mens ye. La baoosgo, bãmb dɩɩ Zʋʋg-n-bas kibsã tɩ ka zems wa Gʋls-Sõamyã sẽn yeelã ye. La a Ezekiyas pʋʋsa Wẽnnaam b yĩng n yeel yaa: «Bɩ Wẽnnaam sõnga kõ ned ning fãa 19 sẽn baood a yaab-rãmbã Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam ne a sũur fãa wã sugri, baa yẽ sãn ka maneg a meng tɩ yɩ yɩlemd wa sɛk-zĩigã tõog sẽn gomd sõmlem yellã sẽn yeelã.» 20 La Zu-soabã leoka a Ezekiyas pʋʋsgã n kõ nebã sugri. 21 La Israyɛll neb nins sẽn be Zeruzalɛmmã maana bur sẽn ka ra-bɩll kibsã rasm a yopoe ne sũ-noogo. La Levi nebã ne maan-kʋʋdbã pẽga Zu-soabã n yɩɩl ne b pãng tɛk bãmb yĩng daar fãa. 22 La a Ezekiyas pẽga Levi neb nins sẽn tar bãngr wʋsg ne Zu-soabã waoogr tʋʋmdã. La nebã rɩɩ kibsã rɩɩb rasm a yopoe, la b maan laafɩ maand n pʋʋs b yaab-rãmbã Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam barka. 23 La nebã fãa yẽsa ne taab n yãk yam n na n kel n maan kibs rasm a yopoe n paase. La b maana woto ne sũ-noogo. 24 Bala, Zuda naab a Ezekiyas kõo nebã lols tusr la piis tus a yopoe maandã yĩnga. La nanamsã me kõo bãmb lols tusr la piis tus piiga. La maan-kʋʋdb wʋsg manega b mens n ya yɩlma. 25 Zuda nebã fãa ne maan-kʋʋdbã la Levi nebã ne Israyɛll neb nins sẽn yi Israyɛll n wa wã, ne sãamb nins sẽn be Zuda wã fãa maana sũ-noogo. 26 Sũ-no-kãseng zĩnda Zeruzalɛm, tɩ bõe, hal Israyɛll naab a Daviid biig a Salomo zamaanẽ, bũmb a woto ka le maan Zeruzalɛm ye. 27 La maan-kʋʋdbã ne Levi nebã yikame n yaas n ning nebã barka. La bãmb pʋʋsgã zẽka yĩngr n ta Zu-soabã yi-sõngã pʋgẽ.
