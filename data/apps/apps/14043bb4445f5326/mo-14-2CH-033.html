Zuda naab a Manaase
= 2Rĩm 21:1-18 =
33
1 A Manaase sẽn wa n na n deeg naamã t'a tara yʋʋm piig la a yiibu. A rɩɩ naam Zeruzalɛm yʋʋm pis-nu la a nu. 2 A Manaase beega Zu-soabã n maan wã buud nins Zu-soabã sẽn da kɩt tɩ Israyɛll nebã rigã. 3 Yẽ le mee kunkub tẽng-kug nins a ba a Ezekiyas sẽn da sãamã, la a me tẽng-kug a Baall yĩnga. yẽ maana bõn-naandg a Astarte yĩnga, la a waoog ãds la a tũ-ba. 4 La yẽ mee tẽng-kug Zu-soabã roogã pʋgẽ, baa Zu-soabã sẽn yeel tɩ yaa Zeruzalɛm la bãmb na n ning b yʋʋrã wakat fãa wã. 5 Yẽ mee tẽng-kug ãdsã yĩng Zu-soabã roogã zags a yiibã pʋsẽ. 6 Yẽ yõoga a kamb bugum Hinnon longã pʋgẽ la a sok sawat ne kinkirsi, la a kẽng bagb ne tɩɩm-dãmb nengẽ. yẽ beega Zu-soabã wʋsgo, n kɩt tɩ b sũur yiki. 7 Yẽ ninga bõn-naandg ning a sẽn maanã Zu-soabã roogã pʋgẽ. Yaa roog ning Zu-soabã sẽn yeel a Daviid ne a biig a Salomo tɩ: «Yaa ro-kãng ne Zeruzalɛm la mam yãk Israyɛll buudã fãa sʋka, la m na n ninga m yʋʋr be wakat fãa. 8 Israyɛll nebã sãn sak n maan bũmb nins mam sẽn yeelã, wa sẽn zems tõodã ne rog-n-mikã la minund nins a Moyiiz sẽn da kõ bãmbã, mam ka na n le kɩt tɩ b bʋdg n yi tẽng ning mam sẽn kõ b yaab-rãmbã pʋgẽ ye.» 9 La a Manaase kɩtame tɩ Zuda ne Zeruzalɛm nebã maan wẽng n yɩɩd buud nins Zu-soabã sẽn da kɩt tɩ Israyɛll nebã kʋ wã. 10 Zu-soabã goma ne a Manaase la ne b nebã, la b ka kelg ye. 11 Woto, Zu-soabã kɩtame tɩ Assiri rĩmã tãb-biisã nanams kẽng n tɩ zab ne bãmba, n yõk a Manaase ne leoos n balg ne bãens n tall n kẽng Babilonne. 12 La a toogã pʋgẽ, a kosa Zu-soabã sõngre, n sik a meng wʋsg a yaab-rãmbã Wẽnnaam taoore. 13 Yẽ pʋʋsa bãmba, la Wẽnnaam leoka a pʋʋsgã, la b kelga a kosgã n lebs-a Zeruzalɛm a soolmã pʋgẽ. La woto, a Manaase bãngame tɩ yaa Zu-soabã bal n ya a Wẽnnaam. 14 Yõwã zugẽ, yẽ mee lalg a Daviid tẽngã yĩng Gihon koomã goabg longã pʋgẽ hal tɩ ta zĩm zakã noor n gũbg Ofɛlle, la a yaa zãonglg wʋsgo. La yẽ kɩtame tɩ tãb-biisã nanams kẽer zĩnd Zuda tẽns nins sẽn tar lalsã pʋsẽ. 15 La yẽ rɩka bu-zĩmsã wẽnnaam-dãmbã ne bõn-naandg ning sẽn be Zu-soabã roogã pʋgẽ wã, la tẽng-kug nins yẽ sẽn da me Zu-soabã roogã tãngã zugã ne Zeruzalɛm pʋgẽ wã n yiis tẽngã pʋgẽ n lobe. 16 La yẽ manega Zu-soabã tẽng-kugrã n maan laafɩ maand ne bark pʋʋsg maand be. La yẽ yeela Zuda nebã tɩ b waoog Israyɛll Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam. 17 Baoosgo, nebã kel n maana maand kunkub zutu, la yaa Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam yĩng bala. 18 A Manaase tʋʋm a taabã ne a pʋʋsgã yẽ sẽn pʋʋs Wẽnnaam, la bãngdbã goamã sẽn togs ne Israyɛll Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam yʋʋrã fãa gʋlsa Israyɛll rĩm-dãmbã sebr pʋgẽ. 19 La yẽ pʋʋsgã ne Wẽnnaam sẽn maan to-to n leok yẽ kosgã, la a kongrã ne a yel-wẽnã fãa, la kunkub nins yẽ sẽn me tẽng-kug la a lugl da-lugdsã n maan bõn-naands b zut a sẽn da nan ka sik a mengã fãa gʋlsa bãngdbã kibayã sebr pʋgẽ. 20 La a Manaase kiime tɩ b muma a yiri. La a biig a Amo deega naam.
Zuda naab a Amo
= 2Rĩm 21.19-24 =
21 Amo sẽn wa n na n deeg naamã t'a tara yʋʋm pissi la yiibu. A rɩɩ naam Zeruzalɛm yʋʋm a yiibu. 22 Yẽ me beega Zu-soabã wa a Manaase. A Amo maana maand n kõ bõn-naands nins a ba sẽn da maanã la a woag-ba. 23 La yẽ ka sik a meng Zu-soabã taoor wa a ba sẽn da maanã ye. yẽ konga bʋʋm wʋsg n yɩɩda. 24 Yẽ tʋmtʋmdbã sɩɩga taab noor n kʋ-a a yiri. 25 La tẽngã nebã kʋʋ neb nins sẽn sɩɩg taab noor n kʋ naab a Amo wã. La b kõo a biig a Zosiyas naam.
