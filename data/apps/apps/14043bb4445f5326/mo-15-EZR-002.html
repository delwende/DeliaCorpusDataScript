Neb nins sẽn lebg n wa Zeruzalɛmmã yʋya
= Neh 7.6-73 =
2
1 Ad Zuda tẽngã neb nins sẽn yi yembd pʋgẽ n kuilã. Yaa bãmb la Babilon naab a Nebukadnetzaar sẽn da yõg n tall n kẽng Babilon tẽngã. La bãmb leba Zeruzalɛm ne Zuda. Bãmb ned kam fãa kẽnga a meng ba-yiri. 2 Bãmb tũu a Zorobabɛll ne a Zozoe la a Nehemi ne a Seraza la a Reelaza ne a Mardose la a Bilsam ne a Mispaar la a Bigvayi ne a Rehum la a Baana n ta be. Ad Israyɛll daopã sõore:
3 A Pareyos yagensã yaa 2172, 4 a Sefatiya yagensã yaa 372, 5 a Arak yagensã yaa 775, 6 a Pakat-Moaab sẽn dat n togs t'a Zozoe ne a Zoaab yagensã yaa 2812, 7 a Elam yagensã yaa 1254, 8 a Zatu yagensã yaa 945, 9 Zakayi yagensã yaa 760, 10 a Bani yagensã yaa 642, 11 Bebayi yagensã yaa 623, 12 la a Asgaad yagensã yaa 1222,
13 a Adonikam yagensã yaa 666, 14 a Bigvayi yagensã yaa 2056, 15 a Adin yagensã yaa 454, 16 a Atɛɛr sẽn ya a Ezekiyaas buudã yagensã yaa 98, 17 a Besayi yagensã yaa 323, 18 a Zora yagensã yaa 112, 19 a Hasum yagensã yaa 223, 20 a Gibaar yagensã yaa 95,
21 Betelhɛm nebã yaa 123, 22 Netofa nebã yaa 56, 23 Anatot nebã yaa neb 128, 24 Asmavɛt nebã yaa 42, 25 Kirzat-Arim ne Kefira ne Berot nebã yaa 743, 26 Rama ne Geba nebã yaa 621, 27 Mikmas nebã yaa 122, 28 Betɛll ne Ayi nebã yaa 223, 29 a Nebo yagensã yaa 52, 30 a Magbis yagensã yaa 156, 31 Elam a to yagensã yaa 1254, 32 a Harim yagensã yaa 320, 33 Lod ne Hadiid la Ono nebã yaa 725, 34 Zeriko nebã yaa 345, 35 Sena nebã yaa 3630.
36 Maan-kʋʋdbã: A Zedayeza sẽn ya a Zozoe buudã yagensã yaa 973, 37 a Immer yagensã yaa 1052, 38 a Pasuur yagensã yaa 1247, 39 a Harim yagensã yaa 1017.
40 Levi-rãmbã: a Zozoe ne a Kidmiyɛll la a Hodaviya yagensã yaa 74,
41 Yɩɩn-yɩɩndbã: a Azaf yagensã yaa 128,
42 Wẽnd-doogã noor gũudbã: a Salum ne a Atɛɛr la a Talmo ne a Akuub la a Hatita ne a Sobayi yagensã fãa yaa neb 139.
43 Wẽnnaam doogã tʋm-tʋmdbã: a Tisika ne a Hasufa, la a Tabaot yagense, 44 a Kerose, ne a Siya la a Padõ yagense, 45 a Labana ne a Hagaba la a Akuub yagense, 46 a Hagaab ne a Samlayi la a Hana yagense, 47 a Gidɛll ne a Gasaar la a Reyaza yagense, 48 a Retesẽ ne a Nekoda la a Gazam yagense, 49 a Uza ne a Paseyak la a Besayi yagense 50 a Asna ne a Mehunim la a Nefusim yagense, 51 Bakbuk ne a Hakufa la a Harhur yagense, 52 a Batsilut ne a Mehida la a Harsa yagense, 53 a Barkos ne a Sizera la a Tamak yagense, 54 a Netsiyak ne a Hatifa yagense.
55 A Salmo yembsã yagense: a Sotayi ne a Soferɛt la a Peruda yagense, 56 a Zaala ne a Darkom la a Gidɛll yagense, 57 a Sefatiya ne a Hatill la a Pokerɛt-Hatsebayim ne a Ami yagense.
58 Wẽnnaam doogã tʋm-tʋmdbã ne a Salmo yembsã yagensã sõor yaa neb 392. 59 Ad neb nins sẽn yik n yi Tɛlmelak la Tɛl-Harsa la Kerubadã la Immeer sẽn ka tõog n wilg b ba roogo, wall b wilg tɩ b yaa Israyɛll buudã:
60 A Delaza ne a Tobiza la a Nekoda yagense, bãmb 652. 61 La maan-kʋʋdbã sʋk yagense: A Habaza ne a Hakot la a Barzillayi yagense. A Barzillayi ra peega Galaad ned a Barzillayi biig n maan a paga, la b kel n dɩka rao-kãng yʋʋr n pʋd yẽ. 62 Nin-kãens baoo b ba-rãmbã yʋy maan-kʋʋdbã sõdb sebr pʋgẽ n ka yã ye. La b sẽn ka yã wã, b gesa bãmb wa nin-dẽgdo n yiis-b maan-kʋʋdbã tʋʋmd pʋgẽ. 63 La naabã yeelame tɩ b ra rɩ maandã rɩɩb hal tɩ t'a wakat ninga maan-kʋʋdbã sẽn na n sok Wẽnnaam ne Urim la Tumim n bãnge. 64 Nebã fãa sõor yaa 42.360 65 n paas bãmb yem-daada ne b yem-pogsã sẽn ya neb 7.337. Raopã ne pagbã sẽn ya yɩɩn-yɩɩndbã yaa neb 200. 66 Bãmb wiid taa 736, la bõn-wees yaa 245, 67 la b yugma yaa 435, la b bõens yaa 6720.
68 Yir-sob-n-dãmb kẽer sẽn ta Wẽnnaam doogã sẽn be Zeruzalɛm pʋgẽ wã, bãmb kõo yam-leoog kũun Wẽnnaam doogã yĩnga, tɩ b tõe n le me-a a zĩigẽ. 69 Bãmb kõo kũun sẽn zems b pãnga, Wẽnnaam doogã meeb yĩnga. Yaa sãnem kilo 512 la pʋ-sʋkã la wanzuri kilo 4092, la pʋ-sʋkã la maan-kʋʋdbã fut 100.
70 Maan-kʋʋdbã ne Levi-rãmbã la neb kẽer zĩnda Zeruzalɛm ne a tẽn-kɩremsã. La yɩɩn-yɩɩndbã ne zakã noy gũudbã la Wẽnnaam doogã tʋm-tʋmdbã ne Israyɛll neb a taabã fãa zĩnda b mens tẽnsẽ.
