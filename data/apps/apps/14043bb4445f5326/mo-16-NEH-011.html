Zeruzalɛm tẽngã nebã
= 1Kib 9.1-17 =
11
1 Kasem-dãmbã zĩnda Zeruzalɛm. La neb a taabã sẽn ketã maana poeere, tɩ neb piigã fãa, bɩ ned a ye zĩnd tẽn-sõngã pʋgẽ Zeruzalɛm, t'a wɛ wã zĩnd tẽms a taabã pʋse. 2 La nebã fãa pʋʋsa neb nins sẽn zĩnd Zeruzalɛm ne yam-leoogã barka.
3 Zuda nebã ned kam fãa zĩnda a meng tẽngẽ. Yaa Israyɛll nebã, la maan-kʋʋdbã, la Levi-rãmbã, la Wẽnnaam doogã tʋm-tʋmdbã, la a Salomo yembsã yagense. 4 Ad nanams nins sẽn zĩnd Zeruzalɛmmã. Yaa Zuda neb kẽere, la Benzame neb kẽere:
Zuda nebã yaa: A Oziyas biig a Ataza. A Oziyas yaa a Zakari biiga, la a Zakari yaa a Amariya biiga, la a Amariya yaa a Sefatiya biiga, la a Sefatiya yaa a Mehalaleyɛll biiga, la a Mahalaleyɛll yaa a Perɛts biiga. 5 La Zuda ned a to yaa a Baruk biig a Maseyiya. A Maseyiya yaa a Kol-Hoos biiga, la a Kol-Hoos yaa a Hazaza biiga, la a Hazaza yaa a Adaza biiga, la a Adaza yaa a Zozariib biiga, la a Zozariib yaa a Zakari biiga, la a Zakari yaa a Sela biiga. 6 A Perɛts yagensã fãa sẽn zĩnd Zeruzalɛmmã yaa gãndaad kobs-naase la pis-yoob la a nii.
7 Ad a Benzame yagensã: Yaa a Mesalum biig a Salu. A Mesalum yaa a Zowɛɛd biiga, la a Zowɛɛd yaa a Pedaza biiga, la a Pedaza yaa a Kolaza biiga, la a Kolaza yaa a Maseza biiga, la a Maseza yaa a Itiyɛll biiga la a Itiyɛll yaa a Ezayi biiga, 8 la a Gabayi ne a Salu ne a saam-biisã yaa raop kobs-wɛ la pissi la a nii. 9 A Zikri biig a Zowɛll yaa bãmb naaba, la a Senuwa biig a Zuda yaa tẽngã na-bila.
10 Maan-kʋʋdbã yaa: Wẽnnaam doogã naab a Zedayeza sẽn ya a Zozariib biiga, la a Zakinni. 11 La a Zozariib yaa a Seraza biiga, la a Seraza yaa a Hilkiza biiga, la a Hilkiza yaa a Mesuliyam biiga, la a Mesuliyam yaa a Sadok biiga, la a Sadok yaa a Merazot biiga, la a Merazot yaa a Akituub biiga, la a Akituub yaa Wẽnnaam doogã naaba. 12 A Zedayeza ne a saam-biisã sẽn get Wẽnnaam doogã tʋʋmd yellã yaa neb kobs-nii la pissi la a yiibu. La sẽn paas bãmba, yaa a Zeroham biig a Adaza. A Zeroham yaa a Pelaliya biiga, la Pelaliya yaa a Amzi biiga, la a Amzi yaa a Zakari biiga, la a Zakari yaa a Pasewuur biiga, la a Pasewuur yaa a Malkiza biiga. 13 A Adaza ne a saam-biis nins sẽn ya yi-kãadbã yaa neb kobs-yi la pis-naas la a yiibu. La sẽn paas bãmba yaa a Azareyɛll biig a Amasayi. A Azareyɛll yaa a Akzayi biiga, la a Akzayi yaa a Mesilemot biiga, la a Mesilemot yaa a Immeer biiga. 14 A Amasayi saam-biisã yaa gãndaad koabg la pissi la a nii. Yaa a Gedolim biig a Zabdiyɛll n ya bãmb naaba.
15 Ad Levi-rãmbã. Yaa: A Hasuub biig a Semayeza. A Hasuub yaa a Azrikam biiga, la a Azrikam yaa a Hasabiya biiga, la a Hasabiya yaa a Buni biiga. 16 Bãmb neb a taabã yaa Levi-rãmbã nanamse, a Sabetayi ne a Zozabaad sẽn da get Wẽnnaam doogã zak yellã, 17 la a Mise biig a Mataniya. A Mise yaa a Zabdi biiga, la a Zabdi yaa a Azaf biiga. Yaa a Mataniya n da lʋɩt taoor tɩ b yɩɩnd n pẽgd b Wẽnnaam la b maand pʋʋsgo. La yẽ saam-biig a Bakbukiya yaa a yiib soabã, n paas a Samuwa biig a Abda. A Samuwa yaa a Galall biiga, la a Galall yaa a Zedutund biiga. 18 Levi-rãmb nins fãa sẽn be tẽn-sõngã pʋgẽ wã yaa neb kobs-yi la pis-nii la a naase.
19 Zag-no-gũudbã yaa: A Akuub ne a Talino ne b saam-biisã. Bãmb yaa neb koabg la pis-yopoe la a yiibu. 20 Israyɛll nebã ne maan-kʋʋdbã ne Levi-rãmbã sẽn ketã zĩnda Zuda tẽnsã pʋse. Ned kam fãa zĩnda a meng zĩigẽ. 21 Wẽnnaam doogã tʋm-tʋmdbã zĩnda Ofɛlle, la yaa a Sika ne a Zispa n ya bãmb taoor dãmba. 22 Levi-rãmb nins sẽn da be Zeruzalɛmmã naab yaa a Bani biig a Uzi. A Bani yaa a Hasabiya biiga, la a Hasabiya yaa a Mataniya biiga, la Mataniya yaa a Mise biiga. Bãmb yaa a Azaf yagens sẽn ya yɩɩn-yɩɩndba, la yaa bãmb n da get Wẽnnaam doogã tʋʋmd yelle. 23 Tɩ bõe, rĩmã ra maana tõog yɩɩn-yɩɩndbã yĩng n wilg b sẽn tog n kõ bãmb bũmb ning daar fãa. 24 A Mesezabeyɛll biig a Petakza ra yaa rĩmã tʋm-tʋmda, la yaa yẽ n da get nebã fãa yelle. A Mesezabeyɛll yaa a Zerak yagenga, la a Zerak yaa a Zuda biiga.
25 La ne tẽnsã ne pʋtã yelle, Zuda neb kẽer zĩnda Kirzat-Arba ne a tẽn-kɩremsã pʋse, la Dibõ ne a tẽn-kɩremsã pʋse, la Zekabseyɛll ne a tẽn-kɩremsã pʋse. 26 La neb kẽer zĩnda Zesuwa, la Molada, la Bet-Palɛte, 27 la Hasaar-Suwalle, la Beer-Seba ne a tẽn-kɩremsã pʋse, 28 la Siklag la Mekona ne a tẽn-kɩremsã pʋse, 29 la Ɩn-Rimo ne Soreya la Zarmuti, 30 la Zanoak la Adulam ne b tẽn-kɩremsã pʋse, la Lakis ne a pʋtã pʋse, la Azɛka ne a tẽn-kɩremsã pʋse. Bãmb siga Beer-Seba hal tɩ ta Hinnom tãngã longo.
31 Benzame yagensã zĩnda Gɛba, la Mikmase, la Aza, la Betɛll ne a tẽn-kɩremsã pʋse, 32 la Anatote, la Nob-Hananiya, 33 la Hatsoore, la Rama, la Gitayim, 34 la Hadiidi, la Seboyim la Nebalate, 35 la Lod la Ono ne tʋʋm-mitbã tãng longẽ. 36 La Zuda Levi-rãmbã kẽer lagma ne Benzame nebã.
