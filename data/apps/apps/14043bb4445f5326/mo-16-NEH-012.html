Maan-kʋʋdbã ne Levi-rãmbã
12
1 Ad maan-kʋʋbã ne Levi-rãmb nins sẽn tũ a Seyalsiyɛll biig a Zorobabɛll ne a Zozoe n wa wã: Yaa a Seraza, a Zeremi, a Ɩsdraase, 2 a Amariya, a Malluki, a Hatusi, 3 a Sekaniya, a Rehum, a Meremote, 4 a Iddo, a Ginetoyi, a Abiza, 5 a Mizame, a Madiya, a Bilga, 6 a Semayeza, a Zozariibi, a Zedayeza, 7 a Salu, a Amoke, a Hilkiza la a Zedayeza. Bãmb yaa maan-kʋʋdbã ne b saam-biisã kasem-dãmb a Zozoe zamaane. 8 Levi-rãmbã yaa a Zozoe, a Binuyi, a Kadmiyɛlle, a Serebiya, a Zuda, a Mataniya. Yaa a Mataniya ne a saam-biisã n da lʋɩt taoor ne yɩɩlã n pẽgd Wẽnnaam. 9 La Bãmb saam-biisã a Bakbukiya ne a Unni yaasa b zĩigẽ bãmb taoore. 10 A Zozoe yaa a Zozakim ba, la Zozakim yaa a Eliyasiib ba, la a Eliyasiib yaa a Zodaza ba, 11 la a Zodaza yaa a Zonatã ba, la Zonatã yaa a Zaduwa ba. 12 Ad a Zozakim wakatẽ kasem-dãmbã yʋya: Yaa a Meraza n ya a Seraza roogã kãsma, a Hananiya n ya a Zeremi roogã kãsma, 13 a Mesullam n ya a Ɛsdras roogã kãsma, a Zokana n ya a Amariya roogã kãsma, 14 A Zonatã n ya a Meluki roogã kãsma, a Zozɛf n ya a Sebaniya roogã kãsma, 15 a Adna n ya a Harim roogã kãsma, a Helkayi n ya a Merazot roogã kãsma, 16 a Zakari n ya Iddo roogã kãsma, a Mesullam n ya Gineton roogã kãsma, 17 a Zikri n ya a Abiza roogã kãsma, a Menzame ne a Moadiya n ya a Piltayi roogã kasem-dãmbã. 18 Yaa a Samua n ya a Bilga roogã kãsma, a Zonatã n ya a Semayeza roogã kãsma, 19 a Matnayi n ya a Zoariib roogã kãsma, a Uzi n ya a Zedayeza roogã kãsma, 20 a Kalayi n ya a Salayi roogã kãsma, a Ebɛɛr n ya a Amok roogã kãsma, 21 Hasabiya n ya a Hilkiza roogã kãsma, a Netaneyɛll n ya a Zedayeza roogã kãsma.
22 B sɩng n gʋlsa Levi-rãmbã rot kasem-dãmbã ne maan-kʋʋdbã rot kasem-dãmbã yʋy a Eliyasiib la a Zozada la a Zokana la a Zaduwa wakat hal tɩ ta Pɛrs naab a Daryis sẽn deeg naam wakate. 23 B gʋlsa Levi-rãmbã rot kãsem-dãmb yʋy kibayã sebr pʋgẽ hal tɩ ta a Eliyasiib yageng a Zokana wakate. 24 Levi-rãmbã kasem-dãmbã yaa a Hasabiya ne a Serebiya la a Kadmiyɛll biig a Zozoe. Yaa bãmb ne b saam-biis nins sẽn yas b taoor n yɩɩnd n pẽgd Wẽnnaam la b pʋʋsd bark wa Wẽnnaam dao a Daviid sẽn da yeelã.
25 A Mataniya ne a Bakbukiya la a Abdiyas la a Mesullam la a Talmo la a Akuub yaa zakã noy gũudba. Bãmb gũu laogã bĩngr dot sẽn pẽ zakã noyã. 26 Bãmb zĩnda a Zosadok biig a Zozoe biig a Zozakim wakate, la gʋvɛrneer a Nehemi ne maan-kʋʋd a Ɛsdras sẽn ya gʋlsdã wakate.
Zeruzalɛm lalgã bark ningri
27 B sẽn na n maan Zeruzalɛm lalgã kibs wakatã, b kẽnga zĩis nins fãa Levi-rãmbã sẽn be wã n tɩ bool bãmb tɩ b wa Zeruzalɛm n wa maan lalgã kibs ne sũ-noogo, la b pʋʋs barka, la b yɩɩl ne kɛm la koal la pendse. 28 Levi-rãmbã yɩɩn-yɩɩndbã sẽn be Zeruzalɛm tẽn-kɩremsã ne Netofati tẽmse, 29 la Bet-Gilgall ne Geba la Azmavɛt pʋtẽ wã tigma taaba. Tɩ bõe, yɩɩn-yɩɩndbã ra tii b tẽmsã n gilg Zeruzalɛm. 30 Maan-kʋʋdbã ne Levi-rãmbã yɩlga b mense, la b yɩlg nebã la tẽngã noyã, la b yɩlg lalsã me.
31 Dẽ, mam talla Zuda nanamsã n dʋ lalgã zugu. La mam tigsa yɩɩn-yɩɩndbã sags a yiibu, tɩ b tong taab n kẽn lalgã zugu. Sak a yembr kẽnga rɩtg n ta tẽngã noor ning sẽn boond tɩ tãmpʋʋr noorã. 32 La a Oze ne Zuda nanamsã pʋ-sʋk pʋgla bãmb poore, 33 n paas a Azariya ne a Ɛsdras la a Mesullam 34 la a Zuda n paas a Benzame ne a Semayeza la a Zeremi. 35 La bãmb sʋka, maan-kʋʋdb kẽer n da be be n tar tãntãado. Yaa a Zonatã biig a Zakari. A Zonatã yaa a Semayeza biiga, la a Semayeza yaa a Mataniya biiga, la a Mataniya yaa a Mise biiga, la a Mise yaa a Zakkur biiga, la a Zakkur yaa a Azaf biiga. 36 Yaa a Zakari ne a saam-biis a Semayeza ne a Azareyɛll la a Milalayi la a Gilalayi la a Maayi ne a Netaneyɛll la a Zuda ne a Hanani. Bãmb da zãada Wẽnnaam dao a Daviid yʋʋm-teedã. La gʋlsd a Ɛsdras lʋɩɩ bãmb taoore. 37 Bãmb looga tẽngã noor ning sẽn teesd bulgã n kẽng taoore, n tɩ rʋ a Daviid tẽngã rʋʋdg ning sẽn dʋʋd n tat lalgã, b looga a Daviid doogã n ta koomã noor sẽn teesd yaangã.
38 Yɩɩn-yɩɩndbã sak a yiib soabã kẽnga goabga, la mam me ne nebã pʋ-sʋk pʋgla bãmb n kẽn lalgã zugu. Tõnd looga gasg ning sẽn be wʋmsã sʋkã n ta lalgã zĩ-yalenga zĩigẽ 39 n loog Efaryim tẽngã noore, la tẽngã no-kʋdgã, la a noor ning b sẽn tũud n kẽed ne zĩmã, la Hananeyɛll gasgo la Meya gasgo, n ta tẽngã noor ning piisã sẽn tũud n yit la b kẽedẽ wã. La bãmb tɩ yaasa tẽngã noor ning bi-beesã roog sẽn be wã.
40 La woto, yɩɩn-yɩɩndbã sags a yiibã yalsa Wẽnnaam doogã pʋgẽ, la mam me ne nanamsã pʋɩ-sʋk sẽn be ne maamã yalsa be, 41 la maan-kʋʋdb a Eliyakim ne a Maseza la a Menzame la a Mise la a Elzoenayi la a Zakari la a Hananiya me ra bee be ne b tãntãadã, 42 ne a Maseza la a Semayeza la a Eleyazaar la a Uzi la a Zokana la a Malkiza la a Elam la a Ezɛɛre. Yɩɩn-yɩɩndbã yɩɩlame, la a Zizrakza n yɩ yɩɩlã taoor soaba. 43 Baraare, bãmb maana maoon-kãsenga, la b maana sũ-noogo. Tɩ bõe, Wẽnnaam kõo bãmb sũ-noog wʋsgo. Pagbã ne kambã me maana sũ-noogo, la nebã zĩnda yɩɩg n wʋm kilsgã sẽn kilsd Zeruzalɛmmã.
44 Baraare, b yãka neb sẽn na n ges laogã bĩngr dot yelle, la nebã kũunã ne dẽenem biisã la piig-pʋɩɩrã yelle, tɩ b rees bũmb nins tõog sẽn yeelã tẽnsã fãa pʋse, maan-kʋʋdbã ne Levi-rãmbã yĩnga. Bala, Zuda rãmbã ra maanda sũ-noog maan-kʋʋdbã ne Levi-rãmbã tʋʋmd yĩnga. 45 Bãmb gesa b Wẽnnaamã tʋʋmd yelle, la b maneg bũmbã fãa tɩ yɩ sõma. Yɩɩn-yɩɩndbã ne zakã noy gũudbã me maana woto wa sẽn zems a Daviid ne a biig a Solomon tõodã. 46 Tɩ bõe, hal pĩnda, a Daviid ne a Azaf wakatẽ wã, b ra tara yɩɩn-yɩɩndb naab t'a lʋɩt taoor ne Wẽnnaam pẽgr yɩɩla, la bark pʋʋsg yɩɩla. 47 La a Zorobabɛll ne a Nehemi wakate, Israyɛll nebã fãa ra yãkda pʋɩɩr yɩɩn-yɩɩndbã ne zakã noy gũudbã yĩng daar fãa, la b ra yãkda Levi-rãmbã pʋɩɩr toore. La Levi-rãmbã me ra yãkda a Aarõ yagensã me pʋɩɩr toore.
