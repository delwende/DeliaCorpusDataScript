Yɩɩn-sõng a 93
93
1 1Zu-soabã rɩta naam, bãmb yeelgda waoogre. Zu-soabã yera pãng wa fuugu, la b sẽg-a-la wa kurga. N-ye, yãmb yẽbga dũniyã la a ka rĩmsd ye. 2 Yãmb naam geer yẽbga hal kʋdemd wakate, yãmb bee hal wakat fãa. 3 Zu-soaba, moagã yikame, moagã zẽka b koeese, moagã ko-pɛm yikdame n guglmdẽ. 4 Zu-soabã yaa kãseng n yɩɩd koom wʋsg koeese, bãmb ziir yɩɩda ko-kãsems ko-pɛma, Zu-soabã tara ziir saasẽ. 5 Yãmb kaset yaa sɩd halaale. Zu-soaba, sõmblem toga ne yãmb doogã hal tɩ ta wakat tɛka.
