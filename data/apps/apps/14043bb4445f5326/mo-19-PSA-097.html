Yɩɩn-sõng a 97
97
1 Zu-soabã rɩta naam, bɩ dũniyã kɩdeme, bɩ soolem wʋsg koom sẽn gũbgdã maan sũ-noogo. 2 Sawatã ne likã gũbga bãmba. Tɩrlem ne bʋʋd sẽn zems yaa bãmb naam geer yẽbgre. 3 Bugum lʋɩta bãmb taoor n dɩt n gũbgd bãmb zabd-n-taasã. 4 Bãmb sa-yãgb vẽnegda dũniyã, la tẽngã yãtame n digimdidẽ. 5 Tãens yẽnegda wa sɩribd Zu-soabã taoore, yaa dũniyã fãa Zu-soabã taoore. 6 Saas wilgda bãmb tɩrlem la neb buud fãa yãta bãmb ziiri. 7 Neb nins fãa sẽn waoogd bõn-peesds wa Wẽnnaamã na n paama yãnde, yaa neb nins sẽn pẽgd b mens bõn-zaals yĩngã. Wẽnnaam-dãmbã fãa, bɩ y wõgemd bãmb taoore. 8 Zu-soaba, Sɩyõ wʋmdame n maand sũ-noogo, la Zuda soolem tẽns kɩdemda yãmb bʋʋdã kaoob yĩnga. 9 Tɩ bõe, yãmb yaa Zu-soab sẽn ka to sẽn so dũniyã fãa, la sẽn yɩɩd wẽnnaam-dãmbã fãa wʋsgo. 10 Zu-soabã nonga neb nins sẽn kis wẽngã, bãmb gũuda neb nins sẽn ya bãmb dẽndã, n yiisd bãmb nin-wẽns nusẽ. 11 Vẽenem pukda nin-tɩrs yĩnga, la sũ-noog yaa pʋ-peelem dãmbã yĩnga. 12 Nin-tɩrse, bɩ y maan sũ-noog Zu-soabã pʋgẽ n tẽeg bãmb sõmblem yell ne waoogre.
