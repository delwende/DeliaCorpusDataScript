Yɩɩn-sõng a 114
114
1 Wakat ning Israyɛll sẽn yi n bas Ezipt soolmã, a Zakoob yagensã sẽn yi n bas gom-zẽn dãmbã, 2 Zuda lebga sɛk-zĩig bãmb yĩnga, la Israyɛll lebga bãmb soolem. 3 Mogr yãame n zoe la Zʋrdẽ kʋɩlgã koom tara po-tula. 4 Tãens yɩkame n tĩims wa boeese, la kũnkubã me tĩimsa wa pe-bi. 5 Mogre, bõe n tar foom tɩ f zoetẽ? Zʋrdẽ, bõe n tar foom tɩ f tared po-tula? 6 Tãense, bõe n tar yãmb tɩ y tĩimsd wa boeese? Kũnkuba, bõe n tar yãmb tɩ y tĩimsd wa pe-bi? 7 Dũniyã, rigimd Zu-soabã nengẽ, a Zakoob Wẽnnaamã taoore, 8 sẽn tekd-a pĩig tɩ lebg kʋɩlgã, sẽn tekd-a kugr tɩ lebg bulgã.
