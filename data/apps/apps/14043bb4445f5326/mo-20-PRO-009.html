Yam ne yalemd boondame
9
1 Yam mee a roogo, n lugl a ra-lugds a yopoe. 2 Yẽ kʋʋ a rʋmsi la a kõg a rãam n segl n sɛ. 3 La yẽ tʋma a pʋg-sadb tɩ b kẽng tẽngã soay zut n tɩ moon n yeel 4 zɩtbã tɩ b wa. Yẽ yeela yalembã yaa: 5 «Bɩ y wa rɩ m dɩɩbã la y yũ m dãamã m sẽn kõgã. 6 La y bas yalemd n tũ bãngre sor n vɩɩnde. « 7 Ned sã n sagl nin-pa-nanda, a paamda paoogre. La ned sã n tãs nin-wẽnga, a paamda toogo. 8 Ra tãs nin-pa-nand t'a na n kisga foom. Tãs yam soab t'a na n nonga foom. 9 Fo sã n wilg yam soab bũmbu, a na n paama yam. Zãms nin-sõng t'a na n zãmsame n paase. 10 Yam sɩngr yaa Zu-soabã zoeese. La fo sã n mi sõng-soabã, fo yaa bãngda. 11 Yaa maam n paasd fo raarã. Mam kɩtame tɩ f yõorã woglem paase. 12 Fo sã n tar yam, f yamã ya f meng yĩnga. La fo sã n ya nin-pa-nanda, yaa foom bal la na n wʋme. 13 Yalemd yaa wa pag sẽn ya gom-to-ned n ya zɩta, n ka mi baa fɩ ye. 14 Yẽ zaoo a goeel a rig-noorẽ tẽngã sor zug 15 n boond neb nins sẽn loogd be n dabd taoorã n yetẽ yaa: 16 «Zɩtba, wa-y n tũ-y ka.» La yẽ yeta yalembã 17 tɩ wagdem koom ne wagdem dɩɩb yaa noogo. 18 La raoã ka mi tɩ kũum n be be la tɩ neb nins sẽn da kẽ be wã bee kɩɩm-kulg ye.
