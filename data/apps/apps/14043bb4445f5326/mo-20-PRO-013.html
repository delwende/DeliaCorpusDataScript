13
1 Biig sẽn tar yam deegda a ba zãmsgo. La wilg-m-meng soab ka kelgd saglg ye. 2 Ned noor sã n mi goama, a paamda bõn-sõmyã. La zãmb soab rata wẽnga. 3 Ned sã tõe a noore, a gũuda a vɩɩm. La gom-to-ned sãamda a vɩɩm. 4 Kʋɩɩm datame la a ka paamd ye. La sẽn maood-a paamda a sẽn data. 5 Nin-sõng kisa zirẽ goama. La nin-wẽng goam ningd-a-la yãnd t'a paamd paoogre. 6 Tɩrlem gũuda pʋ-peelem soaba. La yel-wẽn lubda yel-wẽn maanda. 7 Naong rãmb kẽer zẽkda b mens wa arzegs rãmba, tɩ arzegs rãmb kẽer sikd b mens wa naong rãmba. 8 Arzɛk soab tõee n yaoome n fãag a menga. La naong soab ka tar bũmb sẽn na n yao n fãag a meng ye. 9 Nin-sõmyã vẽenem yãgdame. La b na n kiisa nin-wẽns bugum. 10 Yaa tɩtaam n wat ne zabre. La yam bee sik-m-mens rãmb nengẽ. 11 Ned sã paamd arzɛk t'a ka namse, arzɛkã na n looga tao-tao. La ned sã n tʋmd n paamd bilf bilfu, a paasdame. 12 Fo sã n ka paam f sẽn datã tao-tao, fo sũur sãamdame. La f sã n wa paam-a raare, a lebgda vɩɩm tɩɩga. 13 Ned sã n ka reegd saglgo, a baooda a meng sãoongo. La b sã n tãs ned t'a reege, a paamda yõodo. 14 Yam soab zãmsg yaa vɩɩm bulga. Zãmsgã na n sõnga foom tɩ f gil kũum n looge. 15 Yam-sõng wata ne pẽgre. La zãmb wata ne toogo. 16 Nin-yẽeseng fãa tʋmda ne yam. La yalem wilgda a yalemdo. 17 Nin-wẽng sã n wa ne koɛɛga, a maanda yelle. La sɩd soab sã n wa ne koɛɛga, a sõngdame tɩ laafɩ zĩndi. 18 Ned sã n ka reegd saglgo, naong ne yãnd na n yõk-a lame. La ned sã n kelgd sãglgo, a na n paama waoogre. 19 Ned sã n paam a sẽn data, a sũur yaa noogo. La yalem ka nong n bas wẽng ye. 20 Ned sã n tũud yam dãmba, a na n lebga yam soaba. La ned sã n tũud yalma, a na n paama toogo. 21 Toog pʋgda tʋʋm-wẽns maanda. La nin-sõng tara sũ-noogo. 22 Nin-sõng basda arzegs a yagens yĩnga. Yaa nin-tɩrsã n na n deeg tʋʋm-wẽns maandbã paoongã n sooge. 23 Pʋt nins naong rãmb sẽn kãasda womda wʋsgo. La b sã n ka kao bʋʋd tɩrga, a na n sãamame. 24 Sẽn kisgd-a sãbsg maanda a biig pʋ-toogo. Ned sã n nong a biiga, a sagend-a lame. 25 Nin-sõng rɩtame n tɩgda. La nin-wẽng ka paamd rɩɩb tɩ sek-a ye.
