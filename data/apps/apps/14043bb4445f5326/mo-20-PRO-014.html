14
1 Pagb nins sẽn tar yamã manegda b yiya. La pʋg yalembã sãamda b yiya. 2 Ned ning sẽn maand tɩrg zoeta Zu-soabã. La yel-kɛg maand pa nand Zu-soabã ye. 3 Yalem goam wata ne sɩbgre, a tɩtaamã yĩnga. La yam soab goam gũud-a lame. 4 Koaad sã n ka beẽ, ki me ka be ye. Yaa Wẽnd maag koaad yĩng la kood ya wʋsgo. 5 Sɩd soab ka na n yag zirẽ ye. La zirẽ kaset-soab yagda zirẽ. 6 Nin-tʋʋd baooda yam n ka yãtẽ ye. La bãngr yaa naana ne nin-yẽesenga. 7 Yi yalem nengẽ tɩ yam goam ka be be ye. 8 Nin-yẽeseng yam yaa tɩ bãng a sẽn maand bũmb ninga. La yalem yalemd yaa belgre. 9 Yalem tagsdame tɩ beegr ka yell ye. La nin-tɩrg mii sugr sẽn ya bũmb ninga. 10 Ned fãa mii a sũ-toog ne a sũ-noogo, yẽ ka tõe n yãk-a n kõ ned a to ye. 11 Nin-wẽns rot na n sãamame. La nin-tɩrs rot na n yɩɩ sõma. 12 Sor n be n wẽnd tɩrg nebã nifẽ, la a yaool n dabda kũum zĩigẽ. 13 Baa ned sã n laadẽ, a tara toogo. La sũ-sãoong pʋgda sũ-noogo. 14 Nin-wẽng na n deega a tʋʋm-wẽnsã yaoodo. La nin-sõng na n deega a tʋʋm-sõma wã yaoodo. 15 Zɩt deegda a sẽn wʋmd bũmb ning fãa. La nin-yẽeseng gũusda ne a sẽn maand bũmb ningã. 16 Yam soab gũusdame n gind yell n loogdẽ. La tɩtaam tara yalem t'a maand gãndaalem. 17 Ned sũur sã n yikd tao-tao, a maanda yalemdo. La yam soab maagda a menga. 18 Zɩtb pʋɩɩr yaa yalemdo, la yam dãmb bãngrã la b yõodo. 19 Nin-wẽns wõgemdeda nin-sõmyã taoore. La b bõosda nin-tɩrse. 20 Baa naong soab yak ka nong-a ye. La arzɛk soab tara zo-rãmb wʋsgo. 21 Ned sã n kis a yaka, a maanda wẽnga. La ned ning sẽn maand naong soab neerã tara sũ-noogo. 22 Neb nins sẽn dat wẽngã menemdame. La ned ning sẽn dat n maan neerã maanda nonglem ne sɩda. 23 Tʋʋmã fãa tara yõodo. La gom-vɩɩd wata ne naong bala. 24 Yam dãmb arzɛk la b maanfo. La yalem yaa yalem wakat fãa. 25 Sɩd kaset-soab fãagda neba. La zirẽ-m-be-ned rõta neba. 26 Sẽn zoet-a Zu-soabã ne a kambã fãa ka yɛɛsd bũmb ye. 27 Zu-soabã zoees yaa vɩɩm bulga. A kɩtdame tɩ ned gil kũum n looge. 28 Neb sã n ya wʋsgo, rĩm tara naam. La neb sã n kaẽ, rĩm naam sɛɛme. 29 Ned ning sẽn sigsd a sũur tara yam. La ned ning sũur sẽn yikd tao-tao wilgda a yalemdo. 30 Ned sũur sã n noomẽ, rẽ kõta a yẽns laafɩ. La sũ-kiir sãamda neda. 31 Ned sã n laad nimbãan-neda, a paoogda a naandã, la a sã n maand naong soab neere, a waoogda a naandã. 32 Nin-wẽng tʋʋm-wẽnsã lubd-a lame. La nin-sõng pʋ-peelmã lilld-a lame. 33 Nin-yẽeseng tara yam. La yalem ka mi yam ye. 34 Tɩrlem yɩta buud waoogre. La yel-wẽnd ningda buud fãa yãnde. 35 Rĩm nonga tʋmtʋmd ning sẽn ya nin-yẽesenga. La a sɩbgda tʋmtʋmd ning sẽn ningd-a yãndã.
