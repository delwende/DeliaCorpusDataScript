15
1 Ned sã n leok ne gom-noodo, a sigsda sũuri. La zabr goam yikda ned uuri. 2 Fo sã n wʋm yam soab goama, fo bãngdame tɩ bãngr yaa noogo. La yalem gomda yalem goama. 3 Zu-soabã nee zĩigã fãa. Bãmb nee nin-wẽnsã ne nin-sõmyã wã fãa. 4 Gom nood yaa vɩɩm tɩɩga. La pʋ-to-goam sãamda ned sũuri. 5 Yalem paoogda a ba zãmsgo. La sẽn deegd-a saglg maanda yam. 6 Paoong wʋsg bee nin-sõng yiri. La nin-wẽng yõod yaa toogo. 7 Yam dãmb gomda yam goama. La yalem ka woto ye. 8 Nin-wẽng maoong beegda Zu-soaba. La Zu-soabã nonga nin-tɩrs pʋʋsgo. 9 Zu-soabã ka nong nin-wẽns sẽn maand bũmb ning ye. Bãmb nonga ned sẽn maand tɩrga. 10 Ned sã n bʋdge, a na n paama sɩbgr sẽn ya toogo. Ned sã n ka rat tɩ b tãs-a, a na n kiime. 11 Zu-soabã mii ninsaalbã sũy wa b sẽn mi kɩɩm-kulg ne bog zulungã. 12 Nin-pa-nand ka rat tɩ ned sagl-a ye. Yẽ ka kẽnd yam dãmb nengẽ ye. 13 Sũ-noog kɩtdame tɩ ned neng yɩ neere. La ned sũur sã n sãame, a neng gãnemdame. 14 Sẽn tar-a yam baooda bãngre. La yalem nonga yalemdo. 15 Nimbãan-dãmb tara toog raar fãa. La sũ-noog soab yir yaa wa kibs wakate. 16 Fo sã n tar bũmb bilf n zoet Zu-soabã n são fo sẽn tar arzegs wʋsgo n yaool n maand yɩɩre. 17 Neb sã n nong taab n dɩt medg n são neb sẽn wãbd nemd la b zabd taaba. 18 Sũ-yikr wata ne zabre. La sũ-mar wata ne laafɩ. 19 Gõos yita kʋɩɩm sor zugu. La nin-tɩrs sor yaa wa so-kõodre. 20 Bi-yamdg kõta a ba sũ-noogo. Sẽn tũud-a a ma yaa yalma. 21 Zɩt nonga yalemdo, la yam soab maanda sẽn ya tɩrga. 22 Yẽsg sã n kae, ned ka tõe n maan bũmb ye. La sagendb wʋsg sẽn n beẽ, b tõogdame. 23 Ned leokr sã n zemse, kõt-a-la sũ-noogo. Goam sã n zems a wakate, a yaa noogo. 24 Yam soab tũuda vɩɩm sor n dabd yĩngri, n gind kɩɩm-kulg sẽn be tẽngrã n loogdẽ. 25 Zu-soabã lubda tɩtaam dãmbã roto. B ka basd tɩ b sers kug nins sẽn be pʋg-kõr pʋʋg toadgẽ wã ye. 26 Zu-soabã ka nong nin-wẽng tagsg ye. La gom-sõmyã yaa noogo. 27 Ned sã n maand wẽng n paamd yõodo, a kõta a yir dãmb toogo. La sẽn ka reegd-a nin-wẽng kũun na n vɩɩndame. 28 Nin-sõng tagsdame n yaool n leokdẽ. La wẽng bundame n yit nin-wẽng noorẽ. 29 Zu-soabã zãaga b meng ne nin-wẽnse. La b wʋmda nin-sõmyã pʋʋsgo. 30 Ned ning sẽn yõgemd a noor ne foom kõta foo sũ-noogo, la koe-noog paasda ned pãnga. 31 Ned ning sẽn deegd saglg sõma na n zĩnda yam dãmb sʋka. 32 Ned sã n kɩɩsd zãmsgo, a kɩɩsda a menga. La ned sã n deegd saglgo, a paamda bãngre. 33 Zu-soabã zoees zãmsda ned yam. La sik-m-meng n dengd taoor tɩ waoogr yaool n pʋgle.
