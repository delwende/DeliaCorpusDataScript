16
1 Ninsaalb tagsda b sẽn dat bũmb ninga, la yaa Zu-soabã n so leokre. 2 Nebã tagsdame tɩ b sẽn maand bũmb ninsã zemsame, la Zu-soabã makda b tagsgã. 3 Dɩk f yɛlã fãa n bas ne Zu-soabã tɩ na n sõng-f lame tɩ f tõoge. 4 Zu-soabã ka maan bũmb baa a yembr zaalem yĩng ye. B naana nin-wẽng toog raar yĩnga. 5 Zu-soabã ka nong tɩta-ned ye. Hakɩɩka, bãmb ka na n bas-a taal ye. 6 Yaa ned nonglem ne a sɩd yĩng la Wẽnnaam kõt-a a yel-wẽnã sugri. Zu-soabã zoees kɩtdame tɩ ned gil toog n looge. 7 Ned manesem sã n ta Zu-soabã yam, b kɩtdame tɩ baa a bɛɛbã nong-a. 8 Fo sã n tar bũmb bilf ne pʋ-peelem n são fo sẽn na n maan wẽng n paam arzɛka. 9 Ninsaal tagsda a sẽn na n maan bũmb ninga. La yaa Zu-soabã n tõe t'a tõoge. 10 Rĩm gomda ne Wẽnnaam noore. Yẽ sã n kaood bʋʋdo, a ka maand zãmb ye. 11 Zɩslem makdg sõng ne laas nins sẽn be a zugã yaa Zu-soab n so. Kug nins fãa sẽn be yolgã pʋgẽ wã yaa bãmb n naane. 12 Rĩm-dãmb ka nong tʋʋm-wẽns ye, tɩ bõe, yaa tɩrlem n paasd bãmb naamã pãnga. 13 Rĩm-dãmb nonga nin-sõng koɛɛga. Bãmb nonga ned sẽn togsd sɩda. 14 Rĩm sũ-yikr yaa kũum tʋmtʋmda. La yam soab sigsda rĩm sũuri. 15 Rĩm sã n nong foom, kõta foom vɩɩm wa sigr saaga. 16 Ned sã n paam yam ne bãngre n são a sẽn paamd sãnem ne wanzuri wʋsgo. 17 Nin-tɩrs gũusdame n gind wẽng n loogdẽ. Ned sã n gũusd ne a sẽn maand bũmb ninsã fãagda a vɩɩm. 18 Tɩtaam n lʋɩt taoor tɩ sãoong pʋgda, la wuk-m-meng n lʋɩt taoor tɩ lʋɩɩs pʋgda. 19 Ned sã n ya sik-m-meng soab n tũud naong rãmb n são a sẽn tũud wuk-m-mens rãmb n dɩt b sẽn paam. 20 Ned sã n tagsd goam yelle, a vɩɩm na n yɩɩ noogo. Sẽn teegd-a Zu-soabã tara sũ-noogo. 21 Ned sã n ya yam soaba, b yetame t'a tara bãngre, la gom nood tara pãnga. 22 Ned sã n tar yam, yaa vɩɩm bulg la a tara. La yalembã sɩbgr la b yalemdã. 23 Yam soab tagsdame n yaool n gome. La goamã paasda a pãnga. 24 Gom-nood yaa wa sɩɩdo. B kõta sũ-noogo, la b paasda yẽns keelem me. 25 Sor n be tɩ neb get-a wa so-tɩrga, la a yaool n dabda kũum zĩigẽ. 26 Sẽn tʋmd-a tʋmda a meng yĩng n dat n paam a noor dɩɩbo. 27 Nin-yoog rata neb ne toogo. A goamã yaa bugum. 28 Pʋ-to-ned wata ne zabre. Tẽng-kãn-ned kɩtdame tɩ ned ne a zoa welg taaba. 29 Nin-wẽng belgda a yak n kɩt t'a maan sẽn ka neere. 30 Ned sẽn mumd a nin n tagsd wẽng ne ned sẽn dũmd a noor maanda wẽnga. 31 Zoob peend yaa wa waoogr maanfo. La yaa tɩrlem sor zug la b paamd-a. 32 Sũ-mar soab yɩɩda gãndaoogo. La ned sẽn tõe a meng yɩɩda sẽn zab-a zabr n deeg tẽnga. 33 Ned lobda kug n maand poeere. La Zu-soabã n so yam-yãkre.
