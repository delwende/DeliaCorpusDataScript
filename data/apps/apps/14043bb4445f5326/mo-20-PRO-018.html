18
1 Sẽn ka rat n naag-a neb baooda sẽn noom a menga. Yẽ kɩɩsda yam-sõma fãa. 2 Ka bãngr la yalem dat ye, a rat n wilga a meng tagsg bala. 3 Wẽng sã n wa, wilg-m-meng me watame. La yãnd tũuda paoogre. 4 Ninsaal goam yaa wa ko-zulums ne ko-zoetem. B yaa yam bulga. 5 B ka tog n zoe nin-wẽng nif n mong bʋʋm soab bʋʋm ye. 6 Yalem goam n sãamd-a. A goamã kɩtdame tɩ b pãb-a. 7 Yaa yalem goam n sãamd-a. A meng goamã n yõkd-a wa bẽdgo. 8 No-tʋʋn-ned goam yaa wa ned sẽn dɩ rɩ nood tɩ kẽ n ta hal a pʋgẽ. 9 Ned sã n tʋmd yaal-yaale, a yaa sãamd ba-biiga. 10 Zu-soabã yʋʋr yaa wa gãosg sẽn tar pãnga. Nin-tɩrg tõee n kẽe be tɩ bũmb da paam-a ye. 11 Rakãagr geta a arzɛkã wa tẽng sẽn tar pãnga. Yẽ tagsdame t'a yaa wa lalg sẽn ya zãonglgo. 12 Ninsaal wukda a menga, la sãoong yaool n pʋgd-a lame. Sik-m-meng n dengd taoor tɩ waoogr yaool n pʋgle. 13 Ned sã n leok t'a nan ka wʋme, a yaa yalma, la a na n paama yãnde. 14 Ned sã n ka laafɩ, a sũ-keelem tõeeme t'a sãooge. La a sũ-keelmã sã n sɛ, yaa ãnna n na n yik-a? 15 Nin-yẽeseng paamda bãngre. Yam soab kelgdame n baood bãngre. 16 Ned kũun yalgda a zĩiga, n sõngd-a t'a ta nin-kãsems nengẽ. 17 Togs-yeng soab kõta a meng bʋʋm hal t'a zabd-n-taagã wa tɩ b yaool n bãnge. 18 Poeer kɩtdame tɩ zabre sɛ. A welgda yell n bak n kõ pãens rãmba. 19 Sõng f ba-biig t'a na n sõnga foom wa tẽng sẽn tar pãnga. La fo sã n zab ne-a, a na n paga kʋɩleng tɩ f ka tõog n kẽ ye. 20 Yaa ned noor goam n kõt-a rɩɩbo. 21 Zelemd n tõe tɩ ned vɩɩnde, la zelemd n tõe tɩ ned ki. Neb nins sẽn nong goamã na n dɩɩ b goamã biisi. 22 Ned sã n paam paga, a paama sũ-noogo. Yẽ paama Zu-soabã barka. 23 Naong soab maagda a meng n gomda. La rakãagr leokda ne gãgse. 24 Zõramb kẽer n be n ya noog n yɩɩd rogome.
