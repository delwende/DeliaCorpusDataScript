20
1 Rãam wata ne wuk-m-menga. Toos wata ne rãmbgo. Neb nins sẽn yũud rãamã ka yam ye 2 Rĩm sũur sã n yiki, a lebga wa gɩgemde. Fo sã n beeg-a, foom n maan f menga. 3 Sẽn ka baood-a zabr tara waoogre. Yalem fãa tõee n zabame. 4 Kʋɩɩm sã n ta sẽoog n zãgs koobo, zĩig sã n liki, a ka na n kẽbg bũmb ye. 5 Ned tagsd yaa wa ko-zulumsi. La yam soab mii a sẽn na n maan to-to n tõoge. 6 Neb wʋsg yetame tɩ b yaa nin-sõmyã, la f sã n bao sɩd soab b sʋka, f ka na n yã ye. 7 Nin-sõng ning sẽn maand bũmb fãa ne pʋ-peelmã kamb tara sũ-noogo. 8 Naab sã n zẽẽ n kaood bʋʋdo, a tõee n bãnga sɩd ne zirẽ n bake. 9 Ãnna n tõe n yeel t'a pʋg yaa pɛɛlg n ka tar yel-wẽnde? 10 Zu-soabã ka nong ned sẽn maand zãmb a koosg ne a raab wakat ye. 11 Yaa biig tʋʋmdẽ la b bãngd a sã n maanda neer bɩ tɩrga. 12 Yaa Zu-soabã n kõ tõnd tʋb tɩ d wʋmdẽ, la nin tɩ d yãtẽ. 13 Ra gũs n lebg nimbãan-ned ye. Puk f nin n dɩ n tɩge. 14 Ned sã n daad bũmbu, a yetame tɩ yaa toogo, la a sã n da n looge, a sũur yaa noogo. 15 Sãnem ne kẽndi bee wʋsgo. La yam goam yaa wa bũmb sẽn ya sõma wʋsgo. 16 Ned sã n wẽen sãan yĩnga, bɩ f reeg a fuugu. Kɩt t'a gãneg bũmb sãanã yĩnga. 17 Zirẽ rɩɩb yaa noogo, la sã n kaoose, a na n lebga wa tom sẽn be noorẽ. 18 Saglg kɩtdame tɩ bũmb ning fo sẽn dat n maanã tõoge. Reng n bao saglg sõma n yaool n kẽng zabre. 19 No-tʋʋn-ned pukda yelle. Ra tũ ned sẽn gomd wʋsg ye. 20 Ned sẽn tʋʋd a ba ne a ma vɩɩm na n yɩɩ wa ned sẽn kiis bugum lik pʋgẽ. 21 Ned sã n paam paoong wʋsg zaalem, a ka na n naf-a ye. 22 Da yeel tɩ f na n lebsa wẽng ye. Ges Zu-soabã tɩ b na n fãaga foom. 23 Zu-soabã ka nong koosg sẽn koosd ne wẽgd ye. Zãmb bõn-makdg ka sõma ye. 24 Yaa Zu-soabã n wilgd ned sore. Ninsaal na n yɩɩ wãn n bãng a sore? 25 Ned sã n pʋlem Zu-soabã n ka reng n tagse, yaa bẽdg la a bẽd a menga. 26 Rĩm sã n ya yam soaba, a feesdame n bãng nin-wẽns n sɩbg-ba. 27 Tõnd sɩɩsã yaa Zu-soabã fɩtl n be tõnd pʋse. Tõnd ka tõe n solg bũmb ne bãmb ye. 28 Sõmnem ne sɩd gũuda rĩma. La yaa sõmnem n sõngd a rẽungã. 29 Kom-bɩɩs pãng la b waoogre, la nin-kẽemb me zoob-peend la b waoogre. 30 B sã n pãb nin-wẽng t'a yĩng bugi, manegd-a lame.
