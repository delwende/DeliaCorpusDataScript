21
1 Rĩm sũur bee Zu-soabã nug pʋgẽ wa ko-zoetem. Bãmb wẽnemded-a lame t'a kẽng zĩig ning bãmb sẽn data. 2 Ninsaal tagsdame tɩ bũmb nins fãa a sẽn maandã zemsame. La Zu-soabã feesda sũuri. 3 Ned sã n maand neer la tɩrga, yõwã tata Zu-soabã yam n yɩɩd maoongo. 4 Nin-wẽns maanda tɩtaam ne wuk-m-meng wʋsgo. La yõwã yaa wẽnga. 5 Nin-tɩrg paamda yõod ne bũmb nins fãa a sẽn maandã. La neb nins sẽn yãgd n ka yaasd n tagsdẽ wã paamda naongo. 6 Fo sã n yag zirẽ n paam arzɛka, a na n looga wa wulgu. La a na n talla foom n kẽng kũum zĩigẽ. 7 Nin-wẽns tʋʋm-wẽnsã sãamd-b lame. Bala, bãmb zãgsame n ka rat n maan neer ye. 8 Nin-wẽns maanda yel-kɛga, la nin-tɩrs manesem yaa sõma. 9 Fo sẽn na n be rakõ-sẽokẽ n são fo sẽn na n kẽ kãadem ne pʋg-yoogo. 10 Nin-wẽng rata wẽnga. A ka zoet a yak nimbãaneg ye. 11 B sã n sɩbg wuk-m-meng soaba, yalem dɩkda yam. La b sã n zãms yam soaba a paamda bãngre. 12 Nin-sõng tagsda nin-wẽng yak yelle. B lubda nin-wẽns tɩ b sãame. 13 Ned sã n yẽd a tʋb n ka kelg nimbãan-ned kɛlenga, yẽ me na n wa boolame tɩ ned ka sak ye. 14 Ned sũur sã n yik tɩ f soos n kõ-a kũuni, a sũurã sigdame. 15 Bʋʋd sã n kao tɩrga, nin-tɩrs sũy noomame, la nin-wẽnsã yɛɛsdame. 16 Ned sã n bʋdg n bas yam sore, a na n naaga ne kɩɩmse. 17 Ned sã n nong sũ-noogo, a na n yɩɩ naong soaba. Sẽn nong-a rãam ne kaam ka tõe lebg arzɛk soab ye. 18 Nin-wẽng na n leda nin-sõng zĩigẽ n ki. La zãmb soab na n leda nin-tɩrg zĩigẽ n ki. 19 Fo sã n be weoogẽ n são fo sẽn na n be yir ne pʋg-yoogo. 20 Paoong ne naar be yam soab yiri. La yalem dɩta a paoong tao-tao. 21 Ned sã n baood tɩrlem ne sõmnem, a paamda vɩɩm ne tɩrlem la waoogre. 22 Yam soab tõogda gãndaad tẽng n lub a lalsã sẽn tar pãngã. 23 Sẽn gũusd-a ne a noor goam gũuda a meng tɩ toog ra paam-a ye. 24 Tɩta-ned ne nin-pa-nand boondame tɩ wuk-m-mens rãmba. B maanda tɩtaam wʋsgo. 25 Kʋɩɩm sẽn ka rat n tʋm kʋʋda a menga. 26 Neb n be n zoet bõn daar fãa. La nin-sõmyã kõtame n ka zoet bõn ye. 27 Zu-soabã ka nong nin-wẽns maand ye. La b sã n wa ne yam-wẽng n yɩɩda. 28 Zãmb kaset-soab na n kiime. La b reega neb nins sẽn kelgd goam. 29 Nin-wẽng maanda gãandaalem. La nin-tɩrg mii a sẽn maand bũmb ninga. 30 Neb yam ne b bãngre la b saglg yaa zaalem Zu-soabã taoore. 31 B loeta weef n na n kẽng zabre, la yaa Zu-soabã n kõt tõogre.
