24
1 Ra maan sũ-kiir ne nin-wẽns ye. Ra bao bãmb nengẽ yẽ. 2 Tɩ bõe, bãmb tagsda wẽnga. La b sã n yaag b noyã, neb paamda toogo. 3 Yaa ne yam la ned yẽbgd a zak n metẽ. 4 La yaa bãngr n kɩtd t'a roog pit ne bõn-sõmyã la arzɛka. 5 Yam soab tara pãnga. Ned sẽn tara bãngr yɩɩda pãng soaba. 6 Bɩ f reng n tags f sẽn na n maan to-to n yaool n kẽng zabre. Yaa sagendb wʋsgo saglg n na n kɩt tɩ f tõoge. 7 Yalem ka tõe n bãng yam goam ye. A ka yaagd a noor na-yir ye. 8 Ned sã n tagsdẽ n dat n maan wẽnga, b na n bool-a lame tɩ zãmb soaba. 9 Yel-wẽn yaa yalem tʋʋmde. Neb ka nong wuk-m-meng soab ye. 10 Fo pãng sã n sɛ namsg wakate, rat n togsame tɩ f pãngã ra ka waoog ye. 11 Fãag neb nins b sẽn yõg n na n tɩ kʋ wã. 12 Gɩdg tɩ b ra kʋ-b ye. Fo sã n yeel tɩ yãmb da ka bãnge, sẽn feesd-a nebã fãa sũyã miime. Bãmb nee foom. Bãmb lebsda neb wa sẽn zems b tʋʋma. 13 M biiga, rɩ sɩd t'a yaa noogo. Sɩ-kaam sẽn yit sɩ-vak pʋgẽ yaa noogo. 14 Woto me, bɩ f bãng yam f meng yĩnga. Fo sã n paam-a, daar n be tɩ f tẽeb ka na n sɛ ye. 15 Ra maan wẽng n gũus nin-sõng yir ye. 16 Ra sãam a yirã ye. Tɩ bõe, nin-sõng tõee n lʋɩɩ naoor a yopoe n le yiki. La toog lubda nin-wẽnse. 17 Fo bɛ sã n lʋɩ bɩ f ra maan sũ-noog ye. B sã n wa lub-a, bɩ f ra kɩdem ye, 18 tɩ Zu-soabã sũur ra wa sãam tɩ b bas a sɩbgrã ye. 19 Ra sãam f sũur nin-wẽns yĩng ye. Ra maan sũ-kiir ne bãmb ye. 20 Tɩ bõe, daar ka be b yĩng ye. Nin-wẽns bugum na n kiime. 21 M biiga, zoe Zu-soabã ne rĩmã n da mag ne nin-kɩɩsdbã ye. 22 Tɩ bõe, bãmb sãoong tõee n yika tao-tao. Ãe n mi sãoong ning sẽn na n yi bãmb b yiibã nengẽ wã sẽn ya to-to? 23 Ad yam dãmb goama. Bʋ-kaood sã n tũus neba, yõwã ka sõma ye. 24 Yẽ sã n yeel tɩ nin-wẽng tara bʋʋm, neb na n tʋʋs-a lame. Buud a taab na n kis-a lame. 25 La bʋ-kaoodb nins sẽn sɩbgd nin-wẽnsã na n paama sũ-noogo. Nebã na n pẽg-b lame. 26 Ned sã n leok foom n togs sɩda, yaa zood bãnde. 27 Ges f tʋʋmd yell yɩnga, n maneg f pʋʋg la f yaool n me f zaka. 28 Ra kɩs kaset ne f yak yell zaalem ye. Ra gom belgr gooam ye. 29 Ra yeel tɩ f na n maan-a-la wa a sẽn da maan foomã la lebs-a a tʋʋmdã yaood ye. 30 Mam tũu kʋɩɩm pʋʋg noore, ned sẽn ya zɩt nasaar sãbs pʋʋg noor n looge. 31 La gõos yii zĩigã wã, la mood bee zĩig zãnga, 1a a lalgã me lʋɩɩme. 32 Mam ges-a lame n tags a yell n paam zãmsgo. 33 Fo gõee bilfu. Fo suusdame. Fo kalemda f nus vʋʋsdẽ. 34 Woto, naong na n linga foom wa wagdre. La nimbãaneg na n yõka foom wa bõasa. B maka neb toor toor ne taab n zãms neba
