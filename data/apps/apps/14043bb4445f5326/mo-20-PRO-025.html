B maka neb toor toor ne taab n zãms neba
25
1 Ad A Salomo yel-bũn nins Zuda naab a Ezekiyas nebã sẽn da yãk n gʋlsã. 2 Bũmb nins Wẽnnaam sẽn solgda kõt-eb-la waoogre. La bũmb nins rĩm sẽn mi wã kõt-a-la waoogre. 3 Yĩngr zãntame la tẽngã zulmame, la b ka tõe n fees n bãng rĩm tagsg ye. 4 Yiis wanzuri tãnd pʋgẽ tɩ yõak maan bõn-neere. 5 Yiis nin-wẽns rĩm nengẽ tɩ tɩrlem paas a naamã pãnga. 6 Ra zẽk f meng rĩm nengẽ ye. Ra yaas nin-kãseng zĩigẽ ye. 7 Bala, b sã n yeel foom tɩ: «Wa yĩngr ka» n são b sẽn na n ning foom yãnd naab nengẽ. 8 Ra yãg n bool ned bʋʋd n togs bũmb nins fo sẽn yã wã ye, tɩ f yak ra wa ning f yãnd tɩ f ka mi f sẽn na n maaneg ye. 9 Bɩ f mao ne f yak n maneg f yelle. 10 Ra sak n wilg ned a to yellã t'a soab wa wʋm n mong foom bʋʋm tɩ f yʋʋr sãam ye. 11 B sã n togs goam a wakate, a yaa sõma wa sãnem tʋb-londo. 12 Yam soab tãsg tara yõod ne ned ning sẽn deegdã n yɩɩd sãnem nug-bẽng ne sãnem fan-teedo. 13 Fo sã n tʋm ned t'a sake, yaa wa fo sẽn kẽebd ki tɩ b wa kõ f ko-maasg tɩ f yũ. A nooga a zu-soabã sũuri. 14 Wilg-m-meng soab sã n kãab t'a na n kõo bũmb n ka kõ, yaa wa saag sẽn kʋɩ n vaale. 15 Sũ-mar tõee naaba. Sũ-mar goam tõee n kaooga kõbre. 16 Fo sã n yã sɩɩdo, bɩ f ra rɩ tɩ loog-f tɩ f wa wʋb-a ye. 17 Ra kẽng f yak zakẽ wʋsg t'a yam wa yi-f t'a kisg-f ye. 18 Ned sẽn kɩt zãmb kaset n mongd a yak bʋʋm yaa wa door bɩ sʋʋg wall peemde. 19 Fo sã n teeg zãmb soab toog wakate, yaa wa yẽnd sẽn vigsda, la naoor sẽn dãmbe, 20 la ned sẽn gãe bell waood wakate. Ned sã n yɩɩnd yɩɩn nood fo toog wakate, yaa wa ned sẽn ning nodr yamsem. 21 Kom sã n tar f bɛ bɩ f kõ-a rɩɩb t'a rɩ. Ko-yũud sã n tar-a bɩ f kõ-a koom t'a yũ. 22 Fo sã n maan woto, fo na n dʋʋga bug-saal a zugu. La Zu-soabã na n dola foom. 23 Yaang sobg wata ne saaga, la kãnegr wata ne nen-gãnengo. 24 Fo sã n be rakõ-sẽokẽ n são fo sẽn na n kẽ kãadem ne pʋg yoogo. 25 Koe-noog sẽn yit zĩ-zãrgẽ yaa wa ko-yũud sẽn tar ned tɩ b kõ-a ko-maasga. 26 Nin-tɩrg sã n zoet nin-wẽng n sakd-a, yaa wa bulg koom sẽn dẽgme. 27 Fo sã n dɩ sɩɩd wʋsg me ka sõma ye. 28 Ned sẽn ka tõe a meng yaa wa tẽng lalg sẽn wã.
