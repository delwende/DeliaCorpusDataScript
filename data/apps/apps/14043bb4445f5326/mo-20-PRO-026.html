26
1 Saag ka tog n wa ne sapɩlɩm bõn-bɩʋʋng wakate ye. Woto me, waoogr ka tog ne yalem ye. 2 Ned sã n tʋʋs foom zaalem, yaa wa yirg sẽn looge, wall sɩlaall sẽn yɩke. A tʋʋsgã ka tõe n maan foom bũmb ye. 3 Salbr yaa weef yĩnga, guug yaa bõang yĩnga, la sãbsg me yaa yalem poor yĩnga. 4 Ra togs yalem n yɩ wa yẽ ye. 5 Leok yalem wa sẽn zems a yalemdã t'a ra tags t'a yaa yam soab ye. 6 Ned sã n tʋm yalem t'a tɩ togs koɛɛga, yaa yẽ n bao sũ-toog n kõ a menga. 7 Yalem sã n togs yel-bũndi, yaa wa põor sẽn dat n kẽne. 8 Fo sã n waoog yalma, yaa wa fo sẽn dɩk kugr n loe loaabga. 9 Yel-bũnd sẽn be yalem noorẽ yaa wa rã-yũud sẽn zãad gõaaga. 10 Ned sẽn dɩkd yalem tʋʋmd yaa wa ned sẽn zãad tãp n tãood ned fãa sẽn loogd be. 11 Yalem maanda yalemd wa baag sẽn wʋb n le rɩtẽ. 12 Yalem n são ned sẽn tagsd t'a yaa yam soaba. 13 Kʋɩɩm yetame tɩ gɩgemd n be yɩnga, tɩ gɩgemd n be sor zugu. 14 Kʋɩɩm bilimda a gãagẽ wa ned sẽn pakd kʋɩleng la a pagdẽ. 15 Kʋɩɩm tʋmda a nug laagẽ, la a ka paamd bũmb n ning a noorẽ sã n ka yaams ye. 16 Kʋɩɩm tagsdame t'a yaa yam soab n yɩɩd neb a yopoe sẽn tõe n leok ne yam. 17 Ned sẽn kẽed zabr sẽn ka pak-a yaa wa ned sẽn yã baag sor zug n gãd a tʋbre. 18 - 19 Ned sẽn belgd a yak n yaool n yetẽ t'a reemdame yaa wa gãeeng sẽn lobd bug-vɩlens ne pɛɛm sẽn tõe n kʋ neba. 20 Raad sã n kaẽ, bugum kiidame. La no-tʋʋn-ned sã n kaẽ, zabr sɛtame. 21 Zab-rʋʋs yaa wa ned sẽn wool sagd bɩ mood n zam bug-saal zugu. 22 No-tʋʋn-ned goam yaa wa ned sẽn dɩ rɩ-nood tɩ kẽ n ta hal a pʋgẽ. 23 Nin-wẽng no-noom yaa wa b sẽn ning laag saalem. 24 Zãmb soab gomda no-noom goam n solgd a beem. 25 A sã n gom gom-nood bɩ f ra teeg-a ye. Bala, a sũur pida ne beem. 26 A sã n maan sɩlem n lud wẽnemã, a na n puka neb tigung sʋka. 27 Ned sã n tu boko, a na n lʋɩɩ a pʋgẽ. La a sã n bilim kugr n dat n zãb neba, yaa a meng zug la kugrã na n lʋɩ. 28 Ned sã n kis a to, a yagd-a-la zirẽ. No-noom soab wata ne sãoongo.
