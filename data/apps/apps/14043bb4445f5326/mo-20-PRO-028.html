28
1 Nin-wẽng zoeta a toor tɩ ned ka rigd a ye. La nin-tɩrg tara sũ-keelem wa gɩgemde. 2 Tẽng neb sã n maand wẽng n ka sakd taaba, nanams yaa wʋsgo. La tẽngã sã n tar ned sẽn tar yam ne bãngre, tẽngã paamda pãng n kaoos wʋsgo. 3 Naab sã n nams naong rãmba, yaa wa sa-kẽeng sẽn sãam koodo. 4 Sẽn basd-a tõog pẽgda nin-wẽnse. La sẽn sakd-a tõog kɩɩsda bãmba. 5 Nin-wẽns ka mi tɩrlem ye, la sẽn baood-a Zu-soabã mii a yɛlã fãa. 6 Naong soab sã n tar pʋ-peelem n são nin-wẽng sẽn tar arzɛka. 7 Bi-yamdg sakda tõogo. La nin-yood tũud-n-taag ningda a ba yãnde. 8 Ned sã n pengd neb n baood yõodo, Wẽnnaam na n deega a soab paoongã n kõ sẽn zoet-a naong rãmb nimbãanegã. 9 Ned sã n yẽd a tʋb n ka sakd tõogo, Wẽnnaam ka nong a pʋʋsg ye. 10 Fo sẽn n belg nin-tɩrg t'a maan wẽnga, yaa foom n na n lʋɩ bok ning fo sẽn tu wã pʋgẽ. La pʋ-peelem dãmb na n paama sũ-noogo. 11 Arzɛk soab tagsdame t'a tara yam. La naong soab sẽn ya nin-yẽeseng feesda-a lame. 12 Nin-sõmyã sã n tõoge, a yɩta waoogr wʋsgo. La nin-wẽng sã n deeg naam, neb zoetame. 13 Sẽn solgd-a a yel-wẽn ka na n tõog ye. La sẽn sak n vẽneg-a a yell la a bas-a na n paama nimbãan-zoeere. 14 Sẽn zoet-a Wẽnnaam wakat fãa tara sũ-noogo. La sẽn kengd-a a sũur na n paama toogo. 15 Nin-wẽng sã n soog naong rãmba, yaa wa gɩgemd sẽn yãbd bɩ kom sẽn tar katre. 16 Naab sã n ka tar bãngre, a yaa nin-toaaga. La sẽn ka nong-a zoe-bõn na n dɩɩ naam n kaoose. 17 Nin-kʋʋd zoetame n dabd bok pʋgẽ. Ra gɩdg-a ye. 18 Pʋ-peelem soab na n paama fãagre, la sẽn dat n tũ-a soay a yiib na n lʋɩɩ a ye zugu. 19 Sẽn kood-a a pʋʋg tara rɩɩbo. La naong na n yõka sẽn maand-a yel-yaalse. 20 Sɩd soab paamda bark wʋsgo. La sẽn dat n lebg-a arzɛk soab tao-tao ka na n põs toog ye. 21 Fo sã n tũus neba, ka sõama ye. La baoosgo, neb n be n maand wẽng rɩɩb bilf yĩnga. 22 Zoe-bõn soab rat n paama arzɛk tao-tao. A ka mi tɩ naong na n yõk-a lame ye. 23 B na n nonga sẽn sagend-a neb n yɩɩd no-noom soaba. 24 Ned sã n zuk a ba ne a ma bũmb n yaool n yell t'a ka maan wẽnga, a yaa wa fãada. 25 Zoe-bõn soab wata ne yelle. La sẽn teegd-a Zu-soabã na n paama naare. 26 Yaa yalem n teegd a meng tagsgo. La yaa sẽn maand-a yam n paamd fãagre. 27 Naong ka na n yõk sẽn kõt-a nimbãan-dãmb ye. La ned sã n mumd a nini, neb wʋsg na n kãab-a-la wẽnga. 28 Nin-wẽns sã n deeg naam, neb zoetame. La nin-wẽnsã sã n wa lʋɩ, nin-tɩrs paasdame.
