29
1 B sã n sagl ned wʋsg t'a ka reege, sãoong na n wa ling-a lame tɩ manegr kaẽ ye. 2 Nin-tɩrs sã n ya wʋsgo, nebã tara sũ-noogo. La nin-wẽns sã n deeg naam, nebã yãbdame. 3 Ned sã n nong yam, kõta a ba sũ-noogo. La ned sã n kẽnd pʋg-yoaad nengẽ, a paoong na n sãama zãnga. 4 Yaa tɩrlem n paasd naab tẽng pãnga. La naab sã n dat ligd bala, a tẽngã na n wãame. 5 Ned sã n gom no-noom goam ne a to, a bẽd-a lame. 6 Nin-wẽng yel-wẽn bẽt-a lame. La nin-sõng yɩɩndame n maand sũ-noogo. 7 Nin-sõng mii naong rãmb sẽn tog n paam bũmb ninga. La nin-wẽns ka mi bũmb a woto ye. 8 Wuk-m-mens rãmb rãmbda tẽnga. La yam dãmb kɩtdame tɩ nebã sũy sigi. 9 Yam soab sã n wẽed no-koeemd ne yalma, yalmã sũur sã n yik bɩ a laame, ka paasd laafɩ ye. 10 Nin-kʋʋdb kisa pʋ-peelem dãmba, la nin-tɩrs gũuda b vɩɩm. 11 Yalem sũur yikda wʋsgo. La yam soab sẽndame n maagd a menga. 12 Naab sã n kelgd zirẽ goama, a tʋmtʋmdbã fãa yaa wẽnse. 13 Naong soab ne nin-toaag n seg taaba. Wẽnnaam kõo bãmb b yiibã fãa nini. 14 Naab sã n kaood naong rãmb bʋʋd ne sɩda, a naamã kõn sɛ ye. 15 Sãbsg ne tãsg kõta yam. La b sã n bas biig t'a maan yam, a ningda a ma yãnde. 16 Nin-wẽns sã n dɩt naam, yel-wẽn yaa wʋsgo. La nin-sõmyã na n yãa b lʋɩɩse. 17 Sagl f biig t'a na n wa belsa foom n kõ f sũ-noogo. 18 Wẽnnaam wilgr sã n kaẽ, bũmb ka be sẽn gɩdgd nebã ne wẽng ye. La ned sã n sak tõogo, a tara sũ-noogo. 19 Ka ne goam la ned zãmsd yam ye. Baa a sã n wʋme, a ka na n sak ye. 20 Ned sã n yãgd ne goama, yalem n são-a. 21 Fo sã n maan f yamb neer hal a yãadem wakate, a ka na n wa tẽeg ye. 22 Neb sũy sã n yik tao-tao, b kɩtdame tɩ yel-wẽn ne zabr zĩndi. 23 Ned tɩtaam n wat ne a yãnde. La sik-m-meng soab na n paama waoogre. 24 Ned sã n ya wagdr zoa, a yaa a meng bɛ. A wʋmda kãab-wẽnga la a ka togsd-a ye. 25 Ninsaal zoees wata ne yelle. La sẽn teegd-a Zu-soabã tara laafɩ. 26 Neb wʋsg baooda nanams nonglem. La yaa Zu-soabã n kaood ned bʋʋd tɩrga. 27 Nin-sõmyã ka nong nin-wẽng ye. La nin-wẽng ka nong nin-tɩrg ye.
