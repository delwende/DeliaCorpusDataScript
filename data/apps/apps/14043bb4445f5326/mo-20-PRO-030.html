A Aguur goama
30
1 Maasa rao a Zake biig a Aguur goama. Rao wã goma gom-kãens a Itiyɛll ne a Ukall yĩnga. 2 Sɩda, mam yaa zɩt n yɩɩd nebã fãa. Mam ka tar ninsaal bãngre ye. 3 Mam ka zãms yam ye, la m nan ka bãng Sõng-Soab ye. 4 Ãnna n dʋ yĩngr n wa? Ãe n gãd sobg ne a nug la a pil koom ne a fu-goabga? Ãnna n naan dũniyã? Bãmb yʋʋr la a bõe? B biigã yʋʋr la a bõe? Fo ka mi-b laa? 5 B makame n mik tɩ Wẽnnaam goamã fãa yaa sɩda. Bãmb yaa gãong neb nins sẽn baood tɩ b lill bãmbã yĩnga. 6 Ra paas b goamã bũmb tɩ b wa tãs foom n mik tɩ f yaa zirẽ yagd ye. 7 Wẽnnaam, mam bõosda yãmb bũmb a yiibu. Bɩ y kõ maam bõn-bãmb tɩ m nan ka ki ye. 8 Yiis-y belgr ne zirẽ goam tɩ b zãag ne maam. Ra bas-y tɩ m yɩ naong soab ye, la y ra bas tɩ m yɩ arzɛk soab me ye. Yãmb we n kõ maam dɩɩb sẽn sekd maam bala, 9 tɩ m da wa tɩg n yeel tɩ: «Zu-soabã yaa yɛ neda?» Wall tɩ m da wa yɩ naong soab n zu n paoog yãmb yʋʋr ye. 10 Ra kãneg yamb ne a zu-soab t'a wa tʋʋs foom tɩ f kong bʋʋm ye. 11 Neb n be n tʋʋd b ba-rãmb la b ka waoogd b ma-rãmb ye. 12 Neb n be n tẽed tɩ b yaa sõma n ka rat n maneg b mens ye. 13 Neb n be n pukd b nin n get neb a taabã ne wuk-m-menga. 14 Neb n be tɩ b yẽn wẽnd sʋʋse. Bãmb rɩta naong rãmb n dat n yiis-b dũniyã zugu. B rat n yiisa nimbãan-dãmbã ninsaalbã sʋka. 15 Busli tara kom-pugli a yiibu. B yiibã fãa yʋy boondame tɩ kõ-ma. Bũmb a tãab n ka tol n tɩgda. Bũmb a naas n ka tol n yetẽ tɩ b tɩgame ye. 16 Yaa kũum ne pʋg-kiiri la tẽn-koɛɛnga, la bugum. Bãmb ka tol n yetẽ tɩ b tɩgame ye. 17 Ned sã n yaand a ba la a paoogd a ma a kʋʋlem wakate, togame tɩ gãoobg feoos a nin tɩ sɩls rɩ-a. 18 Bũmb a tãab n ya bõn-bãn ne maam. N-ye, yaa a naas la m ka bãnge. 19 Yaa sɩlg sẽn laand yĩngri, la waaf sẽn be kugr zugu, la koom-koglg sẽn kẽnd ko-kãsengẽ pʋgẽ, ne rao sẽn baood pʋg-sada. 20 Pag sã n maan yoobo, a rɩtame n yẽes a noor n yeel t'a ka maan bũmb ye. 21 Bũmb a tãab n dãmbd dũniyã. N-ye, yaa bũmb a naas la a ka tõe n deege. 22 Yaa yamb sẽn lebg naaba, la yalem sẽn dɩ n tɩge, 23 la pʋg-yoog sẽn paam sɩda n paas yem-poak sẽn deeg a zu-sob-n--poak zĩiga. 24 Bũmb a naas n be dũniyã zug n ya bõonego n yaool n tar yam wʋsgo. 25 Gũur ka pãng ye, la bõn-bɩʋʋng wakate, b wʋkda ki n bẽngdẽ. 26 Kɩɩs me ka tar pãng ye, la b tuuda bogd n maand b yiya. 27 Sʋ-kinkirs ka tar naab ye, la b tũuda taab n kẽnda. 28 Fo tõee n yõka bõn-poak ne nugu, la a bee na-yiri. 29 Bũmb a tãab n be, n-ye, b yaa naase n yaa neer tɩ b sã n kẽnd bɩ b be neere. Yaa gɩgemd sẽn ya we-naab n ka zoet bũmb ye, 30 la no-raoog sẽn zẽkd a zug n kẽnd no-yãans sʋka, la bʋeega, la naab sẽn kẽnd a neb taoore. 31 Fo sã n maan yalemd n wuk f meng n dat n maan wẽnga, bɩ f gũus neere. 32 Tɩ bõe, fo sã n zugs bĩisim, a lebgda bĩis-kaam. 33 Fo sã n wẽ ned yõore, zɩɩm yitame. La fo sã n kɩt tɩ ned sũur yiki, lebgda zabre.
