Bũmb fãa yaa zaalem
1
1 Koɛɛg soabã sẽn ya Zeruzalɛm dĩmã goama. Yẽ yaa a David biiga. 2 Koɛɛgã soab yeelame yaa: Yaa zaalem bala, fãa yaa zaalem. 3 Ninsaal sẽn be dũniyã zug n maandẽ wã yõod yaa bõe? 4 Zamaan a yembr loogdame tɩ zamaan a to wa, la dũniyã ket n paada wakat fãa. 5 Sõor pukdame la wẽndg lʋɩtame, la a yãgdame n na n ta zĩig ning a sẽn na n leb pukã. 6 Sobg kẽngda goabga, n wẽnemdẽ n dabd dɩtgo. Soabgã gilgdame n gilgdẽ, la a le leb n dɩkda a pẽnd sorã n wẽnemdedẽ. 7 Kʋɩlsã fãa koom zoetame n kẽngd ko-kãsengã pʋgẽ, la ko-kãsengã ka pit ye. Zĩig ning ko-zoetmã sẽn kẽngdã, a giligdame n le wa be yɛsa. 8 Bũmb fãa tʋʋmd yaa toogo. Ned goam ka sekd a ye. Bũmb nins nin sẽn yãtã ka sekd-a ye, la tʋbr sẽn wʋmd bũmb ninsã ka sekd-a ye. 9 Yaa bũmb ning sẽn da zĩnd pẽndã n na n zĩndi. La yaa bũmb ning sẽn da maan pẽndã n na n maane. Bõn-paalg ka be dũniyã zug ye. 10 Bũmb beeme tɩ neb gomd a yelle n yetẽ tɩ b ges bõn-paalg bii? Yaa bũmb ning sẽn da zĩnd pẽndã n be tõnd zamaane. 11 B ka tẽegd bũmb nins sẽn da zĩnd pẽndã yell ye. La bũmb ning sẽn na n wa maanã, neb nins sẽn na n wa wa wã ka na n tẽeg bõn-kãens yell ye.
Yam yaa zaalem
12 Mam yaa koɛɛg soaba n ya Israyɛll dĩm n be Zeruzalɛm. 13 Mam yãka yam n dat n tags ne yẽesem n bãng bũmb nins sẽn maand dũniyã zugã yelle. Bũmb ning Wẽnnaam sẽn kõ ninsaalbã tɩ b nams ne-a wã yell yaa toogo. 14 Mam yãa tʋʋm nins fãa sẽn maand dũniyã zugã. La gese, fãa yaa zaalem. Yaa wa ned sẽn digd sobgo. 15 Bũmb ning sẽn golma ka tõe n tẽeg ye. La bũmb ning sẽn ka ta wã ka tõe n sõd ye. 16 Mam da tagsdame tɩ mam tara yam n yɩɩd neb nins fãa sẽn deng maam n soog Zeruzalɛmã. Mam yãa yam la bãngr wʋsgo. 17 La mam da tagsdame n dat n bãng yẽesem la yalemdo la faolem võore. La mam mikame tɩ yõwã me yaa wa ned sẽn digd sobgo. 18 Tɩ bõe, ned yẽesem sã n waoogẽ, a tara sũ-sãoongo. La ned sã n dat n bao bãngr n paase, a paasda a meng toogo.
