Bũmb fãa tara a wakate.
3
1 Bũmb fãa tara a wakate. Bũmb nins fãa sẽn maand dũniyã zugã tara b wakate. 2 Rogem tara a wakate, la kũum me tara a wakate. Bʋdb tara a wakate, la tigsg me tara a wakate. 3 Kʋʋb beeme, la tɩpg wakat me beeme. Lubr wakat beeme, la meeb wakat me beeme. 4 Yãbr wakat beeme, la laad wakat me beeme. Wiisg wakat beeme, la warb wakat me beeme. 5 Kug lobr wakat beeme, la a wʋkr wakat me beeme. Mobg taab wakat beeme, la ra mobg taab wakat me beeme. 6 Baoob wakat beeme, la meneng wakat me beeme. Zãab wakat beeme, la lobr wakat me beeme. 7 Fuug kẽegr wakat beeme, la a sẽeb wakat me beeme. Sẽndim wakat beeme, la goam wakat me beeme. 8 Nonglem wakat beeme, la kisgr wakat me beeme. Zabr wakat beeme, la laafɩ wakat me beeme. 9 Ninsaal namsgã yõod yaa bõe? 10 Mam yãa tʋʋmd ning Wẽnnaam sẽn kõ ninsaalbã tɩ b maanã. 11 Bãmb maana bũmb fãa sõama n zems a wakate. Bãmb ninga wakat sẽn ka sɛt tagsg ninsaal sũur pʋgẽ, baa ne yẽ sẽn ka tõe n tags n bãng tʋʋmd ning Wẽnnaam sẽn maan hal sɩngr tɩ ta sɛɛbã. 12 La mam miime tɩ bũmb ka be sẽn noom ninsaal n yɩɩd yẽ sẽn na n paam sũ-noog la naar a vɩɩmã wakat ye. 13 Ninsaalbã fãa sã n na n dɩ la b yũ, la b yã b tʋʋmdã noom, yaa Wẽnnaam kũuni. 14 Mam miime tɩ bũmb ning fãa Wẽnnaam sẽn maanã na n paa wakat sẽn ka sɛta. Bũmb ka be n na n paas a wall a boog-a ye. Yaa Wẽnnaam n maan-a t'a yɩ woto tɩ nebã zoe bãmba. 15 Bũmb ning sẽn be wã ra pẽnd n beeme. La bũmb ning sẽn na n wa zĩndã ra pẽnd n beeme. La Wẽnnaam kɩtdame tɩ bũmb nins sẽn da loogã leb n wa. 16 La sẽn paase, mam yãame tɩ dũniyã ka, b basda bʋ-sɩd ne tɩrlem n maand wẽnga. 17 Mam tagsa m sũur pʋgẽ tɩ Wẽnnaam na n kaoo nin-tɩrg la nin-wẽng bʋʋdo, tɩ bõe, yɛlã fãa ne tʋʋmã fãa tara b wakate. 18 Mam tagsa ninsaal yell m sũur pʋgẽ n yeel tɩ Wẽnnaam tõee n pʋka ninsaalbã yɛl tɩ b yã b mens n bãng tɩ b yaa wa rũms bala. 19 Tɩ bõe, bũmb ning sẽn paamd ninsaalbã ne bũmb nins sẽn paamd rũmsã yaa bũmb a yembre. A yembr kiida wa a to. Bãmb b yiibã fãa yõy vʋʋsem yaa bũmb a yembre. Ninsaal ka yɩɩd rũng ye. 20 Bãmb fãa rabda zĩ-yende. fãa maana ne tẽng tom, la fãa leb n lebgda tẽng tom. 21 Ãnna n mi ninsaal yõy vʋʋsem sã n kẽngda yĩngr tɩ rũms rẽnd sigd tẽnga? 22 Mam yãame tɩ bũmb ka be sẽn noom ninsaal n yɩɩd a sẽn na n maan sũ-noog ne a tʋʋmã ye. Yaa woto la yẽ rẽnda. Ãnna n na n sõng yẽ t'a yã bũmb ning sẽn na n wa zĩnd yẽ loogr poorẽ wã?
