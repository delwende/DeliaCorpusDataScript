Waoog Wẽnnaam
5
1 Fo sã n dabd Wẽnnaam doogẽ, bɩ f gũus f naoore. Kolg n kelge, tɩ rẽ n são yalembã maand maanego. Tɩ bõe, bãmb ka mi tɩ bãmb sẽn maandã yaa wẽng ye. 2 Bɩ f ra yãg n gom ye. Gũus f menga n da gom yell tao-tao Wẽnnaam nengẽ ye. Tɩ bõe, Wẽnnaam bee yĩngri la fo bee dũniyã zugu. Dẽ, bɩ f kɩt tɩ f goam yɩ bilfu. 3 Tɩ bõe, yɛl wʋsg togsg wata ne yʋng zãmsdo. Woto me, goam sã n waoogẽ, b wʋmda yalem-koɛɛga. 4 Fo sã n pʋlem Wẽnnaam bũmbu, bɩ f pids-a n da bas tɩ kaoos ye. Bala, nin-yaalg ka tat Wẽnnaam yam ye. Bɩ f yao bũmb ning fo sẽn pʋlmã. 5 Fo sã n ka maan pʋlengo, yõwã n são fo sẽn na n pʋlem n yaool n ka rol ye. 6 Ra sak tɩ f noor ning yeel f meng zug ye. La ra yeel b sẽn tʋm a soab fo nengẽ wã tɩ fo ra tudgame ye. Wẽnnaam sũur sã n pʋʋg ne fo koɛɛgã tɩ b sãam fo nug tʋʋmdã, a yõod yaa bõe? 7 Tɩ bõe, zãmsd sã n ya wʋsgo, yel-yaals yaa wʋsgo, la goam me yaa wʋsgo. La bɩ f zoe Wẽnaam.
Arzegs yaa zaalem
8 Fo sã n yã tɩ b maand naong rãmb pãnga, n mongd bãmb bʋʋm n ka kao bʋʋd tɩrg tẽngã pʋgẽ, bɩ f ra yɛɛs ye. Tɩ bõe, ned n be n yɩɩd bãmb n getẽ, la neb a taab n be n yɩɩd bãmb fãa. 9 Fãa paamda tẽngã yõodo. Pʋʋg ned sẽn ko tara soaba. 10 Ned sã n nong ligdi, ligd ka sekd-a ye. La ned sã n nong arzegse, paoong paoosg ka sekd-a ye. Yõwã me yaa zaalem. 11 Paoong sã n ya wʋsgo, a rɩtbã me yaa wʋsgo. La paoong yõod yaa bõe ne ned ning sẽn so bãmbã, sã n ka yẽ sẽn ne bãmbã bala? 12 Tʋmtʋmd sã n dɩt bilf wall wʋsgo, a gõeem yaa noogo. La arzɛk soab tɩgrã ka basd-a t'a gũs ye. 13 Mam yãa yell dũniyã zug sẽn ya toog wʋsgo. Yaa ned sẽn bẽngd arzegs n wat ne a meng toogo. 14 Yẽ arzegsã tũuda ne yɛl sẽn yɩɩd yẽ pãng n menemdẽ. Yẽ roga bi-ribla, la bũmb ka le ket yẽ nugẽ ye. 15 Wa yẽ sẽn da yi a ma pʋgẽ wã, yẽ na n leb n looga zaalem wa yẽ sẽn da wa wã. Yẽ ka na n zã a tʋʋmã yõod a nugẽ n loog ye. 16 Yõwã me yaa toog wʋsgo. Yẽ loogrã yaa toog wa a waoongã bala. Yaa bõe yõod la yẽ paamd a sẽn tʋmd sobg yĩngã? 17 Sẽn paase, yẽ rɩta a rɩɩbã lik pʋgẽ a vɩɩmã tɛka, la yẽ paamda sũ-sãoong wʋsgo, la yẽns toogo, la sũ-toogo. 18 Gese, mam yãame t'a zemsame tɩ ned rɩ la a yũ, la a maan sũ-noogo ne a tʋʋmã fãa yẽ sẽn maand dũniyã zug a vɩɩm tɛk Wẽnnaam sẽn kõ yẽ wã. Yaa yẽ pʋɩɩr la woto. 19 La Wẽnnaam sã n kõ ned arzegs la paoongo, la b kõ-a pãng t'a soog-a, la a rɩ-a n yãk a rẽnd n maan sũ-noog ne a tʋʋmdã, yõwã yaa Wẽnnaam kũuni. 20 La yẽ ka na n tol n tẽeg a vɩɩm dasem yell wʋsg ye. Tɩ bõe, Wẽnnaam kɩtame tɩ yẽ tʋm ne sũ-noogo.
