6
1 Wẽng a ye la mam yã dũniyã zugu. La a yaa zɩsg ne ninsaala. 2 Wẽnnaam kõta nina arzegs la paoong la naam, hal tɩ bũmb nins yẽ sũur sẽn datã baa a ye ka paoog ye. La Wẽnnaam yaool n ka kõ yẽ pãng t'a paam b nafr ye. Yaa neb a taab n paamd a nafre. Yõwã me yaa zaalem la a yaa toog wʋsgo. 3 Ned sã n dog kamb koabga, n paam yõ-woko, t'a yʋʋm yɩ wʋsgo, tɩ bõn-nood nins yẽ sẽn tarã ka sek-a, la a ka paam yaoogo, mam yetame tɩ b sẽn ka paam n dog a soabã n tar sũ-noog n yɩɩd yẽ. 4 Tɩ bõe, a wata zaalem la a loogda lik pʋgẽ, la lik lillda a yʋʋre. 5 La a ka yã wẽndgã n bãng bũmb yell ye. Baoosgo, a paamda vʋʋsg n yɩɩd a soaba. 6 Baa yẽ sã n kaoos yʋʋm tus a yiibu, yẽ sã n ka tar sũ-noogo, bãmb fãa ka rabd zĩig a yembr laa ? 7 Ninsaal tʋʋmdã fãa yaa a noorã yĩnga. La baoosgo, bũmb ka sekd yẽ ye. 8 Yaa ne bõe la yam soab são yalma? La naong soab sẽn mi n kẽnd nin-vɩɩs taoorã yõod yaa bõe? 9 Ned nif sẽn ne bũmb ningã n são yẽ sẽn tagsd t'a na n wa paama bũmb ningã. Yõwã me yaa zaalem la a wẽnda ned sẽn digd sobgo. 10 Bũmb ning sẽn be masã wã ra pẽnd n tara a yʋʋre, la b mii ninsaal sẽn ya bũmb ninga. B miime t'a ka tõe n mao ne sẽn tar-a pãng n yɩɩd yẽ wã ye. 11 Goam sã n ya wʋsgo, gom yood wʋsg me bee be. La a nafda ninsaal ne bõe? 12 Ãnna n mi sẽn ya sõama ninsaal yĩng a vɩɩm ning sẽn ya zaalem n loogd wa sulunsukã wakat tɛka? Ãnna n tõe n togs ninsaal bũmb ning sẽn na n wa zĩnd dũniyã zug ka yẽ loogr poorẽ wã?
