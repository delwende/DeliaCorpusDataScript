10
1 Zõos kiida tɩdar kaam pʋgẽ t'a yũug ka le ya sõama ye Yalemd bilf woogda yam la waoogre 2 Yam soab mii a sẽn dabdẽ, la yalem ka mi a sẽn dabdẽ ye. 3 Baa yalem sã n kẽnd sor zugu, a bãngr paoodame, la yẽ togsda nebã fãa tɩ yẽ yaa yalma. 4 Naab sũur sã n yik ne foom, bɩ f ra zoe n bas f zĩig ye. Tɩ bõe, sũ-maasem gɩdgda kong-kãsemse. 5 Wẽng a yembr la mam yã dũniyã zugu, la a wẽnda tudgri: Yaa ned ning sẽn so-a neb n maand-a. 6 Yalemb zĩi zĩ-bɛdẽ la arzegs rãmb zĩi paoogr zĩisẽ. 7 Mam yãa yembs sẽn zao wiid tɩ nanams kẽnd ne nao dũniyã zug wa yembse. 8 Ned sã n tuud boko, yẽ meng n na n lʋɩ bog-kãng pʋgẽ. La ned sã n lubd lalga, waaf na n zĩnda be n dũm-a. 9 Ned sã lagsed kuga, kugã na n pogl-a lame. Ned sã n wesd raado, a na n paama toog raadã wesb pʋgẽ. 10 Lar noor sã n mud n ka le rɩtẽ, a soab togame n nak a pãng n kɛ. La yam sõngda yẽ t'a tõoge. 11 Waaf sã n dũm ned belsg sẽn kaẽ, bɛlsd ka tar yõod ye. 12 Yam soab yam bee barka, la yalem noor kɩtdame t'a menemẽ. 13 Yẽ goamã sɩngr yaa yalemdo, la yẽ koɛɛgã baoosg yaa gãeemdo. 14 Yalem gomda wʋsgo. Ninsaal ka mi bũmb ning sẽn na n wa zĩndã ye. Ãnna n na n wilg yẽ bũmb ning sẽn na n wa zĩnd yẽ loogr poorẽ wã? 15 Yalem tʋʋmd yɛɛda yẽnda, tɩ bõe, yẽ ka mi a sẽn na n tũ zĩig ning n kẽng tẽng pʋgẽ ye. 16 Tẽng ning rĩm sẽn ya bi-bilfu, t'a na-biis rɩt kibs yibeoog pẽnda, toog bee fo zugu. 17 Tẽng ning rĩm sẽn ya na-biiga, bark bee fo zugu. Tɩ bõe, fo na-biisã rɩta wakat sẽn zemse. Bãmb dɩt n baooda pãnga, b ka baood neng yilung ye. 18 Ned sã n ya kʋɩɩma, a roog lʋɩtame. La ned sã n bas a nus n ka tʋmdẽ, a roog yũndame. 19 Rɩɩb sã n be ned taoore, a tõee n laame. La rãam maanda ned vɩɩm tɩ yɩ noogo. La ligd sakda bãmb fãa. 20 Ra tʋʋs rĩma, baa f sũur pʋgẽ ye. La ra tʋʋs arzɛk soab fo ro-gãagẽ ye. Tɩ bõe, liuuli na n dɩka fo koɛɛgã n looge, la bõn-yɩgdɩ na n togsa yellã.
