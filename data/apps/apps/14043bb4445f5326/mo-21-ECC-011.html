Bur koom zugu
11
1 Lob f rɩɩb koom zutu, tɩ bõe, nindaare, fo na n leb n paam-a lame. 2 Yãk fo sẽn tarã n pʋɩ ne neb a yopoe wall a nii, tɩ bõe, fo ka mi toog ning sẽn na n wa zĩnd dũniyã zugã ye. 3 Saag sã n kʋɩ, a niida dũniyã zugã. La tɩɩg sã n lʋɩ rɩtg wall goabga, a paada zĩig ning a sẽn lʋɩ wã. 4 Ned sã n gũud sobgo, yẽ ka na n bʋd baa fɩ ye, la ned sã n get sawato, yẽ ka na n tigs baa fɩ ye. 5 A wa fo sẽn ka mi sobg sẽn tũud zĩig ninga, la kõab sẽn maand to-to pag sẽn tʋt pʋgẽ wã, woto me, fo ka tõe n bãng tʋʋmd ning Wẽnnaam sẽn maandã fãa ye. 6 Bʋd f bõn-buud yibeoogo, la ra bas f nus tɩ yɩ zaalem zaabr ye. Tɩ bõe, fo ka mi a yembr wall a to sã n na n tõogame, wall a yiibã fãa sã n na n yɩɩ sõama ye.
Tẽeg f Naandã yell f bi-bɩɩlem wakate
7 Vẽenem yaa noogo, la ned nin sã n yãt wẽndga, a sũur yaa noogo. 8 Ned sã n vɩ n kaoos yʋʋm wʋsgo, bɩ a maan sũ-noog yʋʋm-kãens fãa pʋgẽ. La bɩ a tags lika rasem sẽn na n wa yɩ wʋsgã yelle. Bũmb fãa sẽn watã yaa zaalem. 9 Bi-bɩɩga, maan sũ-noog f bi-bɩɩlmãa wakate, la f noog f sũur f bɩʋʋngã wakate, n tũ sor ning f sũur sẽn data la f nin sẽn getã. La bɩ f bãng tɩ Wẽnnaam na n bʋʋ ne foom bõn-kãens fãa yĩnga. 10 Rẽ yĩnga, rig sũ-pa-noom t'a loog n bas-fo, la f zãag f meng ne wẽnga. Tɩ bõe, yãadem ne bi-bɩɩlem wakat yaa zaalem.
