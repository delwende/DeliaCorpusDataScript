1
1 A Salomo yɩɩll sẽn yɩɩd yẽ yɩɩl a taabã fãa.
Pʋg-paala
2 Bɩ a mogs maam ne a noore, tɩ bõe, fo nonglem n são rãam. 3 Fo tɩdarã yũug yaa sõama. Fo yʋʋrã yaa wa tɩdar kaam yũug noom sẽn sãeege. Yaa rẽ n kɩt tɩ pʋg-sadbã fãa nong foom. 4 Wa peeg maam tɩ d zoe. Rĩmã talla maam n kẽ a roogẽ.
Zo-rãmba
La tõnd na n maana sũ-noog fo yĩnga. Tõnd na n pẽga fo nonglmã n yɩɩd rãam.
Pʋg-paala
A zemsame tɩ b nong foom. 5 Zeruzalɛm kom-pugli, mam yaa sablga, la m yaa neer wa Kedaar fu-roto, la a wa a Salomo saman-pamã. 6 Ra ges-y maam m sablmã yĩng ye. Yaa wĩntoog n wʋm maam. M ma-bi-raopã sũy yika ne maam tɩ b kɩt tɩ m kẽng b pʋtẽ n tɩ tʋme. Mam ka tʋm m meng pʋʋgẽ ye. 7 Kelg wɛ, mam sẽn nong a soabã, fo wãbenda f piisã yɛ? Fo gãnegda bãmb yɛɛnẽ tɩ b vʋʋsd wĩntoogo? Ra bas maam tɩ m yɩ wa gõaada, n bʋdg n tũ fo tũud-n-taasã rũms kɩʋʋng zĩisẽ ye.
Zo-rãmba
8 Fo sẽn yaa neer n yɩɩd pagb a tãabã, fo sã n ka mi, bɩ f kẽng n dɩk piisã naoã, n kɩɩm f bʋʋsa pe-kɩɩmbã yiyã sɛɛga.
Noanga
9 M noanga, mam makda foom ne wed-yãang ning sẽn takd Farawõ voatiirã. 10 Fo tʋbã sẽn be fo yagdgẽ wã toga ne tʋblondo. La fo kokorã toga n loyɛrga. 11 Tõnd na n maana sãnem yãnd sẽn tui ne wanzuri n kõ foom.
Pʋg-paala
12 Wakat ning rĩmã sẽn zẽ n dɩtẽ wã, mam tɩdarã kaam yũug piuugame. 13 M noang yaa wa yolg sẽn pid ne tɩdar n be mam bĩisã sʋka. 14 M noang yaa wa tɩ-puud sẽn be Ɩn-Gedi nasaar sãbs pʋtẽ.
Noanga
15 M noanga, fo yaa neere, fo yaa neer wʋsgo. Fo ninã yaa wa wall nini.
Pʋg-paala
16 M noanga, fo yaa neer wʋsgo. Sɩda, fo noogda m sũuri. Yaa mood bal n ya tõnd pĩiri.
Noanga
17 Tõnd doogã sug-raad yaa sɛɛdr raado, la tõnd maana a yẽrã ne sɩprɛ raado.
