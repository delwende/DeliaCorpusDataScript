Noanga
4
1 Fo yaa neere, m noanga, fo yaa neere. Fo ninã yaa wa wall, n be fu-bil ning fo sẽn pil f nengã pʋgẽ. Fo zoobdã yaa wa bʋʋs sẽn sigd Galaad tãng sɛɛga. 2 Fo yẽnã yaa wa b sẽn wõrs pe-bi kõbdo, n tall bãmb n tɩ so koom n leb n watẽ. Pesg fãa tara a tũud-n-taaga, pesg ka be n ka tar tũud-n-taag ye. 3 Fo no-bɩmsã yaa wa gẽs-miuugu, la fo goamã yaa noogo. Fo yagdsã yaa wa b sẽn bok gernade, n be fu-bil ning fo sẽn pil f nengã pʋgẽ. 4 Fo yũblã yaa wa a Daviid gãosg b sẽn me n na n bẽng zab-teedo. Gãnd tusr yaga be. 5 Fo bĩis a yiibã yaa wa yãg-bi a yiib sẽn ya kinkirsi, n wãbd yamd rulg-lɛlma sʋka. 6 Sẽn deng zĩig maagre, zĩigã fãa sẽn na n lebg maasem, mam na n zoee n kẽnga tãens nins miir sẽn be wã, la kunkub nins tɩdar yũug sẽn be wã. 7 M noanga, fo yaa neere. Fo ka tar zang ye. 8 M pʋg-paala, wa tɩ d loog n bas Libã. Tɩ d bas Amana tãng zugu, la Seniir ne Hɛrmo tãens zutu, la gɩgma sẽn gãe zĩis ninsã, la tãens nins ãbs sẽn be wã. 9 M tão wã, m pʋg-paala, fo noogda mam sũur wʋsgo. Fo sã n ges maam vugr bala, tɩ mam yã fo loyɛrg a ye bala, mam sũur yaa noogo. 10 M taoã, m pʋg-paala, fo nonglmã yaa noogo. Fo nonglmã n são dãam, la fo tɩdarã kaam yũug yaa noog n yɩɩd tɩdar buud a taabã fãa. 11 M pʋg-paala, fo no-bɩmsã tokda sɩɩdo. Sɩɩd ne bĩisim bee fo zelemdã tẽngre. La fo futã yũug wẽnda Libã tɩdare. 12 M tãoã, m pʋg-paala, fo wẽnda zẽed zĩig sẽn tar yãgre, la ko-bundem zĩig sẽn tar lalga, la bulg sẽn page. 13 Fo tɩ-tobendã yaa wa gernad tɩɩs sẽn womd bi-noodo. La a tara puud ne tɩdare 14 la safran ne roozo sẽn tar yũ-noogo la sinnamon ne tɩɩs sẽn tar wisdu, la miir ne aloɛs la bũmb nins fãa b yũug sẽn ya noog wʋsgã. 15 Fo yaa wa ko-bundem sẽn be zẽed zĩisẽ, la bulg koom sẽn kõt vɩɩm, la ko-zoetem sẽn yit Libã.
Pʋg-paala
16 Rɩtg sobgo, yiki. Goabg sobgo, wa-y-yã. Bɩ y wẽ mam zẽedã t'a yũug sãeege. Bɩ m noang wa a zẽedã zĩigẽ n dɩ a tɩ-bi-noodã.
