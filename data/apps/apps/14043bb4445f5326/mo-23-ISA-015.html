Pẽnd-n-bãngr goam ne Moab yelle
15
1 Ad bãngr goam sẽn wilgd Moab yelle. B sãama Aar yʋng a ye pʋgẽ, la Moab menemame. 2 B rʋʋme n ta Dibon pʋʋsg roog sẽn be yĩngrã n yãbe. Moab wiisda Nebo tẽngã la Medeba tẽngã yĩnga. Zut fãa põngame, la toeems fãa wõrsame. 3 Neb nins sẽn kẽnd Moab tẽngã Soayã zutã yera boto. La ned kam fãa yãbda nintãm wʋsg tẽngã rot zut la a zĩ-sõsdsẽ. 4 Hesbon tẽngã ne Eleal tẽngã kelemdame. Neb wʋmda b koees hal Zahats tẽnga. Dẽ n kɩt tɩ Moab gãnnaadã kelem t'a nebã pelens yɩke. 5 Mam sũur sãama Moab yĩnga. A nebã zoetame n dat n ta Tesoaar la Eglat-Solisza. Tɩ bõe, bãmb sẽn dʋ tãng n dabd Lukiti, bãmb kẽnda ne nintãm. Bãmb sẽn be Koronayim sorẽ, bãmb kelemda sãoongã yĩnga. 6 Nimrim kʋɩlgã koom sãamame. Tɩ bõe, mood kʋɩɩme la mo-maas sɛɛme. Bõn-bɩtsã ka le be tẽngã pʋgẽ ye. 7 Yaa woto n kɩt tɩ bãmb segend b bũmb nins sẽn ketã la paoong nins bãmb sẽn da bẽngã n loogdẽ n pɩʋʋgd Soles kʋɩlgã. 8 Tɩ bõe, kɛleng yikame n ta Moab soolem todsã fãa. Wiisg rãamd taa Eglayim la Beer-Elim me. 9 Dimon koom pida ne zɩɩm, la baoosgo, mam na n kɩtame tɩ Dimon paam toog n paase: Yaa toog sẽn ya wa gɩgemd Moab neb nins sẽn põsã yĩnga, la tẽngã neb nins sẽn ketã yĩnga.
