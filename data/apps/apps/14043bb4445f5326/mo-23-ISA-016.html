16
1 Bɩ y tʋm pe-bi n kõ tẽn-soaadã, tʋm-y bãmb tɩ b yi Sela n pɩʋʋg weoogã n ta Siyõ nebã tãngẽ wã. 2 Moab nebã sẽn be Arnon kʋɩlgã gindgã yaa wa liuuli sẽn meneme, bãmb yaa wa liuul kamb sẽn yɛɛs n bas b tʋk n sãeege. 3 Bɩ y sagl tõndo, la y kao tõnd bʋʋdo, la y kɩt tɩ yãmb maasem bui tõnd wĩnto-sʋk wa yʋngo, n solg b sẽn digd b-rãmbã. Ra zãmb-y neb nins sẽn zoetã ye. 4 Bas-y Moab neb nins sẽn zoe n põsã tɩ b zĩnd yãmb sʋka. Lill-y bãmb tɩ sãamdã ra sãam bãmb ye. Neb sẽn maand neb a taab pãngã sã n wa n kaẽ tɩ sãamdã looge, tɩ sẽn zɛɛd-a tẽngã meneme, 5 dẽ, mam naam geer na n yẽbga ne nonglem. La sẽn na n zaoond-a be ne sɩdã na n yɩɩ a Daviid yagenga, yẽ yaa bʋ-kaood sẽn nong tɩrlem la sẽn kaood bʋʋd tɩrga. 6 Tõnd wʋmame tɩ Moab rãmbã maanda tɩtaam wʋsgo. Yẽ zẽk-m-mengã ne a tɩtaamã la a wuk-m-mengã ne a gom bɛdã fãa yaa zaalem. 7 Yaa woto yĩng n kɩt tɩ Moab nebã wiisd Moab yĩnga. Nebã fãa wiisdame. Bãmb wẽeda sir Kir-Hareset tẽngã nasaar sãbsã yĩnga, a yaa sũ-sãoong bala. 8 Tɩ bõe, Hesbon tẽngã pʋt wãkame, buud wʋsg rĩm-dãmb nens yilma ne Sibma nasaar sibã rãamã. Yẽ tẽeme n ta Zaezer la weoogẽ. A wila tobame n loog mogrã. 9 Woto yĩnga, mam yãbda Sibma nasaar sibã yĩng wa Zaezer me sẽn yãbd yẽ yĩngã. Hesbon ne Eleale, mam zãgsda yãmb ne m nintãm. Tɩ bõe, zab kelems yika yãmb tɩ-biis wobr la yãmb kood kẽebg poorẽ. 10 Sũ-noog menema pʋt nins sẽn da ya sõama wã pʋse, la yɩɩl ne kilsg ka leb n be nasaar sãbsã pʋtẽ ye. Neb ka le tabd tɩ-biis vẽ wẽkr koglg pʋgẽ ye, la sũ-noog kaasg me sɛɛme. 11 Yaa rẽ n kɩt tɩ mam sũurã miimd m pʋgẽ wa pɛndg zãeedo, Moab ne Kir-Harɛs yĩnga. 12 La Moab kẽngame n ta a zĩ-zẽkdsẽ, yẽ kẽe a zĩ-sõngẽ n na n pʋʋs a wẽnnaam, la yẽ ka tõog n paam bũmb ye. 13 Goam ning Zu-soabã sẽn gom Moab yellã poorẽ hal kʋdemd wakatã yaa woto. 14 La masã, Zu-soabã yeelame yaa: «A keta yʋʋm a tãabo, sẽn zems wa yʋʋm nins tʋmtʋmd sẽn tʋmd yaood yĩnga, la Moab ziirã na n lebga paoogre, baa yẽ sã n ya neb kʋʋng wʋsgo. Neb nins sẽn kellã na n yɩɩ bilf bal sẽn ka tar pãng ye.»
