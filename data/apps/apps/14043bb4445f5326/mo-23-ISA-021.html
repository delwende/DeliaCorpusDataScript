Pẽnd-n-bãngr goam ne Babilon yelle
21
1 Ad bõn-makr sẽn wilgd weoog ning sẽn be ko-kãsengã noorã yelle. A yita weoogẽ tẽn-wẽng pʋgẽ n wat ne pãng wa sob-kãseng sẽn yit goabga. 2 Mam yãa bõn-makr sẽn ya toogo. Namsdã namsdame, la sãamdã sãamdame. Elam soolem, yiki! Medi soolem, kẽng zabre! Mam na n kɩtame tɩ sirã wẽeb sɛ. 3 Yaa yõwã yĩng n kɩt tɩ mam sɛɛgã zabdẽ. Toog yõka maam wa toog sẽn yõkd pag a rogem wakate. Mam kɩɩsdame hal t'a gɩdgd maam tɩ m ka tõe n wʋm ye. Mam yɛɛsame n ka tõe n yã ye. 4 Mam sũur sãamame la pelen-yɩkr yõka maam. Mam sẽn da nong zĩ-sobdã, masã, zĩig sã n dat n sobge, mam yɛɛsdame. 5 B segenda rɩɩbo la b gãnegd pĩiri. B rɩtame la b yũuda. Nanamse, yik-y-yã! Sõog-y gãong kaam. 6 Yaa woto la Zu-soabã yeel maam: «Kẽng n kɩt tɩ tãb-gũudã yaas a zĩigẽ n togs bũmb ning yẽ sẽn yã wã. 7 Yẽ sã n yã wed-rʋʋdbã yẽ sẽn yegl taab yiib-yiib n watẽ, la a sã yã neb sẽn zomb bõens ne yʋgma, bɩ a gũusi, bɩ a gũus sõama.» 8 Dẽ, sẽn yã-a wã kelmame n yeele: «Zu-soaba, mam yasa gãosg zug wĩntoogã tɛka, la m yasa m zĩigẽ yʋng fãa n gũudẽ. 9 La gese, wed-rʋʋdbã yegda taab yiib-yiib n watẽ.» La yẽ le yeelame yaa: «A lʋɩɩme, Babilon lʋɩɩme! La bũmb nins sẽn mamsd yẽ wẽnnaam-dãmbã fãa lʋɩɩ tẽng n wã.» 10 Mam neba, b pãdba yãmb wa ki sẽn be zɛɛg pʋgẽ. Mam wilga yãmb bũmb ning Saasẽ-pãng-soabã sẽn ya Israyɛll Wẽnnaamã sẽn togs maamã.
Pẽnd-n-bãngr goam ne Edom yelle
11 Ad bõn-makr sẽn wilgd Edom yelle. Ned n zĩnd Seyiir soolmã pʋgẽ n zẽk a koɛɛg n sok maam yaa: «Gũuda, rũnna yʋngã yell yaa wãn-wãna? Gũuda rũnna yʋngã yell yaa wãn-wãna?» 12 La gũuda leokame: «Yibeoog ne yʋʋng watame. Yãmb sã n dat n sok a yell bɩ y soke. Bɩ y leb n wa.»
Pẽnd-n-bãngr goam ne Arabɩ yelle
13 Ad bõn-makr sẽn wilgd Arabɩ soolem yelle. Dedan, so-kẽndba, yãmb na n gãanda Arabɩ wetã pʋse. 14 Tema neba, wa-y ne koom n wa kõ ko-yũud sẽn tar b-rãmbã, la y seg neb nins sẽn zoetã ne dɩɩbo. 15 Tɩ bõe, b rigda bãmb ne sʋʋse, yaa sʋʋs sẽn wõoge, la tãbd sẽn dʋʋse. Bãmb zoetame n dat n põs zabr sẽn ya toogo. 16 Tɩ bõe, Zu-soabã yeela maam yaa: «A keta yʋʋmd a ye wa sẽn zems yʋʋmd tʋmtʋmd sẽn tʋmd n baood ligd tɩ Kedaar dãmbã ziirã fãa sɛ. 17 La Kedaar tãb-biisã sẽn ya gãnnaadã na n kella bilf bala.» Yaa Zu-soabã sẽn ya Israyɛll Wẽnnaamã n yeel t'a na n yɩɩ woto.
