Tɩrlem soolem
32
1 Dẽ, rĩmã na n dɩɩ naam ne tɩrlem, la kombɛɛmbã na n sooga soolemã ne zemsgo. 2 Bãmb ned kam fãa na n yɩɩ wa zĩ-lillidg sobg wakate, la a wa zĩ-soalgdg sa-kẽeng wakate. Bãmb na n yɩɩ wa kʋɩls tẽn-koɛɛng pʋgẽ, la a wa kug-kãseng maasem tẽng sẽn ka pãng pʋgẽ. 3 Dẽ, neb nins sẽn yãtã ka na n mum b ninã ye, la neb nins sẽn tar tʋbã na n kelgame. 4 Gom-tʋʋls rãmb na n zãmsa bãngre, la bidg na n goma sõama. 5 B ka na n le bool yalem tɩ nin-sõng ye, la b ka na n ges zɩt wa nin-kãseng ye. 6 Tɩ bõe, yalem gomda yalem-goama, n tagsd wẽng a sũur pʋgẽ n dat n maan sẽn ka zemse, la a gomd Zu-soabã yell n tudgdẽ, la a kɩtdẽ tɩ kom sẽn tar b-rãmbã gãe kom, la a mongd ko-yũud sẽn tar b-rãmbã koom. 7 Zɩt tʋʋm yaa wẽnse. Yẽ tagsdame n maand sɩlem, tɩ baa naong soab sã n tar bʋʋm, yẽ sãamda a yellã ne a zirẽ wã. 8 Nin-sõng tagsg yaa sõama, yẽ maanda tʋʋm-sõama bala.
Zeruzalɛm pagba
9 Pagb sẽn zẽ zaalem tɩ b toor kaẽ wã, yik-y-yã! Kelg-y mam koɛɛgã. Kom-pugli sẽn zẽ bãane, kelg-y mam goama. 10 Yãmb sẽn zẽ bãan masã wã, sã n ta yʋʋmd la wɛdga, yãmb na n digame. Bala, koodã na n sãamame tɩ bõn-bɩʋʋng ka na n leb n zĩnd ye. 11 Yãmb sẽn zẽ zaalem tɩ y toor kaẽ wã, bɩ y yɛɛse. Yãmb sẽn zẽ bãanã, dig-y-yã. Bɩ y piid y futã n yɩ zaala, la y sẽgen boto. 12 Bɩ y wẽ y yãoogã y pʋ-sõama wã la a nasaar sãbsã sẽn womd bi-sõama wã yĩnga, 13 la mam nebã tẽngã gõos ne mood sẽn dɩtã yĩnga, la zak ning fãa sẽn tar sũ-noog tẽng ning a sẽn maand sũ-noog a pʋgẽ wã yĩnga. 14 Tɩ bõe, b na n basa mam doogã zaalem. Tẽng ning sẽn tar neb wʋsgã na n lebga zaalem. Tãngã ne tẽng-gũudbã gãosgã na n lebga bogd wakat fãa. We-bõens na n deema be, 15 hal tɩ Wẽnnaam Sɩɩgã yi yĩngr n sig tõnd zutu, tɩ weoogã lebg pʋʋg sẽn ya sõama, tɩ b ges pʋʋg ning sẽn ya sõama wã wa tʋgre. 16 Rẽ, tɩrlem na n zĩnda weoogẽ, tɩ zemsg zĩnd zĩ-sõngẽ wã. 17 Tɩrlem tʋʋmd na n yɩɩ laafɩ, la tɩrlem tʋʋmd biis na n yɩɩ laafɩ la yẽn-maasem wakat sẽn ka sɛta. 18 Mam nebã na n zĩnda laafɩ zĩigẽ, la rot sẽn tar laafɩ pʋse, la yir sẽn tar yẽn-maasem pʋgẽ. 19 La sapɩlem na n sãama tʋgrã, la tẽngã na n lʋɩɩme n sãame. 20 Yãmb dãmb fãa sẽn kood kʋɩls noyã, n basd y niis ne y bõens n ka loetẽ wã na n paama sũ-noogo.
