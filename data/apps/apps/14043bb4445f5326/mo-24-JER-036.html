A Zozakim yõoga a Zeremi sebrã bugum
36
1 A Zoziyas biig a Zozakim sẽn lebg Zuda rĩm tɩ yʋʋm naasnẽ, Zu-soabã yeela a Zeremi yaa: 2 «Rɩk sebrã n gʋls goam nins fãa mam sẽn togs foom Israyɛll ne Zuda nebã la bu-zẽmsã me poorẽ mam sẽn sɩng n gom ne foom naab a Zoziyas zamaan hal tɩ ta rũndã wã. 3 Zuda nebã sã n wʋm toog nins fãa mam sẽn dat n kɩt t'a paam-bã, daarninga bãmb fãa na n basa b manesem wẽngã tɩ m kõ-b b yel-wẽnã ne b kongrã sugri.» 4 La a Zeremi boola a Neriza biig a Baruk n togs-a goam nins fãa Zu-soabã sẽn kõ yẽ wã t'a Baruk gʋls bãmb sebr pʋgẽ. 5 La a Zeremi yeela a Baruk yaa: «B gɩdga maam tɩ m da le kẽ Zu-soabã roogã pʋgẽ ye. 6 La bɩ f kẽng Zu-soabã roogã pʋgẽ no-loeerã raar n tɩ karem Zu-soabã goam nins mam sẽn togs foom tɩ f gʋlsã tɩ nebã fãa wʋme. Karem sebrã tɩ Zuda neb nins fãa sẽn yi b tẽns pʋsẽ n wa wã me wʋme. 7 Daarninga, bãmb na n pʋʋsa Zu-soabã n bas b manesem wẽngã. Tɩ bõe, Zu-soabã yeelame tɩ b na n sɩbga nin-kaẽns ne sũ-yikr wʋsgo.» 8 A Neriza biig a Baruk maana wa Wẽnnaam no-rɛɛs a Zeremi sẽn yeelã n karem Zu-soabã goam nins sẽn gʋls sebrã pʋgẽ wã Zu-soabã roogã pʋgẽ. 9 Zuda naab a Zoziyas biig a Zozakim sẽn deeg naam tɩ yʋʋm nuunẽ, yʋʋmdã kiuug a wɛ soabã, Zeruzalɛm nebã fãa ne neb nins fãa sẽn yi Zuda tẽnsã pʋsẽ n kẽng Zeruzalɛmmã maana no-loeer Zu-soabã taoore. 10 La a Baruk karma a Zeremi goam nins sẽn gʋls sebrã pʋgẽ wã Zu-soabã roogã pʋgẽ tɩ nebã fãa wʋme. Yẽ karma sebrã gʋlsd a Gemariya sẽn ya a Safan biigã roog sẽn be Zu-soabã zakã No-paalgã pʋgẽ wã. 11 A Safan biig a Gemariya biig a Mise sẽn wʋm Zu-soabã goam nins fãa a Baruk sẽn karem sebrã pʋgẽ wã, 12 yẽ kẽnga rĩmã gʋlsdã roog sẽn be na-yirã n tɩ mik tɩ nanamsã be be. Yaa gʋlsd a Elisama ne a Semayeza biig a Delaza la a Akoor biig a Ɩlnatan ne a Safan biig a Gemariya la a Hananiya biig a Sedesiyas ne nanams a taabã fãa. 13 La a Mise togsa bãmb goam nins fãa yẽ sẽn wʋm a Baruk sẽn karem tɩ nebã wʋmã. 14 La nanamsã fãa tʋma a Kusi biig a Selemiya biig a Netaniya biig a Zehudi t'a tɩ yeel a Baruk t'a tall sebr ning yẽ sẽn da karem tɩ nebã wʋmã n wa. La a Neriza biig a Baruk zãa sebrã n kẽng bãmb nengẽ. 15 Bãmb yeelame t'a karem sebrã tɩ b kelge. La yẽ karmame tɩ b wʋme. 16 Bãmb sẽn wʋm goamã fãa, bãmb yɛɛsame n ges taab n yeel a Baruk tɩ: «Yaa tɩlɛ tɩ d wilg rĩmã gom-kãensã fãa.» 17 Bãmb soka a Baruk yaa: «Wilg tõnd fo sẽn maan to-to n gʋls gom-kãens fãa. Yaa a Zeremi n wilg foom tɩ f gʋls laa? 18 A Baruk yeelame yaa: «Yaa a Zeremi n togs maam gom-kãensã fãa tɩ m gʋls sebrã pʋgẽ.» 19 La nanamsã yeela a Baruk tɩ yẽ ne a Zeremi kẽng n tɩ soonde, la b ra sak tɩ ned bãng b sẽn soond zĩig ning ye. 20 Nanamsã bẽnga sebrã gʋlsd a Elisama roogẽ. La bãmb kẽnga rĩmã nengẽ a zak pʋgẽ n tɩ togs goamã fãa. 21 La rĩmã tʋma a Zehudi t'a tɩ rɩk sebrã n wa. La a Zehudi tɩ rɩka sebrã gʋlsd a Elisama roogẽ n kẽng n tɩ karem tɩ rĩmã ne nanams nins sẽn yas ne rĩmã wʋme. 22 Yaa yʋʋmdã kiuug a wɛ soabã, ra yaa waood wẽnde. La rĩmã ra bee a roog pʋgẽ n woot bugum. 23 La a Zehudi sã n karem gʋlsmã ruls a tãab bɩ a naase, rĩmã rɩkda a sʋʋg n wãag a sẽn karem zĩig ningã n los bugum pʋgẽ. Yẽ maana woto hal tɩ sebrã rɩ bugum zãnga. 24 La baoosgo, rĩmã ne a tʋmtʋmdb nins sẽn be ne yẽ n wʋm gom-kãensã ka yɛɛse, wall pãrs b fut ye. 25 La baa a Ɩlnatan ne a Dalaza la a Gemariya sẽn gɩdg-a t'a ra yõog sebrã bugmã, yẽ ka kelg bãmb ye. 26 La rĩmã kõo a biig a Zerakmeyɛll ne a Azriyɛll biig a Seraza la a Abdiyɛll biig a Selemiya noor tɩ b yõk gʋlsd a Baruk ne Wẽnnaam no-rɛɛs a Zeremi. La Zu-soabã solg-b lame. 27 Rĩmã sẽn yõog sebr ning sẽn tar a Zeremi goam nins a Baruk sẽn gʋlsã zugẽ, Zu-soabã yeela a Zeremi 28 t'a rɩk sebr a to n gʋls goam nins fãa sẽn da be pipi sebrã pʋgẽ tɩ Zuda naab a Zozakim yõog bugmã. 29 Zu-soabã yeela a Zeremi t'a yeel naab a Zozakim yaa: «Yãmb yõoga sebrã bugum la y sok a Zeremi tɩ yaa bõe t'a gʋls tɩ Babilon rĩmã na n sãama tẽn-kãngã n menes ninsaalbã ne rũmsã fãa. 30 Woto yĩnga, mam yeelame tɩ Zuda naab a Zozakim biig bɩ a yageng ka na n deeg a Daviid naamã ye. Yẽ sã n ki, b na n dɩgla a kũumã wĩntoogẽ tɩ wĩntoogã wʋm-a. 31 Mam na n sɩbga yẽ ne a yagensã la a tʋmtʋmdbã b yel-wẽnã yĩnga, n kɩt tɩ tood nins fãa mam sẽn da gomã paam bãmb ne Zeruzalɛm la Zuda nebã wa mam sẽn da yeelã, bãmb sẽn ka kelgã yĩnga.» 32 A Zeremi rɩka sebr a to n kõ gʋlsd a Baruk sẽn ya a Neriza biigã t'a gʋls goamã fãa sẽn da be sebr ning Zuda naab a Zozakim sẽn yõog bugmã n paas goam wʋsg sẽn wẽnd bãmba.
