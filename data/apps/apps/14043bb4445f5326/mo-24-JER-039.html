Zeruzalɛm lʋɩɩse
= 2Rĩm 25.1-12, Zer 52.4-16 =
39
1 Zuda naab a Sedesiyas sẽn deeg naam tɩ yʋʋm wɛɛlẽ, yʋʋmdã kiuug piig soabã, Babilon naab a Nebukadnetzaar ne a tãb-biisã fãa kẽngame n tɩ gũbg Zeruzalɛm n zabe. 2 La a Sedesiyas sẽn deeg naam tɩ yʋʋm piig la a yembre, yʋʋmdã kiuug a naas soabã rasem a wɛ raare, b pooga tẽngã lalga. 3 La b sẽn deeg Zeruzalɛm, Babilon rĩmã nanamsã kẽngame n tɩ zĩnd tẽngã noor ning sẽn boond tɩ tẽn-sʋk noorã. Yaa a Nergall-Saretsɛɛr ne a Samgaar-Nebu la sõgen-kãseng a Sarsekim ne tãb-biisã na-kãseng a Nergall-Saretsaar la Babilon dĩmã nanams a taabã fãa. 4 Zuda naab a Sedesiyas ne a tãb-biisã fãa sẽn yã bãmba, b yii tẽngã noor ning sẽn be lals a yiib sʋk rĩmã zẽed zĩigẽ wã ne yʋng n zoe n kẽng Araba sẽnẽ. 5 Kalde tãb-biisã riga bãmb n tɩ yõk a Sedesiyas Zeriko bãoogã pʋgẽ n tall n kẽng Babilon naab a Nebukadnetzaar nengẽ Ribla sẽn be Hamat tẽngã pʋgẽ wã t'a bʋ a bʋʋdo. 6 Babilon rĩmã kʋʋ a Sedesiyas kambã a nifẽ Ribla la a kʋ Zuda nanamsã fãa me. 7 La a yaool n leoo a ninã ne peemde, la b balg-a ne bãense n tall n kẽng Babilonne. 8 Kalde nebã yõoga na-yirã nebã rot bugum la b wã lals nins sẽn gũbg Zeruzalɛmmã. 9 La b gũudbã naab a Nebuzaradan wʋka neb nins sẽn ket tẽngã pʋgẽ wã ne neb nins sẽn da yi n tɩ sak Babilon rĩmã la neb a taabã sẽn ketã n kɩt tɩ b tall Babilonne. 10 La gũudbã naab a Nebuzaradan basa naong rãmb kẽer sẽn ka tar baa fɩ tɩ b pa Zuda tẽngã pʋgẽ. La a kõo bãmb nasaar-sãbs ne pʋto. 11 Babilon naab a Nebukadnetzaar kõo gũubdã naab a Nebuzaradan noor ne a Zeremi yell n yeele: 12 «Tall-a n ges a yell neere. Ra sak n maan-a wẽng ye. Maan a sẽn dat bũmb ninsã n kõ-a.» 13 La gũudbã naab a Nebuzaradan ne sõgen-kãseng a Nebusasbã la tãb-biisã na-kãseng a Nergall-Saretsɛɛr ne Babilon rĩmã nanamsã fãa 14 tʋma neb tɩ b tɩ yiis a Zeremi gũudbã zak pʋgẽ n wa. La bãmb dɩk-a lame n kɩs a Safan biig a Akikam biig a Gedaliya t'a yãag-a t'a kuili. La woto, yẽ zĩnda nebã sʋka. 15 A Zeremi sẽn da be gũudbã zakã pʋgẽ, Zu-soabã yeelame 16 t'a kẽng n tɩ yeel Etiyopi rao a Ebɛd-Melɛk tɩ Saasẽ-pãng-soabã sẽn ya Israyɛll Wẽnnaam yeelame yaa: «Gese, mam na n pidsa bũmb nins mam sẽn da gom tẽn-kãngã poorẽ wã tɩ yɩ sãoongo, ka na n yɩ noog ye. La a sã n wa, fo na n yã-a lame. 17 Baraare, mam na n fãaga foom tɩ b ra bas foom ne neb nins fo sẽn zoeta ye. Mam yaa Zu-soaba, yaa maam n yeele. 18 Tɩ bõe, mam na n fãaga foom. B ka na n kʋ foom ye. Fo na n paama f vɩɩm fo sẽn teeg maamã yĩnga. Mam yaa Zu-soaba, yaa maam n yeele.»
