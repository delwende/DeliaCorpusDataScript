Toog na n paama Farawõ ne a nebã
31
1 Yʋʋm piig la a yembrã yʋʋmd kiuug a tãab soabã pipi raare, Zu-soabã goma ne maam n yeel yaa: 2 «Ninsaal biiga, yeel Ezipt naaba Farawõ ne a neb wʋsgã yaa: fo yaa kãsengã, la m na n yeelame tɩ f wẽnda ãnna? 3 Gese, fo yaa wa Libã sɛɛdr tɩɩg sẽn tar wil-neeb ne maasem n ya wok wʋsg t'a zug ta saasẽ. 4 Kʋɩlg sẽn be tẽngã pʋgẽ kõt-a-la koom t'a bɩ n lebg woko, la ko-zoetem n gũbg a zĩigã ta kʋɩl-koakã koom tat weoogã tɩɩs fãa. 5 Yaa woto n kɩt t'a lebg wok n yɩɩd weoogã tɩɩs fãa, la a wilã yaa wʋsg n ya wogdo, koomã sẽn ya wʋsgã yĩnga. 6 Bõn-yɩgdɩ buud fãa tʋɩ b tʋgd a wilã zutu. La we-rũms buud fãa roga kamb a tɩɩgã tẽngre. Dũniyã buud wʋsg zĩnda a maasmẽ. 7 Tɩɩgã yaa kãsenga, la a yaa neere, la a wilã yaa wogdo. Bala, a kɩɩ yẽg hal n ta koom wʋsg pʋgẽ. 8 Wẽnnaam zẽed zĩigẽ sɛɛdr rãmbã ka yɩɩd-a ye. La sɩprɛ ne a palatan tɩɩsã wil woglem ka yɩɩd tɩɩgã woglem ye. Tɩɩg ka be Wẽnnaam zẽedã zĩigẽ n wẽnd-a ye. 9 Mam maan-a-la ne a wilã sẽn tẽeg wʋsgã t'a yɩ neere. La Edɛn tɩɩsã fãa sẽn be Wẽnnaam zẽedã zĩigẽ wã maana sũ-kĩir ne-a. 10 Mam yaa Zu-soaba Wẽnnaam, la m yeelame tɩ tɩɩgã wuka a menga, a sẽn bɩ n lebg wok hal ta zug ta sawatã yĩnga. 11 Woto yĩnga, mam kisg-a lame n bas-a ne bu-zẽmsã tɩ b maan-a wa sẽn zems a yel-wẽnã. 12 Sãamb sẽn ya nin-toos n yɩɩd buudã fãa wã kɛ-a lame n bas-a be t'a wilã kao n lʋɩ tãens zutu, ne lond pʋsẽ tẽngã zĩigã fãa. La buud nins sẽn da zẽ a maasmẽ wã yii be n bas-a. 13 Bõn-yɩgdɩ waa n zaonda tɩɩg ning sẽn lʋɩ wã zugu la we-rũms me taba a wilã. 14 A yɩɩ woto tɩ tɩɩs nins sẽn pẽ koomã ra le bɩ n lebg wogd woto tɩ b zut ta sawat ye. Tɩ bõe, bãmb fãa na n kiime n naag neb a taab n sig n kẽng kɩɩm-kulg pʋgẽ. 15 Zu-soaba Wẽnnaam yeelame yaa: «A sẽn sig n kẽng kɩɩm-kulgã, mam kɩtame tɩ zĩ-zulums koom lim-a n maan sũ-sãoongo. Mam gɩdga kʋɩls koom n kɩt tɩ ko-zoetem wʋsg yaase. La mam kɩtame tɩ lik zĩnd Libã, la weoogã tɩɩs fãa zelga a yĩnga. 16 Mam sẽn lob-a kɩɩm-kulg t'a naag ne sẽn sig-b bokẽ wã, a lʋɩɩsã bʋrg kɩtame tɩ buud wʋsg rigi. La Edɛn tɩɩsã ne Libã tɩ-neebã sẽn paam koom wʋsgã paama belsg tẽng ning sẽn be dũniyã tẽngrã pʋgẽ. Bãmb me tũ-a lame n sig n naag neb nins sẽn pa tãp pʋgẽ wã kɩɩm-kulgu. 17 La a sõangdbã sẽn da zẽ a maasmẽ wã sãeegame n kẽng buud a taabã sʋka. 18 Yaa ãnna n zem foom Edɛn tɩɩsã sʋka? Ãnna ziir n ya kãseng wa foom? La baoosgo, fo na n naaga Edɛn tɩɩsã n sig dũniyã zĩ-zulums pʋsẽ n gãand ne neb nins sẽn pa tãp pʋgẽ wã la sẽn da ka kẽ-b bãongã sʋka.
Ad Farawõ ne a neb wʋsgã. Mam yaa Zu-soaba, yaa maam n yeele.»
