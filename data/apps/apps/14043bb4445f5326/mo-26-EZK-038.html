Bãngr goam Goog yĩnga
38
1 Zu-soabã goma ne maam n yeel yaa: 2 «Ninsaal biiga, tees Magoog Goog sẽnẽ n togs Ros ne Mesɛk la Tuball naabã bãngr goam 3 n yeel tɩ m yaa Zu-soaba Wẽnnaam, la m yeelame yaa: Googe, fo yaa Ros ne Mesɛk la Tuball naaba, la m kisga foom. 4 Mam na n kɩtame tɩ f wẽnemd tɩ m ning f noorã bãens n yiis foom ne f tãb-biisã la f wed-rʋʋdbã fãa sẽn ya kʋʋn-kãseng n tar zab fut la b zãad gãnd ne sʋʋsã. 5 Pɛrs ne Etiyopi la Put neb bee ne bãmba, la b fãa tara gãnd ne kut pugli. 6 Gomeer tãb-biisã fãa ne Bɛt-Togarma tãb-biisã fãa sẽn yi rɩtg zĩ-zãrsẽ wã bee ne foom n ya neb buud wʋsgo. 7 Segle. Bɩ foom ne neb kʋʋng-kʋʋng nins sẽn tigim f nengẽ n gubg foomã segl tɩ f yɩ b naaba. 8 La yʋʋm wʋsg sã n looge, fo na n paama noor n lʋɩ bãmb taoor n kẽng tẽng ning b sẽn da maneg tẽn-kũumã zugẽ wã n tɩ zabe. Tẽngã nebã ra yii buud wʋsg tẽnsẽ n tɩ tigim be, Israyɛll tãensã sẽn da ya zĩ-vɩɩd yʋʋm wʋsgã zutu. La masã, bãmb fãa zĩi tẽngã pʋgẽ ne laafɩ. 9 Foom ne f kʋʋngã fãa la buud wʋsg nins sẽn be ne foomã na n kẽnga be wa sob-kãseng n tɩ pid tẽngã.» 10 Zu-soaba Wẽnnaam yeela Goog yaa: «Baraare, fo na n paama tags-wẽns n sɩf wẽng 11 n yeel tɩ f na n kẽnga tẽng ning neb sẽn zẽ ne laafɩ tẽns sẽn ka tar lals ne kʋɩlensã pʋsẽ n tɩ zab ne-ba. 12 La f na n yeelame tɩ f na n kẽng n tɩ fãa neb nins sẽn be tẽns sẽn da sãamã pʋsẽ n tɩ wʋk b paoongã. Yaa neb nins sẽn da yi tẽns a taab pʋgẽ n tigim dũniyã tẽn-sʋkã n le zẽ be n tar niig ne paoongo. 13 Sɛba ne Dedan nebã la Tarsis tẽnsã toandbã fãa na n soka foom yaa: «Fo waame n na n wʋk teedã laa? Fo tigsa f tãb-biisã tɩ b tɩ wʋk wanzuri ne sãnemã la niigã ne teedã la b fã nebã n deeg b paoon-kãsengã laa? 14 Woto, ninsaal biiga, togs Goog tɩ m yaa Zu-soaba Wẽnnaam, la m yeelame yaa: Baraare, m Israyɛll nebã sẽn zẽ ne laafɩ be wã, 15 fo na n yikame n yi f zĩigẽ hal rɩtg zĩ-zãrsẽ wã n wa. Na n yɩɩ foom ne buud wʋsgo. Bãmb fãa na n zomba wiid n yɩ tãb-bi-kʋʋng-kʋʋng tɩ ned ka tõe n sõd-b n sɛ ye. 16 Googe, fo nebã na n waame n wa pid tẽngã n zab ne Israyɛll nebã. Yaoolem wakate, mam na n kɩtame tɩ f wa zab ne m tẽngã tɩ buud wʋsg bãng mam sẽn ya-a soaba. Yaa ne foom la m na n wilg bãmb m sõmlemã.» 17 Zu-soaba Wẽnnaam yeela Goog yaa: «Yaa f yel-wẽnã yell la m da gom ne m tʋmtʋmdb nins sẽn ya Israyɛll nebã bãngdbã kʋdemd wakatã. Baraare, bãmb da pẽnd n togsa yʋʋm wʋsg tɩ m na n kɩtame tɩ f wa zab ne-ba. 18 La Goog sã n wa n na n zab ne Israyɛll nebã b tẽngã pʋgẽ, baraare, mam sũur na n yikame n puugi. Mam yaa Zu-soaba Wẽnnaam, yaa maam n yeele. 19 Tɩ bõe, mam yeela m sũ-yikrã sẽn wɩng wa bugmã pʋgẽ tɩ wakat kãnga, tẽn-digimd-kãseng na n zĩnda Israyɛll tẽngã pʋgẽ. 20 La zẽm sẽn be ko-kãsengã pʋgẽ ne bõn-yɩgdɩ la we-rũms ne bõn-kẽnds fãa sẽn kẽnd tẽngã zugã ne ninsaalbã fãa sẽn be dũniyã zugã na n diga maam taoore. La tãm-bõonegã ne tãm-bɛdã ne lalsã fãa na n lʋɩɩme. 21 Mam na n kɩtame tɩ toog buud fãa yɛɛs Goog t'a nebã fãa kʋ taaba. 22 Mam na n sɩbg-a-la ne bã-loangdg la kʋʋbo, la m kɩt tɩ sa-kẽeng ne sapɩlem la bugum ne kidibr ni yẽ ne a tãb-biisã la buud wʋsg nins sẽn be ne yẽ wã zutu. 23 La woto, mam na n wilgame tɩ m yaa kãsenga, la m yaa sõngo, la m na n kɩtame tɩ buud wʋsg bãng mam sẽn ya-a soaba. B na n bãngame tɩ mam yaa Zu-soaba.»
