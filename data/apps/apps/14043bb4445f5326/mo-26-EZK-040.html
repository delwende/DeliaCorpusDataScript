Wẽnd-do-paallã zĩiga
40
1 Tõnd sẽn be yembdã pʋgẽ yʋʋm pissi la a nu wã yʋʋm vẽkr kiuugã rasem piig raare, rẽ tɩ b sãama Zeruzalɛm tɩ yʋʋm piig la a naase. La Zu-soabã pãng waa mam zug n tall maam n kẽng be. 2 Wẽnnaam talla maam bõn-makr pʋgẽ n kẽng Israyɛll tẽng n tɩ sik maam tãn-kãseng zugu. La a wẽnda wa tẽng n tilg a zug t'a rotã teesd maam. 3 La Wẽnnaam sẽn tall maam n tɩ sik be wã, mam yãa rao sẽn pɩnd wa zũudu, n tar gĩsg ne kãnkãr metr a nugẽ n yas tẽngã noore. 4 La a yeela maam yaa: «Ninsaal biiga, ges neere la f kelg sõama la f tags bũmb nins fãa mam sẽn na n wilg foomã yelle. Tɩ bala, b talla foom n wa ka tɩ m wilg-f bõn-kãense. Togs Israyɛll nebã bũmb nins fo sẽn yã wã.»
Yaang rig-noorã ne yɩng zakã
5 Gese, mam yãa Wẽnnaam doogã tɩ lalg gũbg-a. La raoã rɩka kãnkãr sẽn tar kan-tɩ-wogd a yoob n mak lalgã. A taglem yaa kantɩɩg a yoobe, la a zãntelmã me yaa kantɩɩg a yoobe. 6 La yẽ kẽnga a noor ning sẽn teesd yaangã n dʋ a rʋʋdgã n mak a no-koakã. La a taglem yaa kantɩɩg a yoobe. 7 La a noorã goabg ne a rɩtg la lalsã tara gũudb rot a tãab-tãabo. Bãmb woglem yaa kantɩɩg a yoob-yoobe. La lals nins sẽn be b sʋkã taglem yaa kantɩɩg a nu-nu. La noor ning sẽn be hal pʋgẽ n teesd Wẽnnaam doogã taglem yaa kantɩɩg a yoobe. 8 La yẽ maka zaongã sẽn be hal pʋgẽ wã n mik 9 t'a yaa kantɩɩg a nii, la a lalgã taglem yaa kantɩɩg a yiibu. 10 Gũudbã rot nins sẽn be zakã noor goabgã yaa a tãabo, la sẽn be rɩtgã me yaa a tãabo. La bãmb fãa woglem ne b yalem zema taaba, la b lalsã me taglem zema taaba. 11 Yẽ maka zãoangã yalem n mik t'a yaa kantɩɩg piiga, la a woglmã yaa kantɩɩg piig la a tãabo. 12 Koag n be gũudbã rot nins sẽn be pʋgẽ kɩrems a yiibã noyẽ. A zãntlem yaa kantɩɩg a ye-ye, la koagã yalem me yaa kantɩɩg a ye-ye. La roog a ye fãa woglem ne a yalem yaa kantɩɩg a yoob-yoobe. 13 Yẽ maka gũudbã rot a yiibã sẽn tees taabã n mik tɩ roog a ye pʋgẽ lalgã tɩ ta roog ning sẽn tees-a wã pʋgẽ lalgã yaa kantɩɩg pissi la a nu-nu. 14 Yẽ maka zãongã n mik t'a yaa kantɩɩg pissi. La zakã noor kɩremsã fãa bee zakã pʋgẽ. 15 Yɩng zakã noor tɩ ta zãongã noor yaa kantɩɩg pis-nu. 16 Zakã noor gũudbã rot nins sẽn be kɩremsã tara peem-leese, la lals nins sẽn be rotã pʋsã me tara peem-leese, la zãongã me tara peem-lees a kɩremsã fãa. La b gʋla koms a lalsã zutu.
Yɩng zakã
17 La raoã kɩtame tɩ m tũ noorã n kẽ pipi zakã pʋgẽ. Rot pis-tã n gũbg zakã, b maana zĩig zakã pʋgẽ ne kug n yeg n gũbg zakã pʋgẽ 18 n gilg zakã pʋgẽ noyã zĩigã fãa t'a yalem zems zakã noy woglem. La pipi zag-kãng zãntlem ka ta zak a yiib soabã sẽn be a pʋgẽ wã ye. 19 La yẽ maka sẽn sɩng pipi zakã noor tɩ ta zak ning sẽn be pʋgẽ wã zãongã n mik t'a yaa kantɩɩg koabga.
Rɩtg rig-noorã
20 Yẽ lʋɩɩ mam taoor n kẽng zakã noor ning sẽn teesd rɩtg n tũud n kẽed pipi zakã pʋgẽ wã. La yẽ maka a woglmã ne a yalmã. 21 Gũudb rot n be zakã noor pʋgẽ, a tãab bee kɩreng a yembr t'a tãab me be kɩreng a to. Bãmb ne b lalsã la b zãongã yaa wa pipi zakã noorã. A woglmã yaa kantɩɩg pis-nu, la a yalmã yaa kantɩɩg pissi la a nu. 22 A peem-leesã ne a zãongã la a komsã zema noor ning sẽn teesd yaangã rẽndã. A tara rʋʋds a yopoe, la a zãongã teesda zakã pʋgẽ. 23 La pʋgẽ zak a yiib soabã noor a yembr teesda noor ning sẽn teesd rɩtgã. La bãmb zãra ne taab kantɩɩg koabga.
Goabg rig-noorã
24 La yẽ kɩtame tɩ m kẽng n tɩ ges zakã noor ning sẽn be goabgã. Yẽ maka a rotã ne a lalsã la a zãongã n mik tɩ b zema taaba. 25 Zakã no-kãng rotã me tara peem-lees wa a taabã sẽn tarã. A woglem yaa kantɩɩg pis-nu la a yalem yaa kantɩɩg pissi la a nu. 26 Rʋʋds a yopoe n be a noore. La a zãongã bee zakã pʋgẽ n tar koms gʋlb a lalsã kɩrems fãa. 27 La pʋgẽ zakã me tara noor sẽn teesd goabga. La raoã maka zĩig ning sẽn be yẽ ne zakã noor a to wã sʋkã n mik t'a yaa kantɩɩg koabga.
Pʋgẽ zakã noya
28 Yẽ peega maam n kẽng pʋgẽ zakã n kẽ zakã noor ning sẽn teesd goabgã. La yẽ maka zakã noore, la a yaa wa noy a taabã. 29 A rotã ne a lalsã la a zãongã yaa wa zakã noy a taabã. A rotã fãa tara peem-leese. 30 A woglmã yaa kantɩɩg pis-nu la a zãongã woglem yaa kantɩɩg pissi la a nu, la a yalmã yaa kantɩɩg a nu. 31 A zãongã teesa pipi zakã pʋgẽ. A pʋgẽ lalsã tara koms gʋlbo. La rʋʋds a nii n dabd a noore. 32 La yẽ le peega maam n kẽng zakã pʋgẽ zakã noor ning sẽn teesd yaangã. La yẽ maka zakã noore, la a yaa wa noy a taabã. 33 A rotã ne a lalsã la a zãongã wẽnda zakã noy a taabã. A rotã fãa tara peem-leese. A woglmã yaa kantɩɩg pis-nu la a yalmã yaa kantɩɩg pissi la a nu. 34 A zãongã teesa pipi zakã pʋgẽ. A pʋgẽ lalsã kɩremsã fãa tara koms gʋlbo. La rʋʋds a nii n dabd a noorẽ wã. 35 La a le peega maam n kẽng zakã noor ning sẽn teesd rɩtgã n tɩ mak-a. La a yaa wa zakã noy a taabã. 36 A rotã ne a lalsã la a zãongã yaa wa zakã noy a taabã rẽnda. A rotã fãa tara peem-leese. A woglmã yaa kantɩɩg pis-nu, la a yalmã yaa kantɩɩg pissi la a nu. 37 A zãongã teesa pipi zakã pʋgẽ. A pʋgẽ zakã lalsã kɩremsã fãa tara koms gʋlbo. La rʋʋds a nii n dabd a noorẽ wã.
Maand seglg roto
38 La roogã yega pʋgẽ zakã noor zãongã t'a noorã kẽed be. B pekda maand nins sẽn na n yõog bugmã be. 39 La taabl-dãmb a yiib n be zakã noor zãondã pʋsẽ kɩrems a yiibã fãa. Yaa be la b kʋʋd maand nins sẽn na n yõog bugmã ne kongr la paoosg maando. 40 La taabl-dãmb a naas n be yɩng me ziig ning rʋʋdgã sẽn dabd zakã noor zãongã sẽn be wã. A yiib n be rʋʋdgã kɩreng a yembr t'a yiib me be kɩreng a to. 41 Taabl-dãmb a naas n be zãongã pʋgẽ t'a naas me be yɩnga. Yaa taabl-dãmb a nii tɩ b kʋʋd maand b zutu. 42 La taabl-dãmb a naas me la b maan ne kug maand nins sẽn yõogd bugmã yĩnga. Bãmb woglem ne b yalem yaa kantɩɩg la pʋɩ-sʋk-sʋka, la b zãntlemã yaa kantɩɩg a ye-ye. Yaa be la b rogend tʋʋm teed nins sẽn tar n maand maandã. 43 Kogr sẽn zems nug-yakemd n gilg taabl-dãmbã kɩremse. La b rogenda maand nemd taabl-kãens zutu.
Rot maan-kʋʋdbã yĩnga
44 La rot a yiib n be pʋgẽ zakã. A yembr bee zakã noor ning sẽn teesd rɩtgã goabga, la a to n be zakã noor ning sẽn teesd goabgã rɩtgo. 45 La raoã yeela maam tɩ roog ning sẽn teesd goabgã yaa maan-kʋʋdb nins sẽn gũud Wẽnnaam doogã yĩnga. 46 La roog ning sẽn teesd rɩtgã yaa maan-kʋʋdb nins sẽn tʋmd tẽng-kugrã tʋʋmdã yĩnga. Nin-kãens fãa yaa a Tesadok yagense. Yaa bãmb bal Levi buudã sʋk n tar noor tɩ kolg Zu-soabã n maan bãmb tʋʋmde. 47 La yẽ maka zakã. A woglem ne a yalem yaa kantɩɩg koabg-koabga, a yaa maoko. La tẽng-kugr n da be Wẽnnaam doogã noore.
Wẽnd-doogã
48 La yẽ peega maam n kẽng Wẽnnaam doogã noore. La yẽ peega maam n kẽng Wẽnnaam doogã zãongã pʋgẽ n mak-a. A kɩrems a yiibã fãa taglem yaa kantɩɩg a nu-nu. A noorã yalem yaa kantɩɩg piig la a naase, la a rig-noorã kɩrems a yiibã fãa lalgã yaa kantɩɩg a tãab-tãabo. 49 Zãongã woglem yaa kantɩɩg pissi, la a yalem yaa kantɩɩg piig la a yembre. Rʋʋds piig n dabd a noorẽ wã. La a rig-noorã kɩrems tara ra-lugdg a ye-ye.
