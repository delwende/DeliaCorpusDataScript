Rot maan-kʋʋdbã yĩnga
42
1 La yẽ peega maam n kẽng rɩtgo, pipi zakã pʋgẽ n ta roog ning sẽn be Wẽnnaam doogã rɩtgo n teesd pʋgẽ zakã, la a pẽe ne roog ning sẽn be Wẽnnaam doogã poorã. 2 Roog ning sẽn be Wẽnnaam doogã goabg zakã pʋgẽ wã woglem yaa kantɩɩg koabga t'a yalma ya kantɩɩg pis-nu. 3 A teesda Wẽnnaam doogã ne zakã pʋgẽ zĩ-vɩʋʋg ning sẽn be b sukã. La kɩreng a to n teesd zĩig ning sẽn maneg ne kug pipi zakã pʋgẽ wã. Ro-kãng welga zĩis a tãab n tom taab zutu, la a ye fãa tara zĩ-vɩʋʋgo. 4 La vɩʋʋg n be roogã poor a woglmã tɛka. A yalem yaa kantɩɩg piiga t'a woglem ya kantɩɩg koabga. La roogã pʋsẽ noyã tees-a lame. 5 La yĩngr dotã yalem ka ta sẽn be-b tẽngrã ye, tɩ bõe, bãmb vɩʋʋgã yaa yaleng n yɩɩda. Tɩ bõe, bãmb welga zĩis a tãabo n tom taab zutu. 6 La bãmb ka tar da-lugds wa zakã pʋgẽ dot a taaba ye. Yaa woto n kɩt tɩ rot nins sẽn be b yĩngrã yalem ka ta sẽn beb tẽngrã ye. 7 La yɩng lalgã teesda roogã pʋsẽ noyã kɩreng a yembre, la a kɩreng a to wã teesda pipi zakã pʋgẽ wã woglem yaa kantɩɩg pisnu. 8 Tɩ bõe, roog ning sẽn be pipi zakã pʋgẽ wã woglem yaa kantɩɩg pisnu, la sẽn teesd Wẽnnaam doogã yaa kantɩɩg koabga. 9 La roogã noor teesda yaang n pẽ noor ning sẽn sigd pipi zakã pʋgẽ n kẽed pʋgẽ zakã. 10 La roog sẽn tar pʋs wʋsg n be goabg lalgã sɛɛg me n teesd zakã pʋgẽ vɩʋʋg ne Wẽnnaam doogã. 11 La vɩʋʋg n be a kɩremsã. A pʋsa ne a woglmã la a yalma la a noyã zĩis fãa zemsa roog ning sẽn be zakã rɩtgã. 12 Roogã pʋsẽ noyã teesa goabga. La b tũuda vɩʋʋgã noor sẽn teesd yaang n pẽ ne zakã lalgã n kẽed roogã pʋgẽ. 13 La raoã yeela maam tɩ rot nins sẽn be zakã rɩtg la a goabg n teesd zakã pʋgẽ wã welga toore, tɩ yaa bãmb pʋsẽ la maan-kʋʋdb nins sẽn kolgd Zu-soabã tog n dɩ maandã nemdo. Bãmb zĩigã welga toore. Woto yĩnga, bɩ b bẽng zom ning neb sẽn kõ Wẽnnaam ne yel-wẽnã la kongr maandã be. 14 Maan-kʋʋdbã sã n kẽ zĩ-sõngã pʋg n tʋme, bɩ b ra yi be n kẽ pipi zakã pʋgẽ tɩ b ka reng n yees fut nins bãmb sẽn yer n tʋmdẽ wã n bas ro-kãens pʋsẽ ye. Tɩ bala, fu-bãmb welga toore. Bɩ b yeelg fut a taab n yaool n kolg nebã. 15 Yẽ sẽn mak Wẽnnaam zakã pʋgẽ n sɛ, yẽ peega maam n tũ zakã noor ning sẽn teesd yaangã n yi yɩnga. La a gilga zakã n mak-a. 16 Yẽ rɩka kãnkãrã n mak zĩig ning sẽn teesd yaangã n mik t'a tara kantɩɩg kobs-nu. 17 La yẽ gilga zakã n mak kɩreng ning sẽn teesd rɩtgã ne kãnkãrã n mik t'a woglem yaa kantɩɩg kobs nu. 18 Yẽ maka kɩreng ning sẽn teesd-a goabgã ne kãnkãrã n mik t'a woglem yaa kantɩɩg kobs nu. 19 La a maka kɩreng ning sẽn teesd-a nin-taoorã me ne kãnkãrã n mik t'a woglem yaa kantɩɩg kobs-nu. 20 Yẽ maka kɩrems a naasã fãa. Lalg n gũbg-a. Lalgã woglem yaa kantɩɩg kobs-nu, la a yalmã me yaa kantɩɩg kobs-nu. Lalgã welga bũmb nins sẽn welg toor Wẽnnaam yĩngã ne bõn-zaalsã.
