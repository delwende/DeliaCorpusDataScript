Wẽnnaam doogã tʋʋmde
44
1 Raoã peega maam n kẽng sɛk zĩigã pipi zakã noor ning sẽn teesd yaangã, la noorã pagame. 2 La Zu-soabã yeela maam yaa: «Zakã no-kãng na n paga daar fãa, b ka na n pak-a ye. La ned ka na n tũ be n kẽ zakã pʋgẽ ye. Mam yaa Israyɛll nebã Zu-soaba Wẽnnaam, la mam tũu be n kẽ. Woto, a na n kel n paga wakat fãa. 3 Yaa rĩmã bal n tõe n zĩnd pʋgẽ n dɩ rɩɩb Zu-soabã taoore. Yẽ na n tũu zakã noor zãongã n kẽ la a tũ be n yi.» 4 La raoã peega maam n tũ zakã noor ning sẽn teesd rɩtgã n kẽ zakã pʋgẽ Wẽnnaam doogã noore. La mam yãame tɩ Zu-soabã ziir pida roogã. La m fõgna m neng tẽnga. 5 La Zu-soabã yeela maam yaa: «Ninsaal biiga, tags neere, la f gese, la f kelg bũmb nins fãa mam sẽn na n wilg foom ne m doogã yell sẽn ya to-to la ne a tõodã. La f gũus neer ne neb nins sẽn kẽed roogẽ wã. 6 La f yeel Israyɛll nebã sẽn ya nin-tõtsã tɩ m yeelame yaa: Israyɛll neba, bas-y y yel-wenã fãa. 7 Yãmb kɩtame tɩ sãamb sẽn ka kẽ bãongo la sẽn ka sakd maam kẽ m sɛk zĩigã pʋgẽ n sãam-a wakat ning y sẽn talld maand kaam ne zɩɩm n wat n kõt maamã. 8 Yãmb maana y yel-wẽnã fãa n sãam m kaoolã. Yãmb ka ges bũmb nins sẽn welg toor mam yĩng m sɛk zĩigã pʋgẽ wã yell ye, yãmb basame tɩ sãamb maane. 9 Mam yaa Zu-soaba Wẽnnaam, la m yeelame tɩ sãamb bɩ neb sẽn ka sakd maam sã n be m Israyɛll nebã sʋk bɩ b ra kẽ m sɛk zĩigã pʋgẽ ye. 10 La Levi neb nins sẽn zãag n bas maam n naag ne Israyɛll neb a taab n waoog b bõn-naandsã na n paama sɩbgre. 11 Bãmb na n yɩɩ tʋmtʋmdb m zakã pʋgẽ n gũ zakã noyã, la b maan m doogã tʋʋmde, la b kʋ maand sẽn yõogd bugum la nebã maand a taabã, la b yaas be n sõng nebã. 12 Tɩ bõe, yaa bãmb n da be nebã taoor n waoogd b bõn-naands n kɩt tɩ Israyɛll nebã maan yel-wẽnã. Yaa woto n kɩt tɩ mam wẽen tɩ b na n paama sɩbgre. Mam yaa Zu-soaba, yaa maam n yeele. 13 Bãmb ka na n le kolg maam n maan maan-kʋʋr tʋʋmd bɩ m bũmb nins sẽn welg toorã, wall kẽ zĩ-sõng n yɩɩdã pʋgẽ ye. Yãnd na n yõk-b lame tɩ b paam sɩbgr b yel-wẽnã yĩnga. 14 La mam na n kɩtame tɩ m roogã tʋʋmdã fãa zĩnd b zut tɩ b maan-a. 15 La yaa Levi maan-kʋʋdb nins sẽn ya a Tesadok yagensã n da get m doogã yell wakat ning Israyɛll neb a taabã sẽn da bas maamã. Yaa bãmb n na n kolg n waoog maam la b maan m tʋʋmdã la b yaas m taoore, la b tall maand kaam ne zɩɩm n kolg n kõ maam. Mam yaa Zu-soaba, yaa maam n yeele. 16 Bãmb na n kẽe m zĩ-sõngã pʋgẽ n kolg m taablã n waoog maam la b maan bũmb nins mam sẽn kõ bãmb tɩ b maanã. 17 Bãmb sã n kẽ zakã pʋgẽ noyã, bɩ b yeelg maan-kʋʋr futu. B sã n tʋmd m zakã pʋgẽ zakã noyã wall m doogã pʋgẽ, bɩ b ra yeelg fut sẽn wʋg ne pe-kõbd ye. 18 Bɩ b pil b zut ne fut sẽn wʋg ne lamd gẽssi, bɩ b ra yeelg fut sẽn ya tʋʋlg ye. 19 La b sã n yi be n kẽng nebã sʋk pipi zakã pʋgẽ, bɩ b yees b fut nins b sẽn yer n tʋmdẽ wã n bas rot nins b sẽn welg toorã pʋsẽ la b yeelg fut a taab tɩ b futã ra wa kɩt tɩ nebã lebg bũmb sẽn welg toor ye. 20 Bɩ b ra põng b zutu la b ra bas b zoobd tɩ yɩ wogd wʋsg me ye, bɩ b wõrs-a tɩ zem bala. 21 Maan-kʋʋdb sã n dat n kẽ pʋgẽ zakã pʋgẽ, bɩ b ra yũ rãam ye. 22 Bɩ maan-kʋʋd ra peeg pʋg-kõor wall pag a sɩd sẽn dig ye. Bɩ a peeg Israyɛll bi-pugla wall maan-kʋʋd sẽn ki n bas a paga. 23 Bɩ b zãms m nebã tɩ b bãng bũmb sẽn welg toor mam yĩng ne bõn-zaals n bake. La b wilg bãmb tɩ b bãng bũmb sẽn ya yɩlemd ne bõn-dẽgd sẽn ya to-to. 24 Neb sã n tar bʋʋd ne taaba, maan-kʋʋdbã n ya bʋ-kaoodba. Bɩ b kao bʋʋdã wa sẽn zems m tõodã. Bɩ b maan kibs n waoog maam wa sẽn zems m tõodã ne m minundã, la b sak n welg vʋʋsg rayã toore. 25 Bɩ maan-kʋʋdbã ra kolg kũum n dẽgem b mens ye. La baoosgo, sã n ya b ba bɩ b ma bɩ b biig wall b ba-bi-raoog bɩ b tão sẽn ka kʋɩl sɩda, bãmb tõee n dẽgma b mense. 26 La bãmb sã n maneg b mense, bɩ b yaas rasem a yopoe. 27 La raar ning yẽ sẽn na n le kẽ zakã pʋgẽ zakã n maan a tʋʋmd zĩ-sõngã pʋgẽ wã, bɩ a maan maoong a kongrã yĩnga. Mam yaa Zu-soaba, yaa maam n yeele. 28 Yaa maam n ya maan-kʋʋdbã pʋɩɩre. Bɩ y ra kõ bãmb zĩig tɩ b soog Israyɛll tẽngã pʋgẽ ye. Yaa maam la bãmb pʋɩɩre. 29 Bãmb na n dɩɩ zom ne kongr maand la paoosg maando. Bũmb nins fãa sẽn welg toor mam yĩng Israyɛll pʋgẽ wã na n yɩɩ bãmb dẽnda. 30 Kood buud fãa dẽenem biis ne maand nins fãa b sẽn zẽk n yuumã yaa maan-kʋʋdbã n so. Yãk-y y zom-nagdgã me n kõ maan-kʋʋdbã tɩ m barkã zĩnd y yiya. 31 Bɩ maan-kʋʋdbã ra rɩ bõn-yɩgdɩ bɩ rũng sẽn ki a toore wall rũng a to sẽn kʋ nemd ye.»
