Kʋɩlg sẽn yit Wẽnnaam doogẽ
47
1 Raoã peega maam n le kẽng Wẽnnaam doogã noore. La mam yãame tɩ koom yit rigno-koakã tẽngr n zoet n dabd yaanga. Wẽnnaam doogã teesda yaanga. Koomã siga roogã sɛɛg n zoe n loog tẽng-kugrã rɩtgo. 2 La yẽ kɩtame tɩ m tũ zakã noor ning sẽn teesd rɩtgã n yi yɩng n gilg zakã n ta noor ning sẽn teesd yaangã. La koomã tũ a rɩtg n yi be. 3 La raoã zãa wĩir ne a nug n kẽng yaang n tɩ mak kantɩɩg tusri. La a kɩtame tɩ m dʋʋg koomã t'a ta m nao-sʋgdsi. 4 La a le maka kantɩɩg tusr yɛsa, n kɩt tɩ m le rʋʋg koomã tɩ ta m dũma. La a le maka kantɩɩg tusr yɛsa, n kɩt tɩ m dʋʋg tɩ ta m sɛɛga. 5 La a le maka kantɩɩg tusri n paase, la a lebga kʋɩlg tɩ ned ka tõe n dʋʋg-a ye. Koomã yaa zulung n sek rugbu. 6 La a soka maam yaa: «Ninsaal biiga, fo neeme bii?» La a peega maam n tũ kʋɩl-koakã. 7 La mam sẽn lebg n lebe, mam yãa tɩɩs wʋsg kʋɩlgã noy a yiibã fãa. 8 La a yeela maam yaa: «Ko-kãngã tũuda Zʋrdẽ longã n dabda tẽng sẽn be yaanga. La a sã n ta yamsem mogrã, mogrã na n lebga sãbsgo. 9 La zĩig ning fãa kʋɩlgã sẽn tũudã, bõn-vɩɩlɩ wã fãa na n vɩɩmdame. La zẽm me na n yɩɩ wʋsgo, tɩ bõe, koomã kẽngda be tɩ mogrã koom lebg sãbsgo, tɩ bũmb fãa tõe n vɩɩmd koomã sẽn dabd zĩis ninsã fãa. 10 Zẽm-yõagdb na n yaasa koomã noor En-Gedi tɩ ta En-Eglayim n yadg b zẽm-gãmba. A zẽmã na n yɩɩ buud wʋsg wa sẽn be ko-kãsengã pʋgẽ wã. 11 La a bãgsã ne a londã koom ka na yɩ sãbsg ye, b na n kel n yɩɩ yamsem. 12 La tɩ-rɩts na n yii kʋɩlgã noy a yiibã fãa. Bãmb vãada ka na n kʋɩ ye la b biisã me ka na n sɛ ye. B na n womda kiuug kiuugu, tɩ bõe, ko-kãng yita Wẽnnaam doogẽ. Bãmb biisã yaa rɩɩbo, la b vãadã yaa bãas tɩɩm.»
Tẽngã todse
13 Zu-soaba Wẽnnaam yeelame yaa: «Ad tẽng ning yãmb sẽn na n pʋɩ n kõ Israyɛll buud piig la a yiibã todse. Kõ a Zozɛf buud zĩis a yiibu. 14 Mam da wẽena ne y yaab-rãmbã tɩ m na n kõo yãmb tẽn-kãngã tɩ yɩ y pʋɩɩre. Bɩ y pʋɩ-a tɩ zems taaba. 15 «Ad tẽngã todse. A rɩtg kɩrengã, sẽn sɩng ko-kãsengã noor n tũud n tat Hetlõ n dabd Tesdaad ne Hamat la 16 Berota ne Sibrayim n tũud Damas ne Hamat todsã sʋk tɩ ta Hatsɛɛr-Hatikõ sẽn be Harvaram toadgã zugã. 17 Woto, toadgã sɩngda ko-kãsengã noor n dabd Hatsaar-Eno sẽn be Damas rɩtg toadgã ne Hamat toadgã zugã. Rɩtg toadgã yaa woto. 18 La tẽngã toadg ning sẽn be yaangã sɩngda Harvaram ne Damas sʋka, n tũud Zʋrdẽ kʋɩlgã sẽn be Galaad ne Israyɛll sʋkã, hal tɩ ta yamsem mogrã la Tamaare. Zʋrdẽ kʋɩlgã n ya tẽngã yaang toadgã. 19 La goabg kɩrengã sɩngda Tamaar n dabd Meriba koomã sẽn be Kadɛs n tũud kʋɩlg noor hal tɩ ta ko-kãsengã. Tẽngã goabg toadgã yaa woto. 20 Nin-taoor toadgã yaa ko-kãsengã noor hal tɩ ta zĩig ning sẽn teesd Hamatã. Tẽngã nin-taoor toadgã yaa woto. « 21 Bɩ y pʋɩ tẽngã ne taab wa sẽn zems Israyɛll buudã. 22 Bɩ y maan poeer n pʋɩ tẽngã tɩ yãmb ne sãamb nins fãa sẽn zĩnd yãmb sʋk n dog kambã paam zĩisi. Maan-y bãmb wa y sẽn maand Israyɛll nebã sẽn dog tẽngã pʋgẽ wã. Bɩ b mag yãmb tẽngã pʋɩɩb pʋgẽ tɩ poeerã yãk bãmb me n kõ-b zĩis Israyɛll buudã sʋgse. 23 Bɩ y kõ sãambã zĩis nins b sẽn zẽ wã sʋk tẽnsã pʋsẽ tɩ yɩ b pʋɩɩre. Mam yaa Zu-soaba, yaa maam n yeele.»
