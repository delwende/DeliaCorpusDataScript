B tigsa kaset-dãmb n na n bʋ Issrayɛl bʋʋdo
3
1 Israyɛll neba, kelg-y koɛɛg ning Zu-soabã sẽn togsd yãmb buudã fãa bãmb sẽn da yiis Eziptã. Bãmb yeelame yaa: 2 «Yaa yãmb bal la m yãk dũniyã buudã fãa sʋka. Woto, m na n sɩbga yãmb y yel-wẽnã yĩnga. 3 Neb a yiib tõee n tũ taab tɩ b ka reng n zems ne taab sɩda? 4 Gɩgemd yãbda weoog pʋgẽ zaalem t'a ka yõk bũmbu? Gɩgem-bil na yãb a gãagẽ t'a ka paam bũmbu? 5 Nakalebtong tõee n yõka liuul tɩ b ka bẽd-a? Tãoog na yik tɩ bũmb ka tab-a? 6 No-bɩng na wẽ tẽng pʋgẽ tɩ neb ka yɛɛse? Toog ka wat tẽng pʋgẽ tɩ ka Zu-soabã n maan ye. 7 Tɩ bõe, Zu-soabã ka maand bũmb n ka wilg b no-rɛɛsdbã sẽn ya b tʋmtʋmdba ye. 8 Gɩgemd kelemdame, yaa ãe n ka zoet rabeem? Zu-soaba Wẽnnaam gomdame, ãnna n tõe n ka togs bãmb koɛɛga?» 9 Yeel-y neb nins sẽn be Asdod ne Ezipt na-yirã tɩ b tɩ tigim Samari n ges yãnd ne wẽng sẽn be tẽngã pʋgẽ. 10 Zu-soabã yeelame tɩ neb nins sẽn deegd arzegs ne pãng n bẽngdẽ wã ka mi tɩ maan bũmb ne pʋ-peelem ye. 11 Woto, Zu-soaba Wẽnnaam yeelame tɩ bɛɛb na n gũbga b tẽngã n sãam b pãngã la b wʋk bũmb nins sẽn be b na-yiyã. 12 Wẽnda wa gɩgemd sẽn mi n yõk ned rũng t'a rig-a n deeg a kars a yiib bɩ tʋb-bɛkã. Woto me Israyɛll neb nins sẽn be zĩ-bʋgsgẽ Samari wã na n paamã fãagre. 13 Saasẽ-soab a Wẽnnaam yeelame yaa: «Kelg la f togs a Zakoob buudã 14 tɩ wakat ning mam sẽn na n sɩbg Israyɛll nebã b yel-wẽnã yĩngã, mam na n sãama Betɛll tẽng-kugã. La b na n kɛɛ yɩɩl nins sẽn be tẽng-kugrã sokr zĩigẽ wã tɩ b lʋɩ tẽnga. 15 La mam na n luba si-palg rotã. La rot nins sẽn maan ne wobd yẽnã na n lʋɩɩme. Ro-kãsemsã fãa na n sãamame.» Yaa Zu-soabã n yeele.
