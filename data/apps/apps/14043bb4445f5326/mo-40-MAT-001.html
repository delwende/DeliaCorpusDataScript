A Matiye Koe-noogã
A Zezi roogo
= Luk 3.23-38 =
1
1 A Zezi-Kirist sẽn yaa a Daviid yageng la a Abraham yagengã roog yaa woto :
2 A Abraham n dog a Izaake, t'a Izaak dog a Zakoobe, t'a Zakoob dog a Zuda ne a ba-biisi,
3 t'a Zuda ne a pag a Tamaar dog a Farɛs ne a Zara, t'a Farɛs dog a Esrom, t'a Esrom dog a Aram,
4 t'a Aram dog a Aminadaabe, t'a Aminadaab dog a Naason, t'a Naason dog a Salmo,
5 t'a Salmo ne a pag a Rahaab rog a Boaaze, t'a Boaaz ne a pag a Rut dog a Obɛɛde, t'a Obɛɛd dog a Izayi,
6 t'a Izayi dog rĩm a Daviidi.
A Daviid ne a Uuri pagã doga a Salomo,
7 la a Salomo doga a Roboam, t'a Roboam dog a Abiya, t'a Abiya dog a Aza, t'a
8 Aza dog a Zozafate, t'a Zozafat dog a Zoram, t'a Zoram dog a Oziyaase,
9 t'a Oziyaas dog a Zoatam, la a Zoatam doga a Akaaze, t'a Akaaz dog a Ezekiyaase,
10 t'a Ezekiyaas dog a Manaase, t'a Manaase dog a Amo, t'a Amo dog a Zoziyaase,
11 t'a Zoziyaas dog a Zekoniyaas ne a ba-biisi, sẽn zems wakat ning b sẽn yõg bãmb n kẽng Babilonne.
12 La bãmb sẽn ta Babilonne, a Zekoniyaas yaool n doga a Salasiyɛlle, t'a Salasiyɛlldog a Zorobabɛlle,
13 t'a Zorobabɛll dog a Abiwuudi, t'a Abiwuud dog a Eliyakim, t'a Eliyakim dog a Azoore,
14 t'a Azoor dog a Sadoke, t'a Sadok dog a Akim, t'a Akim dog a Eliwuudi,
15 t'a Eliwuud dog a Eleyazaare, t'a Eleyazaar dog a Matan, t'a Matan dog a Zakoobe,
16 t'a Zakoob dog a Zozɛfo, la a Zozɛf yaa a Maari sɩda, Maari ning sẽn dog a Zezi sẽn boond tɩ Kiristã.
17 Dẽ, zamaan fãa sẽn sɩng ne a Abraham tɩ ta a Daviid yaa zamaan piig la a naase. La sẽn sɩng ne a Daviid tɩ ta b sẽn yõg bãmb n kẽng Babilon yaa zamaan piig la a naase. La sẽn sɩng ne b sẽn yõg bãmb n kẽng Babilon tɩ ta Kirist yaa zamaan piig la a naase.
A Zezi Kirist dogem
= Luk 2.1-7 =
18 Gese, a Zezi Kirist dogem yɩɩ woto. Wakat ning b sẽn dɩk bãmb ma a Maari n kõ a Zozɛfo, la b sẽn nan ka mag taaba, b mikame t'a tʋta Sɩɩg-sõng pʋga.
19 La a sɩd a Zozɛf sẽn ya nin-tɩrg n ka rat n ning-a yãndã, yẽ tagsame n dat n bas-a tɩ nebã ra bãng ye.
20 La yẽ sẽn tagsd bõn-kãense, gese, Zu-soabã malɛk waa yẽ nengẽ zãmsd pʋgẽ n yeele : « A Daviid biig a Zozɛfo, da zoe rabeem tɩ peeg fo pag a Maari ye. Bala, a sẽn tʋtã yaa Sɩɩg-sõng pʋga.
21 Yẽ na n doga bi-ribla, tɩ b pʋd a yʋʋr t'a Zezi. Yaa yẽ n na n fãag a nebã ne b yel-wẽnã. »
22 Bõn-kãens fãa maaname tɩ Zu-soabã sẽn da gom ne b no-rɛɛsã noorã paam pidsgu.
23 « Gese, pa-kuil na n dɩka pʋga, n dog bi-ribla, tɩ b pʋd a yʋʋr t'a Emanuwɛll, sẽn dat n togs tɩ Wẽnnaam bee ne-do. »
24 A Zozɛf sẽn neke, yẽ maana wa malɛkã sẽn togs yẽ wã, la yẽ peega a pagã.
25 La yẽ ka bãng-a hal t'a rog bi-ribla. La yẽ pʋda biigã yʋʋr t'a Zezi.
