Yaoolem wakat yel-beedo
3
1 Bɩ f bãng tɩ yaoolem wakate tood na n zĩndame.
2 Bala, nebã na n yɩɩ beed dãmba, ligd noangdba, wilg-m-mens rãmba, tɩtaam dãmba, Wẽnnaam tʋʋsdba, ba-rãmb la ma-rãmb kɩɩsdba, zɩ-beoog rãmba, nin-zaalse,
3 neb sẽn ka tar nimbãan-zoeere, la sũ-to-neba, tẽn-kãn-neba, neb sẽn ka tar yõk-m-menga, pʋ-to-rãmba, nin-sõmyã kisgdba,
4 zãmb dãmba, neb sẽn ka gũusda, la wuk-m-mens rãmb sẽn nong dũniyã sũ-noog n yɩɩd Wẽnnaam.
5 Bãmb wẽnda wa neb sẽn sakd Wẽnnaam, la b yaool n kɩɩsda bãmb pãnga. Lak ne bãmb dãmba.
6 La neb bee bãmb sʋkã sẽn soos n kẽ rotẽ n belg pagb sẽn ya yalemba, sẽn pid ne yel-wẽnã, la sẽn sakd yĩng ratem buud fãa.
7 Bãmb zãmsda daar fãa, la b ka tõe n bãng sɩd ye.
8 A wa a Zannɛs ne a Zambrɛs sẽn da kɩɩs a Moyiizã, nin-kãens me kɩɩsda sɩdã woto. Bãmb tagsd yaa wẽnse, la ne tẽeb yelle bãmb ka zems ye.
9 La a wa a Zanɛs ne a Zambrɛs sẽn da ka tõogã, bãmb me kõn tõog ye. Bala, bãmb yalemdã na n pukame tɩ nebã fãa bãnge.
Yaoolem bũmb a Poll sẽn yeel a Tɩmote t'a maane
10 La fo ra bãnga koɛɛg ning mam sẽn zãms nebã ne m yel-manesem, la m datem ne m tẽebo, la m sũ-bʋgsem ne m nonglem, la m sũ-mare,
11 la neb sẽn yɩ wãn n nams maam, la mam toogã. To-bʋg la mam ka paam Ãntiyos ne Ikoniyom la Listri ? La nams-bʋg la mam ka paame ? La Zu-soabã fãaga maam ne bõn-kãens fãa.
12 La neb nins fãa sẽn dat n tũ Kirist a Zezi ne pʋ-peelmã na n paama namsgo.
13 La nin-wẽnsã ne zãmb dãmbã na n maana wẽng n paase, b na n belga neb a taaba, la bãmb mens na n paama belgre.
14 kel n sak bũmb nins fo sẽn da zãms la f bãng tɩ yaa sɩdã, la fo mii sẽn da zãms-a foomã.
15 Hal fo yãadem wakat tɩ ta masã, fo bãnga Gʋls-sõmyã sẽn tõe n vẽneg foom tɩ f paam fãagre, fo sẽn tẽed Kirist a Zezi wã yĩnga.
16 Gʋls-Sõmyã zãng yaa Wẽnnaam n kõ tõndo, la a bee bark zãmsg yĩnga, la tɩ wilg ned a kongre, la saglg yĩnga, la tɩ zãms tɩrlem,
17 tɩ Wẽnnaam ned tõe n bɩ la a maan tʋʋm-sõma fãa.
