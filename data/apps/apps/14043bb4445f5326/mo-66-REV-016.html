Wẽnnaam sũ-puugr laas a yopoe wã
16
1 La mam wʋma koe-kãseng sẽn yi Wẽnnaam doogẽ wã n yeel malegs a yopoe wã yaa : « Bɩ yãmb kẽng n tɩ kiig Wẽnnaam sũ-puugrã sẽn be tʋgd a yopoe wã pʋsẽ wã tẽngã zugu. »
2 Pipi soabã kẽngame n tɩ kiig sẽn be-a tʋkẽ wã tẽngã zugu. La nodr buud a yembr sẽn ya toog la sẽn ya wẽng wʋsg yõka neb nins sẽn tar dũngã gʋlsem la b waoogd yẽ bõn-naandgã.
3 Malɛk a yiib soabã kiiga sẽn be yẽ tʋkẽ wã ko-kãsengã pʋgẽ. La a lebga wa kũum zɩɩm, la bõn-vɩɩsã fãa sẽn be kõ-kãsengã pʋgẽ wã kiime.
4 Malɛk a tãab soabã kiiga sẽn be yẽ tʋkẽ wã kʋɩlsã ne ko-bundsẽ zĩisẽ, la bãmb lebga zɩɩm.
5 La mam wʋma koom malɛkã sẽn yeele : ‹‹ Yãmb sẽn be la sẽn dag n be, yãmb yaa tɩrga, la yãmb yaa sõngo, yãmb sẽn kao bʋʋd woto wã yĩnga.
6 Bala, bãmb daaga yãmb nebã ne yãmb no-rɛɛsdbã zɩɩm, la yãmb kõo bãmb zɩɩm tɩ b yũ, la bãmb zemsa woto. »
7 La mam wʋma tẽn-kugrã sẽn yeele : ‹‹ Hakɩɩka, Zu-soab a Wẽnnaam sẽn tõe fãa wã, yãmb bʋʋd kaoob yaa sɩd la tɩrga. »
8 Malɛk a naas soabã kiiga sẽn be yẽ tʋkẽ wã wĩntoogã zugu. La b kõo yẽ pãng t'a yõog nebã ne bugum.
9 La tʋʋl-kãseng yõoga nebã, la bãmb tʋʋsa Wẽnnaam sẽn so to-kãensã yʋʋre, la bãmb yam ka tek tɩ b kos sugr tɩ yɩ Wẽnnaam waoogr ye.
10 Malɛk a nu soabã kiiga sẽn be yẽ tʋkẽ wã rũngã geer zugu. La yẽ soolem lebga lik bida, la nebã wãba b zɛlma toogã yĩnga.
11 La bãmb tʋʋsa arzãn Wẽnnaam bãmb toodã ne b noadã yĩnga, la bãmb yam ka tek tɩ b kos sugr bãmb tʋʋm wẽnsã yĩng ye.
12 Malɛk a yoob soabã kiiga sẽn be yẽ tʋkẽ wã kʋɩl-kãseng sẽn boond tɩ Efartã zugu. La yẽ koomã kʋɩme tɩ yaang nanambs sor zĩndi.
13 La mam yãa sɩ-wẽns a tãab sẽn wẽnd wa bulumbugd sẽn yi dargõ wã noorẽ, la rũngã noorẽ, la belgr bãngdã noorẽ.
14 Bala, bãmb yaa zĩn-dãmb sẽn maand bõn-bãna, la sẽn saagd dũniyã tɛk fãa nanambs nengẽ n na n tɩ tigs bãmb tɩ b wa Wẽnnaam sẽn tõe fãa da-kãsengã zabre.
15 « Gese, mam wata wa wagdre. Bark bee ned ning sẽn gũusda, la sẽn talld a fut n ka rat n kẽn zaal tɩ nebã yã a yãndã zugu. »
16 La bãmb tigma bãmb zĩig sẽn boond ne hebre goam tɩ Harmagedõ.
17 Malɛk a yopoe soabã kiiga sẽn be yẽ tʋkẽ wã pemsmã pʋgẽ, la koe-kãseng yii Wẽnnaam doogẽ wã, la bãmb geerẽ wã n yeele : « A maaname n sɛ. »
18 La sa-yãgb ne koees la sa-kelems zĩndame, la tẽn-digimd-kãseng zĩndame sẽn yɩɩd fãal sẽn dag n zĩnd hal neb sẽn be tẽng zug wakate.
19 La tẽn-kãsengã welga zĩis a tãabo, la soolem toay-toayã tẽns lʋɩɩme, la Wẽnnaam tẽega Babilon-kãsengã yell n kɩt tɩ yẽ yũ bãmb sũ-puug-kãsengã rãamã.
20 La tẽns fãa koom sẽn gũbg loogame, la b ka leb n yã tãensã ye.
21 La sa-pɩlem yii yĩngr n lʋɩ nebã zutu, la a yembr fãa zɩslem kolga kilo pis-nu la a yopoe. La nebã tʋʋsa Wẽnnaam sa-pɩlmã toog yĩnga, bala, to-kãng yaa kãsengã.
