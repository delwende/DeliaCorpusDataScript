4 Ninsaal pa wãbd yɩbrg ye.
Sɩngr wẽndẽ, Wẽnnaam deng n naana rũmsã fãa, sẽn vɩ-b tẽng zugu, sẽn vɩ-b koomẽ, la sẽn yɩgd-ba. B reng n naana moodã la tɩɩsã. B reng n naana bõn-vɩɩlɩ wã fãa, n yaool n naan ninsaala.
Wẽnnaam sẽn wa n naan ninsaala, b naan-a lame t’a ya bi-pɛɛlga, n bas-a a beell kõe, koom noore, tuug sɛɛga. Yɩbrg n yɩ pipĩ, n yã bi-pɛɛlgã. A sẽn yã-a wã, rabeem yõk-a lame, t'á ra ka mi sẽn ya bũmb ning ye. A zoee tao-tao n tɩ tigim weoogã bõn-vɩɩlɩ, n na n togs-ba. A reng n tɩ yãa nao a naas buudã : soomsã, yĩisa wã, bõnyẽesã, wobdã, b fãa. La a yeel-b lame :- Ad-y mam yãa bũmb waeoogã pʋga, koomã noore. La rabeem n tar maam. Mam pa mi sẽn ya bũmb ning ye. La mam tẽedame tɩ tõnd sã n bas yãoã t’a lebg bedre, a na n namsa tõndo.La rũms a taabã sok-a lame :
- Fo bõn-nindã yaa, a tara nao a wãna?Tɩ yɩbrg yeel t'a tara nao a yiibu. La a wilg-b t’a bee koomã noore, tuug tẽngre. La sẽn dat-a, bɩ a soab kẽng be t’a na bãng n paase. La rũmsã laame n kaase, n yeel yɩbrgo :- Foom a yɩbrg sɩd yaa rabɛɛm bala. Yãoã soab tar nao a yiib bala, tɩ tõnd tar hal a naase. Tɩ fo teed t’a na n wa tõog n wudg n yõka tõndo.
Rũmsã sẽn gom woto, b sãeega taab n bas yɩbrgo, tɩ kẽer tʋʋd-a, tɩ kẽer yaand-a.La yɩbrg pɛlgã ket n pa kãnkãe ye. A sẽn loog be, a tɩ yãa we-rũms tar-pɩgs rãmbã : walã, sɩlsã, kodensã, hal ne a rulg rãmb fãa. A goma ne bãmb me wala a sẽn gom ne nao a naas rãmbã.
La bãmb me sok-a lame:- Fo sẽn gomd bõn-vɩɩl ningã yell yaa, a tara pɩgs la?Tɩ yɩbrg yet tɩ ayo. La a le wilg bãmb me bi-pɛɛlgã sẽn be zĩĩg ningã, n yeel tɩ sẽn dat-a tõe n tɩ ges-a lame, n bãng-a n paase.
Bãmbã sẽn wʋm tɩ bũmbã pa tar pɩgsã,b yeela yɩbrgo :
- Fo menga yaanda tõndo; Na n maana a wãn tɩ yãoã wa paam tõndo? Tõnd tar pɩgs n yɩgd yĩngri, tɩ yãoã be tẽng n kẽnda.
B sẽn gom woto n sa, b wɩlgame n bas yɩbrgo.La yellã kell n pa sakd yẽ ye. A sẽn bas liuuli wã buudã, a tɩ yãa koomẽ bõn-vɩɩlɩ wã n yeel-ba :- Ad bũmb n wẽneg n be koomã noor ka waa, mam pa mi sẽn ya a soab ye. La rabeem n tar maam. Pa gũusi, a sã n wa lebg bedre, pa na n paoog tõnd ba a ye ye.Tɩ koomẽ bõn-vɩɩlɩ wã sok-a :- A sã n Wa lebg bedre, a na n tõog n duga koom la?
La yɩbrg leokame n yetẽ t’a pa tẽed bũmbã na n tõog n dugame ye. Tɩ zĩmã buud yeel tɩ yel-kãngã pa pak bãmb ye. Tɩ paka tẽngã zug rãmba.
B sẽn yeel woto, la b sẽn lebg n lisi, n ya mon-mon n teesdẽ.
We-rũmsã fãa sãeegame n bas a yɩbrgo, t'a yɛs a ye masã, n ka mi a sẽn maandg ye. A yamã wa n kõ-a lame t’a babs Wẽnnam menga. n tɩ bãng yellã võore. A sɩd maana boto. A sẽn ta wã, a sõsa ne Wẽnnaam woto :- Naab a Wẽnde, yaa yãmb n naan bũmb ning sẽn be-a koomã noore, tuugã tẽngrã bɩ?- N-ye. Sẽn be tẽng zug fãa yaa maam n naane. Sẽn be-a tuugã tẽngrã me yaa maam n naane. A yʋʋr la ninsaala.
- Mam miime tɩ bõnyẽeg wãbda nemdo, tɩ yĩisr wãbd yamdo; tɩ liuul rɩt ki la mo-biisi. La yãmb sẽn naan ninsaal woto wã yaa, a na n dɩɩ bõe a vɩɩmã pʋgẽ ?
- Sã n ya ne ninsaala, a na n dɩɩ bũmb buudã fãa mam sẽn naanã : rũms sẽn be tẽng zug n kẽnd ne nao a naase, sẽn vɩ-b koomẽ, la sẽn yɩgd-b yĩngri, hal ba ne yãmb dãmbã fãa. Ba moogã menga, a pa na n bas ye.
 
Haya! Yɩbrg sẽn wʋm dẽ, a yɩk n lʋɩɩ kõe, n na n bõs Wẽnde :- Naab a Wẽnde, mam pa rat tɩ ninsaal dɩ maam ye. Mam bul n pa rat tɩ bũmb ba a ye rɩ maam ye.
La naab a Wẽnd sɩngame n tõdge. Tɩ yɩbrg me tar rɩdgr bala :- M yõkd-y-la sɛɛga, m ba a Wẽnde. Zoe-y mam nimbãanega. Kɩt-y tɩ bũmb ba a ye ra rɩ maam ye.
Yɩbrg talla fiinig woto, hal t'a nimbãanegã sɩd kẽ Wẽenaam. Tɩ b yeel-a :
- Mam wʋma f kosgã. M sakame. Bũmb ba a ye kõn dɩ foo tẽng zug ye. La fo me kõn yãk bõn-vɩɩl ba a ye yõor ye.
 
Rẽ n so tɩ ninsaal pa wãbd yɩbrgo, tɩ bõn-vɩɩl ba a ye meng pa wãbd-a.
