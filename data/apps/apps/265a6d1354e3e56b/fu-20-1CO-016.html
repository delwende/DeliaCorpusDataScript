Haala wallugol seniiɓe wonɓe ngalluure Urusaliima
16
1 Joonin en ndaaran kabaaru hawrundurgol ceede faa walla seniiɓe wonɓe Urusaliima ɓeen.
Ngaɗon ko njamirmi kawrite goonɗinɓe gonɗe leydi Galaatiya ɗeen.
2 Alan fuu, mono e mooɗon fuu itta ko waawi, resa.
Ndeen si mi warii, en pilataako hawrundurde ɗum katin.
3 Nde ngarumi fu, mi hokkan ɓe cuɓiɗon ɓeen ɓataakiiji kollooji on cuɓake ɓe, ɓe njaara ballal mooɗon ngaal Urusaliima.
4 Si na foti ko mi yaha miin du, min njaadan.
Haala anniyaaji Pool
5 Si mi feƴƴitii leydi Makedoniya fu, mi waran to mooɗon, sabo toon ndewammi.
6 Hasii mi weeranan on seeɗa naa mi dabban to mooɗon.
Ndeen oɗon mbaawi wallude kam faa mi yaha yeeso e jahaangal am.
7 Sabo mi yiɗaa fantude to mooɗon joonin de mi dilla law.
Miɗo yiɗi ɓooyude to mooɗon seeɗa si Joomiraaɗo jaɓii.
8 Ammaa mi ɓooyan ley Efeesu ga faa iidi ɓenndal alkama hewta.
9 Sabo mi heɓii ɗo laawol manngol no mi gollira gollal nafoowal.
Wooɗi, wayɓe du na keewi.
10 Si Timote warii to mooɗon fu, njakkitoɗon ɗum faa wooɗa taa hula, sabo wo Joomiraaɗo o gollanta hono am ni.
11 Ndelle, taa fay gooto yawa mo.
Mballon mo e laawol ngool faa o warta to am e jam.
Sabo miɗo heɗii mo, kanko e sakiraaɓe ɓeen.
12 Wooɗi, dow sakiike meeɗen Apolos, mi nyaagake mo sanne o yaada e sakiraaɓe ɓeen to mooɗon, de o muuyaay yahude joonin.
Si o heɓii laawol fu, o waran.
Baynundural
13 Kakkilon, ndaroɗon faa wooɗa ley goonɗinal.
Laatoɗon suusuɓe, ɓeydoroɗon semmbe.
14 Ko ngaɗoton fuu, ngaɗiron ɗum njinngu.
15 Oɗon anndi Estefaana e koreeji muuɗum.
Kam'en artii fuu goonɗinde ley leydi Akaya.
Iɓe tinnii e gollande seniiɓe ɓeen.
Sakiraaɓe, miɗo nyaagoo on sanne:
16 njarranee yimɓe wa'uɓe hono noon, kam'en e gollidooɓe e muɓɓen, tinniiɓe e gollal ngaal fuu.
17 Miɗo weltoroo garol Estefaana e Fortunaatu e Akayku ngool.
Ɓe ngaɗanii kam ko onon on mbaawaa waɗande kam saabe on ngalaa ga.
18 Ɓe mbaaltinii hakkillo am, kam e hakkillooji mooɗon fuu.
Teddinee yimɓe hono maɓɓe.
19 Kawrite goonɗinɓe gonɗe leydi Aasiya ɗeen ina njowta on.
Akila e deekum Pirisilla e kawrital goonɗinɓe kawroowal galle maɓɓe ngaal ina njowta on sanne e innde Joomiraaɗo.
20 Sakiraaɓe ɓeen fuu ina njowta on.
Njowtunduron onon du e jowtaali ceniiɗi.
 
21 Inan jowtaango am ngo mbinndirammi e junngo am:
MIIN POOL.
22 Neɗɗo fuu mo yiɗaa Joomiraaɗo, kuddi woodanii ɗum!
Yaa Joomiraaɗo amin, war!
23 Wo hinney Joomiraaɗo meeɗen Iisaa wondu e mooɗon.
24 Njinngu am ina woni e mooɗon on fuu e ley kawtal meeɗen e Iisaa Almasiihu.
