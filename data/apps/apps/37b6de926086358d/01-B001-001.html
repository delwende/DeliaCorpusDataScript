41 Talka e dokkal moodibbo.
 
Waɗiino e jamaanuuji,
gorko wonnoo de yo talka.
Nyannde gom talka men eggidi deekum hoɗowi nder ladde.
No talkaaku makko foti de o walaa fay mbaatoowan jaango.
Ɗo o tawaa nder ladde ɗoon,
moodibbo mawɗo rewi waali wuro makko.
Talka men anndaa fu ko waɗanta moodibbo men.
O walaa ko o hokkora moodibbo,
o walaa njogiiɗo heɓa hokkora o,
o hokkora moodibbo.
Wari garol moodibbo o waylanoke talka men semteende.
O noddi deekiiko heɓa ɓe ndawrida.
Ɓe ndartini ko ɓe korsan gabboy koy ɓe keddodinoo fu ɓe kawrondina ɓe ngaɗana moodibbo hiraande.
Ni moodibbo waɗanaa hiraande.
Heddaa waɗanaa pummaari.
Moodibbo fummidinnoo ni wi'i dawan.
Talka men e deekum ɗootiri o,
faa o woɗɗii.
Ɓe ngarnoo heɓa ɓe njeeƴoo ni moodibbo hokki ɓe paali ɗiɗi.
O wi'i so gataare warii ni ɓe aawa ɗi.
Heddii ɓe mbaynondiri.
 
Gataare warnoo ni talka men aawi paalim.
Kore puɗi.
Ndee fuɗi fusi cardi;
ndee to fuɗi fusi kanŋe.
Ndelle beero yo saawoore.
Ngaɗanɗo ɗum ko wooɗi fu,
yo hoorem waɗani.
 
Yaakuuba Jallo
