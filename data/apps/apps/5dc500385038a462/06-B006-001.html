3 The leaders of the lizards.
There was a man and his family in a village. Behind the man's yard stood an old grape tree with countless holes in the trunk. A lizard and his wife had taken up residence there. The male, as usual, was in charge of going out to bring back food for them. One day, he came back from his walk all happy and at the door of their residence, he shouted to his wife: "Honey, did you learn some good news?"
- What was it? asked the female.
- I am appointed chief, yes chief. He replied.
- Chief? Were the children there when you were appointed? she asked without even thinking.
Furious, he went out to continue his shopping. On his return, he came across the man's children who had gone out for their needs. One of them shouted "lizard" and the others, without wasting any time, armed themselves with sticks or stones. The hunt for the lizard began. In the dust raised by the children, the lump ran at full speed, breaking even its heart, and returned to its hole in a hurry, accompanied by a large stone that hit the tree. He was breathing in fits and starts and was shaking like a leaf. The mistrustful lady approached him and said:
"This is what I wanted you to think about. If the children were present at your appointment, would they want to run a leader let alone kill him?"
So our leaders of yesteryear must know that they were appointed without us and that is why we chase them to their homes. A young man of today is an old man of tomorrow; what an old fool does not know. Our authorities have understood this and have always involved us in the decision-making process, in the management of power. We must not fail in our mission.
