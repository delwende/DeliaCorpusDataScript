4 The origin of witch doctors.
 
Once there was a man and his wife in a village. They had lived together for a long time but had never had a child. Finally, one fine day, providence allowed them to have a boy, the only offspring of this union. He grew up very quickly and one day he said to his father: "I would like to travel so that I will only see misfortune on my journey".
He set off and the distance he travelled was already long. He met some boys who were picking monkey bread and using their sex as sticks for this purpose. He greeted them and they responded in chorus. They rushed over and asked him what brought him there. He told them that he was looking for wonders. They answered him if what they were doing was not a miracle. When he replied in the negative, he continued on his way.
A few steps away, he saw some girls breaking plums on stones with their breasts. The girls asked the same question and on his negative answer, he continued on his way, going straight ahead.
It was not long before he saw a very old woman on her way who, having her head in her hand, set about killing the lice on it. But our young man had a heart of stone and he was not frightened at the sight of this old woman. He greeted her. The old woman stopped him, wanting to know what brought him to this place where no human being lived. He told her what brought him here. The old woman asked him if what she was doing was not. When she said no, he continued on his way.
Finally, at dusk, he reached a village, a huge village that stretched for miles and miles. And in this village, not a single living soul. He walked along streets and streets and finally he saw a young girl coming towards him. She greeted him very kindly and asked him what brought her to this place. He explained to her the reasons for his journey. The astonished girl continued to ask him: "Are you looking for wonders? And he continued to answer yes. She then said to him: "Follow me".
He agreed and followed her. At the first hut she encountered, she asked him to help her lift the roof. This hut was full of bones. Together they did this until they reached three huts all filled with bones. She told him to stop looking for the misfortune, because here it is: all these bones are those of the people who lived in this village. They were all killed by my mother. The young man was very afraid that this woman would come and devour him. The girl took him to an old well where she sheltered him and closed the opening because his mother was away for a week and would be back that very day. The young man spent a terrible night at the bottom of the well. The old witch soon arrived and shouted to her daughter: "Is there anyone here? Apart from me there is no one else," replied the girl.
The next day, the old witch set off on her journey, which was to last a week. Immediately the girl took an egg, a fruit containing the seeds of all the plants and a stone. Then she brought the imprudent hero out of his hiding place and they ran away together.
The week passed and the old witch returned. She found no one, but she soon learned that her daughter had run away with the young man. She went after the couple. When the girl saw her mother coming, she dropped the stone and immediately a mountain appeared. The old witch turned into a whirlwind and crossed the mountain. Angry, she ran even faster. Her daughter saw her and dropped the fruit. An inextricable forest formed. The old witch, with a blow of her teeth, cleared a path and passed. A few kilometres from the young man's village, the girl turned around and saw her mother arrive with frustration. She dropped the egg and a large body of water formed, cutting off the old witch. The old witch, having no means of crossing the road, bit her finger and said: "That's my daughter who wants to oust me", and she returned to her village and was not heard from again for three years.
One day, a very beautiful girl arrived in the village. Young and old alike could not sleep well because this beauty dazzled and bewitched. All had tried to court her assiduously, but none had succeeded. When it was her enemy's turn, she accepted wholeheartedly. He ran home and told his wife, who said: "I beg you never to agree to be alone with her, for she is not a girl, she is my mother, and if the opportunity arises, she will kill you. The young man understood and did as his wife asked.
One day she was taking a group of young men home. Among them was her sworn enemy. Instead of separating when the young people were jostling to shake hands with her, she took advantage of the situation and put her left hand on the boy's head. In that moment he forgot everything his wife had said to him.
He turned and followed her. At the side of the road there was a big baobab tree on which she asked her friend to go up and get some leaves for his landlady. The boy climbed up and she called out to him: "Go to the highest branches because the leaves below are dirty. He climbed to the top of the tree and underneath he found that the young beauty had become an old woman, ugly and frightfully ugly. She uprooted the tree and took it with the young man to the top. His wife, who was at home, saw that her mother had had her husband. She made a flying carpet that went to remove her husband from his uncomfortable position. Once in her village, the old woman crushed the baobab tree with a single stroke of her hand, shook the leaves and found nothing. She bit her fingers again and stayed for another three years, after which she went back to look for her enemy, her daughter's husband, her son-in-law.
Having no other means left, she became an old cow again and started wandering in the forests. Her name was spread throughout the country because she was very wicked. One day a great hunter got up and said to his wives: "Break me all my bowls because I don't think I'll come back from the hunt alive today", and he left without saying anything else.
In his bag he had a black powder which he threw on the ground to find out the direction of the wind and to track the animals. Having done this, he took a trail and saw under a large tree that stood before him, an old woman killing her lice. With very slow steps, he approached her and pointed his rifle less than a centimetre from the old woman's neck. He fired and the head was blown away by the bullets, shouting: "You surprised me! You have surprised me".
The victor ran towards her with his axe and cut her to pieces. Thus witchcraft would have disappeared from the earth, but from the bones of this great witch left under the tree where the hunter had cut her down, many witches were born and scattered all over the earth.
Imperialism in disarray in Burkina Faso is packing up, but its local henchmen remain in all four corners of the country. They must be fought with the utmost energy, as this great hunter did, for the triumph of all subjugated and exploited peoples.
