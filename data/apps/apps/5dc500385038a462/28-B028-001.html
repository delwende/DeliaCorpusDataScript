14 The misfortunes of a lover.
In a village there lived a man and his wife. They loved each other so much that even when a thorn pricked one of them in the bush while the other was at home, the one at home felt the pain. One day, this happened to the husband in the bush and the wife cried until she returned. Unfortunately, fate wanted them to separate. The wife became seriously ill and died. To bury her, they made a grave that could only contain her. They put it in the ditch and just as they were about to cover it with earth the husband called out, "Dear brothers, wait. I have one last word to say to my wife.
Amazed, everyone stood aside to let him do it. He entered the grave and lay down beside his wife and asked to bury her now.
- What do you mean, you want to be buried with your wife while you live? asked the audience.
- Don't bury me if you want, I'm not leaving here anyway," he replied.
They begged him but he refused categorically. So they ended up burying him with his wife. Soon after the men left, the angels arrived. They were surprised and asked: "Who brought you here? God sent us on a mission to the woman's house. You are not dead and you must get out of here.
- I can't leave here without my wife because without her my life is useless," he replied.
- You have fifty years of life left. Will you agree to stay here until the end of that time before you die?
He asked them if they could not give half of the fifty years to his wife so that they could go out together and die together. The angels told him that they could not do anything, but that they would convey his wish to God. This was done and the man was satisfied. They came out and their happy life continued. One day they decided to go on a journey. The road was long, sandy and hard. They reached a large expanse of shifting sand when the sun was overhead. Unable to continue, they sat down under a coconut tree to rest and wait for the right moment. As soon as they sat down, the husband fell asleep with his head on his wife's lap.
A prince, returning from a trip, saw them. He was attracted by the woman's dazzling beauty and approached her gently. When he saw that the husband was fast asleep, he beckoned the woman to get up and come upstairs so that they could go to his house. She refused, but he persisted. And as he was also handsome and better dressed than the sleeper, he finally convinced her. She wanted to get up but was prevented by her husband, whom she pointed at. The corrupter by signs told her to make a pile of sand and put her head on it. The woman obeyed and got up. When the husband woke up, he did not notice any traces, as the wind had blown away the horse's footprints. He immediately concluded that it was God who had punished him. He returned home and explained what had happened to him to their only child. Disappointed, the wifeless husband decided to send the child to a Koranic school. The child, during his garibout walks, saw his mother on a two-storey house and recognised her. The woman did not recognise her child and asked him one day: "Little child, don't you have a relative next door to let you suffer like this in such rags?
- My mother died when I was very small. My father is alone and very unhappy. He gave me to a friend of his so that I could learn to read the Koran and not suffer like he did, who may have more experience. I feel well enough as it is," said the child.
After hearing the woman's voice, the child's doubts were erased from his mind as he had not believed his eyes at first. Back home, he asked his Koranic teacher to do everything possible to make him see his father because he had an important word to say. "My life depends on it," he added.
Surprised to hear such shocking words from a little boy, the teacher took him to his father, to whom he announced that he had seen his mother on a two-storey house. Not believing him on the spot, the father advised him to go and see the woman in question again and tell her everything he had seen when he joined them, promising not to delay. When he arrived, the child swore that it was his mother and nobody else. So his father asked him to go and point out the house in question. The child agreed and went there with his unfortunate father. The latter ran up the stairs with his eyes half closed. But he was stopped by the prince's porter, who told him that a stranger could not enter without first requesting an audience with the prince and explaining the visit. In his anger, the distraught husband could not answer and kept climbing. The guard alerted the others to come and help him to bring down the madman. The prince gave the order not to let this dirty man climb up to him. They whipped him down and he came up a second and third time. At this point the king intervened and said that the man was not mad. If he was, he added, he would at least tell himself that he was not allowed to go up and he would leave. "He must have something up there.
- Indeed, I have my wife upstairs, he confirmed. So the prince promised to give her back to him but told him that he had to wait until the next morning to recognise her among other women. Otherwise he would be considered a liar and punished without mercy. In his displeasure, the man had lost his appetite and had no desire to sleep. He handed over all his toast and meat to a skinny dog standing next to him. After he had feasted and licked his whiskers, the dog apologised and said, "You probably haven't heard a dog talk yet, but today you will. Your wife is here but there are so many of them and they look so much alike that you might not recognise her and you will be killed. Nevertheless, I will help you. Tomorrow morning they will be lined up and you will be sitting. They will walk past you so that you can pick out the one that belongs to you. I will come and mingle with them. I will be chased away, but I will do everything I can to get between your wife's legs. When she passes in front of you, you will point to her and it will be correct. This was done and the man recognised his wife. But she swore in the name of all that she did not know this dirty man. Furious, the man called on God to help him decide, saying, "God, help me. My wife had run away from me. I found her and now she's pushing me away, saying she doesn't know me. So I ask you, if it is she who is pushing me away, to take away my twenty-five years for me.
No sooner had he finished saying these words than the woman turned into a termite mound falling to the ground in clumps. At that moment everyone around knew that the man was right.
This being the case, our sisters must place their trust in those who first sacrificed themselves for them instead of making the choice based on appearance. We, the boys, on our part, must contain ourselves so as not to turn our love into a termite mound, bearing in mind of course that there are home wreckers. The building of our nation will only happen with mutual trust.
