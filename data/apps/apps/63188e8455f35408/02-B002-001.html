927 Putte kaɗataa araawa soodeede.
928 Puyɗo so yiɗii mutan.
929 Rawaandu wakkaandu wo umsataa.
930 Rawaandu njahoowu guga na ɓuri debbo pijoowo.
931 Rawaandu yeɗaaka ndiyam mbulku : faccataa faccantaake.
932 Reedu yooɓantaake.
933 Reedu na ɓuri woyndu luggude.
934 Reedu wo wujjante fenantaake.
935 Rewɓe na keewi kaa debbo mo worɓe koyɗata hoyaa.
936 Riiwii hewtay wartii tawtay.
937 Rona waata wanaa debbo.
938 Ronde riiku na ɓuri ronde jawdi.
939 Roondoke rendere heccere unnii boɓi makko taba.
940 Saa’a ɓuri saadeeji.
941 Saabu cofel yarnata buugalel.
942 Saaraa wo hayre : so a fergoke e mum nawnoɗaa, so fergoke ma du nawnoɗaa.
943 Saɓe wayrude neɗɗo ta waɗa ƴamaa so tagum waylitoke.
944 Saɗi ittataa ko woni nder ɓeernde.
945 Safi walaa safaare so wanaa mayde.
946 Safi wi’ata lamɗam na weli.
