1) Welcome!
Discover how Europeans live and what they believe.
This application is an offer.
It was created because people from other countries are important to us here.
We want to give you an insight into European culture,
because some things about our culture may raise questions for you.
This is not surprising,
because in every culture,
many things happen according to rules that locals know inside out,
but which are not explicitly written down anywhere.
Such rules and values exist everywhere.
We would like to describe some of them from Europe in this application.
Of course,
some Europeans behave differently.
But here are several typical behavior patterns.
People from other countries have also shared a number of observations with us.
As authors we imagine that you and us have met on the train or in a café - or perhaps we are sitting at a meal and chatting,
and of course we have actually sat at the table with people from very different countries and listened and talked.
We consciously publish this app as Christians,
as people who follow Jesus.
We also write something about this in this app.
Because we have also heard many questions about our faith at the dinner table.
Many Europeans are also looking forward to real encounters with you.
They look forward to getting to know something of your culture.
For example,
many appreciate the sense of hospitality and family that people from other countries bring with them to Europe.
We look forward to living well together.
