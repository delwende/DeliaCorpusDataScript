2) HELLO!
In Europe,
people greet each other politely with "Hello" or "Good morning".
To say goodbye,
you say "Goodbye" or "Bye,
see you later".
If you meet a single person or a small group,
you shake hands with everyone.
You briefly look each other in the eye.
Men and women also shake hands.
Friends can also hug each other.
