3) BEING ON TIME
It is important for Europeans to be punctual.
Europe is also known for its many clocks.
If you notice that you are more than five minutes late,
it is polite to call by phone or write a SMS and apologise.
Being late is seen as a lack of respect because the other person has taken out this time to meet you.
