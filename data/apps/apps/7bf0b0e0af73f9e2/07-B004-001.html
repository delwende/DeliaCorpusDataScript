4) SPEAKING OPENLY
Europe is an amalgamation of many languages and cultures.
The many years of peaceful coexistence are also reflected in the respectful way we treat each other:
we endeavour to be open with each other.
Nevertheless,
it is important for Europeans to be honest about what they think.
They also criticise others and even their bosses,
for example at work.
This is not meant maliciously,
but is intended to help people get on well with each other and improve their work.
If you are offered something,
you can therefore honestly say whether you want it or not.
If you don't want something,
you say:
"No,
thank you."
If you want to accept something,
you say:
"Yes,
please" or "Yes,
with pleasure".
If you only want a little,
you can say:
"Just a little,
please".
Unlike some foreign cultures,
Europeans always look their interlocutors in the eyes when they speak.
If you don't,
it could make people think you're lying or have something to hide.
