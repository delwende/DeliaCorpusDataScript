5) ONE LIKES TO BE PRIVATE
It is not meant to be unkind when Europeans protect their privacy.
For example,
they like to sit alone on the bus,
close their doors in the office and many don't want to be approached on the street by people they don't know.
A friendly distance is maintained when dealing with each other.
This is why it is unlikely that you will be immediately invited home by your neighbours or acquaintances.
In the same way,
it is not customary to arrive at the homes of friends and acquaintances unexpectedly;
the custom is to make an appointment or announce yourself by telephone to find out if the friend or acquaintance can or wants to receive you.
Parents also want this privacy for their children.
It is not customary to give sweets to children from the neighbourhood or to touch them.
It is better to ask the parents beforehand what is okay.
