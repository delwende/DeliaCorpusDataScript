“Ahwehwɛ” a ɛkyerɛ yɛn amammerɛ
Bere a yennim no, yɛn nyinaa hyɛ ahwehwɛ. Eyinom nyɛ ahwehwɛ a yɛtɔ wɔ aniwa ho oduruyɛfo sotɔɔ mu, na mmom nea amammerɛ a yenyinii wom no de hyɛ yɛn so. Saa ahwehwɛ yi na ɛkyerɛ sɛnea yebu wiase no na ɛka yɛn nneyɛe. Nanso, ɛte sɛ nea eyinom nyinaa yɛ ade a ɛfata na ɛda adi pefee ma yɛn araa ma anka yɛrentumi nkyerɛkyerɛ saa nneɛma yi mu mpo, na ɛnyɛ sɛ yɛrensakra. Ɛtɔ mmere bi a, sɛ obi yɛ n’ade wɔ ɔkwan pɔtee bi so, ɛyɛ nwonwa, anaasɛ yɛnte ase kɛkɛ a, ɛyɛ yɛn nwonwa, ebia ɛyɛ yɛn abufuw mpo: “Ɛbɛyɛ dɛn na obi atumi ayɛ n’ade saa? “, yebisa yɛn ho sɛ.
Sɛ yɛbisa yɛn ho saa asɛm yi, ɛnyɛ sɛ ahohorabɔ, na mmom sɛ ɔkwan a yɛbɛfa so akɔ akyiri a, ɛno yɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ.
Saa adwene yi a ɛfa ahwehwɛ a ɛwɔ kɔla ho no betumi aboa yɛn ma yɛate ti ahorow a edidi so yi ase a ɛte sɛ “akwantu a ɛkɔ amammerɛ ahorow mu “. Saa akwantuo yi bɛma woakɔ wo ho so te sɛ afoforɔ. Wɔbɛhyɛ wo nkuran sɛ susuw nkɛntɛnso a w’ankasa amammerɛ nya ne nnipa a wofi aman afoforo mu, a wɔhyɛ ahwehwɛ ahorow no nkɛntɛnso nso ho. Ɛyɛ asɛmmisa a ɛyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ kɛse sɛ yebehu sɛnea yɛn ahonim kyerɛ yɛn kwan wɔ ɔkwan foforo so a egyina sɛ ebia yenyinii wɔ amammerɛ a ɛkyerɛ nea ɛteɛ (anaasɛ bɔne) mu anaasɛ nea ɛne no bɔ abira wɔ amammerɛ a ɛhwɛ nidi (anaasɛ aniwu) so mu. Ntease a ɛnteɛ pii a ɛda nnipa a wofi amammerɛ afoforo mu ntam no fi saa nsonsonoe a ɛwɔ ɔkwan a wɔfa so kyerɛ ade mu yi.
