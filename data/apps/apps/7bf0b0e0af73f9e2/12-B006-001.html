6) QUIETNESS IS APPRECIATED
It is rude to be loud,
especially at lunchtime,
in the evening and on Sundays.
This is due to the fact that many Europeans like it quiet.
It is forbidden to listen to loud music or make noise in the evening after 10 pm.
Europeans also make sure that it is quieter at lunchtime and on Sundays.
Their free time and weekends are important to them.
After school or work,
many meet up and play sport or music together.
You can also make new friends there.
