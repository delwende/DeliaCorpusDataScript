7) IN ROAD TRAFFIC
In Europe,
people obey the traffic rules.
This is very important to most people.
If you don't drive properly,
you risk paying a hefty fine or causing an accident.
If there are no other signs,
the following normally applies:
If you are coming from the right,
you have right of way.
It is forbidden for drivers to talk on their mobile phones while driving.
Everyone must wear a seatbelt.
Children up to the age of twelve or up to 150 cm in height need specific child seats for their age,
otherwise they are not allowed to travel in the car.
Compliance with the traffic rules is not only the responsibility of car drivers,
but also of all road users:
All types of vehicles,
whether motorized or not,
including travel with animals or on foot.
