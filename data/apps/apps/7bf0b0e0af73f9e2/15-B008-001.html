8) PUBLIC TRANSPORT
Every city has a slightly different system for buses and trains.
They usually run on time.
Delays are usually indicated from as little as three minutes.
Journey times are displayed at the stops.
Tickets for trains,
buses or trams are often bought from a ticket machine,
sometimes also at a ticket counter and often by internet.
Sometimes tickets have to be stamped before travelling.
Most people are helpful if you ask them for help in this context.
Tickets are not always checked,
but if you are caught without a ticket,
you will have to pay a fine.
