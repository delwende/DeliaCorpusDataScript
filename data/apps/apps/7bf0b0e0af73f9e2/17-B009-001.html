9) EATING AND DRINKING
Depending on the country in Europe,
there are different typical dishes.
Many people also like to have a glass of alcohol with their meal from time to time.
This is also permitted in public from the age of 16 or 18.
Spirits (schnapps,
liqueurs,
etc.)
may only be drunk from the age of 18.
However,
there are also many people who do not drink alcohol.
It is always okay to refuse alcohol.
A lot of pork is also eaten in Europe.
If you are not sure whether it is pork,
you are always welcome to ask.
Almost everywhere there are also dishes without meat,
as some Europeans eat a vegetarian or even vegan diet.
Eating or passing something with your left hand is not considered improper,
it is quite normal.
On the other hand,
it is considered impolite to slurp heavily while eating or to reach far across the table.
If you need something that you cannot reach yourself,
you ask for it politely.
Make sure that no crumbs fall on the floor.
You may look out for cheap shops (in Switzerland,
for example,
these are Denner,
Migros,
Coop,
Aldi and Lidl).
Especially in cities,
there are also Turkish and Arabic shops with halal food.
