10) It's time to celebrate!
Many public holidays in Europe can be traced back to the Christian faith.
Many Europeans no longer officially belong to a Christian church.
Nevertheless,
public holidays still play an important role.
They are often celebrated within the family.
CHRISTMAS:
BIRTHDAY CANDLES FOR JESUS
The most important family celebration in Europe is Christmas,
always from 24 - 26 December.
Children in particular look forward to it long in advance.
The four weeks before Christmas are called Advent.
Christmas is the birthday celebration for Jesus.
He was born over 2000 years ago.
Jesus was born in a stable because all other shelters were occupied.
His mother Mary laid him in the manger of the animals.
This is depicted in many pictures:
The newborn child lies in the manger,
next to him we see Mary and Joseph,
her husband.
Sometimes the animals in the stable are also shown:
ox and donkey.
The birth of Jesus is a happy occasion,
which is why music can be heard everywhere.
There are many Christmas carols.
Houses are decorated,
people stroll through Christmas markets,
and lights and candles can be seen everywhere.
They are a sign that Jesus is the light.
In the Bible,
Jesus says about himself:
"I am the light of the world.
Whoever follows me will not walk in darkness,
but will have the light of life" (The Bible.
John 8:12).
On Christmas Eve,
24 December,
some families go to church together in the evening.
Usually,
there is a special festive meal at home and everyone receives presents.
