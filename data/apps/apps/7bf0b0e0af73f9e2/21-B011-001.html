11) EASTER:
SIN AND DEATH ARE CONQUERED
Easter is the highest Christian festival because it reminds us of the most important message of the Christian faith:
Jesus,
the Messiah and Saviour and Lord sent by God,
died and rose again so that sin and death are defeated once and for all.
Jesus paid the ransom for each one of us to free us from sin,
because man is unable to do it himself.
As soon as man accepts this for himself,
he can live in peace with God and remain with him after death.
Easter is celebrated in spring,
when nature wakes up after the winter and everything turns green again.
It is a sign of Jesus and his resurrection.
