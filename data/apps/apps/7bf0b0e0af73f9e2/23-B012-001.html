12) PENTECOST:
THE SPIRIT COMES AND THE CHURCH IS BORN
After Jesus was resurrected,
he talked a lot with his disciples and they could see that he was truly alive again.
Shortly afterwards,
Jesus returned to heaven.
The disciples continued to meet regularly to pray.
During one meeting,
there was a tremendous roar in the room and something flaming descended on everyone.
The disciples were filled with the Holy Spirit.
This was also evident in the fact that they were suddenly able to speak different languages and the people in the city,
who came from many different countries,
could understand them.
The Bible reports that around 3,000 people began to believe in Jesus on this day and joined the Christians.
This is what the feast of Pentecost commemorates:
the coming of the Holy Spirit and the birth of the Christian church.
