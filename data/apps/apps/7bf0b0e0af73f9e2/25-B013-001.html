13) THE TEN COMMANDMENTS.
In the Bible,
we read that God himself gave laws to Moses and his people.
These laws also shaped the legal system in Europe and are known as "The Ten Commandments".
1) I am the Lord your God,
who delivered you from slavery in Egypt.
You shall have no other gods but me (...).
2) You shall not make for yourself an image to worship.
3) You shall not pronounce the name of the Lord your God in vain.
4) Remember the Sabbath and keep it holy.
Six days in the week you shall labour (...),
but the seventh day is a day of rest for the Lord your God.
5) Honour your father and your mother.
Then you will live long in the land that the Lord your God will give you.
6) You shall not kill.
7) You shall not commit adultery.
8) You shall not steal.
9) You shall not make a false statement about any of your neighbours.
10) You shall not covet your neighbour's property:
his house,
his wife,
his male slave,
his female slave,
his ox,
his donkey or anything else that belongs to your neighbour.
(The Bible.
Exodus 20:2-17)
 
The Bible also says that people cannot keep all the commandments on their own.
But God provided a way for people who have become guilty to become clean again.
Jesus was sacrificed "like a lamb" and thus took the shame upon himself once and for all.
"Whoever believes in him is freed from all guilt and declared righteous before God".
(The Bible:
Acts 15:34-39).
