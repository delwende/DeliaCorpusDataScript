14) OFFICES:
SOMETIMES COMPLICATED,
BUT FAIR
Offices close on time in Europe.
It is therefore a good idea to know the opening hours and to arrive at least half an hour before closing time.
Forms and offices can be very complicated.
The reason for this is that everything is done according to fixed procedures so that everyone is treated equally and fairly.
Of course,
this takes time.
It is against the law to give gifts or money to civil servants.
This is considered as bribery and is penalised.
