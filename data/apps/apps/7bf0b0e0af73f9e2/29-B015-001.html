15) COMPULSORY SCHOOLING
School attendance is compulsory in Europe from the age of five or six,
depending on the country.
Pupils are only allowed to stay at home if they are ill and have excused themselves.
School attendance is free of charge.
From the age of four,
children usually attend kinder garden.
This prepares them for school in a playful way.
Primary school lasts six years,
depending on the country,
followed by several years of upper school.
After this compulsory schooling,
young people either attend a university or they do an apprenticeship in a company.
