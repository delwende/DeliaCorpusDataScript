16) LAW:
ALL ARE FREE AND EQUAL
Freedom and equality are among the highest values in the European constitutions.
All people have the same dignity and all people are free.
They can say what they think,
can decide freely according to their conscience and are equal before the law.
This freedom ends where the freedom of another person is restricted.
 
SPEAK YOUR MIND FREELY
Freedom also means that everyone is allowed to express their own opinion.
Even if it is directed against authorities and religions,
even if it is considered distasteful.
Women wearing short skirts or couples kissing is part of everyday life in Europe.
Photographing private individuals without their permission is considered impolite.
 
STATE AND RELIGION ARE SEPARATE
Freedom of religion prevails.
The state and religion are separate.
Every person is therefore free to choose their faith.
Europe was shaped by the Christian faith.
The Bible is about loving God and loving your neighbour as yourself.
That is why love of neighbours is one of the fundamental values in Europe.
This includes treating others with respect and honesty.
 
MEN AND WOMEN HAVE THE SAME RIGHTS
"All people are equal before the law",
is stated in many European constitutions.
And:
"Men and women have equal rights".
Women are allowed to do and say the same things that men are allowed to do.
There are very strict laws against violence against women and children.
Children have a right to an upbringing without beatings and other violence.
This is also very important to Europeans in everyday life.
