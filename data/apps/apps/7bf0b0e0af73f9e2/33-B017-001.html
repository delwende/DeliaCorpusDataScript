17) ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION:
BIN THE RUBBISH,
SAVE ENERGY
Taking care of nature is very important.
Europeans separate their rubbish so that it can be recycled.
They don't just throw their rubbish on the ground when they are outside.
This can even be penalised with a fine.
If there is no rubbish bin,
they keep their rubbish until they see the next bin or take it home with them.
Waste disposal is subject to a charge in a lot of European countries.
For a fee,
you can buy rubbish bags or sticker labels to pay this charge.
Only rubbish in the right bags or with the right labels is taken away.
In other countries,
it is part of the local tax.
In some cities,
you pay by volume or weight.
Europeans also do their best to save energy,
which is why they close fridges at home and in the shops as soon as they have taken something out.
They don't leave water running unnecessarily long.
Because environmental protection is very important to people,
they often find it disrespectful when others do not comply.
