18) WHAT DO THESE SYMBOLS MEAN?
The top sign 1) means hospital.
Every hospital also has an emergency department that is open at all times for urgent cases.
If you can no longer drive to the hospital yourself,
call the emergency number (for example 112) and an ambulance will come.
The sign 2) means pharmacy.
This is where you can get the right medication for a simple cold,
for example,
or advice on how to treat a wound.
Sign 3):
Parking spaces and seats on the bus with this sign are reserved for wheelchair users and other people with disabilities and should be left free.
Space is also made for pregnant women and older people.
The sign 4):
There is a toilet here,
but no shower.
Men go to the men's toilet,
women to the women's toilet.
To pee and poo in public is considered as shameful and can be penalised.
Only toilet paper is thrown into the toilet,
all other rubbish (including sanitary towels and tampons) into the bin next to it.
The sign 5):
Smoking is forbidden!
In restaurants smoking is only allowed in certain designated areas,
Smoking is also not allowed everywhere in railway stations.
Do not smoke next to children,
pregnant women and non-smokers out of respect.
Cigarette butts belong in an ashtray.
The sign 6):
Tap water is normally safe to drink in Europe because it does not contain any germs.
However,
you should refrain from drinking water from taps with this sign.
