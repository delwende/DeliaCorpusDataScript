19) HOW DO CHRISTIANS LIVE THEIR FAITH?
Historically,
Europe is made up of Christian countries.
But of course,
many people now live here with other beliefs or don't believe in God at all.
In Europe,
faith is usually considered as a private matter.
People don't talk about it much.
Many prefer to express their faith through action by helping others in need.
We would like to share something of our faith here.
We are writing as committed Christians,
as people who want to follow Jesus Christ.
We are not perfect;
we do not say that we know everything.
But we believe in a perfect God and are happy to enter into dialogue.
A HOLY BOOK
The faith of Christians is based on the Bible.
It is the most printed and most read book in the world.
Parts of the Bible are available in almost 3,000 languages.
Many millions of Bibles are distributed every year.
It tells of Adam and Eve,
of Noah and Abraham,
of Moses and all the other prophets.
The Bible is full of great texts that are worth discovering.
There are some prejudices against the Bible.
Some say it is outdated and no longer fits our times.
Others believe that parts of the Bible have been falsified or simply changed.
This is not true.
The Bible is the best surviving book of antiquity.
The translations of the Bible into our modern languages are very accurate and reliable.
A PERSONAL GOD
The Bible contains the great story from the creation of the earth to the end of time.
At the beginning,
the Bible tells us how God created everything - all people,
all animals,
all plants.
Everything!
He doesn't just watch;
he desires communion with mankind.
God is described in the Bible as an all-powerful and merciful God who wants to have a relationship with people.
He lovingly created a large garden for people to live in.
But something came in between.
