20) SALVATION IS COMING
When Adam and Eve lived in God's garden,
they did not follow God's rules.
They wanted to be like God and decide for themselves.
This brought sin into the world.
Humans therefore have a sinful nature.
And thus,
the fellowship between God and man is disturbed,
defiled.
But throughout the Bible,
the prophets announce a saviour who will restore communion between man and God.
This Savior is Jesus Christ.
JESUS IS ON THE SIDE OF THE WEAK
Jesus Christ is the big theme in the Bible.
He is the Word of God.
He was foretold by the prophets.
The Bible says about Jesus' actions:
"For all the prophets and the law foretold this time" (The Bible.
Matthew 11:13).
He is finally born in the town of Bethlehem.
The Bible says:
Jesus came "to seek and to save that which was lost" (The Bible.
Luke 19:10).
The Bible describes how he went to the sick and the outcast,
to the sinners and the weak.
He healed sick people and even raised the dead.
And finally,
he conquered death himself.
SALVATION IS CERTAIN
When his time had come,
Jesus allowed himself to be captured and nailed to the cross.
His mission was to bring salvation.
He was ready to do so.
The Bible says that he died on the cross,
was laid dead in a tomb - but rose again the third day:
he came back to life.
In the Bible,
he is called the "Lamb of God" because he sacrificed himself and freed people from their guilt once and for all.
He conquered death and sin.
Forever.
As the only person who was ever perfect and without sin,
Jesus suffered in the place of mankind.
He paid for them.
Anyone who believes in Jesus Christ is cleansed.
And thus,
the fellowship that God wanted to live with mankind from the very beginning is possible again.
The Bible says that those who believe in him can be sure and certain that they will live with him forever,
even after death.
JESUS PERFORMED MIRACLES
Jesus was born in a stable because his family had no better place to stay along the way.
Jesus was human.
But Jesus also repeatedly declares in the Bible that he is more than a human being and equates himself with God.
He says:
"I am the bread of life" and in another place he even says:
"I am the resurrection and the life" (The Bible.
John 6:51 and John 11:25).
If he were only a man,
he would not be able to say such things about himself.
He also performed miracles that a human being could not do.
One day,
some people brought a paralytic to him on a stretcher.
When he saw their faith,
Jesus said to the paralytic:
"Take courage,
my son!
Your sins are forgiven".
But a few wise men secretly thought indignantly,
"This is blasphemy"!
Jesus perceived what they were thinking,
so he said,
“Why are you thinking such evil things?
Is it easier to say,
‘Your sins are forgiven,’
or to say,
‘Get up and walk’?
 I will prove to you,
then,
that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins.”
So,
he said to the paralysed man,
“Get up,
pick up your bed,
and go home!”
The man leapt to his feet and went home.
The people who had witnessed the scene were seized with awe.
They glorified God for having sent them a man with such authority.
The Bible.
Matthew 9, 1-7
