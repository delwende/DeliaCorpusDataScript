21) FAITH IS A DECISION
Before Jesus went back to heaven,
he said to his friends:
"I am leaving you a gift - my peace.
And the peace I give is not like the peace the world gives.
Therefore,
do not worry and do not be afraid" (The Bible.
John 14:27).
Christians believe and experience that Jesus is still alive today and knows and loves every individual personally.
Christians follow Jesus,
talk to him,
listen to his answers and orient their lives according to what he has taught them.
Believing in Jesus is a decision that everyone can make for themselves.
This decision is possible no matter what culture,
country or religion someone comes from.
TOGETHER WITH OTHER CHRISTIANS
Coming together with other followers of Jesus is important to Christians.
There are many different churches in Europe:
Protestant,
Catholic,
Orthodox,
free churches.
Large and small communities.
Men and women celebrate their services there together,
usually on Sundays.
They sing,
pray,
Bible texts are read out,
someone preaches and afterwards they often chat over coffee or tea.
In some churches,
Christians meet in small groups during the week.
They read texts from the Bible together and think about what they mean for everyday life at home,
in the family or at work.
God calls people who believe in him his children:
"To all who received Jesus and believed in his name,
he gave the right to become children of God" (The Bible.
John 1:12).
Because God is their Father,
Christians are one big family.
PRAYER IS A MATTER OF THE HEART
Jesus calls people who follow him his "beloved".
That is why Christians talk to Jesus with confidence.
They tell him what is on their minds.
They pray alone and together with others.
It doesn't depend on the right wording,
because God knows the heart of his beloved anyway.
Prayer is something very personal.
Anyone who wants to can talk to him anywhere,
anytime and about anything;
anyone can tell him their worries and fears,
their wishes and requests.
And everyone can listen to whether God also wants to say something through thoughts or signs or in dreams.
Jesus said:
"Ask and it will be given to you.
Seek and you will find.
Knock and it will be opened to you" (Luke 11:9).
