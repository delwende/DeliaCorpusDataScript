23) HOW CAN I GET TO KNOW PEOPLE IN THIS COUNTRY?
Many Europeans are very busy in their everyday lives due to work and family,
so it is sometimes difficult to strike up a spontaneous conversation with people.
Hobbies that Europeans also enjoy are a good way to get to know people.
GROUPS AND CLUBS
Many people meet up in groups and clubs after work to do sport or play music together.
For example,
there are choirs,
music groups,
sports clubs,
cookery groups and computer clubs.
People get to know each other through their shared hobby.
Information is often posted on notice boards in shops and libraries.
An overview of leisure activities can also be found on the homepage of the respective village.
WORSHIP SERVICES
Church services often take place on Sunday mornings at around 10 am.
In many churches,
you can have a chat over a cup of coffee after the service.
You can always attend church services as a guest.
Information on times and programmes is often displayed in notice boards outside the church buildings.
PLAYGROUPS FOR FAMILIES
There are often free playgroups for parents with younger children in the neighbourhood or in churches.
Parents with small children meet up there.
The children can play together and the parents can talk to each other.
