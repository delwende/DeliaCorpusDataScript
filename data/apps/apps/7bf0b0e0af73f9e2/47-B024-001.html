“Glasses” of our culture
Without realizing it,
we all wear glasses.
These are not glasses that we buy at the optician’s shop,
but those imposed on us by the culture in which we grew up.
These glasses shape our view of the world and influence our behavior.
However,
all this seems so normal and obvious to us that we would not even be able to describe these processes,
much less change them.
Sometimes we are surprised,
perhaps even indignant,
when someone behaves in a particular,
bizarre,
or simply incomprehensible way to us:
“How can someone act like that?
“,
we ask ourselves.
If we ask ourselves this question,
not as a reproach,
but as a way to go further,
that's perfect.
This idea of colored glasses can help us understand the following chapters which are like a “journey to different cultures “.
This journey will lead you as much towards yourself as towards others.
You will be encouraged to think about the influence of your own culture as well as the influence of people from other societies,
who wear different glasses.
It is more precisely a question of knowing how our conscience guides us in a different way depending on whether we grew up in a culture oriented towards right (or towards wrong) or on the contrary in a culture oriented towards honor (or shame).
Many misunderstandings between people from other cultures are due to this difference in orientation.
