Gɛlɛyaw ɲɛnabɔli
Ka ɲɛda ɲuman don ani ka i yɛrɛ tanga maloya ma, o nafa ka bon kosɛbɛ ladamu caman na, bawo o bɛ bonya jira mɔgɔ wɛrɛw la ani i yɛrɛ la. O de y’a to mɔgɔw minnu bɛ bɔ ladamu minnu na minnu bɛ bonya da u kan, olu b’a fɛ u kana kuma gɛlɛyaw kan walasa tɔ kelen ɲɛda kana bɔnɛ. Nka don o don ɲɛnamaya kɔnɔ, gɛlɛyaw man teli ka ɲɛnabɔ u yɛrɛ ye.
Misali la, jagokɛyɔrɔw la, finiko yɔrɔ min bɛ kɛ ɲɔgɔn fɛ, o ye yɔrɔ ye min bɛ se ka bɛn faga. Azi muso dɔ y’a fɔ an ye ko Farafinna sigiɲɔgɔn dɔ y’a kɔrɔfɔ finiko so cogoya kan baara kɛlen kɔfɛ. A fɔ man di nka, farafinna dimina kosɛbɛ. O kɔfɛ, a maloyara o la, fo a ma a ja gɛlɛya ka kuma Azikaw fɛ tugun. Kabini o don, ɲɔgɔnye bɛ kɛ Azi cɛ fɛ.
O sigiɲɔgɔn fila bɔra laadalakow la minnu bɛ bonya ɲini, nka u ka laadalakow tɛ kelen ye kosɛbɛ.
O muso fila ninnu bolo, a tun ka gɛlɛn ka sennateliya kɛ ka bɛnbaliya in ɲɛnabɔ u ɲɛ na. Gɛlɛya lakika tun tɛ finiko yɔrɔ saniya ye tugun, nka a tun ye ɲɛda bɔnɛni farati ye.
