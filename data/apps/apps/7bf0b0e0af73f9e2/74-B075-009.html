9)CI DA SHA
Dangane da ƙasar a Turai, akwai jita-jita na yau da kullun daban-daban. Mutane da yawa kuma suna son samun gilashin barasa tare da abincinsu lokaci zuwa lokaci. Wannan kuma an halatta shi a bainar jama'a tun daga shekara 16 ko 18. Ana iya shan ruhohi (schnapps, liqueurs, da dai sauransu) kawai daga shekaru 18. Duk da haka, akwai kuma mutane da yawa waɗanda ba sa shan barasa. Yana da kyau koyaushe a ƙi barasa.
Ana kuma cin naman alade da yawa a Turai.
Idan ba ku da tabbacin ko naman alade ne, koyaushe kuna maraba don tambaya. Kusan ko'ina kuma akwai jita-jita ba tare da nama ba, kamar yadda wasu Turawa ke cin ganyayyaki ko ma cin ganyayyaki.
Cin ko wuce wani abu da hannun hagu ba a yi la'akari da shi bai dace ba, al'ada ce. A wani bangaren kuma, ana ganin rashin mutunci ne a yi ɗimuwa da yawa yayin cin abinci ko isa ga tebur mai nisa. Idan kana buƙatar wani abu da ba za ka iya isa ga kanka ba, ka nemi shi cikin ladabi. Tabbatar cewa babu crumbs ya faɗo a ƙasa. Kuna iya neman shaguna masu arha (a Switzerland, alal misali, waɗannan sune Denner, Migros, Coop, Aldi da Lidl). Musamman a garuruwa, akwai kuma shagunan Turkiyya da Larabci da ke da abincin halal.
