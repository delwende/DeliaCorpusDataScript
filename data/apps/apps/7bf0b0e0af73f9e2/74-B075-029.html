Sasanci
Yadda za a warware rikice-rikice yayin da ba ku san al'ada daga ciki ba? Abin farin ciki, akwai hanya: sulhu.
Menene halayen matsakanci nagari? Dole ne ya sami amincewar bangarorin biyu, ko a kalla a mutunta shi. Kuma dole ne ya fahimci matsalar, ba tare da shiga tsakani kai tsaye ba.
Ga Bature, duk wannan yana buƙatar sake duba tunanin mutum. Turawa sun dauka cewa dole ne wanda ya yi laifi ya nemi gafara. A cikin mahallin girmamawa, wannan yana nuna rashin kunya da cutarwa. “Ya kuskura ya bayyana a gabana bayan duk abin da ya faru? » Mai laifin ya nuna cewa ya yi nadama, daidai da yadda ya shiga tsakani. Wannan ba madogara ba ne, kamar yadda mutum zai iya tunani ta fuskar Turai.
Shiga tsakani yana sauƙaƙa wa mutum cikin wahala sosai. Rashin hasara na wannan hanya shine cewa yana ɗaukar lokaci. Amma shin bai fi kyau a ɓata lokaci kaɗan a farkon ba don guje wa ƙarewa cikin rashin jituwa bayan faɗa kai tsaye?
A ina za mu sami ƙwararrun masu shiga tsakani? Da'irar mutanen da ke da gogewar al'adu suna haɓaka: waɗannan mutane ne waɗanda suka yi dogon lokaci a ƙasashen waje, ko kuma ƙaura na ƙarni na farko ko na biyu waɗanda ke jin daɗin al'adun biyu, ko ma mutanen da ke da masaniyar yin sulhu. Duk suna iya koyo, ta hanyar rashin gujewa rikice-rikice tsakanin al'adu, amma ta hanyar neman mafita mai ma'ana, don haka sun cancanci zama masu shiga tsakani.
Ba tare da sasantawa ba, akwai haɗarin cewa kowane ɓangare zai nemi tabbaci da jituwa a cikin ƙungiyar zamantakewar jama'a mafi maraba, kuma wannan shine yawancin rukunin da ke da irin wannan al'ada. Tare da kowace gazawar, takaici yana ƙaruwa, kuma rata tsakanin ƙungiyoyi yana ƙaruwa.
