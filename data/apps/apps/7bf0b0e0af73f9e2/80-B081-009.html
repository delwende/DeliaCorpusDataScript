9) KURYA no KUNYWA
Ukurikije igihugu cyo mu Burayi, hari ibyokurya bisanzwe. Abantu benshi bakunda kandi kunywa ikirahuri cya alcool hamwe nifunguro ryabo rimwe na rimwe. Ibi biremewe kandi kumugaragaro kuva kumyaka 16 cyangwa 18. Imyuka (schnapps, liqueurs, nibindi) irashobora gusinda gusa kuva kumyaka 18. Ariko, hariho nabantu benshi batanywa inzoga. Burigihe nibyiza kwanga inzoga.
Ingurube nyinshi nazo ziribwa mu Burayi.
Niba utazi neza niba ari ingurube, burigihe urakaza neza kubaza. Hafi ya hose hari ibyokurya bidafite inyama, kuko abanyaburayi bamwe barya ibikomoka ku bimera cyangwa se ibiryo bikomoka ku bimera.
Kurya cyangwa gutambutsa ikintu ukoresheje ukuboko kwawe kwi bumoso ntabwo bifatwa nkibidakwiye, nibisanzwe. Ku rundi ruhande, bifatwa nk'ubupfura kunyerera cyane mu gihe urya cyangwa kugera kure y'ameza. Niba ukeneye ikintu udashobora kugeraho wenyine, urasaba ubupfura. Menya neza ko nta bisambo bigwa hasi. Urashobora kureba kumaduka ahendutse (mubusuwisi, kurugero, aba ni Denner, Migros, Coop, Aldi na Lidl). Cyane cyane mumijyi, hariho amaduka ya Turukiya nicyarabu afite ibiryo bya halale.
