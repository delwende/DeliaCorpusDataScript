10) Igihe kirageze cyo kwishimira!
Iminsi mikuru myinshi i Burayi irashobora kuva mu kwizera kwa gikristo. Abanyaburayi benshi ntibakiri mu itorero rya gikristo. Nubwo bimeze bityo, iminsi mikuru rusange iracyafite uruhare runini. Bakunze kwizihizwa mumuryango.
NOHELI: AMAFARANGA YAVUKA YESU
Ibirori byumuryango byingenzi muburayi ni Noheri, burigihe kuva 24 - 26 Ukuboza. Abana byumwihariko barabitegereje mbere. Ibyumweru bine mbere ya Noheri yitwa Adiventi. Noheri ni umunsi mukuru w'amavuko kuri Yesu. Yavutse hashize imyaka irenga 2000. Yesu yavukiye mu kiraro kubera ko ubundi buhungiro bwose bwari butuwe. Nyina Mariya amushyira mu kiraro cy'inyamaswa. Ibi bigaragarira mu mashusho menshi: Umwana wavutse aryamye mu kiraro, iruhande rwe tubona Mariya na Yozefu, umugabo we. Rimwe na rimwe, inyamaswa ziri mu kiraro nazo zirerekanwa: impfizi n'indogobe.
Ivuka rya Yesu ni ibihe bishimishije, niyo mpamvu umuziki ushobora kumvikana ahantu hose. Hano hari karoli nyinshi. Amazu arimbishijwe, abantu bazenguruka mumasoko ya Noheri, kandi amatara na buji birashobora kugaragara ahantu hose. Ni ikimenyetso cyerekana ko Yesu ari umucyo. Muri Bibiliya, Yesu avuga ibye kuri we ati: "Ndi umucyo w'isi. Uzankurikira ntazagenda mu mwijima, ahubwo azagira umucyo w'ubuzima" (Bibiliya. Yohana 8:12). Ku mugoroba wa Noheri, ku ya 24 Ukuboza, imiryango imwe ijya gusengera hamwe nimugoroba. Mubisanzwe, murugo hari ifunguro ryihariye ryibirori murugo kandi buriwese yakira impano.
