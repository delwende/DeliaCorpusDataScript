23) NASHOBORA GUTE KUMENYA ABANTU MURI IKI GIHUGU?
Abanyaburayi benshi bahuze cyane mubuzima bwabo bwa buri munsi kubera akazi nimiryango, kubwibyo rimwe na rimwe biragoye gutangiza ibiganiro byizana n'abantu. Ibyishimisha Abanyaburayi nabo bishimira ni inzira nziza yo kumenya abantu.
AMATSINDA N'AMASOMO
Abantu benshi bahurira mumatsinda na clubs nyuma yakazi kugirango bakore siporo cyangwa bakine umuziki hamwe. Kurugero, hariho amakorari, amatsinda yumuziki, clubs za siporo, amatsinda yo guteka na clubs za mudasobwa. Abantu bamenyana binyuze mubyo basangiye. Amakuru akunze kumanikwa kumatangazo mumaduka no mumasomero. Incamake y'ibikorwa byo kwidagadura urashobora kubisanga kurupapuro rwumudugudu.
SERIVISI ZO GUSENGA
Ibikorwa by'itorero bikunze kuba ku cyumweru mugitondo nka saa kumi. Mu matorero menshi, urashobora kuganira hejuru yikawa nyuma yumurimo. Urashobora guhora witabira ibikorwa byitorero nkumushyitsi. Ibisobanuro ku bihe na gahunda bikunze kugaragara mu mbaho ​​zimenyekanisha hanze yinyubako zitorero.
IMIKINO YO MU MURYANGO
Hariho udukino twubusa kubabyeyi bafite abana bato mubaturanyi cyangwa mumatorero. Ababyeyi bafite abana bato bahurira aho. Abana barashobora gukina hamwe kandi ababyeyi barashobora kuganira.
