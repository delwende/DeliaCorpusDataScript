Nigute kwizera kuvuka?
Mu muco ushakisha cyane cyane kugabanya ibidashidikanywaho bishoboka (ibi ni ko bimeze mu bihugu by’Uburayi), tubanze dusabe ko abantu bubaha amategeko. Muri uyu muco, ntabwo wubaka umubano kugeza igihe uzi neza ko ushobora kubizera.
Ibinyuranye, mumico nyafurika nuburasirazuba, itumanaho rishyirwaho vuba, riterwa no gushishikazwa nibiri mumahanga. Niyo mpamvu kwakira abashyitsi ari ngombwa. Noneho, umubano urakomera uko tumenyana, kandi nyuma yubu buryo niho tubona mubice dushobora kwizera. Icyizere cyubakiye ku mibanire, ntabwo ari ukundi.
Aha niho kugerageza gushiraho umubano nabasaba ubuhunzi byananiranye. Abanyaburayi baribwira bati: "Nigute nshobora gushiraho umubano mugihe ntazi niba inkuru ye ari impamo? Kubera gutinya gutenguha, nahisemo kwirinda. ” Ku ruhande rwe, usaba ubuhungiro aribwira ati: "Nigute nakubwira amateka yanjye niba ntakuzi kandi sinzi neza ko utazambwira?"
Nigute dushobora kubaka umubano wizerana? Dore bimwe mubisubizo:
  • Ntugashyireho amategeko nkibisabwa.
  • Witondere abandi.
  • Shakisha umubonano: ntacyo bisaba usibye igihe, ariko iki gihe kiboneka nyuma mugihe havutse ikibazo.
Ntabwo ari impinduramatwara cyane, ariko icyangombwa ntabwo ari ukubanza gutegereza ibintu byose kurindi, ahubwo ni ugutera intambwe yambere.
Icyizere nikintu cyagaciro gishobora gutangwa gusa nubwitonzi nubwenge.
