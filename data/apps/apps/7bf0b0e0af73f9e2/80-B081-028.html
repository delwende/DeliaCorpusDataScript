Gukemura Ibibazo
Kwambara isura nziza no kwirinda isoni bifite agaciro gakomeye mumico myinshi, kuko byerekana ko wubaha abandi ndetse nawe wenyine. Niyo mpamvu abantu bava mumico bumva bubaha icyubahiro bahitamo kutavuga kubibazo kugirango undi muntu atabura isura. Ariko mubuzima bwa buri munsi, ibibazo ntibikunze kwikemurira ibibazo.
Kurugero, mumitungo ikodeshwa, icyumba cyo kumeseramo ni ahantu hafite ubushobozi bwo kwica ubwumvikane. Umugore wo muri Aziya yatubwiye ko umuturanyi wo muri Afurika yamututse uko icyumba cyo kumesera kimaze gukoreshwa. Kubwamahirwe, umunya nyafurika yararakaye cyane. Nyuma yaho, yaramuteye isoni, ku buryo atagitinyuka kuvugana n'umunyaziya. Kuva uwo munsi, umubonano wanyuze kumugabo wa Aziya.
Abaturanyi bombi bakomoka mu mico ishaka icyubahiro, ariko imico yabo iratandukanye cyane.
Kuri aba bagore bombi, byari bigoye gutera intambwe no gukemura amakimbirane. Ikibazo nyacyo nticyari gikiri isuku yicyumba cyo kumeseramo, ahubwo cyari ibyago byo gutakaza isura.
