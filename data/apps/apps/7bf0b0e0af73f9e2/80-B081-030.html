Tanga ubufasha bufatika
Iyo amakimbirane avutse, abunzi barashobora gutanga ubufasha bufatika kubantu bubaha icyubahiro, urugero nkubufasha mubucuti nabayobozi. Umuhuza arashobora gusobanurira urukiko uko ikibazo gikemurwa n’ishyaka ryubaha icyubahiro. Mu rwego rw'amakosa, umuhuza ashobora kwerekana ukuri ko uwakoze amakosa yicuza. Kuva icyo gihe, agomba kwatura muri make amakosa ye, akirinda inzira zose zo kwitegura, ziteye isoni cyane.
Kubantu bumva ko bubaha, biragoye kandi kwerekana kunegura, kuko bashaka kwirinda undi muntu gutakaza isura. Hano na none, umuhuza arashobora gufata umwanya wo gusobanura uko ibintu bimeze atanga ibitekerezo bifite ishingiro muburyo bwapimwe kandi bukwiye, kugirango iterambere rishobore gushyirwa mubikorwa. Ibi birashobora gukenerwa hagati yibisekuru bibiri byabimukira. Mubyukuri, ababyeyi nabana ntibashimira kimwe ibintu bijyanye numuco wicyubahiro numuco wuburenganzira (cyangwa inshingano) kandi hariya, abunzi bashobora koroshya gusobanukirwa.
Abunzi ni ingirakamaro cyane mugukemura ibibazo byubuyobozi mugihe cyo kwishyira hamwe. Iyo ubuze amafaranga, fagitire ziteye isoni. Iyo utinya kwangwa icyifuzo, buri baruwa yemewe nisoko itera impungenge. Umuntu uherekeza arashobora gufasha abimukira kutibagirwa igihe ntarengwa. Niba umubano utagarukira gusa kubibazo byubuyobozi, kumva ko uri umwe birashobora gutera imbere, nibyingenzi cyane kubantu bumva neza.
