7 Katr ne soaamb gɛl baoobo.
Daar a yembre,
soaamb ne katr kẽnga gɛl baoobo.
B sẽn tar moogã faoodg-faoodgã,
soaamb wa tãsame:
- Ad-m m me ne m gɛla!
Tɩ katr sok-a:
- Yaa gɛl a wãna?
- Yaa gɛl piig la a ye.
Tɩ katr yeel tɩ yaa yẽ pag a Pok n dʋʋg n yet tɩ yẽ wa wʋke.
Soaamb yaool n miime tɩ yaa zĩrimbeed bal fass.
La a pãngã sẽn pa tõe katr yĩnga,
a yeel-a lame t'a wʋke,
tɩ katr sɩd wʋk n sui a waoogẽ.
B sẽn le tar gẽesg n tar gẽesgo,
soaamb le wa n yãa vak ne gɛla.
T’a yeele:
- Ad m vak ne gɛla.
Tɩ katr le soke:
- Yaa gɛl a wãna?
- Gɛl a yopoe.

Tɩ katr le wʋk rẽnda.
Soaamb bãnga masã tɩ pa gũusi,
yẽ pa na n kul ne gel-yend mengã ye.
Woto,
a sã n yã vak ne gɛla,

- M ba Katre!

- Woto pa yãmb pʋgdb a Kayam sẽn bĩngã?
La Katr sẽn pa mi tɩ soaamb tũnugda gɛlã baoob n tʋʋd-a wã,
a wudgda fulll n tɩ wʋke,
la a maande tɩ:
- Fo sɩd bãngame.

woto me yaa m pʋgdb a Kayam n bĩngi.
