Nhanganyaya
 
Tsamba kuVaEfeso yakanyorerwa vaKristu vari “mungozi yokushaya zvokudya zvinovaka muviri zvomudzimu, nokuti havashandisi zvokudya zvakawanda nezvinhu zvomudzimu zvavanazvo.” Tsamba iyi kuvaEfeso yakanzi ‘ bhanga remutendi, bhuku recheki, imba yepfuma yeBhaibheri iyi inotaurira maKristu nezvepfuma huru, nhaka, uye kuzara kwavainazvo muna Jesu Kristu nekereke yake ." (Johani MACARTHUR).
Iyi haisi pfuma yenyama, asi pfuma yemweya. Efeso raive guta rakakosha kwazvo, rimwe remaguta mashanu akakosha ehushe hweRoma. Zvamazvirokwazvo rakanga riri guta gurusa (kunze kweRoma) iro Pauro akashanyira. Yakanga yakapfuma zvekuti yakanzi "Bank of Asia". Vatengesi vakauya vachibva kumativi ose kuzotenga nokutengesa zvinhu zvavo. Uye vashanyi vakashanya kuzoyemura temberi yakatsaurirwa kuna mwarikadzi Artemis, chimwe chezvishamiso zvinomwe zvenyika panguva iyoyo. Netsamba yake, muapostora Pauro zvirokwazvo aida kuratidza mitezo yechechi yeEfeso, yakakomberedzwa nepfuma huru yokunyama, kuti mufaro nokugutsikana zvomuKristu hazvibvi pamashoko. Aida kuvayeuchidza nezvepfuma yechokwadi yatinayo muna Kristu, pfuma isingaparadzi uye isina anogona kuba!
Dingindira iri richiri kushanda nanhasi. Nyika yanhasi inoda kuti tidavire kuti mari inounza mufaro! Asi Shoko raMwari rinotiudza kuti idzi inhema. Chokwadi, mari inogona kutitengera zvinhu zvinoita kuti upenyu hwedu hwezuva nezuva hureruke, asi mari haina simba pakurwisana nematambudziko, kutambura uye matambudziko makuru eupenyu hwedu panyika.
Ndima inokosha muna VaEfeso iri pakutanga chaipo: Ngaarumbidzwe Mwari uye Baba vaIshe wedu Jesu Kristu, akatiropafadza nemaropafadzo ose emweya munzvimbo dzekudenga muna Kristu. VaEfeso 1, 3
Muna Jesu tine maropafadzo ESE emweya! Uye mundima dzinotevera, Pauro anotipa mamwe mashoko pamusoro pezvikomborero izvi. Mushanduro yapakuvamba yeBhaibheri yechiGiriki, ndima 3 kusvikira ku14 dzeganhuro rokutanga raVaEfeso mutsetse mumwe! Maererano naPauro, ndaza yepfuma yomudzimu yeavo vari “muna Kristu” irefu: Sarudzo, Kufanotemerwa, Kurerwa, Rudo, Rudzikinuro, Kukanganwira, Nyasha, Uchenjeri no kunzwisisa, Nhaka, Mweya Mutsvene, Tariro, Simba, Kereke
 
Mumapeji mashoma anotevera, tichadzidza chokwadi chebhaibheri ichi, asi kwete sedzidziso kana ruzivo rwemisoro yedu. Pane kudaro, tinenge tichitsvaga kuti vangave sei pfuma chaiyo yehupenyu hwedu hwezuva nezuva nezviito zvezuva nezuva.
