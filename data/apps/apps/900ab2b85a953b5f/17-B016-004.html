Adoption
 
Murudo rwake, Mwari akatitemera kare kuti tive vana vake vakagamuchirwa kubudikidza naJesu Kristu (VaEfeso 1: 5).
Chii chinonzi adoption?
Munguva yomuapostora Pauro, varoorani vasina mwana kazhinji kazhinji vaigamuchira mwanakomana anova mugari wenhaka wavo. Kunyange kana vabereki vokubereka vomwanakomana wokurera vakanga vachiri vapenyu, vakanga vasisina kodzero ipi neipi paari pashure pokunge kurerwa kwake kwapera. Plenary adoption chiitiko chiri pamutemo. Mwana haangochinji mhuri. Pane kuzorora kwakakwana nemhuri yekare. Mwana wekurerwa anochinja zita rake opihwa gwaro rekuzvarwa patsva. Haachakodzeri kuwana nhaka kubva kumhuri yake yekuzvarwa, asi akabatanidzwa zvakakwana mumutsara wemhuri yake itsva. Mhuri yachembera haisisina kodzero paari. Mune dzimwe nyika, vabereki vokubereka havasisina kana kodzero yokuona mwana wavo. Pauro anoshandisa iyi tsika yemagariro kudzidzisa chokwadi chebhaibheri chinotevera:
- Isu takasarudzwa tisati tazvarwa, asi isu tinova vana vaMwari nekugamuchirwa panguva yatinogamuchira ruponeso rwaMwari.
- Tiri vana vaMwari zvizere. Kuzorora nezvakaitika kare kwapera. "Mhuri yedu yekare" haisisina simba pamusoro pedu.
- Kugamuchirwa kwakagoneka nerufu rwaKristu. Zvakaitika panguva yatakatenda uye tikava nhengo dzemhuri yaMwari (VaRoma 8, 15), asi zvinozoonekwa zvizere patinopfeka muviri wedu wokumuka (VaRoma 8, 23).
Zviito zvekuita kuti kugamuchirwa kuve hupfumi muhupenyu hwako:
- Munamato: Tenda Mwari nemhuri yako itsva yezvemweya. Taura mazita eavo vakava vanababa naamai vechokwadi, hama nehanzvadzi mukereke nokuda kwako. Tenda Mwari nokuda kwavo.
- Kuverenga Bhaibheri: Verenga VaEfeso 4, 17 kusvika ku32. Ita runyoro rwekutanga rwemaitiro kubva kumhuri yekare iyo isingachabvumirwi mumhuri itsva, mhuri yaMwari. Zvino ita runyoro rwechipiri rwemaitiro aunofanira kudzidza sevanakomana nevanasikana vemhuri yaMwari.
-Kufungidzira pachako: Ongorora hupenyu hwako kuti uone kana uchine chero maitiro ehupenyu hwako hwekare. Kana zvakadaro, tsvaka kuvashandura, nerubatsiro rweMweya Mutsvene.
- kune vamwe: Kumbira Mwari kuti vakuratidze kuti muchechi mako ndiani anoda baba vemweya kana amai kana mukoma kana hanzvadzi. Swedera pedyo nemunhu iyeye uye umuperekedze muupenyu sezvaungaita mukoma kana hanzvadzi mumhuri yako chaiyo.
