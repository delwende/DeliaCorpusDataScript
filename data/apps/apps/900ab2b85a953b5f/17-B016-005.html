Rudo
 
Mwari anotidauye agara achida kuti tive vanakomana vake kubudikidza naJesu Kristu (VaEfeso 1, 4-5).
 
Chii chinonzi rudo rwaMwari?
Rudo rwaMwari rwakakwana, haruna magumo uye rwakachena. Hakusi kungonzwa. Mwari akaratidza rudo rwake kwatiri nechiito chinokosha zvikuru: akapa mwanakomana wake mumwe oga kuti atiponese (Johane 3, 16 ).
- Rudo rwaMwari haruna magumo: haandibvunze kuti ndivandudze hupenyu hwangu asati andiponesa! Chimiro chisingachinji cherudo rwaMwari hachichinji. Mwari anoramba achitida, kunyange tikawira muchivi, kunyange tikamusiya. Rudo rwake kwatiri runoramba rwakafanana. Hazvideredze.
- Rudo rwaMwari haruna udyire, ndiko kuti, rwakanangana nesu. Mukupa mwanakomana wake, haana kufunga nezvake, asi isu! Uye anoramba achitibatsira, kuti ave webasa kwatiri.
Rudo rwaMwari rwakakura: Mwari anotida zvokuti akapa chipo chake chinokosha kupfuura zvose, mwanakomana wake mumwe chete. Hapana chimwe chinhu chikuru chaangadai akatiitira.
Zviito zvekuita kuti rudo rwaMwari rufume muhupenyu hwako:
- Munamato: Tenda Mwari zuva nezuva nekuda kwerudo rwavo kwauri. Teerera kune zviratidzo zviduku zverudo rwaMwari mukati mezuva. Pakupera kwezuva, nyora pasi zviitiko zvinoratidza rudo rwaMwari kwauri mubhuku rokunyorera womutenda.
- Kuverenga Bhaibheri: Verenga VaEfeso 3, 17 kusvika ku19.
- kufungisisa pachako: Mundima iyi, muapostora Pauro anoshandisa mifananidzo miviri kukurudzira vaverengi vake kuti vanakidzwe norudo rwaMwari:
- dzika midzi murudo urwu: semuti unoparadzira midzi yawo muvhu kuti urege kupeperetswa nemhepo uye kuwana chikafu ...
- kuvaka parudo urwu: Rudo rwaMwari ndirwo hwaro hwakasimba, runotigonesa kuvaka nekufambira mberi muhupenyu hwedu hwemweya.
Funga nezvekuti mifananidzo iyi miviri ingakubatsira sei kurarama uye kuona rudo rwaMwari muhupenyu hwemazuva ese.
- Kune vamwe: Govera uchapupu hwako pamusoro perudo rwaMwari muupenyu hwako neshamwari dzako, mudzimai wako kana murume wako kana mabhuratha nemasisita muchechi mako. Uye varatidze nezviito zvako kuti Mwari vanovada.
