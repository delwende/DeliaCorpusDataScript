Rudzikinuro
 
Muna Jesu tine rudzikinuro kubudikidza neropa rake (VaEfeso 1, 7).
 
Chii chinonzi rudzikinuro?
Izwi rekuti “rudzikinuro” chikamu chemazwi anotsanangura ruponeso. Chaizvoizvo, zvinoreva "kusunungura kure", "kusunungurwa". Inomutsa pfungwa yerudzikinuro, semuranda, akasungwa kuna tenzi, uye anosunungurwa nekubhadhara kwechitsama chemari. Munhu muranda wechivi nemigumisiro yacho, irwo rufu. Rudzikinuro runomusunungura kubva kusimba rechivi nemigumisiro yacho yepamutemo.
Mubasa rake rerudzikinuro, Kristu akabhadhara muripo werudzikinuro kuvanhu vose. Mutengo unoratidzwa zvakajeka maererano nemasikirwo awo: iropa raKristu. Sezvo takadzikinurwa nemutengo uyu, tinofanira kushandira iye akaubhadhara (1 Vakorinde 7, 22-23).
Ruregerero runogona kupfupikiswa mupfungwa nhatu dzinokosha: (1) vanhu vanodzikinurwa kubva pane chimwe chinhu, kureva uranda hwechivi; (2) vanodzikinurwa nechimwe chinhu, nomubhadharo womutengo, iro ropa raKristu; (3) vanodzikinurwa nokuda kwechimwe chinhu, kureva kusununguka. Kana vangosunungurwa, vanokokwa kuti vazviite nhapwa dzaShe akavadzikinura.
 
Zviito zvekuita kuti ruponeso kana kuregererwa kuve hupfumi muhupenyu hwako:
- Munamato: Mumunamato wako wemazuva ese, unogona kutenda Mwari nekukuponesa kubva kuchivi, rufu, utere ...
- Kuverenga Bhaibheri: gara uchiverenga nhoroondo dzerufu rwaJesu, semuenzaniso Ruka 23, 33 kusvika ku49. Zvifungidzire uri muboka revanhu uchiona kurovererwa. Zviyeuchidze pachako nezvechibayiro chikuru chaJesu.
- Kufungisisa pachako: Wakabatsirwa nerudzikinuro rwemwanakomana wake Jesu.
