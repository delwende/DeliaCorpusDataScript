Nhaka
Muna Kristu tavewo vadyi venhaka...( VaEfeso 1, 11 ).
 
Chii chinonzi nhaka yechiKristu?
Ndiyo midziyo yakasiiwa nemunhu akafa kune vachiri kurarama, kazhinji nhengo dzemhuri.
Maererano neShoko raMwari, Mwari ane nhaka kwatiri isu vana vake. Ndirwo ruponeso rwakaunzwa naJesu Kristu runotipa kodzero yenhaka iyi. Zvose zvatinodzidza mubhuku rino chikamu chenhaka iyoyo. Pauro anoidana kuti inhaka ye“pfuma inobwinya” ( VaEfeso 1, 18 ).
Kuve vadyi venhaka zvinoreva kuti hatisati tave nepfuma iyi zvizere. Asi kana tichigara naMwari, kana Jesu noMweya Mutsvene vakagara matiri, tinogona kutoravira pfuma yaBaba vedu vekudenga. Sezvinongoita mwana anogara pachipfuva chemhuri yake anogona kutonakidzwa nezvinhu zvababa vake kusvika pamwero wakati. Uye kuwana kwedu kuzere kwenhaka yokudenga kunovimbiswa neMweya Mutsvene (ona chitsauko chinotevera).
 
Zviito zvekuita kuti nhaka ive hupfumi muhupenyu hwako:
- Munamato: Tenda Mwari nepfuma yaakakupa. Nyora imwe neimwe. Kumbira Mwari kuti akubatsire kuzvishandisa zviri nani.
- Kuverenga Bhaibheri: Verenga VaEfeso 1 uye nyora mazita ezvikomborero zvako zvepamweya.
- kufungisisa pachako: Pane nguva dzaunonzwa urombo here? Unoitira godo vamwe vanoratidzika kuva vane zvakawanda kukupfuura here? Rangarira kuti pfuma yaMwari ndiyo yauinayo. Yeuka kuti iyi pfuma isina munhu anogona kuba uye isingaparadzi.
Pakupera kwezuva, wana nguva yokufungisisa kuti ndeipi yepfuma iri muna VaEfeso 1 yawakwanisa kushandisa zuva rose. Tanga kabhuku kadiki unyore pasi maropafadzo aMwari mazuva ese.
- kune vamwe: Mwari anoda kuti tigoverane nhaka yedu yomudzimu nemhuri dzedu nehama dzedu nehanzvadzi muna Kristu. Mangwanani oga oga, sarudza imwe yehupfumi hwenhaka yako uye uwane nzira yeku "kupa" kune mumwe munhu waunosangana naye masikati.
