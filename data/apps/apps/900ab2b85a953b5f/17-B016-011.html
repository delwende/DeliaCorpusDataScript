Mweya Mutsvene
 
Muna Kristu nemiwo makatenda, makagamuchira kubva kuna Mwari Mweya Mutsvene, waakavimbisa uye waakasimbisa naye kuti muve vake. ( VaEfeso 1, 13 )
 
Zvinorevei kuiswa chisimbiso neMweya Mutsvene?
Pakati pevaJudha, chisimbiso chairatidza kuguma kwekutengeserana: apo chibvumirano chakanga chasvikwa, chibvumirano chaitwa uye mutengo wabhadharwa, chisimbiso chainamirwa pachibvumirano kuti chive chaicho. Chisimbiso chidhindo chakagadzirirwa kuvimbisa huchokwadi hwegwaro kana chidimbu cheruzivo, uye kuratidza kana zviri mukati zvakashandurwa kana kukuvara.
- Chisimbiso chinosimbisa kodzero dzemuridzi uye chinozivisa pfuma yake. Mweya Mutsvene nokudaro vanova simba rehumwari patiri. Isu tiri pfuma yaMwari.
- Chisimbiso chinoratidza kuti ibasa rechokwadi, rakapedzwa uye risingagone kutaurirana, rine chisimbiso chaMwari.
- Chisimbiso chinoratidza kuti ibasa rechokwadi, rakapedzwa uye risingagone kutaurirana, rakatakura chitambi chevane masimba. Basa reruponeso rakakwana uye harichagoni kupokana.
- Avo vanotakura gwaro rakanamwa nechisimbiso vari pasi pekuchengetedzwa kwemuridzi wechisimbiso. Mwari ndiye mudziviriri wedu. Anovimbisa kuchengeteka kwedu. Mweya Mutsvene anoitawo semutsigiri kana chitsidzo chenhaka yedu. Ani naani anoripira chitsidzo, anoita chitsidzo chakasimba kune mumwe kuti achapa zvose gare gare. Mweya Mutsvene vanotivimbisa kuzara kwenhaka yedu.
 
Zviito zvekuita kuti Mweya Mutsvene vafume muhupenyu hwako.
- Munamato: Tenda Mwari nechipo cheMweya Mutsvene. Kumbira kuti Mweya Mutsvene akuzadze zvakazara, kuti ugone kuterera inzwi rake.
- Kuverenga Bhaibheri: Verenga Evhangeri yaJohani 15, 7 kusvika ku15. Ndima idzi dzinokudzidzisei nezveMweya Mutsvene?
- kufungisisa pachako: Mucherechedzo weMweya Mutsvene unonyatsoonekwa muhupenyu hwako here? Munhu wose anogona kuona kuti uri waMwari here?
Kumbira Mweya Mutsvene kuti vakuratidze zvakaipa pauri, uye kuti vakushandure kuva mufananidzo waMwari mupenyu.
- kune vamwe: Ziva zvipo zvako zvemweya, uye tanga kuzvishandisa kuitira kunaka kwehama dzako nehanzvadzi muna Kristu.
