Chechi
 
akaisa zvinhu zvose pasi petsoka dzake, akamupa somusoro mukuru kukereke, unova muviri wake, kuzara kwaiye unozadza zvose mune zvose. (VaEfeso 1: 22-23).
Muapostora Pauro akashandisa mifananidzo yakawanda kutsanangura kuti chechi chii.
Muchikamu chedu, VaEfeso chitsauko 1, anotaura nezvemuviri waKristu (onawo 1 VaKorinte 12), uye mutsamba imwecheteyo chitsauko 3, 19 kusvika ku22, anoshandisa mufananidzo wevanhu, mhuri uye imba.
Chechi chii, muviri waKristu? Kristu ndiye musoro. Musoro ndicho chikamu chakakosha chemuviri. Iri mumusoro umo uropi huripo, umo ruzivo rwese, pfungwa uye zvisarudzo zvakasimbiswa. Ndimo munochengeterwa hungwaru, kuda uye ndangariro. Nhengo dzese nenhengo dzemuviri wedu dziri pasi pehutongi hwehuropi hwedu. Kana tichiti Kristu ndiye musoro weKereke, tinonzwisisa kuti nhengo dzayo dzese dzinotsamira paari. Mufananidzo wemuviri unosimbisawo kubatana kwekereke, kuwirirana kwenhengo dzayo uye kubatana kunofanira kutonga pakati pavo. Chechi chii, mhuri yaMwari?
Patinova vaKristu, tinovawo nhengo dzemhuri itsva. Nouyu mufananidzo, muapostora Pauro akakweva kurudziro kubva kumhuri yechiJudha, umo mutezo mumwe nomumwe wakanga une nzvimbo yakagadzwa ine mabasa nemitoro. Ndizvo zvimwe chete mukereke, umo nhengo imwe neimwe ine basa rekuita maererano nezvipo zvayo.
Humwe hunhu huviri hwakakosha hwemhuri yemaJuda hwaive kubereka zvibereko nekubatana. Kubereka zvibereko mukereke kunoreva kuikudza nokukunda vatendeuki vatsva! Solidarity zvinoreva kubatsirana panguva yenhamo.
Chechi chii, imba, chivakwa? Mufananidzo uyu unoratidza kukosha kwenheyo (Jesu Kristu) uye chokwadi chekuti kereke "iri kuvakwa", haisati yapera.
Zviito zvekuita kuti chechi ive yakakosha muhupenyu hwako:
- Munamato: Tenda Mwari nekereke yake munyika yako, nekusasimba kwayo nekuperevedza kwayo.
- Kuverenga Bhaibheri: Verenga VaEfeso 2, 13 kusvika ku22 uye fungisisa pamusoro pendima idzi.
- kufungisisa pachako: Enzanisa chechi yako yeko nemifananidzo mitatu iyi. Hunhu hwakasimbiswa naPauro huripo here? Kumbira Mweya Mutsvene kuti akuratidze zvaunogona kuita kuti kereke yako ive muviri nemhuri zvechokwadi!
- kune vamwe: Nzvera mwoyo wako kuti uone kana vakoma nehanzvadzi dzako kuchechi vave vabereki vako zvechokwadi, sezvakaita mumhuri yako chaiyo.
