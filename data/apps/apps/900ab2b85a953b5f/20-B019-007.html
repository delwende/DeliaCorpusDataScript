Bɔne fafiri
Yesu mu na yɛwɔ bɔne fafiri (Efesofo 1, 7).
 
Dɛn ne bɔne fafiri?
Asɛmfua "fakyɛ" kyerɛ sɛ wobɛpopa biribiara, wobɛpopa slate no ho tew, wobɛma adom, wobɛtwa ɛka bi mu. Wɔmfa bɔne fafiri mma efisɛ ɔfata sɛ wɔde firi obi a odi fɔ. Fafiri yɛ gyinaesi a wosi sɛ worenkura obi ho abufuw, ɛmfa ho nea wayɛ. Obiara mfata sɛ wɔde kyɛ no. Bɔnefakyɛ yɛ ɔdɔ, mmɔborohunu ne adom adeyɛ.
Bible ka kyerɛ yɛn sɛ yɛn nyinaa hia Onyankopɔn fafiri. Yɛn nyinaa ayɛ bɔne (1 Yohane 1, 8). Bɔne biara yɛ adeyɛ a ɛkyerɛ sɛ metew atua tia Onyankopɔn na ɛsɛe me ne no ntam abusuabɔ. Na ne bɔne fafiri nkutoo na ebetumi ama saa abusuabɔ no asan aba bio. Wɔrentumi mfa nnwuma pa so nnya Onyankopɔn bɔne fafiri. Worentumi ntɔ Onyankopɔn bɔne fafiri. Wobɛtumi anya no nko ara, ɛnam gyidie so, ɛnam Onyankopɔn adom ne ne mmɔborɔhunu nti. Nea ɛsɛ sɛ woyɛ ara ne sɛ wobɛsrɛ Onyankopɔn sɛ ɔmfa Yesu so mfa bɔne nkyɛ wo, a wogye di sɛ Yesu wui, sɛ ɔmfa saa bɔne fafiri yi mma wo - na Onyankopɔn de bɛkyɛ wo. Onyankopɔn bɔne fafiri yɛ nea edi mũ. Bere a wɔagye atom no, bɔne a ɛsɛee me ne Onyankopɔn ntam abusuabɔ no nni n’anim bio.
 
Nneyɛe a ɛbɛma bɔne fafiri ayɛ ahonyade wɔ w’asetra mu:
- Mpaebɔ: .Srɛ Honhom Kronkron no sɛ ɔnkyerɛ wo sɛ bɔne bi wɔ w’abrabɔ mu a. Sɛ wuhu bi a, srɛ Onyankopɔn bɔne fafiri wɔ Yesu mu. Yɛda Onyankopɔn ase wɔ ne bɔne fafiri no ho. Sɛ bɔne dedaw bi a woasrɛ bɔne fafiri dedaw no da so ara haw wo a, srɛ Onyankopɔn sɛ onnyi mfi w’adwene mu.
-Bible akenkan: Kenkan 1 Yohane ti 1, 8 kosi ti 2, 2. Bɔ mmɔden sɛ wode w’ankasa wo nsɛm bɛda nea ɔsomafo Yohane reka wɔ ha a ɛfa yɛn bɔne fafiri ho no adi.
- ankasa nsusuwii: Hwɛ yiye na woamfa Onyankopɔn bɔne fafiri no nni dwuma ɔkwammɔne so. Mma nnka sɛ "Ɛyɛ yie, Onyankopɔn de bɛkyɛ me". Saa su yi kyerɛ sɛ woredi Onyankopɔn ho fɛw, na Onyankopɔn tan saa.
- a wode kyere afoforo: Kɔ wɔn a wɔahwe ase wɔ bɔne mu na nea asi no ahyɛ wɔn so. Fa Onyankopɔn bɔhyɛ hyɛ wɔn nkuran, boa wɔn wɔ mpaebɔ mu.
