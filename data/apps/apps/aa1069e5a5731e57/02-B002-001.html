20 B kʋʋ wãamb la wãamb paam n sãama teedo.
 
21 B sã n bool ligdã rãmb kamb tɩ fo zĩi, fo n paoog f ba rãmba.
 
22 B sã n gomd lʋg yelle, pondr sĩndidame.
 
23 B sã n kõ-f bĩn-bok naam bɩ f sake, tɩ f paamda tals tɩ kõdg-a.
 
24 B sã n yeel yalem t'a suk zẽed neer tɩ sek-a, a yetame tɩ b kis-a lame.
 
25 B tẽedame tɩ bugem na rɩ sãe yir, t'a wa rɩ bag yiri.
 
26 B yeelame tɩ gãndaoog sã n be ripoore a to n be yaanga.
 
27 B yeelame tɩ gãndaoog sã n be yaanga, a to n be taoore.
 
28 B yet tɩ baag yõke, bɩ b yet t'a wõbe?
 
29 Ba-raoog pãng la a yiri.
 
30 Ba-tutg pa wʋmd wɩɩg ye.
 
31 Ba-yẽd-gõadg n baood zab n kõ baas fãa.
 
32 Ba-yir pa lobgd ne kugr ye, lobgda ne tãndagre.
 
33 Baa bʋẽeg rat paga, a ka na n bobl soaas ye.
 
34 Baa f sã n pa nong soaamba, f mi ta tʋb yaa wogdo, la f sak t'a zoetame.
 
35 Baa rapeok kaoos koomẽ a pa lebgd yẽgb ye.
 
36 Baa yũug ka yõgd noos me, a ka sõmb ne noos roogẽ gãag ye.
 
37 Baa yũug kẽng maka, a kõn bas yõns yõgb ye.
 
38 Baa zãmsd pʋgẽ, boẽeg ka wãbd bõnyẽeg ye.
 
39 Baa zũr kẽ pĩisim a kõn wẽneg bãng ye.
