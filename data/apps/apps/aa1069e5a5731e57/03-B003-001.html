40 Baag ka wʋmd a zugẽ yũug ye.
 
41 Baag mii a sẽeg yalem la a vend kẽgla.
 
42 Baag rat kũum bɩ a wes wẽnde.
 
43 Bag sã n bʋg-f bɩ f bʋg f menga.
 
44 Baag sũ-noog bãngda a zʋʋrẽ.
 
45 Bagr yaa yam.
 
46 Bakarg natẽng la a rasem-pɩʋʋ go.
 
47 Barg pa barga, la zug n pa zugu.
 
48 Bars toog vẽnegda koaas yam.
 
49 Bas m nug yaa ra bas tɩ b gãde.
 
50 Base base, ka bugum to ye.
 
51 Bãad-wagr n são ne laasr kɩbare.
 
52 Bãmb ka rat yaa bãmb na waa n yʋbe.
 
53 Bãn-kẽes biig kiida ne yo-gãongo.
 
54 Bãng f to la f bãng f menga.
 
55 Bãngẽ yaa pag bi-yaooga.
 
56 Be-zʋʋr pa gãngd bugum ye.
 
57 Beegẽ mi tĩiri n mi tẽng wãabo.
 
58 Beleng n tõe kʋɩɩma.
 
59 Bendr na boos n kõ lʋnga.
