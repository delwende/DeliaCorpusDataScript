180 F sã n wa tɩ lubd gomtɩʋʋg n yeel tɩ yaa wiuugu fo n bao bũmb fãa yaa gũusgu.
 
181 F sã n yã tɩ weef zoet ne gaar bɩ f bãng t'a luba neda.
 
182 F sã n yã yũug ne wĩin bɩ f bas-a la f looge.
 
183 F sã n yeel tɩ wĩntoog kẽ-f lame, f mikdame tɩ beoog vẽega nina t'a kẽnda.
 
184 F sã n yet tɩ f kõn tʋm tʋʋm-yalenga, yãn-yaleng n yãt-fo.
 
185 F sã n yet tɩ f tẽng zãrame, yaa ziri bala tẽng a to n be taoore.
 
186 F sã n yet tɩ fo pa sõsd ne ned maasem a ye, yaa ziri, yaa zĩig n pa sobge.
 
187 F sã n yẽ zĩm n da bʋʋg tɩ yẽbg yõke, a leba a zĩigẽ.
 
188 F sã n yi f zakẽ n bao ned yɩnga, f mikdame tɩ ned yɩɩd-fo.
 
189 F tõe tɩ baag gãand la f pa tõe t'a mum a nin ye.
 
190 Fãob pa lubd lolg ye.
 
191 Fo sã n maan ned neere, a rond-f-la beem.
 
192 Fo sã n pa bas wĩntoog wẽdga, f basda yʋng wẽdga.
 
193 Fo sã n vʋʋg ned yoore, a baooda f paga.
 
194 Fo sã n yeel biig t'a ra tu bogd tɩ yaa wiisi, t'a yet tɩ yẽ baooda wag-bi bɩ f bas-a.
 
195 Fo sã n yiis biig saagẽ, f yiisda f menga.
 
196 Fo tũuda tũng nao n tʋg n dɩk f kãande.
 
197 Gar pa yão ye.
 
198 Gãeeng me tara zoa.
 
199 Gãeeng zug sã n kẽ, a to n põngd-a.
