240 Katr na ki t'a ka walem baag ye.
 
241 Katr ne baag beem ka tõe n sa ye.
 
242 Katr ne gadrã fãa, baag zɛt-a lame.
 
243 Katr pa gũlgd bʋʋg ye.
 
244 Katr yet f tõog ne f tõog me, zãnkit yaa n wa kẽ f noorẽ tɩ b yet tɩ foo n be yãoodo?
 
245 Kãar be gũuda, bɩ noor togs a sẽn date.
 
246 Kãnd fãa tara a gãdb zĩiga.
 
247 Kãnenkãag roog wẽnda sĩ-vagse, la a ka mi bũmb a to sẽn ka rũmb ye.
 
248 Kãnkã-wɩɩsdb yẽsda ne taab tɩ sebg pa yi ye.
 
249 Kãnkãn-dɩgr n dog ligdi, n na n malg zɛns la wãooba.
 
250 Kãnkãr ra rɩk a meng n mak ne raoog tɩ koom n sek-a ye.
251 Kãnkãr ra wɩd a taab tɩ b fãa yaa rʋda.
 
252 Kãoong miime tɩ noaag pa a ma ye, la a pʋgd-a lame.
 
253 Kãoong yet t'a solga a meng n tũud noaaga.
 
254 Kẽnd n bʋt yaa beem tẽnga.
 
255 Kẽeg wook bee tɩɩm, la kẽeg koẽeg ka be tɩɩm ye.
 
256 Ki n boond noaaga.
 
257 Ki na yɩ neer bãngda a vãadẽ.
 
258 Kĩnkirs roog rat wãabo, tɩ b kẽnd n sõsd kɩparengẽ.
 
259 Kɩba wẽng pa loogd t'a pa pɩʋʋg n pʋʋs a soab ye.
