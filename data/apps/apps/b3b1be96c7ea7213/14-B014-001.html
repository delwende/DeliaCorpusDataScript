851 No aduna waylorii fu nyaalal tokkataa rawaandu.
852 No awotooɗo yiiri ndiyam fu, noon naatirta ɗam.
853 No ɓokki foti mawnirde fuh e waalolde yalti.
854 No jemma niɓɓiri fu, haɗataa wilwilndu heɓude ko nyaama.
855 No koosaajo ƴoƴiri fuh, naa may yoppida fulɓe e na'i.
856 No ladde sukkiri fuh, giire ronkataa laawol.
857 No mbanyirɗaa nyiiwa fuh, a wi’ataa heewataa fayannde.
858 No ndontoori mawniri fuh semtataa daraade sera unooɓe.
859 No neɗɗo naywiri fuh, ta yeggita ladiino.
860 No ngaɗɗaa faa ɓirɗaa lewla nih, so a waɗii noon a ɓiran mbeewa.
861 No ngesa boniri fuh, sonnataake nyannde tayri.
862 No njiɗɗaa hammadi maa wa'a noon, wo hammadi goɗɗo wa'ata noon.
863 No njiɗuɗa ɓiya wa’a noon , wo hammadi goɗɗo wa’ata noon.
864 No noowra ɓooyiri e seeno fuh, yeggitataa njinam.
865 No polle lammiri fuh, yottataako jamme.
866 No weendu heewiri fuh, na yiɗi hebbiteede.
867 No wolde boniri fuh, heddoto kaaltoowo.
868 No wolde wa’i fuh, na ɓuri wiide yah mi yoppidii ma e Alla.
869 No wuro woɗɗiri fuh, wonngo na yeesom.
870 No ƴoogoowo ɓooyiri fu, yo waddu ndiyam laaɓuɗam.
