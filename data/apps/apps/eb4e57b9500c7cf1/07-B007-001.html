7 Bi-bɩɩg a yembr kibare.
Bi-bɩɩg a ye n paam zu-noogo, tɩ Wẽnnaam ning-a barka, t’a sɩng tolbo, n daad ki, bɛnga, la kood buud toor-toore, n paas ne rũm-bãoonego, n leebdẽ, tɩ sɩd tar barka.
La, yʋʋm piig loogr poore, a arzɛkã sɩd fɩɩga sõma.
La rao wã, sẽn da tar sulg-m-meng yĩnga, kɩtame, tɩ nebã ra waoogd-a.
Ra yaa ned me, sẽn da get a roagdbã yell sõma.
La, nebã wa n sɩngame, n yet rao wã:
«Haya!
fo sã n ka gilim n bao kogl-m-menga, f bɛɛbã na n kʋ-f lame, wala b sãam f paoongã ».
La rẽ tɛka, rao wã pa le tõe n gũs ye.
Tɩ bala, a ra tara tagsg wʋsg a mengã ne a paoongã yĩnga.
T’a na n dɩk yellã, n togs a zoa a yembre.
La, a zoa wã, yãag-a-la tɩɩm soab nengẽ, n tɩ ta n togs a zu-loɛɛgã.
La tɩɩmã soab yeel-b lame :
«Yãmb sẽn ta ka wã, bɩ y ra le maan yɩɩr ye.
Yãnd ka na n yã yãmb ye, y yellã manegame.
Tɩɩm ning mam sẽn na n kõ foomã, fo sã n wa n lebg n ta yiri, bɩ f ning-a bẽerẽ, n dɩ la f tall raoodo.
Ned sã n bao n na n maan foom wẽng marsã, wẽngã na n leba a soaba.
La, tɩɩmã kisa fo sẽn na n kãab ned wẽnga, bala, kãabgã na n paama pidsgu, a soabã vɩɩm pʋgẽ, fo me na n paama yelle».
B sẽn sɩd reeg tɩɩmã n kuili, b sɩd tɩ maana wa a sẽn togsã.
La, biigã sẽn bãng tɩ ned a to ka le tõe n maan-a wẽngã, wuk-m-meng kẽ-a lame, t’a toor ka le be ne ned a to yas ye.
La, moos maana b yel-bũndi :
«Lar sã n noome, a to n kɛ a saala».
A tʋʋm-yoodã, la nebã kãabgã, la b paoogrã, Wẽnnaam bas-a lame, t’a paoongã sat n dabda, t’a zakã pʋga, wʋm-taab ka le be yẽ ne a pagbã sʋk ye.
Bi-bɩɩgã yõor yɩɩ koɛɛga, bala, a ka le tõe n vɩɩmde, wa a sẽn da tar ligda wakat ye.
Ra lebga sũ-toogo, rɩɩbã ka le yaa sõma wa pĩnda ye.
Yẽ ne a zĩnd-n-taasã, la tẽngã nebã, ra yaa beem.
Rẽ wã, wuk-m-meng rolb yaa lʋɩɩse
